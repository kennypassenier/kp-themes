Navigation

Every way the package takes a reader somewhere else: the bar .kp-nav in its .kp-nav-wrap with a dropdown .kp-nav__menu, .kp-breadcrumb, .kp-pagination, .kp-tabs, the side navigation .kp-sidenav in its three shapes, the command palette's trigger .kp-nav__search, and the back-to-top control .kp-to-top. Whatever opens is shown open, and the two extremes a bar meets in real life are here too: fourteen items, and one label sixty characters long.

Bar

Look at: the bar is on the card surface with a strong rule under it, and must read as the top of a page rather than as a card lying on one. The brand leads; the file code beside it (.kp-nav__brand-tag) is quieter than the brand, not a second brand — and still readable: in nostromo it reads at better than 4.5:1 on the dark strip, where it was 1.8:1. Incidents is the current page: it must be marked by more than colour — weight and an underline — and a screen reader hears aria-current. It keeps the size and the baseline of the other items: in nostromo its LED dot sits centred beside the text instead of pushing Incidents 1.6px below its neighbours. Tab through the links: the focus ring must stay visible on the bar's own ground, and no link may shift its neighbours when it takes focus or hover.

Dropdown

Look at: the dropdown under Readings is frozen open with data-kp-nav-menu-open on its item, so switch themes and judge it in each: it sits under the link on the popover surface with a strong boundary, its caption ("Readings · line 2") above a hairline in small capitals, and its links each on a full-width row. It must sit over the stage, not push it down, and must not be cut by the bar's edge. The second bar is the same dropdown with nothing declared: hover Incidents or Tab onto it and it opens the same way, and the next Tab lands on the first link inside it, which keeps it open while focus is there.

Collapsed bar

Look at: both bars sit in a box narrower than 40rem, so they collapse behind their three-bar toggle. The first is frozen open: the toggle wears the theme's accent as a plate (open is a state, and a state is visible), the links stack in a column under the brand, and the dropdown under Readings is no longer a floating panel but an indented list in the flow — no frame, no ground and no shadow in any theme, while the register's own type and item marks stay. Judge whether the indented list still reads as belonging to Readings, without touching it: in nostromo there is at least 0.5rem between Readings and the dropdown's caption, and again between the caption's rule and Pressure. The second bar is live: press its toggle, press Escape with focus inside, or click anywhere outside it — each must close it, and the toggle's accessible name must change between "Open the navigation" and "Close the navigation". Drag the panes' edges wider than 40rem and both must turn back into a plain bar.

Fourteen items, one label sixty characters long

Look at: the wide bar must wrap its fourteen links onto more rows with an even gap between rows and columns — never scroll sideways, never push the page wider. The tenth link is sixty characters: it must wrap inside itself or take a row of its own, and its current-page underline must follow the text onto its second line rather than draw under one line only. No link's plate or rule may touch or cover a link in the row above it — in dark the current page's plate used to lie over the first row. The bar in the narrow pane collapses; open it with its toggle and check that the long label wraps in the column instead of running out of the pane.

Search in the bar

Look at: the command palette's trigger sits in .kp-nav__search at the bar's far end, pushed right by the slot itself, and speaks in the voice of this theme's own bar links (Kenny, 2026-09-14, "Per thema"): the word Search with the key hint Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac) at its end, in the links' typeface, case, letter-spacing and shape, and pointing at it or tabbing to it must do what pointing at Overview does — the same plate, rule, slash or ink — without the underline a link carries. Compare it with the links in every theme: it must read as one more item of this bar, not as a box from another theme. In the narrow pane the bar collapses and the trigger stays beside the toggle on the brand's row without pushing the pane wider. Press either trigger, or Ctrl+K: the palette opens with the focus in its input; type "tabs" and press Enter — the page must jump to Tabs and the palette close. Open it again and press Escape: the focus must return to the trigger you pressed, with its focus ring. Open it once more and click anywhere outside the palette: it must close the same way, focus back on the trigger. Choosing Breadcrumb with the mouse jumps there the same way.

Breadcrumb

Look at: the separator between crumbs is the theme's glyph in the muted ink, and it belongs to the crumb after it — it must never be the last thing on a line with its crumb on the next. The last crumb is the page you are on: it is not a link, and it must look like plain text beside the links rather than like a link that forgot its underline. The second trail has seven levels and a long last crumb; in the narrow pane it must wrap onto more lines with the separators still between the words, never scroll sideways.

Pagination

Look at: the current page carries aria-current and must be marked by weight and a thicker underline, not by colour alone, and marked once: a theme that plates the current page (nostromo) draws no dot beside it. The gap (.kp-pagination__gap) is an ellipsis in the muted ink that nobody can click, and it must not look like a link. On the first row there is no page before page 1, so Previous is plain text: the package has no disabled style for it, so judge whether it reads as unavailable. The third row has twelve numbered pages in a narrow pane; it must wrap, and every number must stay a target large enough to hit.

Tabs

Look at: the selected tab is marked three ways — the ink, the weight, and a rule in the primary colour on the list's bottom edge — and the unselected tabs must still pass contrast in their muted ink. Click into the first list and use the arrow keys: selection follows focus, Home and End jump, and the panel under it changes with no jump in height you did not cause. The second list is data-kp-activation="manual" and does not loop: the arrows only move focus, and Enter or Space selects, so for a moment the focused tab and the selected tab are different tabs — both must be visible at once.

Many tabs in a narrow pane

Side navigation

Look at: .kp-sidenav in its default side mode stands beside the content on the card surface with a rule on its inner edge. The header and the footer stay put while the list scrolls — scroll inside the panel to check. The current page (Line 2) is marked by a ground and weight, not colour alone; hover any row and the hover must be different from the current mark. Readings is an open category: its chevron points down and its links are indented; Maintenance is closed — press it and it must open smoothly, not jump. The fourth link's label is sixty characters: it must end in an ellipsis inside the panel with its count (.kp-sidenav__badge) still whole at the end of the row; the panel must never scroll sideways.

Slim rail

Look at: the left panel is a collapsed rail (data-kp-sidenav-slim-collapsed): icons only, each still with an accessible name, the wordmark swapped for the monogram "N". Hover it — it must not widen, because it was not asked to. The middle rail has data-kp-sidenav-expand-on-hover: hover it and the labels must come back without the rail pushing the content beside it. The right panel is the same rail expanded. Press Collapse or expand the right rail: the width must change with the theme's own timing, and the labels must not overflow while it narrows. The button is wired by the package alone (data-kp-sidenav-slim-toggle with aria-controls), and a screen reader hears it as expanded while the rail is wide.

Rail toggle

Look at: each rail carries its own toggle in its footer (data-kp-sidenav-slim-toggle), drawn as a rail entry (.kp-sidenav__link) holding only an arrow. The left rail starts collapsed and its arrow points out (»); the right one starts wide and its arrow points in («). Press either: the rail must change width with the theme's own timing, the arrow must turn, and the button must stay where the pointer and the focus are — press it again from the keyboard (Enter or Space) without moving. The toggle must look like part of the rail in every theme, not a stray button, and its focus ring must stay visible in both widths. A screen reader hears "Expand the side navigation" or "Collapse the side navigation to its icons", and every link in the collapsed rail keeps its name.

Application shell

Look at: the three navigations composed into one screen, as examples/app-shell.html builds it: the bar across the top holds the product's areas (Invoices is current), the rail beside the content holds the pages of that area, and the breadcrumb above the content says where in it. Judge them as one family: the bar, the rail and the breadcrumb must read as three levels of the same interface in every theme — no double border where the bar meets the rail, the rail running from the bar to the bottom of the box, the breadcrumb lined up with the content rather than with the rail. Collapse the rail from its arrow: the content must take the room without jumping, and the breadcrumb and the heading must move with it. Open Reports in the bar: its dropdown must lie above the rail and the content, never under them — that is the bar's layer, now the same in all 22 themes (--kp-z-nav). Then make the window narrower than 40rem of this stage (or open examples/app-shell.html at phone width): the rail leaves the page, and a ☰ appears in the bar. Press it: the rail slides in over the page with every label written out, even if you had collapsed it to icons, and the page behind it dims. It covers the bar, so it must close from inside — the × in its footer, Escape, or a press on the dimmed page — and the focus must land back on the ☰. Widen the window again: the rail is back beside the content as it was, and the ☰ and × are gone. On the examples page at full width the rail must run to the bottom of the window, not stop where the content ends.

Over the page

Look at: the left box is an over panel frozen open inside its box (data-kp-sidenav-position="absolute"): it covers the content, and the backdrop behind it dims the box — by darkening, never by tinting it with the theme's colour. The panel must have an edge against the dimmed content in every theme. Clicking the backdrop or pressing Escape closes it, as it should; reload the page to get it back. The right box is live: press Open the panel and it must slide in from the left edge of the window with a backdrop over the whole page, take focus to its first link, keep Tab inside itself, and on Escape give focus back to the button — whose accessible name must change while it is open.

Back to top

Look at: .kp-to-top is fixed to the corner of the window and invisible until the reader has scrolled; the two in the box are frozen in their shown state (data-kp-to-top-shown) and pinned to the box's corner instead. The left one is written the way the package documents it — an empty button with only an accessible name — and holds the glyph the package puts in such a button (.kp-to-top__glyph, drawn from --kp-glyph-to-top): judge whether the arrow reads in every theme. The right one carries its own words. The live control is in the real corner of the window: it must fade in once you are 600 px down, sit above the page without covering the feedback field you are typing in, and on press scroll to the top and move focus there.
