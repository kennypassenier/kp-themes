The catalogue

Every component the package ships, on a fixed page, in every state and at its extremes — long text, empty, far too many rows — with a note beside each block saying what to look at. Switch the theme in the bar and the whole page answers in it. Nothing here is generated: these pages are written by hand and a gate refuses a component that has no page, so a new variant cannot hide.

How to review a page

Read the Look at note first, then the block beside it. If what you see disagrees with the note, that is a finding — the note is the promise. Open Devtools (Alt+D) for the three tools: the colour and token under the cursor with its contrast, a timeline that pauses and scrubs every animation on the page, and a ruler for sizes and gaps.
