A plain table

Look at: the header row must stay told apart from the body in every theme — by weight, by rule, by ground, whatever the theme chooses, but by something. Hover a row: the highlight must not swallow the text. The zebra striping, where a theme has it, must not fight the hover. Check that the rule between rows is visible without being louder than the numbers.

Long text in a cell

Look at: drag the right edge of the pane narrower and watch all three columns. The prose column uses .kp-cell-truncate: it must end in an ellipsis and keep its full text in the title attribute, never cut a word with no sign that more exists. The identifier column uses .kp-cell-break: a 76-character key with nowhere to wrap must break inside itself rather than push the table sideways. The link column has neither — it is the fault to see: a long URL with no help widens the table until the page scrolls. Nothing in this block may make the page scroll horizontally; the wrapper scrolls instead.

Forty columns

Look at: the wrapper scrolls on its own and the page never does. Two ways to reach the columns off screen, both to check. With Tab: the first cell of each row is a link, so Tab walks down the rows, and when the wrapper itself has focus the arrow keys scroll it sideways — a table cell is not a Tab stop, so Tab alone never walks across a row (Kenny found the earlier note promised that, and it cannot). With the pointer: scroll sideways inside the wrapper. The header must stay readable while it scrolls, and in a theme that paints a frame or a bezel, the frame must not scroll away with the content.

Empty, loading, failed

Look at: three tables with no data, and they must not look the same. Empty says plainly that there is nothing and what would put something there. Loading shows the shape of what is coming, not a spinner alone, and it must not flash — watch it in a theme with a loud register. Failed says what went wrong and offers the way back. None of the three may be a blank rectangle.

Data table: search, filter, sort, select, page

Look at: .kp-datatable is the table with behaviour, all of it declared in the markup. Type gate in the search box, then set Search in to Site: the rows whose note mentions a gate drop out. Press Filters: tick Watching under Status and give Hours open a range. The two Opened bounds are the theme's own date picker (.kp-datepicker), never the browser's date input: type a date or press the calendar button (▦), and the calendar that opens must look exactly like the date picker on its own page. Each filter leaves a pill above the table, its remove button takes that one filter away, and the count on Filters matches the pills. Sort Severity: Low, Medium, High, Critical, by meaning and not by the alphabet. Switch the density to Compact: the rows and the search box both come down. The status line reads Showing 1–24 of 24; set Rows per page to 10 and page through. Tick rows: the action bar appears with the count; the header checkbox takes the rows on this page only, and after a search hides a ticked row it must not stay half-ticked. Every checkbox here must look exactly like the theme's own checkbox in a form. The status badges must never break inside a word. Narrow the window: the table turns into cards and a Sort by control appears in place of the hidden headers.

Data table: sorting on more than one column

Look at: data-kp-sort-multi. It opens sorted by Severity high to low, then Hours open high to low: the small numbers in the two headers give the order, and the line above the table says it in words. A plain click on a header sorts on that column alone; Shift + click (or Shift + Enter on a focused header) adds the column as the next key, a second Shift + click turns it round and a third takes it out again. Back to Severity, then Hours open restores the opening sort. Severity sorts by its meaning, Critical above Low. Check that the numbers read clearly beside the theme's sort arrow and stay on the header's line in every theme.

Data table: choosing which columns show

Look at: data-kp-column-menu. Press Columns: the menu is the theme's own .kp-menu on a .kp-popover, under its button, with the theme's checkboxes. Untick Note and Owner: the header and the cells go together, and the count beside the button follows. Reference (always shown) cannot be unticked, because a row without its key cannot be told apart. Show every column brings them all back. Check the open menu in every theme: its ground, its items under the pointer, and the disabled item.
