Switch

A switch is a checkbox that says on and off instead of ticked: the real control is an <input type="checkbox" role="switch">, so it submits with a form, reads its state from checked, and flips without a script. It is the package's .kp-switch since your approval of the concept on 2026-09-13 ("Switch: Akkoord"): the concept's geometry and states, on knobs (--kp-switch-width, --kp-switch-height, --kp-switch-radius, …), and every theme's register answers it in its own voice. The words beside the track come from js/strings.js (switchOn, switchOff), filled in by attachSwitches() here and by <Switch> in React.

States

Look at: off, on, and both of them disabled. Off is the theme's resting track with the thumb at the start; on moves the thumb to the end and changes the paint the theme's way — a primary fill in most, a lit thumb on a dark track in cyberpunk, synthwave and terminal, an inverted slab in grotesk. The position says the state as well as the colour, and the word beside the track says it a third time. Compare the track with the same theme's checkbox on the Fields page: the corner, the edge and the ground should be the same family. Click the label, not only the track: the whole row is the target. Tab to each: the focus ring must be visible around the track on the page's ground. Disabled fades and refuses the pointer, and a disabled switch that is on must still read as on.

role="switch" on a checkbox is what makes a screen reader say "switch, on" rather than "checkbox, checked". The word beside the track is aria-hidden, because the role already announces the state. The state span is empty in the markup: attachSwitches() puts the two words in from the dictionary, so setStrings() reaches them, and a span a server already filled is left alone.

Invalid

Look at: a switch that must be on before the form goes through. It takes the same outline an invalid checkbox takes, in the alarm colour, and the message under it says what is wrong in words. The outline must show in every theme, including the ones whose track carries its own shadow (brutalism, retro, synthwave). Turn it on: nothing here clears the message, because this block is not inside a form — in a data-kp-form, js/forms.js would.

A long label

Look at: a label of sixty characters. It wraps beside the track, every line starting at the same inset, and the track stays whole at its own size on the first line rather than shrinking or dropping under the text. Drag the pane narrow to see it wrap.

Keyboard

Look at: Tab lands on the switch, once per switch. Space flips it, as it ticks a checkbox — that is the browser's own behaviour, and the reason the switch is a checkbox rather than a button with a script. Enter does not flip it; inside a form it submits, as it does on a checkbox. The disabled switch is skipped by Tab. Shift+Tab walks back the same way. With reduced motion asked for, the thumb jumps instead of sliding.
