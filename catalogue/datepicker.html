Date picker

.kp-datepicker: a text input that is the real control, and a month grid that js/datepicker.js builds on demand as an aid. The open calendars on this page are frozen copies of the exact markup the module builds for September 2026, so every theme can be compared on the same month; each has a live twin beside or above it.

Closed

Look at: the input and its calendar button sit on one line and share a height; the button must not be taller or shorter than the field it opens for. The empty picker shows its format as a placeholder (dd/mm/yyyy in British English) — it must read as a hint, never as a value. Type 14/09/2026 into the empty one without opening the calendar: typing is the primary way in and must work on its own. The calendar button is an icon, so it has a name; hover it and check that the name says what it opens.

Open

Look at: the panel floats over the page on its own surface with a boundary. The month title sits between two arrow buttons and must be centred between them. Seven columns of equal width, weekday initials above them in a muted ink, and the first of the month under the right weekday — 1 September 2026 is a Tuesday; if the 1 sits under any other heading the grid is lying. The chosen day (13) is filled and framed, so it stays the chosen day even when the fill is close to the hover colour. Hover other days: the hover must be clearly weaker than the chosen fill.

Disabled dates, min and max

Look at: this picker allows 7 to 25 September 2026 and no weekends (data-kp-min, data-kp-max, data-kp-disabled-days="0,6"). Every day before the 7th, after the 25th, and every Saturday and Sunday is disabled: it must look unavailable — faded, struck, or otherwise plainly not a choice — and clicking it must do nothing. A disabled day that looks exactly like an enabled one, so that the only sign is that the click is ignored, is the fault to look for here. Disabled days stay in the grid rather than leaving gaps.

Another locale

Look at: this picker sits under lang="nl" and nothing else changed. What the module takes from the locale must follow: the value is written 13-09-2026 with dashes, the placeholder of the empty one says dd-mm-yyyy, and typing 14-9-2026 is read day first. What it takes from the string dictionary does not follow the locale: the month name, the weekday initials and the arrow labels stay English until a consumer calls setStrings. A Dutch page showing "September" over a Dutch date is therefore the package's current behaviour, and worth a finding if it should not be.

Keyboard

Look at: Tab to the calendar button and press Enter. Focus must land on the chosen day (or today) with a focus ring visible on the grid's ground. Arrows move by a day and a week, PageUp and PageDown by a month, Home and End to the ends of the week; crossing into another month redraws the grid and keeps the focus on the day you moved to. Enter or Space chooses, closes and returns focus to the input. Escape closes and returns focus to the button. Tab from the grid must leave the grid in one press — exactly one day is a tab stop — and the panel closes when focus leaves it.
