Colour picker

.kp-colorpicker: three sliders for hue, saturation and lightness, a swatch, the value, and the one thing a theme system's picker adds — the contrast of the chosen colour against a token of the theme on screen, measured by js/colorpicker.js with the same function the contrast gate uses. Every picker here is live; switch the theme and every verdict is measured again.

Default

Look at: each slider has its label to its left, and the three sliders line up in one column. The slider tracks and thumbs are the browser's, tinted by the theme's accent: in a dark theme the track must not sit on a grey or white slab of its own. The swatch has a boundary, so a colour close to the page's ground is still a visible square. The value is in the mono face. The sentence under it gives the ratio and the verdict in words; move a slider and both must update as you drag, not only when you let go.

Passes and fails

Look at: a pale yellow on the left and a deep blue on the right, both measured as body text against --background. In a light theme the yellow must fail and the blue pass; in a dark theme the verdicts swap. A failing verdict is written in the alarm colour and a passing one in the muted ink — but the words "too little" and "passes" are what carry it, so read them, then switch the theme and read them again. A verdict that does not change when the theme flips from light to dark means the picker is not re-measuring.

Against another token, and a lower bar

Look at: the left picker sits on a card and measures against --card; the sentence must name --card, not --background. The measurement reads the token from the root of the document, not from the card around the picker, so in a theme that repaints its cards locally the number can describe a ground the picker is not on — worth a finding if you see a verdict that contradicts your eye. The right picker measures the same orange with data-kp-against-kind="non-text": its bar is 3:1, not 4.5:1, so it may pass where the left one fails.

A token that does not exist

Look at: this picker is pointed at --sidebar-highlight, which no theme defines. It must say that there is nothing to measure against, in words, in the alarm colour — never show a blank line, and never a ratio. A picker that silently stops measuring looks exactly like one that says the colour is fine, which is the failure this block exists to catch.

In a narrow pane

Look at: drag the pane narrow. The labels keep their width and the sliders take what is left; at the narrowest a slider must still be long enough to drag with a finger, and no label may overlap its slider. The contrast sentence is the longest thing here: it must wrap under the swatch rather than push the picker wider than the pane. The value must not break in the middle of a number.
