Charts

The one time chart every page draws (.kp-chart, js/chart.js), so its controls mean the same everywhere: a legend that singles a source out and keeps sources on or off, a crosshair every chart of a group shares, a tooltip beside it with the change over the hour before and the events within reach, drag to zoom, event markers, and the spark variant, a tile's 24-hour line on the same crosshair. The data is made up and comes from each chart's JSON child: a water company's pump houses on Sunday 4 October 2026, up to 09:00 in Brussels. Every date and time reads dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm on a 24-hour clock in Brussels time, whatever the reader's zone and language (attachCharts(root, { timeZone, time }) changes either).

Time chart

Look at: point at either chart: one crosshair runs through both and through the three sparklines, and only the chart under the pointer shows its tooltip, beside the crosshair on the side with room: every visible source's value, ▲/▼ and the amount over the hour before, and the event near it. Under the lines a soft area while one or two sources are on: click a single source in the legend and its area appears; with three or more on, the lines alone. Hover a source in the legend to single it out; click two, then Show all. In Flow the sixth and seventh pump houses repeat the first two colours as dashed lines. Drag across one chart: both zoom, and Zoomed: … · Reset appears beside the ranges; a double-click or Esc resets. Click the red marker above the plot (the alarm at 07:32): the tooltip pins with its Open link. Tab to a plot and use ←/→, Shift, Home/End, Enter and Esc. Try 1 h and 7 d: the page hands each range its own readings, and under 7 d the axis reads a date at each Brussels midnight; a zoom across two days names both dates in the chip.

Time chart on a phone

Look at: the same group in a pane as narrow as a phone. The plots keep their height and their time labels do not crowd (fewer of them); the tooltip stays inside the plot, beside the crosshair on the side with room. The legend wraps, and the hint under it speaks to a finger on a touch screen and to a pointer elsewhere, never both. The three sparklines go to fewer a row.

One source alone

Look at: a chart with one source: no legend and no hint, and a stronger soft area under its line than with two. The dashed red line is the alarm level (threshold); the dip under it this morning lines up with the alarm and the restart markers above the plot.

Units

Look at: the control room's telemetry server, one chart per unitKind. The axis carries the unit: memory in binary steps (0 B, 1.0 GiB, 2.0 GiB), the network per second (MiB/s), the processor in percent with an axis of 10, 25, 50 or 100, here above 100 because the import job runs on several cores (nothing is cut off at 100). Point at any chart: the tooltip prints values such as 1.5 GiB, 3.1 MiB/s and 94%. Meter readings are counts: the axis may shorten them (20.0k), but the tooltip, the legend and the day's totals in the legend (with nothing under the crosshair) print every digit: 1,183,402, never 1.2M.

States

Look at: four charts that have no lines to draw, each at the height a chart with lines has, so the page does not move when the readings arrive. Loading (data-kp-chart-loading): the plot pulses (it stands still under reduced motion) and the legend row keeps room for the three sources data-kp-chart-sources="3" announces; setChartData() ends it. No readings: the words No readings in this window yet. in the middle. Error ({ error } in the data): the page's sentence with a red dot. One reading: a dot per source, and with onePointNote the note under it.

Zoom chip outside its group

Look at: the chip sits in the page's toolbar, outside the group it belongs to, named by data-kp-chart-zoom-for="cat-chart-far". Drag across the chart: Zoomed: … · Reset appears in the toolbar; Reset, a double-click or Esc hides it again.

Charts outside any group

Look at: two charts with no group element: they still share one crosshair and one zoom (the page's), and the chip in the toolbar, with no group around it and no -for, is theirs. Drag across either chart: both zoom and the chip appears.

Network graph

Look at: .kp-graph (js/graph.js): the control centre in the middle, the other sites on a ring around it from the top, clockwise, A to Z and then the two addresses outside the network (a dashed grey ring). Each node has its own colour; Pump house 5 carries a dotted ring (its settings differ from the plan). Over the picture the kinds of link, each a colour and a dash: four are listed, the four the links use (the fifth kind the page gives is left out). Hover Pump house 3: only its links stay, the rest fades; two kinds between one pair (telemetry and remote control) bend apart. Click two nodes: both stay picked, and Show all appears in its place on the right. Turn a kind off in the list. Tab once: the focus lands on the hub, → walks the ring, Enter picks, Esc shows all; the graph is one tab stop. Press Fifteen long names: in the phone pane no two labels touch and none is cut off at the edge; a shortened one ends in … and shows the full name on hover. Size by flow sizes the nodes by their weight, Live update changes the data and keeps the focus and the picks, and the three states keep the picture's height. The hint sits under the picture, never over it.
