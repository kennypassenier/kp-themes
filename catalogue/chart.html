Charts

The one time chart every page draws (.kp-chart, js/chart.js), so its controls mean the same everywhere: a legend that singles a source out and keeps sources on or off, a crosshair every chart of a group shares, a tooltip beside it with the change over the hour before and the events within reach, drag to zoom, event markers, and the spark variant, a tile's 24-hour line on the same crosshair. The data is made up and comes from each chart's JSON child: a water company's pump houses on Sunday 4 October 2026, up to 09:00 in Brussels, written with a 24-hour clock (lang="en-GB" on the group).

Time chart

Look at: point at either chart: one crosshair runs through both and through the three sparklines, and only the chart under the pointer shows its tooltip, beside the crosshair on the side with room: every visible source's value, ▲/▼ and the amount over the hour before, and the event near it. Under the lines a soft area while one or two sources are on: click a single source in the legend and its area appears; with three or more on, the lines alone. Hover a source in the legend to single it out; click two, then Show all. In Flow the sixth and seventh pump houses repeat the first two colours as dashed lines. Drag across one chart: both zoom, and Zoomed: … · Reset appears beside the ranges; a double-click or Esc resets. Click the red marker above the plot (the alarm at 07:32): the tooltip pins with its Open link. Tab to a plot and use ←/→, Shift, Home/End, Enter and Esc. Try 1 h and 7 d: the page hands each range its own readings.

Time chart on a phone

Look at: the same group in a pane as narrow as a phone. The plots keep their height and their time labels do not crowd (fewer of them); the tooltip stays inside the plot, beside the crosshair on the side with room. The legend wraps, and the hint under it speaks to a finger on a touch screen and to a pointer elsewhere, never both. The three sparklines go to fewer a row.

One source alone

Look at: a chart with one source: no legend and no hint, and a stronger soft area under its line than with two. The dashed red line is the alarm level (threshold); the dip under it this morning lines up with the alarm and the restart markers above the plot.
