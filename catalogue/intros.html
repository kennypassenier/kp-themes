Theme intros

The screen a theme shows once when a page first opens: the arrival of js/effects.js, asked for by a theme with --kp-arrival. A reader sees it once per session and never when their browser asks for less motion, so it is hard to look at twice. Here every theme that has one plays it in a window of its own, in that theme, whatever theme this page wears.

Play the intro forgets that it was seen and plays it again. The speed slider sets --kp-arrival-rate on the window: 0.5× takes twice as long, 2× half as long, and the next play uses it. Beside each window are the words the intro shows, taken from the dictionary (js/strings.js), the line on screen right now, and how long the last play took. A theme that asks for a counting boot and has no words of its own shows the neutral words Loading, Progress and Ready, never another theme's.

Your browser asks for reduced motion, so the package never plays an intro for you and the windows below stay at rest. For inspection only, this page can play them anyway.

Synthwave

Look at: the words changed. Words were: "▶ Calibrating neural uplink", "Progress N%", "OK" — cyberpunk's voice on a VHS night. Now: "▶ Play", "Tracking N%", "Press start" — a VCR's on-screen display settling its tracking, then an arcade cabinet's attract screen. Do the words belong to the outrun horizon? Then the look: the void with the lines in the cyan OSD face, uppercase, with a glow; the percentage counts up in uneven steps; Skip in pink at the bottom right; at 100 % the CRT switches the screen off in 550 ms at 1×. A click anywhere ends it. Nothing flickers.
