Seven Registers, Seven Reflexes

kp-themes · round seven · scope-12

Seven registers,
seven reflexes

Every panel below is one theme's own tokens with its own quirk on top, exactly as the register now carries it. Hover the buttons, hold them down, tab to them. Six of these are in the package; the seventh is shade-dark, which is new.

Nothing fades

high-contrast

The constraint of this theme is the idea. A transition is by definition a run of half-contrasts — every frame between two legible states is less legible than either. So a state change is a switch, and the touched control inverts outright, keeping the two solid bars its side navigation already uses for the row you are on.

↳ hover: instant inversion · hold: the base layer's pressed grey, so the press is its own thing

Every switch has a lamp

nostromo

Your own example of what a quirk is, carried through: the dot no longer marks only the page you are on but every control there is. Dark at rest, lit under the pointer, full when pressed — a console where each switch has its own lamp. Same size and same gutter as the one in the navigation.

↳ the lamp burns in the control's own ink, so it is pale on the dark button and dark on the pale one

One light, one direction

shade-light

Not a colour but a law: a single source at the top left that everything obeys. At rest a control lies on the surface; under the pointer it lifts and its shadow lengthens; pressed, it goes below the surface and the shadow turns inward. The press is instant on the way in, because a click is shorter than a transition.

↳ hold the button down and let go — the inward shadow arrives with no run-up

The same light, the other material

shade-dark · new

The twin. On a dark ground the shadow has to fall further and softer to be seen at all — 2px against the light twin's 1, blurred 5 against 3, and cast in the theme's own near-black rather than its text colour. Both things you found on 2026-09-11 are fixed underneath: the hover was a relative colour resolving to nothing, and the press was a register cancelling a state it did not replace.

↳ this is the one to judge — it was not approved, it was rebuilt

The marginal note

sepia

Aged paper, and on paper you mark what you are reading in the margin. Touching a control draws a bracket to the left of it — beside the thing, not on it, because that is where a reader writes. The same gesture its side navigation already uses for the row you are on.

↳ tab to the buttons as well: the bracket answers the keyboard too

The low sun rakes across

solstice

A low sun is the whole picture of this theme, and a low sun moves. Touching a control draws a warm band across it once, from the leading edge to the far one. Once, never in a loop: a loop would be an animation demanding attention rather than light falling on a thing.

↳ move away and back to see it again · nothing runs under reduced motion

The thing names itself

brutalism

Brutalism labels its own construction: services on the outside, functions painted on the wall. So a control says what it is when you touch it. Your two conditions are in it — it reaches more than the buttons, and the words are English by default and replaceable through a custom property, so a consumer writing VERZENDEN gets VERZENDEN.

↳ the third button carries an overridden label, to show the door works
