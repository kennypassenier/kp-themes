The "N new lines" pill

The pill for new lines on a live list

A log or a feed follows its newest line while you are at that end. Scroll away and the lines that arrive out of sight are counted in a pill: "12 new lines · back to the tail". One press jumps there. Scrolling back to the end follows again by itself.

Expected: changed since round 1: in the log rows the time, the level (INFO or ERROR) and the line each have their own column and never touch or cover each other, at the desk width and at 390 px; the “New since” divider is on by default (data-kp-since-left, off turns it off).

  • Add 5 lines and read the rows: 14:32:08, INFO or ERROR, the line. Scroll up, add lines, press the pill; switch the tab away and back (or press the Divider on return buttons to compare): the ruled line “New since …” stands before the first line that came while you were away. Everything else is as in round 1.

The pill is an opaque paper plate in a navy hairline, its words in the package's sans with the count in tabular figures; it is ruled in from its start edge in 0.4 s in a straight line and taken out the same way backwards. The divider is a navy hairline either side of the time.

        The JSON box

        The JSON box that checks what you type

        A text field that checks JSON, points at the error line and can format. By default text that is not JSON blocks the form (data-kp-json="strict"); a field that may also carry plain text says so itself (="allow-text"), decided per field.

        Expected: changed since round 1: the line-number gutter is exactly as tall as the text box, clips what the box does not show, and moves together with the text when you scroll; it never lists more lines than the box shows and the frame does not grow with the number of lines.

        • Press Long (an object with 8 entries, about 40 lines): the box grows to 20 lines and then scrolls inside itself. Scroll it: the numbers scroll with the text, line 1 beside the first line shown, and the gutter and the box keep the same height. Press Broken (line 3): line 3 is marked in the gutter. Everything else is as in round 1.

        The box is the package's field in the mono face; the gutter a muted column with tabular numbers; the error line in the destructive ink and bold, with the field's edge turned red ink (the accountant's entry), never a tinted plate. Valid is quiet grey: nothing celebrates.
