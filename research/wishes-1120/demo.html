Homelab wishes built in the package

The homelab admin dashboard hit the edges of the package in 22 places. Each is now an option or a part of the package; this page shows all of them in formal only, one section per wish. Everything is opt-in: a component that is not given the option looks and behaves as you approved it. 22 sections, 22 pairs; all data is mock.

Every section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does.

What is asked (homelab 90): A card that reads data (loading, empty, failed, ready) drew its own frame, so inside a section card it made a card in a card. Is the plain version, which sits in the section card with no frame of its own, what you want? It is an option (data-kp-card="plain"); the framed card stays the default everywhere else.

A card in a card, or just the section

The backups section holds a state card. Switch the inner card between its own frame (before) and plain (new) and walk its four states.

Expected:

  • Own frame: a bordered card inside the section card, with its own padding. Plain: no border, no shadow, no padding; the rows sit on the section card, and the section card is the only frame.
  • The four states keep the same room in both: Loading has the busy card centred, Empty and Failed a large centred plate, Failed with a Retry. Nothing jumps when you switch.

Formal is a paper plate: one border weight. In plain there must be exactly one frame around the rows; in the other mode two nested ones.

Step 3 of 8, 1.2 of 4 GB, about 2 min left

The step list and the transfer bar are the package’s own; the job line under them joins what they know. Press a preset, or run the whole job.

Expected:

  • Waiting says Waiting to start. Steps only says Step 3 of 8. Steps and data says Step 3 of 8 · 1.2 of 4 GB · about 2 min left (the unit once). Size unknown says Step 3 of 8 · 1.2 GB so far: a part that is not known is left out, never shown as 0.
  • Run the job: the steps tick on, the bar fills and the line follows both; the line is the same height at every moment.

Check that the numbers do not shimmer (tabular figures), that the separator dots have room and that the line wraps rather than cuts on a phone.
