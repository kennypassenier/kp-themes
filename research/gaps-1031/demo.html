Seven options homelab and kyu asked for

For 12.1.0 (“zie dat alles gefixt is in deze release”): seven things the homelab dashboard and kyu had to draw themselves, now options of the package. Every one is opt-in: without it the component is exactly as you approved it. Formal only, on the apps’ own data (the names, times and the token are mock). 7 sections, 7 pairs.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. The width control shows the stage at a phone’s 390 px.

What is asked (homelab gap 22): The homelab Inbox drew its own plain button to answer every waiting question at once. data-kp-questions-batch on the questions container puts the package’s own bar over the cards: one press answers every open question that has a default answer with that default, the answer it would get at zero anyway, each card through its own button. off (no bar) stays the default, so the approved card is unchanged; confirm asks first. data-kp-questions-batch-min (default 2) is how many must wait before the bar shows.

Three questions from the host

Two prunes and a restart wait for an answer; each has “Leave it stopped” as its default. The clocks stand still so you can read them.

Expected:

  • On: a bar over the three cards says “3 questions wait and each has a default answer.” with “Answer 3 with their default” at its end. A press turns every card to its outcome (“Stopped. Nothing was deleted.”) and the bar goes, because nothing waits any more. The line under the cards names each answer the page heard.
  • Confirm: the first press asks “Answer 3 questions with their default now?” with Answer all and Cancel; Cancel puts the bar back.
  • Off: no bar, the cards exactly as approved. Three (ask again) asks the three questions again; One asks only the first, and the bar stays away, because one card already has its own buttons (data-kp-questions-batch-min, 2). Phone: the bar’s button goes under its line.

Formal: the bar is a quiet card-coloured plate over the alert cards, its button the package’s small button. Check that the bar reads as belonging to the cards under it and that the count is exact.

What is asked (homelab gap 26): The homelab Overview wants to show the newest notices in the page without the Inbox’s own heading, Mark all read and per-row Mark read: those belong on the Inbox page. data-kp-notices-look="preview" is that look: the same rows as the Inbox page, newest per severity, each with its Fix, and “All 5 in the Inbox” under them. It leaves the tab title and the toasts to the page’s own counter. The approved looks (both, panel, page) do not change.

What is asked (homelab gap 70): The homelab cannot rename a token (its kit has no call for it) and does not record when a token was last used, yet every row said “Never used” and offered Rename. data-kp-rename="off" (or rename: false) leaves Rename out. data-kp-missing-use says what a row without a last use reads: never (“Never used”, the default, as approved), unknown (“Last use not recorded”, and never flagged as unused) or none (nothing).
