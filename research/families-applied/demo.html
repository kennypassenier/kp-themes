What the family picks do

  1. What you see: per theme and family, the picture you picked (one component's) translated onto every other component of the theme. Each component twice, side by side: Today, its own approved pick, and Speaking the family, the same component drawn in the picked picture.
  2. What is translated: the idea, not a copy: the material, the motion, its pace, direction and curve. The picture itself is shown first, unchanged. Where a component cannot take it sensibly, its card says so and it is left as it is.
  3. Apply to the package (↑): the theme's components are reworked like this at the port. None of these (↓): not like this; say what should change.
