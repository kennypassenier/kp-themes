Touch for the hover card, a kiosk wheel

The AuDHD recipes app asked for two things the package could not do: ingredient notes that a phone can open, and a wheel for a page of its own, with no controls, with choices that are resting and with a card for the answer. 4 pairs in all, formal only (nothing here differs per theme). Each section says under What changed since you approved it what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: The hover card ignored touch altogether, so on a phone the note behind an ingredient could not be read. Now a tap on an ingredient opens its note, a tap on the same ingredient or anywhere outside the card closes it, and a tap inside the card keeps it open. A tap on another ingredient moves the card there. The keyboard works as before (focus opens, Escape closes), and a link or a button used as an anchor keeps its own tap unless the page asks for two taps with data-kp-touch="tap".

Hover card: reachable with a finger

The ingredient list of a recipe. Each ingredient has a note that a mouse reads by pointing; on a phone there is no pointing.

Expected:

  • On a phone: tap Ripe bananas, its note opens under it; tap the white page, it closes; tap Cinnamon while it is open, the card moves there.
  • On a desktop: the button Tap it like a finger at the end of each row sends the same touch tap, so you can see it without a phone. Pointing with the mouse still opens the card after a short wait.

Formal: a tap opens the note under the ingredient, a tap outside closes it, and the rows stay in their four columns.

What changed since you approved it: Until now a wheel always carried its Spin button in the middle, which a page for someone who should only see the wheel could not remove. The new option data-kp-wheel-kiosk takes the button away: the wheel itself is the one control. Tap it, or press Enter or Space while it has the focus, and it spins. The middle stays as a quiet plate, the line under the wheel says Tap the wheel to let it choose, and a screen reader hears one button, “Spin the wheel”. Tapping the answer spins again.

Wheel: a page with only the wheel

The wheel over a chosen list for a page that has nothing else on it: left as it was, right with the new data-kp-wheel-kiosk option.

Expected:

  • The left wheel is as you approved it (Spin in the middle). The right one has a plain middle, nothing to press but the wheel.
  • Tap the right wheel: it turns, stops on a name, says it. Tab to it and press Space: the same. Tapping its answer spins again.

Formal: the right wheel has no button anywhere, its middle is a calm plate, and both wheels keep the same size and place.

Kiosk: the wheel is the control

What changed since you approved it: A choice could only be left off the wheel (it vanished) or stay on it (it could be drawn). The new item field resting: true is the third state: the choice stays on the wheel so the list is whole, but it is stepped back (faded, a dashed edge, its name in italics, “Resting” when you point at it) and the wheel never lands on it while another choice is awake. If every choice rests, they are all in play again.

Wheel: a choice that is resting

A list where yesterday’s dinner and the one from two days ago are on the wheel but should not come up.

Expected:

  • Fried rice and Tomato soup are faded with a dashed edge and italic names; the others are as before.
  • Press Spin a dozen times: the wheel stops on Curry, Pasta, Wraps or Stew, never on a resting one. The slice sizes are unchanged.

Formal: the two resting slices read as quieter at a glance, with dashed edges, and their names are whole.

What changed since you approved it: The answer was two bare lines under the wheel and a choice could say nothing but its name. Now an item can carry a tag (a short line about it), and data-kp-wheel-answer turns the result into a built-in answer card: a bordered card with the line “The wheel chose”, the name, and the tag under it. The card keeps one size whether or not the choice has a tag, so nothing under it moves. Without the option the result is exactly as you approved it.
