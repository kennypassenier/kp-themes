What is high-contrast's anchor element

Kenny, 2026-10-07 18:13: every theme gets one recognisable anchor element, the one thing every later decision of the theme departs from (forest: the tree progress bar, grotesk: the red plate that falls into register, synthwave: the page horizon, blueprint: the tracing pen). The colours are already right everywhere and stay the shared base. High-contrast has no anchor yet: it is black on white with one signal yellow and nothing in between, its register changes by a switch and never by a fade, and what it has beyond the meter is a set of small signature pieces (the walking spinner, the hazard-striped bar, the "✕ REMOVED" plate on a leave) that are not yet one idea.

One question, seven bold candidates, each drawn only from what high-contrast's world already owns (the sign: a yellow plate that says it, a marker that walks cells, a frame that thickens, a sign in the corner, a plate that flips, hazard tape, a board placed cell by cell). Nothing fades and no contrast drops in any of them. Each one is shown the same three ways, on one clock: in its own scene, as a progress bar and as a button press, so you see whether it carries the theme. Pick one in the review dialog (arrow keys flip between the options); the first option is the recommendation, and each option says what follows from it and why it is or is not recommended.
