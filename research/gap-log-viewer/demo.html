The live log viewer

The admin dashboard of the homelab draws its host log by hand: the buffer, the search, the pause and the copy button. This page shows the package’s version of it, built on the log you already have and on the “N new lines” pill. The lines are mock (a seeded homelab with jellyfin, sonarr and traefik); nothing is read from a host. 4 sections, 4 pairs, in formal.

Lines keep arriving and the log keeps only the newest

A host log that grows while you read it. The viewer keeps a fixed number of lines (here a small number, so you can see it), drops the oldest first and says how many it dropped. Following the newest line, Pause and the “N new lines” pill are the package’s own.

Expected:

  • It opens with 46 lines and a cap of 60. Add 10 lines adds them at the foot and the view follows; the second press crosses the cap and the foot says N earlier lines dropped, the newest 60 are kept, with the exact number.
  • Scroll up a little, then Play once: the view stays where you are and a pill says N new lines with the exact count; pressing it (or End) goes back to the newest line and follows again. Pause in the bar holds the view and becomes Resume.
  • Lines kept 30, 60 or 200 changes the cap at once: at 30 the oldest lines leave the log and the dropped count grows, at 200 nothing is dropped.
  • The level is a word first (WARNING, ERROR) and a tint second; each stack keeps its own hue; Copy says Copied without changing its width, Download saves a text file.

The viewer is the log of a homelab host: stacks such as jellyfin, sonarr and traefik write into one list. The panel is an opaque paper plate in a strong hairline with a 3 px navy rule on its start edge; things are ruled in from that edge in a straight line, nothing fades. Check that the text keeps clear of every edge, that the level colours still read as words first, and that nothing moves when a state changes.

Find a word in the log and walk the matches

Type in the search box, or press one of the words below it. Every match is marked, the count says how many there are, and Enter and Shift+Enter (or the two buttons) go from one to the next.

Expected:

  • It opens on timeout: the matches are marked in bold with an underline, the count says N matches, and the first press of Next match makes the first one the filled plate and reads 1 of N, scrolling it into view.
  • Nothing clears the find; zzz finds nothing and says No matches with both buttons off.
  • Only matching lines hides every line without a match; the foot says N of 170 lines shown. Off shows all again and the marks stay.
  • / jumps to the search from anywhere in the viewer; Esc in the box clears it.

The marks are the one place the page uses the primary colour for text; the current match is the filled plate with a rule round it. Wrap, the time column and the level

Three settings of how the lines look. Wrap decides whether a long line breaks or scrolls sideways, the time column can show the time, the date and time, or nothing, and the level hides the quiet lines.

Expected:

  • Wrap On: the two long lines (a stack trace and a database message) break and every line starts at the same column.
  • Wrap Off: every line is one row and the log scrolls sideways; the time stays at the start edge while you scroll.
  • Time column Time, Date and time or Off: the columns after it close up with no gap left behind.
  • Level Warning and above or Error and above hides the lines below it and the foot says N of 28 lines shown.

Warnings carry a yellow plate on the word and errors a red one, with the same tone faint behind the whole line. Reading, no lines yet, could not read, nothing matches

The states of the viewer. Each one fills the whole log area, so the bar above it and the foot under it stay where they are.

Expected:

  • Reading: the theme’s busy card is centred over the whole log area and moves; the bar stays.
  • No lines yet: an empty plate at heading size, centred, saying new lines show up as they arrive. Could not read: a red plate with a normal-size Retry; Retry reads again for a moment and shows the lines.
  • Filter hides all: a plate saying no line matches, with Clear search and level that brings the lines back.
  • The height of the log area is the same in all five states, and so is everything around it.

Compare the height of the log area in every state against the lines; the plates are the theme’s own empty and alert plates.
