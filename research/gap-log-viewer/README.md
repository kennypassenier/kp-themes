# research/gap-log-viewer: the live log viewer

Round `gap-log-viewer-efbfabb8-a2c1-47e3-b3e1-235e42b0ddbf` (2026-10-19): 4 sections, 4 pairs, formal only (a new component is demoed in formal first; the other eleven themes
come after the verdict).

The homelab admin dashboard found the live log viewer missing (gap 1 of its gap report): its host log explorer and its Stack logs tab
draw a buffer, a search with marks, a pause button, a "N new lines" pill, copy and download by hand, about 1000 lines in all. The
package had the rows (`.kp-log`, js/log.js) and the follow, pause and pill (js/newpill.js) but not the viewer that owns the buffer.

What is built (css/components.css `.kp-logviewer`, js/logviewer.js, js/strings.js `logviewer…`):

- a ring buffer with a cap (`data-kp-log-max`, 2000), the oldest lines dropped first and counted exactly; a line is kept once per `id`;
- find with every match marked, an exact count, next and previous (Enter, Shift+Enter, two arrow buttons), `/` to jump to the box, and
  "Only matching lines";
- a wrap toggle (off: one line each, sideways scroll under a sticky time column), a time column of time, date and time, or off that closes
  up without a gap, the level as a word, a plate on the word and a tint behind the line, a lowest-level filter;
- Copy (width held) and Download of the lines shown;
- loading (the busy card), empty, no-match and failed (Retry) states over the whole log area, so nothing around it moves;
- following the tail, Pause and Resume, the "N new lines" pill and the source hues are the existing js/newpill.js and js/log.js.

No virtual windowing: docs/FEATURES.md TH42 marks virtual lists "Don't do", so the DOM rows are capped by the ring buffer instead.

Sections (one component each, its own controls above it, at most three groups):

1. `ring`: a cap of 60 with Add 10 lines, Play once and Lines kept 30 / 60 / 200.
2. `find`: Find (Nothing, timeout, jellyfin, zzz) and Only matching lines (Off, On); opens on the first match of "timeout".
3. `look`: Wrap (On, Off), Time column (Time, Date and time, Off), Level (All, Warning and above, Error and above).
4. `states`: Lines, Reading, No lines yet, Could not read, Filter hides all; Retry reads again for a moment.

The lines are mock and seeded (`demo.js`): a homelab with jellyfin, sonarr, traefik and prometheus; nothing is read from a host.
Hand-written page; the markup of the viewer is generated by js/logviewer.js.
