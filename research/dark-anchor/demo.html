What is dark's anchor element

Dark has no anchor yet: it is a spectral instrument whose one mechanism, the oxide film that runs along every edge that matters, turns with the pointer and otherwise stands still, and the rest of its signature (the bracketed button, the ticked progress bar, the darkroom's develop) is a set of parts without one behaviour to depart from.
