Nine faults against the text rules

The project threads found nine places where an approved component broke one of your standing rules: text touching an edge or an icon, text cut with an ellipsis, a word breaking letter by letter, a stray mark. 11 pairs in all; every fault is shown in formal, the wheel also in dark and cyberpunk (their names are set in a wider face). Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: A name too long for its slice was cut with an ellipsis (“Chicken curry wit…”), and a slice too thin for any word was left blank. Now a name is never cut: it takes the largest type its slice allows, goes onto two lines when that reads larger, and if it still does not fit it uses the dish’s short name when the page gave one (Pasta for Pasta with roasted vegetables and feta). A name that fits in no form is not drawn on the disc; it is the slice’s tooltip and it is always whole in the result under the wheel. The slices, the weights, the spin and the colours are as you approved them. Shown in formal, dark and cyberpunk because the last two set their names in a wider mono face.
