The form grid: forms, settings, facts and buttons on one grid

You rejected seven settings sections for misalignment: a second column that was not uniform, rows off their labels, and “Send test” off the line of “Format” and “Minify”. The package now has one layout for all of these, .kp-formgrid: four columns, label, control, value and actions, that every row shares, so things line up by construction rather than by care. 6 sections, formal only; the content is made up. Every section names its grid under The grid. The project threads move their own pages onto it once you approve it, and a new measurement holds every demo to it.

What changed: New in the package: .kp-formgrid. Before, a settings row was a label beside a control in its own flex row, so each row decided its own edges: in the rejected homelab settings the controls ended on four different edges (x 649, 686, 706 and 711), a stepper stood 4 px and a switch 6 px off its label. Here every row is a subgrid of one grid, so every control starts on one edge, ends on one of two (a field fills the track, a time, stepper or number takes one step of 14rem), and every cell stands on the label’s first baseline. The three switches above the grid change its options live. Fixed after your verdict of 19/10 11:22 (“de eerste input en de items daarachter op de eerste rij zijn niet verticaal aligned met de eerste kolom”): the first row’s label has a note under it, so the label is a block of three lines, 58 px high, and the field, the value and Reset stood on its first line only, hanging 31 px above the block’s bottom (centres 20 px apart). A row whose label has a note now centres every cell on the label block: the field, the value and Reset share the block’s centre (0 px apart).

The grid: four columns, label (as wide as “Bandwidth cap (MB/s)”) | control (3 parts of the width left) | value (2 parts) | actions (as wide as “Reset target”); seven rows, each a subgrid spanning all four; the Save row puts its two buttons in the control column and its note in the value column.
