kyu, the Topic page: dead letters and messages

The lower half of kyu’s Topic page (notify.kenny) on kp-themes 11.0.0, in formal. Dead letters first: what died, why, and what to do now. Then the recent messages, a message opened large, and the box that publishes a test message. All data on this page is mock. Two sections are new use cases that need kyu backend work; they say so in their first sentence.

Dead letters: what died, why and what now

.kp-alert with setProblems() (js/problems.js) above the dead-letter table: one alert for each subscription that lost messages, saying what died, why, and the remedy, with Requeue beside it. kyu has this data now.

Expected:

  • On load: 3 problems, the worst first (3 messages died on desktop, then two warnings), each with What now and a Requeue button. 6 problems adds an almanac alert whose remedy is a command with Copy, an archive alert with 104 messages (110 dead letters in all), and a note about the oldest dead letter.
  • Press a Requeue button: it says Requeued for a moment, then that problem leaves and the rest close up; the others do not move or lose focus. Bring them back returns all of them. The layout is an option below (each, list, fold) and so is the number where the fold starts.
  • None shows the empty plate (Nothing has died), Loading lays the busy card over the region and Failed a failed plate with Retry. All three fill the same box as the filled region, so the page does not jump.

Each alert is a paper plate in a strong hairline with the 3 px navy rule on its start edge; the three lines (what, why, what now) share one column and the Requeue buttons stand in one column at the end. Nothing fades or slides.

Dead letters: payloads you can read, and a link to each

.kp-datatable with the payload as .kp-code--cell and data-kp-anchors="rows": sort, search and filter the dead letters, read each payload as JSON, and point a chat at one failed message. All of it works on data kyu already holds.

Expected:

  • Twelve dead letters. The first is the real one: {"title":"eerste echte bericht","body":"hello from HA"} on notify.kenny / desktop. A payload shows keys in the key colour with Pretty and Raw; the 38-line one folds to three lines with Show all 38 lines; the camera one says binary, 2.1 kB with Download; one says only the first 1 kB of 4 kB was kept; one says empty payload. Copy copies the whole payload, folded or not.
  • Sort a header; Shift+click a second header for a second sort key; pick a subscription in the filter; search a word. The sort is remembered. The Requeue and Delete buttons stand in one fixed column on every row. Requeue says Requeued and the row leaves; Delete hides the row and offers Undo below.
  • A # sits before each row. Press it: Copied. Open a link that is here lands on the row and marks it for two seconds; a link to a requeued one says That item is no longer here with a button to clear it.
  • Loading lays the busy card over the rows, Empty shows the empty plate (Nothing is dead), Failed the failed plate with Try again. The first payload can also be loading or failed on its own.

The payload cell is the code block on the package’s muted ground in the mono face, keys in the primary navy, punctuation quiet, a hairline above its tools. The # mark is a small ruled link in the first cell. The table is the ledger: hairline rules, tabular figures, headers that say how they are sorted.
