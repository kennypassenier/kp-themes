Dashboard components to port

The admin dashboard grew a handful of components that any app built on the package needs: buttons that line up from row to row, tiles of one height, key figures, a page header, a band of problems, a word that keeps its width, and a calmer loading panel on a phone. Each section below is that component written already in the package's style (ports.css and ports.js next to this page), shown in formal only. Where there is a real choice, the review dialog asks it as options to tick, and the buttons in the section let you see each option first.

Each section shows the component at full width and, beside it, in a frame as narrow as a phone, so both widths can be judged on one screen. The data is made up: a water company's pump houses, readings and incidents.

Buttons in shared columns

A list's row buttons share columns: a button of one role starts at one edge and has one width on every row, whatever its label, and a row without that button leaves its column empty. In a list the browser does it (a subgrid); by position from the end it needs no script at all. A table measures, through attachActionColumns().

In the list, every Open sits in one column, every Assign… and the second row's Reassign… in the one before it, one width, and the incident without Acknowledge leaves that slot empty. Press Longer label: the whole Assign column widens, on every row. In the table the buttons are named by role, so Open stays under Open even on the rows without Recalibrate. Compare with Package today at the bottom, where the buttons wander.

      Tiles of one height

      .kp-tiles lays the package's own .kp-card out in a grid: every tile one height, and each card's footer kept to its bottom edge, so the footers of a row form one line however long the text above them is.

      Every card in a row is equally tall, and every footer line (Read … ago and Open) sits at the same height. Switch between the two options: the first makes the short second row as tall as the first, the second lets it shrink.

      Key figures

      .kp-kpis is a strip of .kp-kpi tiles, one height on every row it wraps to: a label, the number (with its unit and a note such as avg 15 min beside it), a trend line, and at the bottom a 24-hour sparkline or a meter. A tile can be a link to its detail, or a button that turns a filter on this page on and off.

      The labels, the numbers and the trend lines sit on one line across the strip, and the sparklines and meters keep to the bottom. Open incidents and Pressure alarms are filters: press them and watch the corner word and the edge. The amber and red tiles carry a coloured edge and a coloured number. On the phone frame the strip goes to two tiles a row.

      Page header

      .kp-page-header: the page's title and its one-sentence description on the left; on the right two secondary buttons, one primary and an overflow menu for the rare things. On a narrow page it reads top to bottom.

      At full width the buttons sit against the right edge, level with the title, and the description keeps under the title. Open the overflow menu. In the phone frame the title takes the width and the buttons fold under the description; try both options for the overflow button and for the phone.

      Attention band

      .kp-attention: one .kp-alert per problem, worst first, each with the action that fixes it. With nothing wrong the band takes no room at all, never an "all is well" line. attachAttention() keeps the order in the page itself, so a screen reader meets the problems in the order the eye does.

      The markup lists the problems as info, critical, warning; the band shows critical, warning, info. Use Add a warning: it lands under the critical one, not at the bottom. Resolve all makes the band vanish without leaving a gap; Bring them back restores it. In the phone frame the fix buttons go under the words.

      A state word that keeps its width

      .kp-state-word is as wide as the widest word it can show, listed in data-kp-words, so a button beside it never moves when the state changes: a second click without moving the mouse hits the same button. setStateWord(el, word) changes it and adds a new word to the list.

      Press Change the states. In Package today the Stop / Start buttons jump left and right with each word; in the Proposal they stand still, while the words change in front of them.

          Already in the package, and later

          Nothing here to judge; the README next to this page has the detail.

          • A row that opens from anywhere in it (the dashboard's rowtoggle.js): already the data table's own. A click anywhere in a data-kp-expandable row opens it, except on a control of the row's own; it waits on main for the next release. Nothing to port.
          • "Read 12 s ago" words that tick (ago.js): not covered. js/as-of.js is about something else (attaching a late module to the page as it stood), so nothing merges; the proposal is a new js/freshness.js with one timer per page, words through the dictionary, and a stale mark. Its own round.
          • A folded group that remembers: covered. A <details class="kp-accordion__item" data-kp-remember="…"> keeps its open state through js/remember.js, across pages. Only "fill the body the first time it opens" is the dashboard's own, and that is app code.
          • Later: the time chart, the calendar heatmap, the help tour and the topology graph.
