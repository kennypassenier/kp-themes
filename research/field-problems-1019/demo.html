Two grid faults

The project threads found two places where an approved component left a loose edge: a form field that stopped short of its card, and the fixes of a problem list that began on different lines. 2 pairs, shown in formal. Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: A form field stopped at 28 rem (448 px) however wide its place was, so inside a card or a grid column the box ended 42 to 47 px before the edge that its label, its help and the blocks around it ran to: a loose end edge. Now a field that stands in a card, a stack, a dialog or a grid column fills the place it is in; --kp-field-max-width caps it again where a page wants a measure.

Form field: it fills its card and its column

Fields that stand in a card, in a stack of blocks and in the two columns of a grid. Each card is a plate; the label and the help always ran to its edge.

Expected:

  • In the wide card the box ends on the same vertical line as the paragraph above it and the help text below; in the two cards side by side each box ends at its card’s inner edge.
  • The stacked fields in the first card end on one line; narrow the window and they keep ending at the card’s inner edge.

Formal: every box ends exactly where the blocks around it end, in every card and column; nothing is left hanging short.
