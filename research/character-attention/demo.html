An attention band of its own, per theme: one pick per aspect

The eighth component of the character round, built as the trend tile and the meter were (Kenny, 2026-10-05: keep every aspect separate so one can be picked over another, "it should be like this in the future"). Six things are picked, each on its own: the shape, how a problem arrives, how it leaves when it is acknowledged (the mirror of its arrival), the tone a warning or a critical problem reads in, the empty state, and the picture while it loads. Nothing is ticked for the reviewer.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so arriving and leaving happen at once and the loading picture stands still. Turn reduced motion off in the system settings to judge arrival and leave.

js/attention.js is not changed: every band below is drawn by attachAttention() and setAttention(), which rank problems worst first, update an item by its key, and hand a removed item to leave() (js/motion.js) when it is acknowledged. An option only restyles the band from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (attention.css), one attribute per aspect, and keeps the band's height steady in every state.
