Sentence form

Sentence form: a schedule that reads as a sentence

<fieldset class="kp-sentence" data-kp-sentence> (js/sentence.js): the fields stand inside the words, so you read what will happen as you fill it in. The sentence runs on over several lines, and a field too wide for the line takes a line of its own.

Expected: changed since round 1: the list that opens from a field (for example every week) is on the popover’s paper surface with the ink of formal’s other lists, no longer almost black; the underline is the default look; look and size are options you can switch below (data-kp-look, data-kp-sentence-size).

  • Run [backup] on [nextcloud] [every week] on [Mon Thu chips] at [03:00]. Open every week, backup and nextcloud: each list reads dark ink on paper with the highlighted option plainly visible. Choose once: the chips go and on [20/10/2026] appears.
  • Look (Underline, Boxed) and Size (Large, Medium) change the sentence at once. The grey box shows what a screen reader hears after a change.
