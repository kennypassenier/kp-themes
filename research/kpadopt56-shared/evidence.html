Shared package faults: Now beside Proposed

Eight faults that sit in the approved package (css/components.css and one theme register), not in the demo pages. Each is drawn twice from the real package: Now as it is, Proposed with one small rule that applies only inside the Proposed box. Pick a theme to check the proposal in it. Under every pair the measured numbers for all twelve themes. Nothing in the package has been changed; this page is evidence for your decision per item.

1. The segmented control never shrinks (S1)

The segmented track (.kp-segmented) keeps its full width, so the auto-refresh bar and the request-inspector bar stick out of their card at 390 px (formal: 186 px and 91 px). The proposal lets the track scroll inside its own bar and keeps the card its size.

2. A version badge with a 64-character digest runs out of its row (S2)

A raw sha256: digest has no place to break, so .kp-badge--version grows past its row (formal: 211 px at 390). The proposal caps the badge at the width of its row and lets only an over-long part break inside the word.

3. The checklist detail squeezes the label to a sliver (S3)

.kp-checklist__detail is nowrap and takes its full width, so the label “Hotel booking” is left with a few pixels and breaks inside the word. The proposal gives the detail a column of at most half the row and lets it wrap there.

4. Entity card buttons break “Restart” in the middle (S4)

.kp-entity__actions gives every button an equal minmax(0, 1fr) column, so a theme with wide button padding cuts the word. The proposal makes a column at least as wide as its button text, and a button never breaks inside a word.

5. The chart’s last x-axis label is cut at the plot’s right edge

The last date label is centred on the last tick, so half of it lies outside the plot and past the chart’s box at 1280 px in every theme. The proposal keeps room on the chart’s right for it.

6. The availability grid hides the column name “Kenny (you)” at 390

In the phone list the row that is yours loses its name (“Kenny (you)”), because it is squeezed to the width of its answer cell. The proposal puts your three answer buttons under your name instead of beside it.

7. The shortcut sheet overlaps what follows it at 390

Is the overlap the package’s dialog, or only the demo’s frame? Answer: only the demo’s frame (.kb-frame--stage is 24 rem high and the open dialog lies outside the page flow, with 41 rows); the package’s own dialog, opened as a modal, fits a phone. The proposal is a change to the demo frame, not to the package.

8. The titanium version badge is a pill with two cut corners

The badge keeps its 999 px radius under titanium’s chamfer (clip-path), so it reads as a pill with two corners cut. The proposal squares the radius for titanium only; every other theme is untouched.
