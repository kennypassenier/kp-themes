Intros and loading: can the intro wait for the page?

Measured on 2026-09-15 in chromium and firefox on examples/concept.html, served gzipped with a cache like GitHub Pages, at full speed and on a throttled "Fast 3G" link (562 ms per request, 180 kB/s shared). Stylesheets never flash: they block the first paint, the lazily fetched register included. What does show is the page itself before the intro: the intro is built by js/auto.js, which runs after the page has painted — up to 61 ms on a fast load, 0.6 to 4.1 seconds on a cold Fast 3G load, with the fallback font visible for up to 2 seconds of that. The page opens in synthwave; the theme menu, or the buttons below, switch it, and the windows follow.

Every theme's on-load motion

Look at: the arrival column. Four themes have an intro: synthwave, terminal, retro and phantom. Cyberpunk has none: what it plays on load is its headline decipher — glyph noise resolving letter by letter, then one slice burst — and its marks clearing. The line "Calibrating neural uplink" that may be remembered as cyberpunk's intro was cyberpunk's voice shown by synthwave's boot until scope-84. Also check the last column against the intro column: in all four intro themes the headline reveal is over before the intro ends, so on a first visit nobody sees it.

A, B and C side by side

Look at: pick a network and press Replay. The white at the start is the browser waiting for stylesheets. In A (today) the page appears, often in a plain fallback font, and the intro only drops over it when the scripts have run — a page you were already reading is covered. In B the intro is there from the first paint, its percentage never passes the share of real milestones done (stylesheets, scripts, fonts), and it lifts after all three — never earlier than today's sequence, never later than 4 s. In C a plain veil in the theme's background with a thin progress bar covers every theme until the same milestones, then the intro plays as today. Switch to cyberpunk to see C cost a theme without an intro its early, readable page. Under reduced motion A and B show no intro and C still shows the veil, without its fade.

What is mock, what is the package

Look at: nothing to judge here; this says what the windows are made of. The windows are frame.html. Its page (nav, headline, card, button) uses package classes (kp-nav, kp-card, kp-button) and the package registers. The network is MOCK: the stylesheets are already there, and the blank, the late fonts and the late scripts are timers set to the measured moments. Option A is REAL: it imports js/effects.js and attaches it, so the intro and the headline are the package's. Options B and C are a PROTOTYPE: proto/intro-proto.js is a copy of the arrival with the package's overlay classes (kp-boot, kp-boot__line, kp-boot__bar, kp-boot__skip) and proto/intro-proto.css is the veil; nothing in css/ or js/ changed. The numbers are from measure.mjs on real loads, not from these windows.
