The Topics page, rebuilt with the new components

kyu’s page that lists every topic on the hub (/topics), drawn with the package’s own components. Everything here is mock data: five made-up topics, five subscriptions and a stand-in for kyu that answers the automatic refresh. The two sections marked New show something kyu cannot do today.

Page header, lede and headline

The title of the page, one plain sentence saying what the page is for, and a line saying how many topics there are and how old the numbers are.

Expected:

  • The title Topics arrives with the theme’s headline reveal, the lede stands under it, and the line below reads “5 topics · updated 4 s ago”; the age keeps counting, and its full time shows when you point at it.
  • Replay the reveal plays the title’s arrival again. Loading keeps the title and the lede and shows the line as a waiting line of the same size; Could not read keeps them too and says in that line that the topics could not be read, with a Retry of normal size.

Formal’s headline is a plain arrival (no decipher): the title appears once and stays. The lede and the line below start at the title’s left edge, nothing touches an edge, and the loading and failed lines are as tall as the filled one.

Key figures, attention and the topics table

What the hub looks like at a glance: four key figures (Backlog and Dead filter the table), a band for what needs a decision, and one table with a row per topic that refreshes by itself.

Expected:

  • On load: Topics 5, Messages 3,820, Backlog 207, Dead 5; above them the attention band, worst first (a dead letter on notify.kenny, dead letters on almanac.sync, the desktop subscription flagged, newsflash.items quiet). Its buttons take you to the row.
  • Press the Backlog tile: it is marked as pressed, says “Filtering ×”, and the table keeps the topics with a backlog (a pill shows the filter); press it again or remove the pill and the tile lets go. Dead does the same for dead letters.
  • Table: click a header to sort (the arrow and aria-sort follow), Shift + click for a second key, type in the search, filter Status by its words, use Columns to hide a column; your sort, columns and density are remembered on this device. Backlog and Dead are bars (amber from 75 % of the largest, red from 90 %), the Flow bar splits each topic into waiting / in flight / dead / lapsed, Last published counts up and turns to a warning plate when the topic is quiet, and pointing at a cell opens a card with the details.
  • Refresh now (or R): the stand-in for kyu answers, the four figures count up to their new numbers, the changed cells and the status words change in the theme’s way, and the attention band keeps the items that did not change. Choose 15 s and it repeats; it waits while the pointer is on the table or a field has focus.
  • States: Loading puts the theme’s busy card over the table at its loaded height; Empty is the page of a hub with no topics yet, a drawing, a sentence, the same height; Could not read is one plate with a Retry. All quiet empties the band; pressing Dead then shows “Nothing is dead” with the all-clear drawing. Phone turns every row into a card and puts the tiles two by two.

Formal is paper and ink: ruled lines, a navy hairline for the pressed tile, sharp corners. Look at the columns: the numbers end on one right edge, the bars start in one place on every row, the idmarks are the same colours as in the band, and no text touches a cell edge. In the loading state the card covers the whole table, and the empty and failed plates are as tall as the filled table.

New: a heat map of topic by hour

New: kyu would need to count, per topic and per hour, the messages published and the messages that died, and keep those counts for a day or more (a small history table); it keeps no such history today. The grid shows when each topic is busy and when things die.

Expected:

  • Five rows (one per topic, with its mark) by 24 columns (the last 24 hours, the hour number on the axis), darker where more messages were published, and a bottom row for the whole hub. Point at a cell: a card says the topic, the hour and the count. Press a cell: the whole hour is pinned and the line under the grid names it.
  • Dead letters shows the same grid for messages that died: a calm cell where none did, a warning or red cell where some did. Press a topic name to keep only the rows you press. Arrow keys move between the cells.
  • Loading keeps the grid’s shape with the theme’s waiting cells and the busy card over it; Empty says there is no history yet, over the grid’s whole footprint; Could not read says so with a normal Retry.

Formal tones the grid like a ledger: one ink in steps of strength for the counts, a quiet cell for nothing. Check that the hour axis labels are not cramped, the row names wrap rather than cut, and the loading, empty and failed plates are as tall as the grid.
