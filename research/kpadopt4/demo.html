Formal’s five chart inks in order, a 2 px paper seam between the parts, the free rest as the muted track, the legend in aligned columns with the figures to the right. Loading is the meter’s ruled line, formal’s own; Not read and Failed are one note at the full size, formal’s empty-state title. Check no text touches the swatch or the figure, and nothing moves between states.

Uptime strip: 90 days and 48 hours

.kp-uptime[data-kp-uptime] with setUptime(el, { label, periods, from, to, open }) (js/uptime.js): one cell per period, toned up, degraded, down or no data, the window’s summary in exact figures over it. For the homelab Health page per service and for kyu’s delivery per hour.

Expected:

  • Four strips, each one line high: the name on the left, the summary on the right (100% · 0 incidents for jellyfin; kyu’s share is cut, never rounded up, and its incident count is the exact number). The cells never wrap.
  • Up is a soft green bar, degraded a lighter amber bar with fine horizontal rules, down a red bar with diagonal stripes, no data an empty frame (almanac’s first 26 days). So every state is a shape as well as a colour.
  • Point at a cell: it grows a little upwards (nothing beside it moves) and the hover card opens under it with the day, its state and its figures. Tab into a strip: one stop, on today’s cell, with the card open; the arrow keys walk the days, Home and End jump to the first and today.
  • The cells open something (new, both opt-in). jellyfin, kyu and kyu delivery open a period’s quick info: press a cell, or Enter or Space on the focused one, and formal’s popover plate opens under it with the day, a swatch of the cell with its state word, the exact figures, what happened (kyu’s bad days and hours) and, for the days, an Open 16/10/2026 button to that day’s page. The cell stays raised with the focus ring, so the plate reads as its own; the hover card rests meanwhile. The arrows carry the quick info along the strip; a second press, Escape (the focus goes back to the cell) or a press outside closes it, the open played backwards.
  • almanac has a page per day and no quick info: every day with data is a link; a press, Enter or Space follows it (here the grey box says which page it would open, the demo has no day pages). The grey box also logs kp-uptime-pick and kp-uptime-open.
  • Under each strip: “90 days ago” at the start and “Today” at the end (“48 hours ago” and “This hour” for the hours).

The strips in formal’s ink: the title in its body face at semibold, the share in bold tabular figures, the cells thin with a 1 px corner, and the hover card is formal’s own tooltip plate. A pressed cell’s quick info is formal’s popover plate, opened and closed in formal’s own motion, never detached from its cell.

How this differs

                        

                            

                                
                                    

                                        
                                        1 · Wizard
                                        2 · Step list
                                        3 · Journey
                                    

                                
                                
                                    

                                        What it is for
                                        A form split into steps that you fill in
                                        A run the program is doing now
                                        Where something outside you stands, and since when
                                    

                                    

                                        Who moves it
                                        You: Back and Next, each step checked first
                                        The program, live, step by step
                                        The world (a recruiter’s mail); the page records it
                                    

                                    

                                        What only it does
                                        Shows one step’s fields at a time, keeps you from skipping
                                        A spinner on the running step, a note and a duration per step
                                        
                                            The date each step was reached, one colour per step, a path that can end in Rejected, the dated line under
                                            it
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                        In the package
                                        .kp-wizard, js/wizard.js
                                        .kp-steplist, js/steplist.js
                                        The same .kp-steplist and js/steplist.js, option --journey
                                    

                                
                            

                        

                        

                            The answer: it is not the wizard again. The wizard is a form you move through yourself; the journey moves nobody
                            and asks nothing: it records how far an application got and on which day, the way a step list records a run. That is why
                            it was never a component of its own: it is the step list with one option, --journey (the steps side by side,
                            a date under each, data-state="current" without a spinner, a colour per step), the same markup, the same
                            script and the same states.
