# What makes formal formal

**Open (2026-10-08).** Nineteen questions wait for Kenny's verdicts in the review dialog (round `2026-10-08-r1`); the first option of each is the grammar proposed in [themes/formal/CHARACTER.md](../../themes/formal/CHARACTER.md) (G1 to G21).

**Why.** Kenny, 2026-10-08, in the review dialog of research/formal-anchor: formal's anchor is **The account is closed: the double rule** (option 2 of 7): a navy rule is ruled under the figure from the start at an even pace, a thinner second rule closes it the moment it reaches the end, and the rule formal already draws inside a hovered button is doubled on press. The anchor is picked; nothing else of formal's character is. The analysis ([CHARACTER.md](../../themes/formal/CHARACTER.md)) measured every decided formal pick against that one picture, proposed the grammar G1 to G21, and found eleven outliers (the register's settle curve, the fold that leaves, the dialog's fade and rise, the checked stamp, the watch dial, the dotted leader, the seal pressed, the voided stamp, the lift, no press). This page turns the proposal into the questions Kenny answers, the way research/forest-character did for forest and research/grotesk-character for grotesk.

**How.**

- `aspects.js` is the designer's data (id, label, rule, question, why, kind, scene key, options with key, name, what you see and the verdict, plus the story). `demo.js` imports it, adds the scene builders (one per `scene` key, the same package markup in every option of a row), builds the rows (`data-fm-aspect`, option cells `data-fm-option`) and writes `data-review-choices` from the same data, so the page and the dialog cannot disagree.
- `grammar.css` is the proposed grammar as tokens and shared keyframes on `.fm-page, .fm-scene` (the ruling time, the curves and their inverses, the wipe, the pen, the ledger loop, the busy bar and the arc), documented in comments. `options.css` (in `@layer kp.signature`) draws every option, scoped by `.fm-scene[data-fm-<question>='<key>']`; nothing hangs on `.fm-page` alone, because the review dialog moves the section into its stage. Colours are tokens only; every motion sits under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; the reduced pose is the ruled, closed picture. The "today" options use the register's own values (css/formal-register.css, §2 of CHARACTER.md), "the pick" options the decided demos'.
- One clock in `demo.js` plays every scene that arrives, opens, updates or leaves (`data-fm-phase`: gap, in, hold, out; `data-fm-kind="cycle"`), so the options of a row start together. A close is its open reversed: every one-shot motion is a keyframe pair (`fm-x` and `fm-x-out`) played forward in both phases, the close on the inverse curve (cubic-bezier(1-x2, 1-y2, 1-x1, 1-y1); steps(n, jump-end) and steps(n, jump-start)) and with its delay mirrored inside the clock's span T (`--fm-T`, 1400 ms), so what was ruled last is lifted first. Loading, the busy bar and the spinner loop in CSS. Replay restarts the clock; the speed buttons stretch every duration by 2 or 4; the dialog's Pause (Space) stops it.
- The pen: a ruled line carries a 2 px navy rule that is ruled ahead of its words and lifted when it reaches the margin; the finished pose is bare. It is how the ruling is seen without a decoration the part does not have at rest.
- Hover, focus and press are shown standing still on marked parts (`fm-pointed`, `fm-pointed-focus`, `fm-pointed-press`); the State buttons give the same classes to every control of questions 14 to 17 and take them away at Rest; the review dialog loops the three classes (`data-review-point`) so the pointer arrives and leaves, and presses Hover by itself on question 14.
- The gallery of decided components loads the six character demos through the review kit's embed mode (`?embed=…&theme=formal`) only when it is opened.
