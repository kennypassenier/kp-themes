The side navigation: four behaviour options

Kenny, 2026-10-13 (translated from Dutch): the minor options from the libraries survey. These four change what the navigation does, not how it looks, and each is off until the page asks for it with one attribute: data-kp-sidenav-cancelable (events the page can refuse, each with its reason), data-kp-sidenav-resizable (a handle that sets the width), data-kp-sidenav-backdrop="static" (a backdrop that does not close on a click) and data-kp-sidenav-close-on-backdrop="false". Every page plays itself with a pointer and keys over the real navigation; the log beside it lists the events the navigation really sends. The button at the top left, the slide from the left edge and each theme's own opening and closing are unchanged. Judge them in the review dialog ("Review in a dialog" at the top) or open each page on its own.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the navigation appears and disappears without sliding, and the refused cue is a ring without movement.

1 · Cancelable events with a reason

The page can say no, and every event says why it was asked.

What to watch, after "Play once": while the draft is not saved, the bars and Escape both try to close the menu and the page says no, so the menu stays where it is. The panel on the right, "What the menu reports", shows each try as cancelled with the way it was asked (toggler is the bars, escape is the key). After Save, Escape closes the menu and the line says allowed.

2 · Resize by dragging

The reader sets the menu width, and the page follows.

What to watch, after "Play once": the pointer drags the handle on the menu's inner edge and the page moves along. The bar under "Menu width" shows the width; it stops at the narrowest the page allows, the arrow keys change it too, and after a reload the width is still the same.

The pointer takes the handle on the menu's inner edge and drags it wider: the page moves with it and the bar shows the width. Dragging too far narrow stops at the limit. Then the arrow keys widen it (Shift takes five steps), the page is loaded again and the width is where it was left, and a double press gives the original width back. Check that the handle is visible in formal's colours, sits inside the menu and covers no link.
