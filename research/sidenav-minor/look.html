The side navigation: six small options

Six options for the window-edge side navigation, each one attribute or one token and off by default. Every window below plays by itself: the panel opens and closes on a loop and shows itself pushing the page, keeping a gutter and lying over it. The toggle shows and hides only the panel: nothing dims and nothing to the right of it covers the page. Each theme keeps its own opening and closing, the close being the opening backwards. Judge them in the review dialog ("Review in a dialog" at the top), one theme at a time.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the panels appear and disappear without their effects. Turn it off to judge the movement.

1 · Width on a narrow screen

On a phone the panel can be narrower.

The token --kp-sidenav-width-narrow, read while the panel is held as an over panel on a narrow screen.

In formal, compare the two phones: with the token the panel is clearly narrower (13rem, against 20rem). The panel is ruled in where it stands and ruled away again, in the same time both ways. Every label stays readable.

In light, compare the two phones: with the token the panel is clearly narrower (13rem, against 20rem). The panel comes out of a glare and goes back into it, in the same time both ways. Every label stays readable.

In dark, compare the two phones: with the token the panel is clearly narrower (13rem, against 20rem). The panel is laid in by a bright line that crosses it, and the line takes it away again, in the same time both ways. Every label stays readable.

In cyberpunk, compare the two phones: with the token the panel is clearly narrower (13rem, against 20rem). The panel glitches in and out in three jumps, in the same time both ways. Every label stays readable.

In synthwave, compare the two phones: with the token the panel is clearly narrower (13rem, against 20rem). The panel is uncovered from its foot behind a glowing beam, and covered again, in the same time both ways. Every label stays readable.
