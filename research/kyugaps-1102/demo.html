kyu: the selection bar's empty room and the tour on a tall part

Two things kyu ran into on its topic page. The selection bar kept an empty band above the dead letters while nothing was ticked: a new option folds it away, and you pick which is the default. The tour card on a part taller than the window stood out of reach above it: it now stays in the window, nothing to choose. Formal only.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. State puts every window of the section in that state; Width shows them at a phone’s 390 px.

Dead letters of orders.created

kyu’s dead letters card, with the selection bar over its six dead letters.

What is asked (kyu 34): kyu’s dead letters card kept an empty band of 72 px (86 px on a phone) above its table while nothing was ticked: the selection bar holds its room so the table never jumps. New option data-kp-selection-room="fold": the empty bar takes no room, and the first tick opens it. Holding the room stays the default unless you pick Fold.

Expected:

  • Nothing ticked: in Hold a blank band of 72 px stands between the card’s sentence and the table. In Fold the table sits right under the sentence, with the usual 8 px gap of the card.
  • Two ticked: both show the bar with “2 selected”, the two names, Requeue, Delete and Untick all. In Fold the room opens from 0 to 72 px in the theme’s rule time (formal: 400 ms, even speed) while the bar is ruled open from its left edge, and the table moves down with it; in Hold only the bar is ruled open.
  • Press Nothing ticked again: in Fold the bar is ruled closed and the room closes with it, the exact reverse. Phone: the same, the bar 86 px with the count on its own row.

Formal: the bar’s blue start edge and hairline frame, ruled open at an even speed. Check that in Fold nothing of the empty bar is left (no line, no gap) and that the table never overlaps the opening bar.

Dead letters on a busy day

kyu’s topic page with thirty dead letters, so the card is taller than the window, and the tour standing on the whole card.

What is asked (kyu 33): kyu’s tour step on the dead letters card put its card above the card’s top, out of the window (top −124 px), because the card is taller than the window; kyu pointed the step at the heading instead. The tour card now takes the other side when its own side has no room, and lies over the part, 16 px inside the window, when neither side has room. A fault fix: nothing to choose.

Expected:

  • Tour on the dead letters: the card is ringed and the page around it dimmed; the tour card (“Dead letters”, 1 of 1, Done) stands 16 px under the window’s top edge, over the top of the ringed card, fully readable, with nothing of it cut off.
  • Scroll the frame: the tour card stays inside the window and follows the part; it never leaves the top or the bottom edge. Esc or Done ends the tour. Closed: no tour.
  • Phone: the same, the tour card the window’s width less 16 px on either side.

Formal: the tour card on its popover plate with the focus-coloured ring round the part. Check that the card is wholly in the window and that its text never touches its edges.
