What nostromo is

Nostromo is the ship's console, 1979. The ground is a moulded beige case: vent slots, label tape, screws, and an indicator lamp on every switch. Set into it, where the ship reports, are amber screens: dark glass in a thick bezel, amber phosphor, scanlines.

So everything that happens is something a console does: a screen is drawn by the raster, row by row from the top (opening, arriving, a change, waiting, leaving), a lamp lights on the case (where you point, what changed), the tube is struck on and switched off. It moves frame by frame, the way a 1979 screen redraws. Nothing grows, glides, floats or bounces.
