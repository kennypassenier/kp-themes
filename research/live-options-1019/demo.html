Options that follow their attribute at runtime

A choice a page could make with an attribute (segments or a menu, where a pill stands, how a table sorts, how a palette matches) was read once, when the component appeared, so changing the attribute later did nothing. Your rule (17/10) is that such a variant is an option with a default that switches at runtime. These ten components now do, and the refresh control also stops ignoring Refresh now, the R key and Retry now while it is paused. 10 pairs in all, formal only. In each section the buttons above the component set the very attribute a page would set; the component follows at once, and the line under it shows the effect. Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: Whether the choices were segments or a menu was decided once, when the control appeared; changing data-kp-look afterwards did nothing, so a page could not offer the person the other look. Now the control is drawn again with the look the attribute names, with the chosen interval, the countdown and the “updated” line kept. The choices (data-kp-refresh-choices), the stale time and a new data-kp-refresh follow their attributes the same way. Segments stay the default.

Automatic refresh: Refresh now while paused

Expected:

  • The control opens on Paused. Press Refresh now: “updating…”, then “updated just now” and the count under the component goes up by one. Nothing else refreshes by itself.
  • Press the R key (the focus not in a field): the same. Press Every 30 s in the group above the component and the control counts down by itself; press Paused and it stops.
  • Press Fails and then Refresh now: the yellow note “Last refresh failed.” with Retry now under it. Retry now asks again (press Answers first to see it succeed).

the control shows updating and then updated while paused, and the failure note and Retry now keep their place in the control.
