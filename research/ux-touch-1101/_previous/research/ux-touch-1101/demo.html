Weekly review, the Boards page

Four lanes as the Habits app has them: Inbox, Doing (at most 2), Waiting and Done. The cards are the app’s task rows (mock titles).

What changed (your answer of 01/11/2026, board-phone: one column with a switcher): On a phone each lane took 85% of the width and the board scrolled sideways; you rejected that. Under 40rem of its own width the board now shows one lane, with a switcher above it that names every lane and its exact count.

Expected:

  • Phone: one row of four buttons, “Inbox 4 · Doing 2 / 2 · Waiting 1 · Done 7”, the name over its count; under it only the chosen lane’s cards. Nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Press a lane’s button (or Tab to the switcher and use ← →) and its cards replace the others.
  • Move to: open a card’s ⋯ menu (or right-click it, or Shift+F10): “Move to” lists the other lanes with what they hold. Pick one: the card leaves this lane, the counts change at once, the line under the board says where it went, and the lane you were in stays on screen.
  • Move a card into Doing: it reads 3 / 2 and its count turns red (over the limit); nothing is refused.
  • Full width: the lanes stand side by side and drag as approved; the menus carry Move to as well.

Check that the four buttons fit one row at 390 px with every count readable, that the chosen lane is plain to see, and that a move from the menu never leaves you in another lane.

kyu’s API token

The token shows for 10 seconds; the drain under the value runs out with the countdown. The dashed row under the field is the next row of a form.

What changed (your answer of 01/11/2026, secret-reveal: a fixed height): In kyu, Reveal grew the field and added a “Hides in 10 s” line, so everything below jumped 61 px and jumped back when it hid. The field now keeps one height and the countdown stands inside the value’s box.

Expected:

  • Before (held still in its shown state): the countdown on a line under the buttons.
  • New: press Reveal. The token and “Hides in 10 s” stand inside the value’s box, the drain along its foot; the dashed row does not move. The line under it says how far the row moved: 0 px.
  • The token never runs under the countdown: it stops before it, and a longer value scrolls inside the box.
  • Wrap: a long value wraps in a box of fixed height (two lines) and scrolls inside it; the row below stays put.

Check that the countdown reads as part of the field, not as a second value, and that nothing under the field moves when it shows and hides.

Medicine Cabinet, on a phone

Chips, a switch, a check box, radios, a quantity, a text field, a select, a time field, an uptime row, name links in a table and a list, and a dialog’s ✕. A mouse cannot be a finger, so the switch makes this stage behave as a touch screen does (data-kp-touch).

What changed (your answer of 01/11/2026, touch-targets: invisibly bigger; dialog-close: the ✕ stays, 44 px): The apps counted up to 340 targets under 44 px on a phone. Under a touch screen every small part now takes a 44 px hit area that you cannot see; only text fields and selects stand taller, because a field cannot carry an invisible area.

Expected:

  • Mouse: every part as approved.
  • Touch: the parts look the same; text fields, the select and the time field stand 44 px tall. The line under the stage counts the targets whose hit area is under 44 px.
  • Show hit areas: each invisible area is tinted, so you can see it is at least 44 by 44 px around its part (uptime cells only in height: a tap on the row opens the nearest hour).
  • The ✕ of the dialog keeps its place and look; its hit area is 44 px.

Check that Touch changes nothing you can see except the fields’ height, and that the tinted areas do not swallow a neighbour’s text.

The Badge page, 390 px wide

The page itself, in a window of a phone’s width. Wider than 40rem the navigation is the column it always was.

What changed (your answer of 01/11/2026, docs-phone-nav: fold): At 390 px every page of the documentation drew the whole side navigation first, so a page’s own text started about 5194 px down. On a phone the navigation now folds behind a menu button, as .kp-sidenav does for apps.

Expected:

  • Closed: a menu button top left and the theme button top right on one row; the page’s title right under them.
  • Open: the navigation slides in from the left over the page, the theme’s own panel, no dimmed backdrop. Escape or the button closes it and the focus goes back to the button.
  • The line under the window says where the page’s text starts.

Check that the menu button is easy to find and that the opened panel reads as the theme’s own navigation.
