Week agenda and run timeline for the admin dashboard

Two things the dashboard still draws itself: the week of the schedules page (sch-week, sch-pill) and the timeline of the Activity page (ac-tl). Both are the package’s now: .kp-week with js/week.js and .kp-gantt with js/gantt.js (both new, wired into js/auto.js).

Mock on this page: everything prefixed kt-, the sample data (a household server’s jobs) and the buttons that drive the timeline. Package: every kp- class and the scripts. Every section says what you should see under Expected, and each section also shows the component in a 360 px column.

Week agenda: seven days, every item at its time

A column per day, an hour axis, every item a pill at its time of day, a line across today at the current time. Below 40rem of its own width the days become rows. Items open with a click or Enter.

Expected:

  • Seven day columns starting today. Today carries the word Today under its number, a coloured number and a tinted column; a line crosses it at the current time and the pills above the line are quieter.
  • Every pill shows its time and its name, ending in an ellipsis when the name is long. The pill with a ring is the next run; the struck-through ones are off or skipped; the dashed one is the host’s own round, for reference, and does nothing when pressed.
  • Hover a pill (not the dashed one): a card with its detail. Click it or press Enter: the readout under the agenda logs the item.
  • Tab enters the agenda once, on a pill. The arrows move: up and down inside a day, left and right to the nearest item of the next day. Tab leaves.
  • Prev, Next and Today move the range by seven days and the title follows; the demo then hands the agenda the same weekly pattern for the new days (a real page would load them).
  • The 360 px column holds the same days as rows, with every item shown and nothing cut off.

Check the full-width agenda and the 360 px one. Ledger entries: a heavy 4px rule on the left of each pill, 2px corners, the weekday in small capitals, dotted dividers between the days like leaders.

Run timeline: bars per job, growing live

                    

                        A lane per job, a bar per run on a time axis that follows the clock. Running bars grow, queued ones wait hollow, finished ones
                        are solid, failed ones stand taller. Drag across the plot to select a range.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            An axis on top with times, one lane per job with its name on the left, and a line for now. The six runs that are running
                            or queued now are at the right edge: running is striped with a bright leading edge and grows; queued is
                            hollow and dashed and grows too.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Succeeded bars are solid; failed bars are solid, taller, with a cap at the end and a cross when wide enough.
                            The Updates lane has two overlapping runs on two rows; the Reports lane has small bars close together.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Hover a bar: a card with name, times, length and state (a running one counts up). Click it or press Enter: the readout
                            under the timeline logs it. Tab enters once; the arrows move along a lane (left, right) and between lanes (up, down).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            The range buttons 1h 6h 24h 7d change how much time shows. Live (pressed) makes the right edge follow now;
                            press it to freeze the window, press again to follow.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Drag across the plot: a dashed band, then a chip with the range, Zoom (the window becomes the range and stops
                            following) and Clear. A click on a bar without a drag still opens it.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            The buttons under the timeline drive it: Start a run adds a running bar, Finish turns the oldest running one
                            solid, Fail turns it failed, Queue a run adds a hollow one, Start the queued run turns it striped.
                            Fast time runs the clock sixty times faster so growth is visible; no lane changes height when a run ends.
                        
    • 
                    

                

                

                    

                        Press Start a run, then Fast time, and watch a bar grow; then drag across the plot. Inked strips with 2px corners, the axis in
                        small capitals with dotted rules.
