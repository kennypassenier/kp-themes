Action tiles, status rows and the facet list

Three parts Kenny’s admin dashboard still draws itself because kp-themes 10.1.1 has no component for them (its nx-action, perstack-chip and hl-opt). Each is built here on the package: the rules are in css/components.css and all sixteen registers, the behaviour in js/action-tiles.js, js/statusrow.js and js/facets.js. Everything on this page is the package; only the sample data (a homelab’s stacks and log lines), the scripted runs, and the rows that stand in for hover, focus and press are this page’s. Judge in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top): formal first, three sections.

Hover, focus and press rows are drawn by copying the package’s own rules (the page reads the stylesheets and re-writes :hover as a class), so they show the theme’s real look. That needs the page to be served over http (as the catalogue does); opened as a file, those rows show only the plain tile.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so hover and press change the look but nothing moves or fades.

Theme:

1 · Action tiles

A button or a link drawn as a tile: a title and the one sentence that says what it does, in a grid whose tiles on one row are all as tall as the tallest. Use it where a short list of things to do (or places to go) needs a sentence each, on a landing page or in a drawer. A button tile does something; a link tile goes somewhere (and shows a chevron).

An engraved plate with a 3 px corner. Point at a tile: nothing moves, the title takes a 2 px navy underline (the quill). Press and hold: a double navy rule is laid inside the plate (the seal). The destructive tile has a red bar at its start edge and a red title; the disabled one is a quieter plate.

A white card with a hair of shadow and a round corner. Pointing settles it 1 px toward the paper, a press lands it flat 2 px down with the shadow gone. Check the destructive bar is inside the corner, not over it.

A machined panel with a square-ish 2 px corner. Pointing lights a thin line of the four chart colours down the start edge, with a faint glow; keyboard focus is an inset double ring, not an outer one. A press is a plain colour step.

A holo plate: a hairline rim, spaced capitals for the title. Pointing lights a 14 px circuit grid over the tile and the rim goes yellow with a glow; the grid must not make the sentence hard to read. A press washes the tile yellow.

An arcade marquee: a pink frame with a glow, the title in spaced display capitals. Pointing widens the glow; a press dims the tube and drops the tile 1 px. Check the glow stays inside the grid gap and does not touch the next tile.

A sticker on a dashed washi outline with its own shadow. Pointing lifts it 2 px on a longer shadow, a press sticks it down. The outline stays dashed in every state.

A cell of the screen, all mono, with a gutter at the start. Pointing writes a green > prompt in the gutter and boxes the tile in a 2 px green outline 3 px out; a press is reverse video (green ground, black type). Nothing eases.

A herbarium sheet with the leaf corner (top-start and bottom-end square) and an italic title. Pointing lays a dashed trail ring round the tile (the blaze); a press washes it moss. Check the ring follows the leaf corner.

A drawn box: a dashed frame, mono capitals. Pointing closes the frame to a line and lights an 8 px drafting grid in cyan across the tile; a press is a cyan wash. Keyboard focus keeps a dashed outline.

The horizon: an amber line along the foot of the tile with a warm glow rising from it. Pointing thickens the line to 3 px; a press thins it to 1 px and lowers the tile 1 px.

A slab: a 3 px black line on a 6 px hard shadow, a lavender tint, heavy capitals. Pointing grows the shadow to 8 px (the tile does not rise); a press drives the slab into its footprint, shadow gone. Focus is a 4 px ink outline.

Lacquer with a fine gold inlay rule inside the plate, spaced capitals. Pointing brightens the inlay to full gold and the rim to gold; a press sinks the plate 1 px under an inner shadow (the wax seal).

Cut paper: a square slab on a 3 px deep-red shadow, condensed capitals. Pointing throws a red bar in behind the title in two cuts and flips its ink to black; the tile itself does not move. A press drops the slab 3 px onto its shadow.

A transit board: no frame, a red bar hanging from the top edge. Pointing grows the bar from 6 to 10 px, a press to 14 px (it is an inset shadow, so the tile never changes size). The bold titles must stay on one line where they fit, in every column width.

An indicator panel: a muted plate with a dark well along its top and mono capital titles. Pointing wakes the console: heavy scanlines over the plate; a press darkens the plate and seats it 1 px. Check the scanlines do not cut through the sentence.

An anodised plate with a 4 px edge along the top. Pointing turns that edge to the primary; every change is instant and linear (metal does not ease). A press keeps the primary edge and lights a hairline on top.

Expected:

  • The grid: the first row has a tile with one short line and tiles with three lines, and all of them are the same height. The grid fills the width with columns of at least 15rem and goes to one column when narrow; resize the window.
  • Press the button tiles in the live grid (Back up now, Restart the dashboard, Delete all snapshots): the tile becomes busy at once, the theme’s own spinner appears in the right-hand corner and the readout under the grid says what happened. The tile keeps its size (the title does not rewrap), the keyboard focus stays on it, and pressing it again does nothing until it is done (about 2.5 s). Link tiles only move the readout; they do not leave the page.
  • The disabled tile (Check for updates) does nothing and shows no hover. The tile with a spinner already in it (Rebuild the search index) is busy from the start and does not answer a press.
  • Keyboard: Tab goes tile by tile (one stop each); Enter or Space presses a button tile, Enter a link tile; the focus ring is the theme’s own and whole in every theme.

2 · Status rows

One row per item that is read or checked one by one: a mark, the item’s name, a short detail and, when it failed, a Retry that tries that one item again. The four states are each a shape and a word as well as a colour: a disc with a tick (ok), the theme’s spinner (loading), a disc with a cross (failed, with its reason and a Retry), a dashed ring (empty, with the reason there is nothing).

Ledger rows ruled underneath, no box: a tick disc, the name in bold, the reason under it. A failed row keeps the red rule under it and a Retry button; an empty row is dashed and quiet.

Soft cards with a hair of shadow and a round corner; round marks. A failed row has a red edge and a Retry in the light button.

Machined rows with square-ish marks (3 px) and a mono detail line. Loading shows the theme spinner in the mark.

Square plates with a hairline rim; a loading or failed row gets a 3 px bar on its start edge. The detail line is mono. Marks are square.

Marquee rows with a pink hairline; a loading row glows. The detail is in the tech mono.

Round 2 px sticker rows; the ok mark is tilted -8 degrees like a stamp. The failed row keeps its red edge.

Plain text rows with a dashed rule at the start, all mono: + ok, x failed, - empty, and the theme spinner for loading. A loading row has a solid rule.

Rows with a 4 px start edge: dark for the quiet states, green for loading, red for failed. Marks are round.

Dashed drafting boxes, square marks, mono detail; a loading row is drawn in a solid line.

Plain warm rows; the ok mark carries a faint green halo. Nothing else changes size.

Slabs on a hard shadow with a 3 px line; marks are square with a heavy ring. A loading row takes the muted ground.

Plates with an inner hairline inlay, square marks. The failed row's red edge must not hide the inlay.

Slabs with a 4 px start edge (dark, red while loading, red-edge for failed), square marks, condensed type.

Rows ruled underneath by a 2 px black line, no box, bold names. A failed row keeps its red rule. Check no row wraps into two lines where one fits.

Panel rows with a dark frame and 2 px marks; a loading row has an amber inner line. Detail in mono.

Plates with a strong edge, mono detail. Loading uses the theme spinner; nothing eases.

Expected:

  • Press “Read all twelve”: every row goes to loading (the spinner, “reading (n s)” with the seconds counting), then the rows settle one by one: most become ok, two fail with a reason and a Retry button, one is empty with its reason. A screen reader hears one summary (for example “12 updated, 2 failed”), not twelve sentences.
  • The list does not move while this happens. The “height of the list” readout under it shows the same number before, during and after (in the second list, whose rows reserve room for a two-line reason). In the first, compact list a row grows by one line only when its reason is longer than one line.
  • Press Retry on a failed row: that row alone goes back to loading and, after about 1.5 s, becomes ok (the second Retry of “traefik” fails again, on purpose, to show a failing retry). The keyboard focus is not lost: after pressing Retry with the keyboard the focus moves to the row’s name.

3 · Facet list

Checkbox rows that narrow a list by one property, each with how many items it would show, and an only button that selects just that row. “Everything” turns every row on. The checkboxes are the theme’s own; only is the theme’s ghost button, a sibling of the label (never inside it), shown when the pointer is on the row or focus is in it, and always on a touch screen.

Square-cornered rows. The name is truncated with an ellipsis, the count is right-aligned in tabular figures. Point at a row: a quiet wash and the only button appears; none of it moves the counts.

Pill rows with a soft wash; the only button appears on hover or when focus is in the row. The counts keep one column.

Square rows, mono counts, a faint wash. The checkbox is the theme's own.

Square rows with mono counts; the section title is a spaced mono label. The only button is the theme's ghost button.

Slightly rounded rows with mono counts; the only button shows on hover.

Round rows with a soft wash; checkboxes are the sticker kind.

All mono; rows are plain text with a wash on hover. The checkbox is the terminal's.

Rows with the leaf corner, so the wash shows it; counts right-aligned.

Mono counts, square rows; the checkbox is drawn in line.

Softly rounded rows with a warm wash.

Heavy: on hover a row turns into a solid block of the secondary colour (yellow) with black type. The title above is bold.

Square rows; the section title is in very widely spaced capitals.

Square rows with a grey wash; the title is condensed capitals. The only button is phantom's key cap.

Bold counts in black, square rows; the names must stay on one line and cut with an ellipsis, never wrap.

Slightly rounded rows with mono counts; the wash is the panel's.

Square rows, mono counts. Contact is instant, there is no fade on the wash.

Expected:

  • Click a checkbox or its name in the live log below: that source’s lines appear or disappear, the “Showing x of y lines” readout follows, and the Everything checkbox becomes mixed (a dash) while only some rows are on.
  • Point at a row (or Tab into it): the only button appears at the row’s end without moving the count. Press it: that row stays on, every other turns off, and the readout says “Showing only …”. Press Everything: all rows are on again.
  • Keyboard: Tab reaches each checkbox (Space turns it); when a row has focus its only button is the next stop (Enter presses it). After pressing it the focus stays where it was.
  • “A new source appears” adds a row in its place without touching the others: if you hold the keyboard focus on a checkbox while it is added, the focus stays. “Counts are loading” replaces the counts with quiet bars that keep their place, and “Counts arrive” writes them back. Reset the filters turns everything on.
  • The row of facets (last block) is the phone form: one line of bordered rows that scrolls sideways.
