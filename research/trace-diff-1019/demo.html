Two faults of the lists

HTTPSwitchboard found two places where an approved component did not line up at a phone’s width: the verdict words of a trace, and a long word in a diff line. 2 pairs in all, both in formal. Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: In a trace whose hops stand in a column (a phone, or a narrow box) the verdict word of each hop (Allowed, Refused, Not Reached) took only as much room as its own word, so the words started and ended on a different edge from hop to hop and the lines of words beside them ended on different edges too. Now the trace has one column for the verdict word, as wide as the longest word of any hop, and every hop uses it: the words start on one edge, the hops’ own words end on one edge. The marks, the words, the chips, the dimmed hop after a refusal, and the row of hops side by side on a wide box are as you approved them.

Path trace: the hops in a column

One message followed from its sender to its destination, at a phone’s width, where the hops stand under each other.

Expected:

  • Under each other, the verdict words Allowed, Refused and Not Reached all start on one edge and the hops’ own words all end on one edge.
  • In the second frame (words only) the same: one column for the verdict word.

Formal: the verdict words of every hop start on one vertical line, and so do the ends of the hops’ own words.

The trace, the word only

What changed since you approved it: A line of a diff holding one long word (an address or a file name with no space in it) made its text column as wide as that word, so the line ran past the other lines and past the plate’s edge at a phone’s width. Now the text column may be narrower than its longest word, and the word breaks inside the column, so every line ends on the plate’s inner edge. The line numbers, the signs, the colours and the diff view with its words marked are as you approved them.

Diff: a line with a long token

Before and after of a message, one line holding an address that has no place to break.

Expected:

  • The long address breaks onto a second line inside the text column; every line ends on the plate’s inner edge.
  • The line numbers stand in one column and the signs in the next, on every line, including the broken one.

Formal: the right edge of every line is the plate’s inner edge, none runs past.
