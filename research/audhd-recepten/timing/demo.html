Recipe app: counting back from dinner

Time blindness means “I have plenty of time” is true until it suddenly is not. The app answers it backwards: you say when you eat, it works out when each part of the meal has to start, shows it as lanes you can see at a glance, shrinks the wait to the start as a disc instead of ticking numbers, and nudges you once, kindly. The sample dish is spaghetti with meatballs; the step times are mock data and nothing is saved. The page runs on a demo clock that starts at 17:22 on 18/10/2026.

We eat at: the sentence that counts back

A sentence you fill in. Say when dinner is and it counts back through the recipe’s step times to the moment you start; change the start instead and dinner moves.

Expected:

  • The sentence reads We eat at 18:30, so I start at 17:40. Both times are fields in the words. Change We eat at (type, use the arrow keys, or pick from the list) and I start at follows at once; change I start at and We eat at follows. Pace and to spare move the start too: a slow pace stretches the steps you do with your hands, spare time makes the food ready earlier.
  • Under the sentence one line says in words when each part starts. The clock says 17:44 puts the start in the past: a calm blue note offers a new dinner time (18:34) instead of an error, and pressing it moves dinner.
  • Loading shows a skeleton in the start field only, at its final width, while you can still type the dinner time. No step times and Failed are plates that fill the card, with a normal-size button.

Formal: the sentence is the biggest thing in the card, the underlined times sit inside the words with air round them, and the late note is blue and calm, never red. In the plates the title and sentence are centred and the button is normal size.

Tonight’s plan: one lane per track

Every part of the meal on its own lane, counted back from dinner, so you see when each one starts (the pasta water goes on at 18:05) and what runs at the same time.

Expected:

  • Three lanes, Pasta, Sauce and Oven, each with the moment it starts under its name (Start at 18:05 for the pasta) and a diamond where it starts. Each bar is one step; all three lanes end where the blue band of dinner begins. The line for now stands at 17:22.
  • Press a bar or a diamond: its card says the step, the times and whether you can walk away from it, and the line under the plan repeats it. We eat at moves every lane; Slow day stretches only the steps you do with your hands. Run moves the clock a minute a second and the line for now crosses the plan; Hold stops it.

Formal: three clearly separate lanes in three calm colours, lane names with their start under them, nothing cut or touching the edge, and the busy and plate states take the same height as the plan.

Time until you start: a disc, not ticking numbers

The wait until the start moment as a coloured disc that shrinks to nothing, so the time left is seen at a glance and no numbers change; the digits are one switch away.

Expected:

  • A disc named Start cooking · 18 min, full, with Start and Reset. Press Start: the disc turns back towards twelve o’clock and shrinks to nothing at 17:40, while the line under it says what the demo clock reads. No digits change anywhere. At the end it says Start cooking: time is up, and a note says what to do first.
  • Running, Last minute and Time is up jump to those moments. Real time runs the clock at normal speed, A minute a second makes 18 minutes pass in 18 seconds. We eat at sets how long the disc is (19:30 is a 78 minute wait, so its digits show hours).
  • Loading puts the busy spinner over the disc; No step times and Failed are plates that fill the card, with a normal-size button.

Formal: the disc is round, centred and large, its edge moves smoothly and the buttons under it keep their width as the words change. With digits on, they sit beside it without moving the disc.

A gentle nudge before the start

Shortly before the start the page says so once, in plain words, and offers Not yet. Past the start it offers a new dinner time, never a reproach.

Expected:

  • 30 minutes before is a quiet blue heads-up with nothing to press (only with the early nudge option). 10 minutes before says Start in 10 minutes, names the first step and has I am starting. At the start turns to the warning tone and says what comes first. Past the start has it too, says nothing is lost and offers Eat at 18:34; it never uses the red failure style.
  • Under it, Not yet offers 5 minutes, 10 minutes or your own time, each with the exact moment. Pick one and the band becomes a quiet line, with Snoozed until 17:35 and End snooze.
  • Loading puts the busy spinner over the card; No step times and Failed are plates that fill it, with a normal-size button.

Formal: the band is calm and readable, blue for information and warning-toned for the start, the button sits at the right edge of the band, and the snooze row lines up under it.
