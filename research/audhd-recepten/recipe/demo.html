The recipe page and calm settings

The screen of a recipe app for someone with AuDHD: the recipe with its amounts that follow the number of portions, a big Begin cooking button, a code that opens cook mode on the phone, a printable sheet, the calm switch with theme and language, a cooking log in which an empty week is quiet, and the states of the page. Nothing here counts, scores or judges: no streaks, no red for a day without cooking. The recipe is a Belgian one (witloof in the oven); the dates are sample dates, today is Friday 16/10/2026 in this demo.

Recipe page: read it, scale it, begin

The title, a short summary, the time and effort it costs, a big Begin cooking button, and the ingredients with every amount following the number of portions. Press Begin cooking: it shows busy and done here; cook mode is the next screen and is not part of this demo.

Expected:

  • The card is Fetching the recipe under the theme’s busy card from the first frame, then fills: title with an Effort: Low badge, the summary folded to two sentences with More, Total time, Hands-on and Effort in three columns, the big Begin cooking button with its one line under it, and Last time (04/10/2026 18:40) with what you wrote then.
  • Portions stands at 4. Press + or −, hold either, or type a number: every amount changes at once and lights for a moment (not in calm mode). Chicory 8 becomes 12 at 6; onion 1 becomes ½ at 2; milk 500 ml becomes 1.5 l at 12; the pinch of nutmeg never changes.
  • Point at Chicory, or Tab to it: a hover card with the note. The amounts, units and names stay in three columns that line up in every row.
  • Begin cooking: the spinner, then Opening, then the button is as before. The first step it announces is only to get the nine things out.
  • Buttons above: Summary folded or whole, Notes on a hover card or written under the name, Parts of a whole as fractions or decimals. Each redraws the card at once and keeps the portions you set.

Formal’s paper and navy: the big button is the one filled navy plate on the card, the amounts are bold and right-aligned so the numbers read down in one column, the dotted underline marks the names that carry a note, and nothing else competes with Begin cooking. Look at the line between each row and at the air round the amounts.

Recipe page: loading, not found, could not load

The same card when the recipe is being fetched, when it does not exist any more, and when the server did not answer. Each state keeps the card’s size, so nothing around it moves.

Expected:

  • Loading: the theme’s busy card centred over the whole card with Fetching the recipe; the recipe under it is dimmed and cannot be pressed. It keeps moving.
  • Not found: the card’s empty plate fills the card, centred, at heading size: We cannot find this recipe, It may have been renamed or removed. Nothing you saved is lost. and one button under it. It is calm: no red, no exclamation mark, no “error”.
  • Could not load: the destructive alert fills the card, centred: The recipe could not be loaded, why, and a normal-size Try again. Press it: it loads for a second and the recipe is back. The words say the connection failed, never that anything you made is gone.
  • The card is exactly as tall in all four states. The buttons above switch them; the dialog sets the default of the button under Not found.

Formal’s empty plate and its destructive alert, in the middle of the card and large; the busy card is formal’s navy ring. Look at the text centre against the card centre, and at the card’s height in the four states.

Cook on your phone: a code that opens cook mode

Scan the code with the phone’s camera and cook mode opens there with the same number of portions, so the laptop can stay in the other room. The code is drawn on this page; the link never leaves the device.

Expected:

  • A black-on-white code on its own light plate (the one place the theme does not colour, so every camera reads it), the link as text under it, and a Copy link button. Press it: a spinner, then Copied, same width.
  • Beside it, three numbered steps in plain words: Point the camera, Tap the link, Cook, and a line saying the screen stays on while you cook.
  • Buttons above: Size standard or large, Link shown or hidden, Dots square or round, Show always or behind a Show QR code button (then a Hide button covers it again). Each redraws the code at once.

The plate is white with a thin formal border, the link is formal’s small muted text, Copy is the plain button. Look at the quiet zone round the code and at the words beside it.

Printable recipe: the sheet

The same recipe on paper for a cook who wants no screen: the ingredients as a list to tick, the steps below, no buttons, black on white. The frame shows what the printer gets; Print this recipe opens the real print dialog.

Expected:

  • An A4 sheet: the title, For 4 portions, the ingredients as a checklist in three groups (Vegetables, Meat and dairy, Pantry) each with its amount and, in small text, its note, then five numbered steps. At the foot, Printed 16/10/2026 14:05. No buttons, no colour, no theme.
  • Buttons above: Notes on the paper or left out, Box square or round, Columns one or two, Lines to write on or not. The sheet changes at once.

Paper has no theme, so this is black on white in every theme; look at the boxes, the room round the amounts and whether the steps read as one clear column.

Calm settings: motion, theme and language

The three settings a person with a sensitive day reaches for first: one switch that turns movement and effects off, the theme, and the language of the recipe. Each change applies at once and nothing asks you to confirm it.

Expected:

  • Four rows on one grid: a name with one sentence, its control, and what it says now, each column on one edge. Calm mode is off; switch it on and the line under it says what is quiet now; the rest of this demo page keeps its calm until you switch it off (this demo does not remember it between visits).
  • Theme: an icon button with a menu of twelve themes (light and dark apart). Pick one: the check shows which, and the line says it would apply at once and be kept for next time; this page stays in formal so you can judge it.
  • Language: NL and EN. Pick one: the preview line under it (the recipe title, the portions, the button) is in that language, and the recipe page of the first section opens in it next time.
  • Try it: Save settings shows the spinner and then Saved; press it again and it says Not saved with the reason in a line under it. In calm mode the spinner breathes or holds still, as set.
  • Buttons above: what Calm switches off (movement, movement and effects, everything), what Loading does in calm mode (slow pulse, still), how the Theme is chosen (menu or twelve swatches) and how the Language is chosen (switch or chips).

Formal’s switch is a navy track with a white thumb, the language switch has a navy plate under the chosen side, and the rows are separated by thin rules. Look at the left column: every name and sentence starts at the same edge.

Cooking log: a quiet week is only quiet

The weeks you cooked, as a grid: a day you logged is filled, a day you did not is a pale plate with no mark, no number and no red. It never counts days, never says missed, and a whole week with nothing in it looks like a week of rest.

Expected:

  • Four rows of seven days, named 3 weeks ago, 2 weeks ago, Last week and This week (the weeks starting 21/09, 28/09, 05/10 and 12/10). The first week is all quiet: seven pale plates and nothing else, no warning, no count. The second has three logged days; the third has three paused days (hatched: you were ill) and two logged; the fourth ends with two days that have not come yet (dashed).
  • Point at a logged day: Witloof in the oven · felt easy. Point at a quiet one: 14/10/2026, nothing logged. Press a day: it is marked, and the line above the grid says Log dinner for Wednesday 14/10/2026: adding a day afterwards is always allowed. Press it again to go back to today.
  • Log today’s dinner: choose how it felt (Easy, Fine, Heavy, or nothing at all) and press Add to the log: today fills in, the button says Added, and Remove today’s entry takes it back. There is no streak and no total.
  • A quiet week (button above): every day of every week is quiet and the note under the grid says Nothing logged. That is fine. Nothing yet: the empty plate fills the card, centred: Nothing logged yet, That is fine: the log starts whenever you do. Loading: the busy card over the grid. Could not read: the alert says the log could not be read and that the entries are safe, with Try again.
  • Buttons above: State, the Colour of a logged day (accent or success), how a week is named (This week, Last week, or its first day) and how many Weeks show (four or two).

Formal’s navy is a logged day; a quiet day is a tone barely darker than the paper with no border; the paused hatch is the one the heat grid already uses. Look at the quiet week: it must draw no eye, and nothing in it may look like a fault.
