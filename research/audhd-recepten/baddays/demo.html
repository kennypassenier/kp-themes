Bad days: food that works when nothing else does

The screen for a day with no energy. Five parts, each answering one question without asking for effort: which dishes always work, what is in the freezer, what can be eaten without cooking, how a recipe shrinks to its smallest version, and when things went in. Nothing here counts calories or weight, nothing turns red, and every action can be taken back. This page asks formal only. The dish names and dates are sample content; the portions, the clock and the server are pretend.

Safe dishes: always possible

A short, fixed row of dishes that work on any day, as large tiles with one sentence each. They never reorder, so the thumb learns where they are.

Expected:

  • Six large tiles in a grid, all one height; each has a title, one sentence and a small line (time, pans, last made).
  • Press a tile: a spinner shows in its right-hand corner, the tile does nothing a second time while it works, and a line under the grid says what happened.
  • Loading puts the package’s busy card over the tiles; Empty and Could not open fill the same area, centred, with a button.

Formal: six tiles of one size, the title in the heavier weight, the sentence and the small line quieter underneath, the busy corner empty at rest.

    The freezer

    A table of what is frozen: the oldest on top, how long each has been in, and one button to take a portion. Taking a portion can be undone; nothing asks “are you sure”.

    Expected:

    • Seven dishes, the oldest first. The columns are Dish, Portions, Frozen on (dd/mm/yyyy), How long (it ticks) and a quiet hint; Take one portion stands in the last column in every row.
    • Press Take one portion: the count drops at once and a message offers Undo with a clock. Take the last portion of a dish and the row leaves; Undo brings it back.
    • An old dish never turns red or amber: after about two months it only gets the grey words Use these next.
    • Loading lays the busy card over the rows; Empty freezer says kindly that this is fine and offers a button; Could not read is the alert with Try again. All three keep the table’s height.

    Formal: a ruled table, the oldest dish on top, the grey hint beside the oldest two, and the empty freezer as a large, calm notice in the middle of the table’s own area.

    The zero-prep shelf

    Food that is eaten without cooking, sorted by how much you can manage today. Say how much that is, and the list shrinks to what fits.

    Expected:

    • Nine foods in rows, each with a lead (minutes or a word), a sentence, what it needs and a This one button in one column.
    • The three choices above the rows filter them: Just open it 4, One easy step 7, Anything here 9. The numbers are exact and never move the rows.
    • Press This one: a line under the list says it is chosen. Loading, Nothing on the shelf and Could not open fill the same area.

    Formal: the three choices as one segmented control, the rows ruled, every title starting at one edge and every button in one column.

    Lead:

    What I can manage today

    No stove, no oven, no pan. Choose what fits today, not what you think you should manage.

      A mini version of a recipe

      The same dish in its smallest form: fewer steps, one pot, one portion. Switch between the mini and the full recipe, then follow one step at a time.

      Expected:

      • It opens on Mini version: three steps, about 12 minutes. The line under the switch says what was dropped from the full recipe.
      • Full recipe shows the nine real steps; Back and Next stay in the same place whatever the step’s length.
      • Loading, No steps and Could not open fill the same area as a step.

      Formal: one large step, the bar of progress above it, the two buttons the same size at the foot, the line of what changed in quiet ink.

      When things went in

      A month of days: the days you froze something are filled, every other day is quiet. It helps you remember cooking days, and it can never show a day as missed.

      Expected:

      • The days something went in are filled with the number of portions; other past days are lighter, with no border, no number and no word like “missed”.
      • Mark the holiday hatches one week; press a quiet day and it behaves like any day.
      • Spinner per day puts the theme's spinner in each day while the month loads.
      • Loading, Nothing frozen this month and Could not read keep the six rows of the month.

      Formal: filled days in the accent with a small count, quiet days barely darker than the paper, the legend with three plain words.
