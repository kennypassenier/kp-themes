Retro's alarm headline: which face

mock The same retro alarm four times, differing only in the headline's face at the size the alarm actually draws it. Cost: A (VT323, 6.8 kB) and B (Instrument Sans, 75.3 kB) are already on every retro page — VT323 as the register's DOS voice for labels, help and status, Instrument Sans as the body — so neither adds a byte; C (Archivo, 92.7 kB upright) is shipped for grotesk and dark and would be a new download on a retro page. All four are OFL-1.1 with no Reserved Font Name, so nothing needs renaming. Whichever you pick replaces Pixelify Sans on this headline only, in both alarms at once — they read the same knob — and the rest of the theme keeps Pixelify Sans on its headings.
