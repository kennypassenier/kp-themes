Four faults found in the package

Checking the components page turned up four faults in components you had approved, so they come back to you here. The Copy button of a command block and the Back and Next buttons of the check-in broke their words letter by letter on a phone; the flash button set to grow by hand was an empty button; a lede asked to fold and to carry a link never folded. 48 pairs in all: each fault in the twelve themes. Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now; the first two are shown in a frame the width of a phone.

What changed since you approved it: The Copy button of a command block broke its word letter by letter (“C o p y”, one letter under the other) when the head was too narrow for the terminal’s name, the admin badge and the button on one line; the button was squeezed because every package button is allowed to break a word anywhere. It showed in dark, cyberpunk and synthwave at 270 px (what a 390 px phone leaves a component) with the admin badge, and in brutalism, deco and nostromo a few pixels narrower. Now the button keeps its word whole and its own width, and when the three do not fit on one line the button goes under the name and the badge, at the right. The head, the badge, the prompt and the code are as you approved them.
