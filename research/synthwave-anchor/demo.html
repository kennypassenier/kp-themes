An anchor is the one recognisable element from which every decision about the whole theme is made: forest's is the tree progress bar, titanium's the loading animation, cyberpunk's the glitch, solstice's the sun on its arc, nostromo's the LED bank, brutalism's the hard slab, blueprint's the dimension line, terminal's the blinking block cursor. The colours are already right in every theme and are the shared base, so they are not what is chosen here: the anchor is a shape and a motion.

One question: which element is synthwave's anchor? Six candidates, the recommendation first. Each board shows the element alone in the middle of its stage, playing by itself (away, arriving, standing, leaving), then the same element as a loading indicator (busy with no end known, and a progress from 0 to 100 %), and under them three tiny chips: how the same element would shape a button press, a card arriving and a toast leaving. What the anchor has to carry

Synthwave is the drive into the sunset, 1984: a neon horizon on the night, the grid floor running to it, the striped sun sinking behind it, an arcade marquee and a VCR's on-screen display. Pink signals, cyan labels, laser yellow warns; the light is neon tubes, a near-white core with the colour in its glow; every cut is horizontal, nothing is notched, radius 2 px, and nothing flickers. "Cyberpunk is diagonal and day; synthwave is horizontal and night."

Already decided: the progress bar is the sun's ramp with a glowing head and the busy road, the spinner the striped sun setting, the toast has its stripe, the link its stripe, the band its neon rule, and the loading family is the marquee. The anchor is the element that makes those parts one family. It must therefore be able to carry a progress and a busy state, and it must not be another theme's anchor.

Kept out on purpose, because another theme owns them: a glitch or chromatic split (cyberpunk), a sun on an arc (solstice), an LED lamp bank (nostromo), a hard offset slab (brutalism), dimension lines (blueprint), a blinking block cursor (terminal), growth (forest) and mis-registered print plates (grotesk). Where a candidate still sits close to one of them, its note says so. The network graph is in no board and changes in no theme.
