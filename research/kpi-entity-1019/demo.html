Key figures and entity cards: four faults

The project threads found four faults in two approved components: a key-figure name broken inside its words, a note that began a line with a stray dot, entity-card actions that were another width on every card, and two missing options of the card (a good or quiet bar, an empty plate with no action or your own). 4 pairs in all, in formal. Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: In a narrow tile the hint (Show in the bar) took all the room it asked for and left the name 30 px: Delivered since start broke inside its words, letter by letter (DEL / IVE / RED / SIN / CE), seven lines tall. Now the name keeps the width of its longest word. The hint gives way instead: its own words wrap in the room that is left (Show / in the bar), and neither is ever broken inside a word.

Key figures: a tile name beside its hint

A strip of key figures whose middle two tiles can be pressed to filter the journal. A pressable tile says in a small hint, beside its name, what pressing does.

Expected:

  • In the phone-width frame, Delivered since start and Failed since start read as whole words on two or three lines, with the hint right-aligned beside them.
  • In the wide frame the names sit on one or two lines and the hint stays on the first line, at the end.
  • No word in any tile is cut anywhere inside the word.

Formal: every tile name is made of whole words; the hint never pushes a letter of the name onto its own line.

Cards: the same action, the same width

Profile cards with three actions each. The words differ from card to card (Recheck this profile, Try a translation, Open the log), the cards are one size.

Expected:

  • The buttons of every card start and end on the same edges as the buttons in the same position on the card above or beside it.
  • A long label (Recheck this profile) wraps onto two lines inside its button; no word is broken.

Formal: the same action has the same width and the same left edge on every card; nothing runs past a card.

What changed since you approved it: A card’s bar could only turn amber or red (and it did so by itself above 75 % and 90 %), so a share where high is good, such as free space, was drawn like a warning. A bar now takes success (green) or neutral (quiet grey) as its tone, which stops the automatic amber and red. The empty plate always carried a fixed “New” action; it can now have no action or your own words and your own function.

Cards: a good or quiet meter, and an empty grid with its own action

A card whose bars say whether a high reading is good (free space), quiet (a queue that is fine at any height) or worth a warning; and the plate shown when a grid has no cards, with no action, or with the action you word.

Expected:

  • On the first card, Free disk at 82 % is green and Queue at 91 % is grey, while CPU at 91 % with no tone is red as before.
  • The first empty plate has a title and a line and no button; the second has a button with its own words (Reload profiles) that writes a line under the plate when pressed.

Formal: each bar’s colour matches its meaning; the plate without a button keeps the same size and place as the one with it.
