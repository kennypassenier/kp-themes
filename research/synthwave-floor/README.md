# How deep is synthwave's page floor?

**Decided (Kenny, 08/10/2026 01:00): the floor is the page, behind the plates** (8.5 rem from the tube down, painted by the header's `::before` at `z-index: -1`; applied in css/synthwave-register.css; research/synthwave-floor/decided.json).

**Question (one, four options, first publication, no update.json).** Kenny's anchor (2026-10-07 21:21) is the page horizon: one neon tube for the page on the header's foot and the floor being the page below it. In the anchor demo the floor ran deep; the register port (cb37e595) drew a 3.5 rem band inside `.kp-page-header`, because the header is a layout container (`container-type: inline-size` gives it layout containment) and a floor any deeper, painted by the header, would lie over the plates that follow. This demo shows what each depth looks like and costs on a real page.

**What.** One page, synthwave only, review kit aspect mode (`data-review-themes="synthwave"`, id `synthwave-floor`, round `2026-10-08-floor`): one question, four options, one per dialog page. Each option is applied to the same real page of the package's own components at real size: `.kp-page-header` with Export, Schedule, a primary and a real popover trigger (`More ▾`, with the inline `anchor-name` a real one has), a toolbar (a search field and ghost buttons), four `.kp-kpi` with meters, two `.kp-card` (a `.kp-progressbar` and a `.kp-table`; a `.kp-field` and buttons). The scene holds two pages: page 1 has the toolbar under the header (room under it), page 2 the plates right under it. The tube, the floor, the piece of tube a hand lights and the loading are the package's own; `options.css` (layer `kp.signature`, tokens `--sf-*` on `:is(.sf-page, .rv-dialog__stage)`) only changes how deep the floor runs and what carries it, scoped by `data-sf-floor="<key>"`. Controls as the anchor demo: state (rest then wait, at rest, waiting), replay, speed x1/x2/x4; the one clock in `demo.js` sets `aria-busy` on the pages, so the tube is a track the ramp flows through and the floor drives toward you. In the dialog `demo.css` forces `zoom: 1 !important` on `.rv-dialog__stage .sf-col`.

**Measured** (Firefox 153, `getComputedStyle` and a pixel comparison of each option with and without the header's `::before`, motion off, over every `.kp-kpi`, `.kp-card`, the search field and every non-ghost `.kp-button`): options 2, 3, 4 differ in 0 pixels; option 1 differs in 0 pixels inside the plates and controls shrunk by 2 px, and in at most 50 pixels per plate in the outermost 2 px ring (the translucent frame of the search field and the edge of three tiles: the far line of the grid passes there) at 1600 and 390 px. The floor is 136 px (8.5 rem) deep in 1, 3 and 4 (page 1), 56 px in 2 and in 4 (page 2). In the dialog (`[data-rv-open]`, Firefox, 1600 x 1000) the stage is 1054 x 579 px, the tokens are defined, the column's zoom is 1, nothing overflows, and `sf-drive`/`kp-sw-flow` run while the page waits. Motion is not measured with `measure-motion.mjs`: the scenes loop (rest, waiting) and nothing arrives or leaves, so there is no leave to mirror.

**What could not be made true.**

- At 390 px the review kit's dialog leaves the stage 24 px tall (the bar, the buttons and the mirrored controls take the screen; the anchor demo does the same, measured), so the options are judged on the page at 390 px, where nothing overflows; the demo hides its own mirrored state and speed rows there.
- Option 1 makes what follows the header `position: relative` unless it says otherwise; a consumer whose first sibling positions something against a farther ancestor with `position: static` would see it move. Option 1 also needs a header that is followed by content in the same stacking context (not inside its own `overflow: hidden` clip).
- Option 4 cannot know a plate it has no class for; the list is `.kp-card`, `.kp-kpis`, `.kp-alert` and the demo's `.sf-grid`.
