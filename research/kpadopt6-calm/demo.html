Calm screens: six parts for a day that is allowed to be quiet

Six parts for a daily check-in and a today screen, built in the package: a picker with the choices you use most on top, one question per screen, a quiet card for what is open, done or paused, a card that quotes your own earlier words, a calendar that shows a day with nothing logged as quiet instead of failed, and a star rating. Four extend a component that already exists (the combobox, the one-step view, the status list, the calendar with its heat grid and uptime strip) with options that are off until asked for; two are new. This page asks formal only; the other themes follow once formal is approved.

Judge in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top): 6 topics, one step each in formal. Every section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. Everything with a kp- class and every script under js/ is the package; the sample content (symptoms, check-in questions, the day’s tasks, the diary sentences, recipes), the fixed clock (Saturday 17/10/2026), the frames marked “phone width” (390 px boxes standing in for a phone), the event readouts and the buttons that drive a state are this page’s. Nothing here is saved anywhere.

Today card

Today card: open, done, paused, not known

data-kp-status-vocab="calm" on a status list (js/statusrow.js) inside a card: one row per thing in the day, each only Open, Done or Paused since a date with one-press Resume. No red, no counters, no “you still have”.

Expected:

  • Six rows, each 56 px high with a round mark that has its own shape: Morning pills and Walk after dinner open (a grey ring with a dot), Evening check-in and Water the plants done (a soft blue disc with a check, not green), Beweging paused (a dashed ring with two bars, a dashed edge, Paused since 03/10/2026 and a Resume button), Zamioculcas Raven: water not known (a dashed ring with a question mark and Soil sensor has no reading: a plant that reports “ok” without any data must never look done). There is no total anywhere on the card.
  • Resume: press it on Beweging: the row becomes open at once (same place), a toast says Beweging resumed with Undo; Undo puts it back paused since 03/10/2026, the original date. With the keyboard: Tab into the list (one stop), ArrowDown and ArrowUp move between rows, r resumes the paused row you are on.
  • Buttons above the list: Date, Length and Both change how the pause is told (the choice to tick); Pause button adds a quiet Pause on open rows. The second card has a pause longer than 30 days: Long pauses shows them as rows (the default) or gathers them in one line that opens them.
  • A row that is told it failed (the grey box under the first card has a button for it) is drawn as Not known, never red; the console says why.

Formal’s paper card, hairline rows; done is the quiet info blue and never the success plate; paused and unknown are dashed hairlines in muted ink. Look at the marks side by side: each state has its own shape.

Calm days

            

                

                    
Calm days: a day with nothing logged is quiet, not failed

                    

                        data-kp-tones="calm" on the month calendar, the heat grid and the uptime strip: the plates for a tracker of a
                        person. A day you logged is filled in the theme’s accent, a day you did not is simply quiet, and a pause is hatched. No
                        red, no green, no “missed”, no count of the empty days.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            First calendar (October 2026, movement): days 1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 14 and 15 are logged (navy plates, the
                            number of minutes under the day number); 3 to 6 are paused (hatched, no fill); the other past days are
                            quiet (a plate barely darker than the paper, no border, no number under it, no glyph); days after today are dashed.
                            The legend reads Logged, Nothing logged, Paused. Press a quiet day: you can, it is a normal 52 px target
                            (adding a missing entry is allowed); its name is 14/10/2026: nothing logged, never “missed”.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Second calendar: the same days drawn for a backup with the standard tones: green for done, red for a miss.
                            That is right for a machine and is what calm replaces for a person. Standard stays the default; nothing changes for
                            backups.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Heat grid (three habits over fourteen days) and uptime strip (thirty days of a plant being watered):
                            logged is the accent, quiet is the pale plate, the strip’s old down reads nothing logged in its tooltip, and its summary counts the good days only (N of 28 days logged: no share down, no incidents).
                            A cell asked to be bad is drawn quiet (the console says so once).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Buttons above: Accent and Success change the colour of logged days in all three (a choice to tick);
                            Legend shows or hides the legend (a choice to tick).
                        
    • 
                    

                

                

                    

                        Formal’s paper and navy: a logged day is a navy plate with paper ink, a quiet one a barely-there tone of the paper
                        (lighter than the grey of “nothing to do”), the paused hatch is the one the heat grid already uses before a record
                        begins. Look at the three tones side by side and at the quiet days not drawing any eye.

Star rating

            

                

                    
Star rating: give one, show one

                    

                        <div class="kp-rating" data-kp-rating> (js/rating.js): five whole stars to give something a score, and the
                        same row read only to show one. An empty row says it is empty; it never looks like a zero.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            Lasagne (first case) starts with five outlined stars and the words Tap a star under them. Press the fourth
                            star: four are filled in the accent colour (navy in formal, never red or green), the words say Great, a small
                            Clear button appears under the row (on a mouse only), a toast says Lasagne: 4 stars with Undo, and
                            the grey box under the case shows the event. Press the fourth star again: the rating goes (rating removed).
                            Nothing under the row moves when the words change.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Keys: Tab reaches the row once (one tab stop); the arrows move and choose, Home and End go to the
                            first and last star, Backspace or Delete removes the rating. No digit keys (they need Shift on your
                            keyboard). With a mouse, the stars up to the one under the pointer show lighter before you press (the second row of the
                            first case has this switched off, to compare).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Show one: a read-only row of small stars with the number beside it (4.5 (12), the count exact), and the row
                            of nobody’s rating reads Not rated yet with outlined stars. A screen reader gets one name for the row
                            (“Rated 4.5 of 5 by 12 people”).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Half stars (an option, not the default): the same row answers to a press on the left or right half of a star;
                            the keys step by one half. Save rating case: a tap only picks, the two buttons appear, and the page keeps the old
                            value until Save.
                        
    • 
                    

                

                

                    

                        Formal’s stars are drawn with a thin rounded outline, filled solid in the primary navy. Look at the filled stars against
                        the empty ones at the three sizes (small 16 px, medium 28 px, large 36 px), at the focus ring on a star, and at the touch area
                        around each star (44 px).
