What solstice is

Solstice is the low sun: the last hour before dusk on warm charcoal, firelight rather than screenlight. Amber leads, rust supports, the words are cream and set in a serif; the corners are the softest in the set; nothing is neon, nothing is a grid. "No neon. Still legible. Unhurried." (the concept demo's laurels, 2026-09-08).

The sun is low, so its light comes from below: it catches the foot of every plate and rises up it, warm. What opens is kindled, the first light spreading up it as a dome; what is new rises into its line, like the sun clearing the horizon; while something waits the sun crosses along its foot; what leaves is eclipsed and what arrives comes back into the light; a warning reddens the sky at its foot. Everything moves on the sun's own sine, slower than in any other theme.
