chassis-rs kit page Storage: the budget, the kinds of data, a targeted delete and compacting

The new kit page for the sqlite storage of chassis-rs 3.11.0 (docs/STORAGE_DESIGN.md, section 8), formal only, built from kp-themes parts and the form grid. It shows what is used against the 50 MB budget (with the sign-in data marked read-only), one row per kind of data the application keeps, a targeted delete in two steps (a read-only preview, then a typed confirmation), and compacting the application file on demand. 4 sections, 4 pairs, two choices (each only asks for its default). All figures are sample data in the shape of the admin dashboard’s storage, and the page says so; the budget only warns and nothing is ever deleted for you.

The Storage page: what is used, what it is made of, and where to clean up

One page on a 12-column grid. The space used (columns 1 to 8) stands against the empty space that can be given back (9 to 12), and the kinds of data stand across all 12 columns below, so the biggest kind and its Clean up button are the first thing under the figures.

Expected:

  • Budget (the buttons above): Warning is the sample the admin dashboard would show (47.9 of 50 MB, past the 40 MB warning tick), with one amber band that says what, why and what now, and a button for the biggest kind. Over budget turns the band red and says nothing was deleted and the service keeps saving. Within budget has no band.
  • Space used: the share bar splits the three parts (application data, sign-in data marked Read-only and never cleaned up here, pending writes) with exact sizes and a tick at 40 MB; the three rows under it keep one label column, one size column and one note column.
  • What is stored: a datatable, one row per kind with its amount, size, share of the budget, oldest and newest, and Clean up… in one fixed column. Inbox snoozes says it cleans itself. Dates are dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm, counts keep every digit, sizes are KB or MB.
  • Phone: every card is one column and the kinds become cards; the page says on screen that all figures are sample data.

The two top cards share one height, the kinds table starts on the same left edge as the cards above it, and every Clean up button stands in one column; at Phone nothing scrolls sideways and the amber band keeps its three parts.

The Storage cards while they load, when there is nothing and when they fail

Every card uses the package’s own states: the busy card while the storage is read, the empty plate when the application stores nothing of its own yet, the failed plate with Try again. Each fills the footprint of the loaded card.

Expected:

  • Loading: the busy card sits centred over each card and the table, at the loaded height. Empty: the kinds card says the application stores nothing of its own yet; the figures above show only the small sign-in data. Failed: each card shows one plate at heading size with a normal-size Try again, which returns every card to Ready.
  • Nothing jumps: press Ready, Loading, Failed in turn and the cards keep their place.

The three cards keep the same grid in every state; the busy card is the theme’s own spinner, centred; the empty and failed plates are centred at heading size.

Clean up one kind: choose, preview, then type to confirm

A targeted delete in three steps on one composition. The choices (columns 1 to 5) and what would go (6 to 12) stand side by side; the confirm band under them keeps the same three tracks in every step, so the dialog never changes size.

Expected:

  • Step 1: Older than (a date picker, dd/mm/yyyy) and the kind’s own declared filters: Notifications has level (chips), read state and stack; Update flows a result and part of a stack name; Inbox snoozes a switch and says expired rows go by themselves; Fleet trend only the date. Preview counts without deleting: how many rows, the space they free, the oldest and newest, and the five oldest rows.
  • Preview states (the buttons above): not run, counting (busy card), ready, nothing matches and failed, each filling the same card. Changing any choice makes the old preview stale on purpose.
  • Step 2: Continue to confirm shows what will be lost and asks you to type the kind’s name; Delete for good stays off until it matches exactly, and the server recounts and refuses if the data changed or the words differ. Deleted says the file keeps its size until it is compacted and offers Compact.
  • Press Phone: the two cards stack, the steps become a column and the buttons share the width.

The date picker, the chips, the select and the buttons stand on the form grid’s tracks; the preview’s four figures sit on one row at the wide width; the sample rows are a table with a fixed header; the dialog is the same height in Choose and Confirm.

Compact the application data: see what is given back first

Compacting rewrites the file without the empty space deleted rows leave. It shows the reclaimable size before anything happens, never runs by itself, and touches only the application data.

Expected:

  • Ready: three figures (now, can be given back, after), the three steps of what happens, and the budget bar before and after with the 40 MB tick. Nothing to reclaim: Compact now is off and says why. Running: the steps move on; Done says how much was given back; Failed is one plate with Try again, and nothing was changed.
  • Phone: the figures stack and the buttons share the width.

The three figures share one row, the steps and the budget bars stand in two cards of one height, and the buttons stand on the right edge of the foot; a state never changes the dialog’s size.
