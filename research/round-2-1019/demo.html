Fixes from the project threads, round 2

Faults that the project threads found in components you approved earlier, each fixed in the package. Six components on a shared line (activity feed, lead list, problems, share bar, shortcuts, week), the calendar and the pipeline when empty or failed, the run timeline and the uses map, and the data table with its removed and held rows, date pills and action column. 19 sections, 19 pairs, all in formal. Every section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: The chip of whoever started a row, and the time at the end of it, stood 2 px higher than the title they belong to: each was centred in its own, smaller line, while the title is set in the larger body size. They now share the title’s baseline, the way a caption stands beside its heading, and the mark-only chip stands in a cell one title line high. The marks, the rail, the day headings, the states and the sizes are as you approved them.

Expected:
