Almanac: delivery chain, retry schedule, set-aside entry

Three new pages for Almanac's Status area, built from kp-themes 11.0.0 parts and shown in formal only. The numbers of the healthy chain are the live service's on 19/10/2026 (version 4.1.7: 3 events accepted, 3 delivered, 0 failed, 0 dead). Every outage, every waiting entry and every time on the schedule is sample data, labelled as such under each part; the timing rules (15 s, 1 min, 5 min, 15 min, then every 30 min; set aside after the third permanent refusal) are Almanac's real ones from src/core/pacing.rs and src/shell/worker.rs.

Delivery chain: where every event stands

An event passes four stages: received at the door, written to the journal, picked up by the worker and put in Google Calendar; the stage that is failing is lit red, and an event Almanac gave up on waits in the last lane.

Expected:

  • Healthy (real totals): three events in Google Calendar, none anywhere else, the other stages say none; no stage is red.
  • Google refusing (sample): the Google Calendar plate and its lane edge turn red with the reason in the plate's name, and the events that cannot get through stand in Journal, each with how often it was tried.
  • Token refresh failing (sample): the red moves to Worker; the journal fills the same way, so you see which stage is at fault, not only that something is.
  • Loading keeps the four lanes and lays the busy card over all of them; Failed to load is one plate with a normal Try again that loads and then fills. Counts are exact. At 390 px the lanes turn to rows.

Press each state: the lanes never move or change height between healthy, one lit stage, loading and failed; the red stage is the only one in colour; the line under the board names the data as real or sample.

Retry schedule: when the waiting entries are tried next

While Google refuses, Almanac tries all waiting entries together after 15 s, 1 min, 5 min, 15 min and then every 30 min; this shows the coming tries and, per entry, how many tries it has left.

Expected:

  • A list of the next tries (default): four rows with weekday, dd/mm/yyyy HH:MM and "in 21 min", 51 min, 1 h 21 min and 1 h 51 min; the first row carries a mark saying the kyu entry is set aside on that try. Twice the same 30 minutes apart: that is the ladder's last rung repeating.
  • A timeline: four lanes (home-assistant, homelab, claude-sessions, kyu). Red dots are tries that Google refused, hollow rings are coming tries, the triangle in the kyu lane is the third refusal after which Almanac sets it aside (tries left: 1). The other three have no limit, because a temporary refusal is retried for ever.
  • Nothing waiting is a plate at the height of the list (or the timeline) with "Nothing is waiting" centred; Loading is the busy card over the same footprint; Failed to load says why. The list has a Try again; the timeline has only words (see the report).

Press each state with the list and again with the timeline (Draw it as): the footprint stays as tall as the loaded one; switching between list and timeline never loses the state you pressed.

An entry Almanac gave up on

After the third permanent refusal Almanac sets an event aside and tells you what is wrong and what to do about it.

Expected:

  • Each set-aside entry is one red problem with three parts: what happened (source and title), why (Google's refusal and when) and What now (Almanac's own remedy line). Two entries stand as two problems, the newest first.
  • There is no Retry now button, on purpose. In Almanac 4.1.7 a set-aside entry is kept in the journal and shown on the debug surface but is never tried again, and no route or command re-queues it (src/shell/worker.rs, PERMANENT_FAILURES_BEFORE_DEAD). A button here would promise something the service cannot do; the note under the problems says so. The remedy is to fix the cause and send the event again from its source.
  • Nothing set aside is a plate at the same height with "Nothing has been set aside".

The problem reads in the order what, why, what now; the page never shows a Retry now button, and the note says why. Switching between one, two and none keeps the box the same height.

Nothing has been set aside

Every event Almanac accepted is either delivered or still being tried.

A set-aside entry cannot be retried from here Almanac 4.1.7 keeps it in the journal for inspection and never tries it again; it has no re-queue action. What now Fix the cause, then send the event again from its source.

Sample data: no entry has been set aside on the live service (0 dead since start).
