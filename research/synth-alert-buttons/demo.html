Buttons on a red alert

Two rulings of yours disagree about the buttons on a red alert in synthwave. This page shows every place such a button stands, one under the other, and lets you flip between the two rulings on the component.

What is asked: On 15/09/2026 (scope-94, “Alleen Try again”) you ruled that on a synthwave red alert only the data table’s Try again takes the alert’s ink as its frame, and every other button on a red alert keeps its pink frame. On 18/10/2026 (round t56) you approved, in the eleven-theme review, that buttons on a coral plate are dark keys with a near-white frame. That rule was written for the failed state, an urgent question and a refused question, but its selector reaches every red alert, so the plain Try again in the empty-table example is a dark key too, against what you ruled in September. Look at the five places below with each option and say which look is right: dark keys on every red alert, or dark keys only on the plates named on 18/10 and a pink frame on the rest.

Every button on a red alert, in synthwave

Five red alerts, each with its button, in the order they come up in the package. The option at the top of the component swaps the rule that dresses them.

Expected:

  • Dark keys on every red alert (as approved on 18/10), the package as it is now: the plain Try again and the button of the plain alert are dark keys with a near-white frame, like the data table’s.
  • Dark keys only on the plates named on 18/10, the rest pink (scope-94): the failed-state plate and the two questions stay as they are, the data table’s Try again takes the alert’s ink as its frame, and the plain Try again and the button of the plain alert get the pink frame back.
  • Every label stays whole and clear of the plate’s edge in both options.
