Fixes from the project threads, round 4

Fixes from the project threads, each in the package: the heat grid's row and column names and the dependency list's status column; the path trace's verdict column and a long token in a diff line; a key figure's name and note, and the entity card's actions, meter tones and empty grid; and a form field filling its place and the problems' fix column. 11 pairs, all in formal. Every section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: On a phone a row name was broken inside the word (“PAPERLE” over “SS”, “VAULTW” over “ARDEN”), because the name column was a fixed share of the grid, narrower than the longest name. Now the name column is as wide as the longest word of any name (never more than it was, unless a word needs it) and the cells give way; a name of several words wraps at its spaces. The cells, the legend, the axis and pressing a name to pick a row are as you approved them.

Heat grid: row names stay whole words

Backups per stack per night, rows named after the stack; the grid is shown at the width of a phone and at a wide width.

Expected:

  • In the narrow frame every row name is whole: Paperless, Vaultwarden and Almanac each on one line, Photos and family videos on three lines at its spaces.
  • Every row’s cells start on one edge, the same distance from the names, in both frames.

Formal: no row name is cut in the middle of a word, and the cells of every row start on one vertical line.

What changed since you approved it: Two faults. First, every (how many columns share one name) was ignored as soon as a column was marked minor: the mark decided alone, so a page could not both mark the weekend and thin out the names. Now the columns that are not marked are counted every everyth, the last of them always named; a marked minor column is never named and does not count. Second, a name that was not shown was still in the grid, only drawn in no colour, so a measure of the page still found it standing under its neighbour. Now it is not written at all: the column keeps its header for a screen reader (the name is its label) and there is no text to find. The grid, the cells and the picked column are as you approved them.

Heat grid: the column names

Fourteen nights with the weekend nights marked as minor columns, first with the default spacing of names and then with a name on every second night.

Expected:

  • The first axis names the first night, every third night after it and the last; the two weekend columns carry no name.
  • The second axis (every second night) names every second night; the weekend columns are still blank, and nothing is written in a blank column.

Formal: the names stand over their own columns with air between them; a blank column is empty, not faint.

What changed since you approved it: The state badges (Running, Stopped, Unknown) each took their own word’s width and started on the column’s start edge, so they ended on three different edges.   • In the narrow list the badges are the same width too, under the names, aligned on their start edge.

Formal: every state badge starts and ends on the same two vertical lines.
