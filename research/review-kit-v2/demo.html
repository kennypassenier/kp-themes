The review dialog, v2: the component first

Kenny, 2026-10-18 15:26: "the component itself is the most important part of the screen, so it must always be clearly visible and central." Each section below is one improvement to the dialog you judge in. It shows the real dialog, live, at a real window size; switch the option under This option to see it off and on, and under The other options to see it among the rest. The dialog's own View menu switches the same options in every review, and keeps them for you.

The question under each preview is only which default the dialogs should open with; every option stays switchable, and nothing is asked that you must answer. Until you choose, all other reviews look as they did.

Near-fullscreen dialog

The dialog takes 99% of the window instead of 94% by 92%, and its header, margins and gaps get tighter. Every pixel the chrome gives back goes to the component you are judging; on a 1920 by 1080 window the component gains about 120 px of width and 80 px of height.

Each title once

The section title stands once per frame, in the frame's own head; the topic heading above it and the section's own heading inside it are not repeated. Three copies of one title cost two lines of height and make you read the same words three times before you reach the component.

Compact controls

The controls of a component flow side by side across the full width, each group's label and buttons on one line, in the package's small button size. Four groups fit in two lines instead of a tall stack of left-aligned rows with huge buttons, so the component starts higher; the controls stay directly above their component.

Jump list of sections
