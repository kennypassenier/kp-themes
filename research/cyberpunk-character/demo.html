What cyberpunk is

Cyberpunk is the netrunner's HUD over Night City: signal yellow on the void, cyan read-outs, blood red where something is wrong, violet only in a glitch. The ground is the void; every plate on it is a holo card (a yellow neon rim, diagonal scan lines, a cut corner); over a part the system draws its HUD: the reticle that locks on, mono read-outs with their prefixes, the red and cyan copies of a signal that changes.

So everything that happens is something a hacked signal does: it is glitched in (opening, arriving), decoded start to end, hunted and locked on (loading, a warning), lit like a circuit (where you point), stuttered home (a live change), torn out (leaving). It moves in hard ticks of 60 ms, and when it is off, it is off to the side. Nothing eases, glides, floats, grows or falls.
