Three options for the recipe app

The AuDHD recipe project asked for three things its pages could not do: action tiles in a smaller or larger size, a freshness line that says “frozen 97 days ago” on a calm plate instead of a warning, and a sentence form with words it works out itself. Each is an option of a component you approved, with its old look as the default. Three sections, formal only; under What changed since you approved it each says what is new.

What changed since you approved it: Action tiles had one size in every place they were used, so a page with many small actions and a page with three important ones looked the same. The board, one tile, or any element above them can now say data-kp-action-size="compact" or "large". The tile’s padding, title, sentence and mark scale together, and so does the width a column may shrink to, so a compact board fits more tiles on a line and a large one fewer. With no attribute nothing changes. The option follows the attribute at runtime, in every theme.

Action tiles: a size

A board of buttons and links, each with a title and the one sentence that says what it does. The board can now be compact, the theme’s own size, or large.

Expected:

  • The board opens at the theme’s own size. The block beside it reads Default and lists the tile height, title size, padding and tiles per line.
  • Press Compact in the control group above the board: every tile shrinks together, more tiles stand on a line, and the readout follows. Press Large: tiles grow and fewer fit on a line. Press Default to come back.
  • All six tiles keep one height whatever their state: a button, a link with its arrow, a destructive one with its bar, one that is busy with its spinner and one that is disabled.

What changed since you approved it: Once a line was older than its limit it turned into an amber warning plate, and it counted in two units (“97 days 4 h”). Two options are new. data-kp-stale-tone="calm" draws the stale line as a quiet plate in the muted ground and ordinary ink, with the same size, so turning stale still moves nothing. data-kp-ago-units="days" counts whole days from one day on (“frozen 97 days ago”), and keeps hours and minutes under a day. With neither attribute nothing changes. Both follow their attribute at runtime.

Freshness line: a calm stale tone and days only

The line that says how old data is (“updated 12 s ago”). For data that is old on purpose, such as a frozen recipe, it no longer has to look like a warning.

Expected:

  • The first row is the amber plate as before: frozen 97 days 4 h ago. The second is the same age on a quiet grey plate, the third reads frozen 97 days ago.
  • The fourth row is only 3 h old, so it has no plate at all and still reads 3 h 12 min, also with days only.
  • Press Calm and Days only in the control group: the last row (the live line) changes tone and units, and the block beside it shows the exact moment and the limit.

the four lines start on the same edge of the control column and sit on their label’s baseline; the plates change colour but not size.

What changed since you approved it: Every field in a sentence form was something the person typed or chose. A sentence can now hold a read-only computed field: <output class="kp-sentence__computed" data-kp-sentence-computed="ready"> inside a .kp-sentence__field, with a function that gets the value of the other fields and returns its words (attachSentences(root, { computed }), sentence(form).compute(name, fn), or setSentenceComputed(form, name, text) to push the text in). It is drawn on a quiet plate with no underline in the primary colour, so it is not mistaken for something to fill in; it is not a tab stop, is not sent with the form, and is part of the spoken summary. With nothing to say it shows a dash.

Sentence form: a field the sentence works out

A form that reads as a sentence. Some words in it can now be worked out from the fields you fill in, and are not fields you type in yourself.

Expected:

  • Read the sentence: Start cooking at 17:30. It takes 70 min, so dinner is ready at 18:40. It serves 4 people at 150 g each: 600 g in total. The two grey words are worked out.
  • Change the start time or the minutes, the people or the grams: the grey words change at once. Press 95 min and dinner is ready at 19:05; press Clear minutes and it shows a dash and the spoken summary says (Ready at).
  • Press Tab through the sentence: the focus visits the four fields you type in and never the grey ones. The block beside it shows what is sent with the form: only those four.

the grey words stand on the line of the words around them, as tall as the fields beside them, with no underline.
