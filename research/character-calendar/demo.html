A month heatmap of its own, per theme

Kenny, form v18 (2026-10-05): the character round, one component at a time, all 22 themes in one demo. After the meter and the time chart comes the month heatmap. Today .kp-calendar has the same shape in every theme (rounded plates in the status colours, the number over its count, dashed days to come, an inner ring for today, an outer ring for the picked day), drawn in the theme's colours only. Here every theme gets two calendars of its own, drawn in the theme's own material, beside the plain calendar as it is today. Pick one of the three per theme.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: every loading day shows its still frame. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Each column is one month of nine services' nightly backups, "now" fixed at 20/10/2026 14:40 in Brussels: green nights had every service, amber some, red none (08/10, the power cut), 11/10 had nothing to back up, the report of 14/10 was lost, today (20/10) still misses two late copies, and the rest of October is still to come; August starts with nine nights from before the first backup. 16/10 is picked. Every calendar is the package's own (js/calendar.js draws it, so the month buttons, the keys, picking a day and the live update all work); a character only restyles it from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (calendars.css), and keeps the grid's height in every state and month.
