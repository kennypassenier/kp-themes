Round two (Kenny, 2026-10-05 20:03: "I want separate options for the heatmap as well, just like you did for the meters"). Nothing is bundled any more: per theme you pick the shape, the loading picture, how the month arrives, how the tones and today read, and the picked day (with the hover and its count), each from three options and each on its own. Options 1 and 2 are round one's two characters split into their parts; option 3, and every arrival, are new. Nothing is ticked yet.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: every loading day shows its still frame and the month arrives at once. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Each calendar is one month of nine services' nightly backups, "now" fixed at 20/10/2026 14:40 in Brussels: green nights had every service, amber some, red none (08/10, the power cut), 11/10 had nothing to back up, the report of 14/10 was lost, today (20/10) still misses two late copies, and the rest of October is still to come; August starts with nine nights from before the first backup. 16/10 is picked. Every calendar is the package's own (js/calendar.js draws it, so the month buttons, the keys, picking a day and the live update all work); every option only restyles it from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (calendars.css), one attribute per aspect.
