Printing a checklist: the sheet

css/_print.css and js/print.js: Ctrl+P on a page with a checklist gives a clean black-and-white sheet. The frames below are the page’s real print rules shown on screen, in the shape of an A4 sheet.

What changed since you approved it: In the checklist on screen, the small detail beside an item used to squeeze the item name to a few letters (broken words) at phone width. Now the detail wraps inside at most half of the row and the name keeps its words.

Expected:

  • First sheet (the one the dialog’s choice drives): black on white, no colour, no buttons or menus. Each item has a drawn square box, at least 4 mm; ticked items show a tick in a bold box and keep their text. Group headings (Clothes, Documents, Kitchen) are bold with a rule; an item and its note never split across pages. At the foot: Printed 17/10/2026 14:05.
  • Second sheet: the same list with two columns for groups (data-kp-print-groups="columns") and a dotted line under each item for handwritten notes. Third sheet: a list that was still loading prints The list was still loading when this sheet was printed. at heading size, never a blank page. Fourth sheet: a list with nothing on it prints its title and its empty sentence.
  • Tick under How a ticked item prints in the dialog: the first sheet follows (filled box and text, struck through, or the ticked items left off). The list on screen above the sheets does not change: the rules are for paper only.
  • Changed, at 390 (use the width switch): in The list on screen every item name keeps whole words; a long detail wraps in at most half of the row; a short detail stays on one line; nothing runs past the list. The four paper sheets are not affected.

At phone width the detail beside an item wraps inside at most half the row, and the item name keeps its whole words; a short detail stays on one line and the list never runs past its frame.
