Cyberpunk, as applied: what changed

Kenny, 2026-10-12: "finish cyberpunk the way forest was finished". Every block below is the package itself in cyberpunk, as an app gets it: the real components, the real stylesheets and the real scripts, nothing drawn for this page. The anchor is the glitch: a yellow and a cyan ghost 6, 4, 2 and 1 px away close in four 120 ms ticks (480 ms) to open, close and arrive. Look for notched corners, the split edge on hover, four focus brackets, a press that closes the circuit without lifting, and the word LOADING for anything that waits.

Judge each block in the review dialog ("Review in a dialog" at the top): approve or not, with a note where something is wrong. How this page was audited is in research/cyberpunk-applied/README.md. Inside the review dialog, Escape closes the review dialog itself: close what you opened there with its own button or a click outside it.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page grows or withers: every part shows its finished pose. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the openings and closes.

1 · The bar

The bar is now a notched holo plate; its dropdown, mega menu and phone menu open by the channel split and close as that reversed, and its links, call to action and toggle answer the pointer.

Hover Readings (or Tab onto it): the dropdown splits in as a yellow and a cyan ghost that meet, and splits out again when you leave. Press Equipment for the mega menu, then Request access and the toggle in the narrow frame: split edge on hover, closed circuit on press.

2 · Tabs, breadcrumbs, pagination

Tabs, crumbs and page numbers now answer the pointer with the split edge, take the closed-circuit press and the four focus brackets, and the page numbers are cut with the notch.

Hover an unselected tab, a crumb and a page number to see the cyan and red split edge. Press one and hold: it closes, no lift. Tab along them to see the four brackets close in. Labels carry their // prefix, the prose is in Rajdhani.

The command palette now opens by the channel split and closes as that reversed, with a notched plate, split-edge options and prose in Rajdhani.

Press Open the commands, hover the options and type "lock" to filter. Close it with Escape: it should split out the way it came in.

5 · Date picker

The date picker panel opens by the channel split and closes as that reversed; days take the split edge, the closed-circuit press and the focus brackets.

Press the calendar button, hover the days, then move with the arrow keys to see the brackets on the focused day. Pick a day or press Escape and check the panel leaves as it arrived.

6 · Theme menu

The theme menu, in its attached and React shapes, opens by the channel split and closes as that reversed, with split-edge options and focus brackets.

Press the palette icon, hover the options and Tab through them to see the brackets. Do the same with the second (React) button. Choosing a theme here does not switch this page.

Open the dialog and close it with a button or Escape; do the same with the drawer. Plate and backdrop should arrive and leave on the same four ticks, the close the exact reverse of the open.

11 · Fields, select, upload zone

Fields, selects and the upload zone now take the split edge on hover, keep the four focus brackets, and are cut by the 8 px control notch.

Hover each field, the select and the drop zone: the cyan and red edge doubles. Click into one and Tab between them to see the brackets. The invalid ones keep their red. Drop a file on the zone, or press it to choose one.

    Who may reopen a locked-out line?
    Only the shift lead who signed the lock-out, or the site manager when that person has left the site.
    What counts as a pressure drop?
    A fall of more than one bar within thirty minutes on any line.
    Which reading do we believe when the gauge and the sensor disagree?
    Neither, on its own: close the line and log both readings at the same minute.

    The reorder rows, the chart legend keys and the colour swatch now take the notch and the split edge, and the legend keys are no longer pills.

    Hover the reorder rows and drag one by its handle (or focus the handle and use the arrow keys). Press a legend key under the plot to single a source out, then Show all. The swatch is cut, not round.

    16 · Calendar and the busy month

    The calendar's busy layer arrives and leaves by the channel split with the word LOADING, and each day takes the split edge and the focus brackets.

    Press Loading: the first month shows one busy layer over the whole month, the second shows each day waiting. Press Read and the layer splits out. Click a day and use the arrow keys to see the brackets.

    17 · Buttons

    Every button now draws the four closing focus brackets, is cut by the notch (14 px for plates, 8 px for controls), splits its edge on hover and closes the circuit on press without lifting.

    Tab through the buttons: the four brackets close in on each. Hover for the split edge, then press and hold: it closes the circuit and does not move. Check the primary and destructive plates stay readable while pressed.

    18 · Links, headline, rule, alert edge, meter, back to top

    Links, the headline, the rule, the alert edge, the meters and back to top now follow the glitch: prose in Rajdhani, prefixed labels, and everything that arrives or leaves does so by the channel split.

    Point at the link in the text. Press Play again for the headline, the rule, the alerts and the meters. Press Right to left and play again to check the direction flips. Press Show back to top twice to see it arrive and leave.

    19 · Skeletons, spinner, busy button, loading meter

    Everything that waits now shows one picture: four bars with a glitch copy, or the on-screen word LOADING; the old pulsing loops are gone.

    Watch the bars, the skeletons and the meter. Press Start together to restart them on the same frame. Look at the spinner (four bars and a glitch copy), the busy button and the loading meter.

    20 · Popover menus

    A menu in a popover is one notched plate that opens by the channel split and closes as that reversed, with split-edge entries and a [TARGET] prefix on the destructive one.

    Press Row actions, hover the entries and close with Escape or a click outside. Press More ▾ for the richer menu and check it opens right under its button and never covers it.
