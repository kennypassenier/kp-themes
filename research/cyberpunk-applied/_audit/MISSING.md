Measured with `research/cyberpunk-applied/_audit/` (audit.mjs: 19 catalogue pages, 175 pointable parts; notches.mjs: shape of every kp part; loading.mjs: every waiting picture; flows.mjs: 23 flows in the review dialog's stage with real mouse clicks and every frame; pairs-meter.mjs: 25 pairs; raw numbers in `out/*.json`), Firefox and Chromium. Engines agree unless stated. Severity: B = breaks a decided rule, I = inconsistent, C = cosmetic. Rows are grouped by cause (A to H) so the fixing can be split; inside a group the worst first. No file in `css/` or `js/` was changed.
