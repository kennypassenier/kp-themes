## Not measured, and why

- Chromium (not run, by Kenny's decision). Network graph (G18, never a target), the empty state inside the demo's table (no `data-kp-datatable-empty` in the demo markup), RTL, reduced motion, contrast beyond the pressed button, the loading word's changing glyphs (`LOADI%@`: the decided on-screen glitch).
- Meter limitations: `steps()` animations sought to their end read a one-step difference at frame 20/20 (dialog, drawer, palette, alarm); the tooltip re-open in the stage showed only after its delay, the meter's tooltip pair is the reading.
- The tabs swap in the stage (flows) and the Tab run are Firefox; the two tab-swap files in `out/` named `flows-firefox-tabs...` and `flows-firefox-accordion_tree_tabs_upload.json` are older and are not the present state (flows-firefox.json is).
