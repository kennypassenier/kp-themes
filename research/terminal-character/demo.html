What terminal is

Terminal is the text screen: a phosphor screen of character cells, one green at three brightnesses, one mono face, and a block cursor that writes it. "Green Phosphor" (2026-09-08) gave it the screen, the scanlines, the headline that types itself with a block cursor riding the last character, and inverse video as its only emphasis; your verdicts gave it the cursor in the box (R6-Q7), the cursor under the hand (gap-4), the braille spinner, the printed dialog and the leave in which the cursor deletes the line (2026-10-04).

The grammar takes one idea from them: the cursor writes the screen. What arrives is typed out, the cursor at the head of the line; what opens is printed, a line per line feed; while something waits the cursor waits where the reading will be written; a new value blinks; what is pointed at takes the cursor; what is pressed is entered in reverse video; a tone is printed in reverse in its colour and says its level; what leaves is deleted by the cursor.
