Calendar and pipeline states

Your rule is that a failed or empty component fills the whole size it has with its data, centred, with a normal-size Retry, and that everything sits on a grid. Two components you approved still kept a small line: the month calendar in its normal colours, and the pipeline board, whose lanes also went on showing a count of 0 behind a failure. Both use the package’s one state plate now, the pipeline can mark a lane as failing, and its stage names wrap instead of ending in an ellipsis. 2 sections, formal only (both fixes are the theme’s own plate, so the other themes follow). Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now. Press a state under the section’s title to see it.

What changed since you approved it: In its normal colours (ok, warn, bad) a calendar with nothing to show or that could not be read was one small grey line under the month, with the days still drawn as grey plates and no Retry. Only the calm calendar had a plate. Now every calendar puts the package’s state plate over its six weeks: the words at heading size and centred, and for a failed month a normal-size Retry (kp-calendar-retry); the weeks stay under it, dimmed, and nothing under a failed plate can be pressed. The plate sits on the same left and right edges as the month title and the legend, from the weekday row to the last week. The calm calendar’s words, the loading pulse and a loaded month are as you approved them.

Month calendar: empty and failed month

A month of backup nights, a plate in the colour of each day. Press a state: the month waits, has nothing to show, or could not be read; a failed month has a Retry.

Expected:

  • Empty: one plate over the whole grid of weeks, the page’s sentence at heading size and centred; the month title, Prev, Next and Today stay above it.
  • Failed: the same plate in red with a Retry of normal size; pressed, the days pulse for a moment and the month returns.
  • The legend stays under the grid and the page does not jump between Ready, Empty and Failed.
  • Ready and Loading as you approved them. Loading look only matters while loading: the days pulse, or each day carries a spinner (kp-calendar--busy-days), or one spinner stands over the month (kp-calendar--busy-whole). Calm keeps its own words.

Formal: the plate fills the grid of weeks, its words centred, Retry a normal button; no second line of the same words under the month.

Pipeline: failed board, a failing lane and lane names

Where each application stands, a lane for each stage. Press a state; a failed board has Try again, one lane can be marked as failing, and long stage names are shown in full.

Expected:

  • Failed: one red plate over the whole board with “Could not read the board.”, the reason under it and Try again; no lane and no count of 0 behind it.
  • Empty: the same plate, neutral, with the sentence; the board keeps the height it had.
  • Failing lane: the third lane’s name plate is red with the words Needs attention; the other lanes are unchanged.
  • The long stage name Awaiting the reference letter wraps on two lines, every name plate is as high as the highest, and the counts, shares and first cards line up across the lanes.

Formal: the plate covers the lanes and the closed row; names are whole words; plates, shares and first cards sit on one line across the lanes (also at 390 px).
