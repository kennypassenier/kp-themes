Hover card and plain-click multi-sort

Two things the admin dashboard still drew itself, now in the package (Kenny's rule: a consumer draws nothing). Both are the package's own code: the card is js/hovercard.js with .kp-popover.kp-tooltip, the tables are .kp-datatable with data-kp-sort-multi="click". A green package tag marks that. The amber mock tag marks page-only script in demo.js and hc- layout classes that borrow package classes (badge, tag, table, button) and restyle none of them: the rebuild button, the dataset, the rich content built when the card opens and the docked strip. The card needs a focusable anchor for the keyboard: tabindex="0" on a cell or chip that has no control in it.

Every click adds a sort keypackage

Look at: data-kp-sort-multi="click". In the first table a plain click on a header adds that column as the next key (ascending, then descending, then out again), the numbers in the headers give the order and the line above says it in words; Enter or Space on a focused header does the same. Shift + click (Shift + Enter) sorts on that column alone, the quick way back to one key; Clear the sort puts the opening sort back (here: none). The second table opens on three keys and its button restores them. The third is the default, unchanged: Shift adds, a plain click sorts alone. The fourth adds data-kp-sort-cycle="three". The summary line must keep one line high whatever it says.
