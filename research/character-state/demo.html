A state word of its own, per theme: one pick per aspect

Phase 1 of the character round for the state word (Kenny's standing shape, as the trend tile and the meter): per theme you pick the shape of the word and its dot, the tone a state reads in, and how the word changes, each from three options and each on its own. The word is the package's own (.kp-state-word, setStateWord() in js/components.js); an option only restyles it from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (state.css), one attribute per aspect, and keeps the word's width in every state.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: the word still changes but the picture behind the change stands still. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.
