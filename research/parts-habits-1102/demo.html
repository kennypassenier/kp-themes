Five parts for the Habits app: the day, the swipe, chords, settled rows and swatches

The five parts you approved for Kenny’s Habits todo app, built: Today’s timeline as one day from top to bottom, a row a finger swipes for Done or Tomorrow, a key line that can say “O then T”, a row that stays where it was once its task left, and a colour picker of twelve swatches. Every one is opt-in: without it everything is exactly as you approved it. Formal only, on the app’s own shapes and words (the titles, times, projects and events are mock). 5 sections, 5 pairs.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. The width control shows the stage at a phone’s 390 px.

What is asked (habits gap: vertical day timeline, with read-only events): The package had the run timeline and the sequencer (both sideways) but no day running down the page, so Habits drew Today’s timeline itself. .kp-dayline[data-kp-dayline] with data-kp-start-hour and data-kp-step draws setDaylineItems(el, items, { allDay }): blocks as long as their estimate, overlapping items side by side in lanes, a now line, moves in steps of the step by pointer and keyboard (kp-dayline-change), read-only calendar events dashed without a handle, an all-day strip, and a task dropped from the list beside it onto an hour (kp-dayline-drop).

Today, Monday 2 November

The day from 06:00 with the tasks that have a time, the calendar’s events among them, and beside it the tasks without a time.

Expected:

  • Ready: the box opens at about 08:00, the now line at 10:40 a quarter down with “Now 10:40” on the hours. “Write the quarterly report” (09:00, 90 min, amber edge) and the dashed “Dentist” (10:00, no handle) stand side by side where they overlap; “Morning pages” is struck through. The strip above says “All day” with two dashed plates. Drag a ⠿ handle down: the block steps in quarter hours, its time follows, and on release the line under the day says kp-dayline-change; ↑ ↓ on a handle do the same (Shift: an hour), Escape puts it back.
  • Drag “Pay the energy bill” from the list onto 15:00: a dashed band says “Starts 15:00”; let go and it becomes a 30-minute block there and leaves the list. Loading: the busy card over the hours. Empty and Failed: the package’s plate over the whole box (Failed with Try again). Phone: the list goes under the day.

Formal: the blocks are the card stock with a coloured edge, the events the muted plate with a dashed rule, the now line navy. Check that a clash reads at a glance, that every time and title can be read, and that nothing scrolls the page sideways.

    What is asked (habits gap: swipe actions on a list row): On a phone Habits swiped a task row right for done and left for tomorrow with its own code. data-kp-swipe on a list with data-kp-swipe-start="done:Done:success" and data-kp-swipe-end="tomorrow:Tomorrow:info" does it: touch only, locked after 12 px sideways so a vertical move still scrolls, a threshold, the plate in the tone’s colour with its word, a tick on a phone, kp-swipe { key, action }, and the slide home in the theme’s motion. The ⋯ menu carries the same two actions, so nothing needs a swipe.

    Today

    Four tasks of the Today page. A mouse swipes here only because this page sets data-kp-swipe-pointers="any"; in the app only a finger does.

    Expected:

    • Any pointer: press a row’s title and drag right: the row’s parts follow and the room they leave is green with “Done”; past about a third of the row the word turns bold and the green plate gets an edge. Let go there: the row slides home and settles as “Done” (the line says kp-swipe c1 done). Drag left: the blue plate with “Tomorrow”. Let go early: it slides home and nothing happens. The check box, the ⋯ button and the handle never start a swipe.
    • Touch only (the default): a mouse does nothing; the ⋯ menu’s Done and Tomorrow do the same. One swiped already shows the first row after a swipe to the right (settled as Done); Rows back puts the four rows back. Phone: the same rows at 390 px.

    Formal: the plate is the success or info plate with its own ink, the slide home formal’s eased glide. Check that the word is never cut, never shows through the moving row, and that the row’s frame does not move.

      What is asked (habits gap: chord keys in a key strip): Today’s overdue banner registers the chord O then T, but setKeys could only draw keys pressed together, so the app wrote one cap “O T”. setKeys now takes [["O", "T"], what, { chord: true }] and draws the caps with the dictionary’s “then” between them (keysThen), the way the shortcut sheet shows g t.

      Overdue

      The banner over Today when tasks are past their day, with the keys that act on them.

      Expected:

      • The banner’s key line reads: O then T “move them to today”, O then L “open the list”, and CtrlZ “undo” pressed together for comparison: the chord’s caps stand a little apart with a small italic “then”, the pair pressed together touch.
      • Press O then T on the page: the line under the banner says the chord was heard. Phone: the items wrap; a chord never breaks between its caps.

      Formal: the caps are the package’s key caps on the warning alert. Check that “O then T” can never be read as O and T together.

      3 tasks are overdue

      Call the plumber, Renew the passport and Buy oat milk were planned for earlier days.

      What is asked (habits gap: a settled (left) list row): When a task left a list while it was open (ticked off, deleted, moved), Habits kept the row in place so nothing slid under the pointer, with its own CSS. data-kp-settled="Deleted" on a reorder, checklist or task list row does it: the row keeps its place and height, quiet and inert, and says where the item went, until the list is left (clearSettled(list)).

      Inbox

      Five Inbox tasks; “Buy oat milk” was deleted a moment ago.

      Expected:

      • “Buy oat milk” stands where it was at its own height: dashed frame, its parts faded, the title struck through, and “Deleted” at its end on the muted plate; it answers no press, no key and no drag. Tick another task, or pick Delete or Move to in its ⋯ menu: it settles the same way with “Done”, “Deleted” or “Moved to Work”, and nothing under it moves.

      Formal: the dashed rule and the muted plate of formal’s quiet states. Check that a settled row reads as finished at a glance and never as a row to press.

        What is asked (habits gap: a colour swatch picker): A project or a tag takes one of twelve colours, and the package had no swatch picker, so Habits drew twelve icon buttons in a popover. .kp-swatches[data-kp-swatches] with setSwatches(el, [{ value, label, colour }], current) draws the theme’s twelve categorical colours (--kp-cat-1..12) as a grid: arrow keys, aria-pressed and a ✓ on the current one, 44 px under a coarse pointer, kp-swatch-change { value }.
