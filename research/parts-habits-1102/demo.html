Habits parts, round 2: blocks you drag, and twelve colours that stay apart

Round 2 of the five Habits parts. You approved the swipe row, the chord keys and the settled row; they have left this page. Two come back: the day timeline, whose blocks you asked to drag by themselves (now the whole block moves, and the tasks beside the day drop in by mouse, pen or finger), and the swatch picker, whose colours were the theme’s already but too much alike (now every pair in all twelve themes stands clearly apart, with a row per theme to judge it). Formal only, on the app’s own shapes and words (the titles, times, projects and events are mock). 2 sections, 2 pairs.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. The width control shows the stage at a phone’s 390 px.

What is asked (habits gap: vertical day timeline, with read-only events): The package had the run timeline and the sequencer (both sideways) but no day running down the page, so Habits drew Today’s timeline itself. .kp-dayline[data-kp-dayline] with data-kp-start-hour and data-kp-step draws setDaylineItems(el, items, { allDay }): blocks as long as their estimate, overlapping items side by side in lanes, a now line, moves in steps of the step by pointer and keyboard (kp-dayline-change), read-only calendar events dashed without a handle, an all-day strip, and a task dropped from the list beside it onto an hour (kp-dayline-drop).

Today, Monday 2 November

The day from 06:00 with the tasks that have a time, the calendar’s events among them, and beside it the tasks without a time.

Expected:

  • Round 2: you asked whether the blocks themselves could be dragged. They can now, in the package: press a block anywhere on its body and drag it up or down (a press without moving still opens it, the line says kp-dayline-open); on a touch screen hold the block still for half a second, it lifts with a ring, then drag (a quick swipe still scrolls the day and the page). The tasks without a time are picked up the same way, by mouse, pen or finger: a copy follows the pointer and the dashed “Starts 15:00” band shows where it lands. Held near the top or bottom of the box, the hours scroll under it.
  • Ready: the box opens at about 08:00, the now line at 10:40 a quarter down with “Now 10:40” on the hours. “Write the quarterly report” (09:00, 90 min, amber edge) and the dashed “Dentist” (10:00, no handle) stand side by side where they overlap; “Morning pages” is struck through. The strip above says “All day” with two dashed plates. Drag a block by its body or its ⠿ handle: it steps in quarter hours, its time follows, and on release the line under the day says kp-dayline-change; ↑ ↓ on a handle do the same (Shift: an hour), Escape during a drag puts it back. The dashed events never move.
  • Drag “Pay the energy bill” from the list (its title or its free space; its check box stays a check box) onto 15:00: a dashed band says “Starts 15:00”; let go and it becomes a 30-minute block there and leaves the list; let go outside the day and nothing happens. Loading: the busy card over the hours. Empty and Failed: the package’s plate over the whole box (Failed with Try again). Phone: the list goes under the day.

Formal: the blocks are the card stock with a coloured edge, the events the muted plate with a dashed rule, the now line navy; a block in hand wears formal’s ring, the carried copy the card with the ring and formal’s offset shadow. Check that dragging a block or a task feels direct, that a plain press still opens, and that nothing scrolls the page sideways.

    What is asked (habits gap: a colour swatch picker): A project or a tag takes one of twelve colours, and the package had no swatch picker, so Habits drew twelve icon buttons in a popover. .kp-swatches[data-kp-swatches] with setSwatches(el, [{ value, label, colour }], current) draws the theme’s twelve categorical colours (--kp-cat-1..12) as a grid: arrow keys, aria-pressed and a ✓ on the current one, 44 px under a coarse pointer, kp-swatch-change { value }.

    Edit the project

    The project’s name and its colour, with the project’s dot and tag as a task row will show them.

    Expected:

    • Round 2: the colours were already the active theme’s (each swatch paints var(--kp-cat-N), so a theme switch repaints it at once); they were too close. Measured in OKLab (×100), the closest pair per theme went from 7.2 to 11.5 to between 12.0 and 13.1, every pair in every theme now at least 12 apart: each colour kept its hue family and its theme’s chroma, and moved in lightness and a little in hue. The rows under the editor show each theme’s twelve on its own ground.
    • Twelve round swatches in two rows of six, Blue pressed with a ✓ and a ring, “Blue” under the grid. Press Teal: the ✓ moves, the name says Teal, the project’s dot and its tag below turn teal and the line says kp-swatch-change teal. Tab into the grid: one stop; ← → walk a row, ↑ ↓ change rows, Enter picks.
    • Name off: the line under the grid goes; the swatches keep their names for a screen reader and as a tooltip. Phone: the same two rows of six; the theme rows wrap to two rows of six.
    • Every theme: twelve rows, formal to titanium, each on its own ground with its twelve colours and their names. No two in a row should look alike. Nostromo’s amber, green and cyan read about 3.5:1 on its pale ground, below AA for text: the price of twelve that stay apart there.

    Formal: formal’s own twelve inks, the ring in the page’s ink. Check that the current swatch is plain at a glance, and that in every theme row each of the twelve can be told from the others at a glance.
