Data table and disabled-reason fixes

The project threads (homelab, kyu, almanac) found five faults in components you had approved: a data table that went on counting a row the page had taken out, one that un-hid a row the page held for an Undo, date pills that printed the stored form of a date, a one-row-open rule read only once, and a disabled button that split its column of actions. 5 pairs in all, every one in formal (none differs per theme). Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: When a page took a row out of the table, the table went on counting it: after 14 shown rows became 13 the line still said of 27, and the pager kept the pages the row had filled, until the page called refresh(). Now the table notices rows that come and go in its body by itself: the count, the “filtered from” total and the pages follow at once, and the reader stays on the page they were on instead of being sent back to page 1. Sorting, searching, the pager and the look are as you approved them.

What changed since you approved it: A page that wants an Undo window had to take the row out of the table and put it back, because every redraw and every refresh() set hidden on every row again and could not keep one hidden. Table: a deleted row waits for Undo

Delete hides the job and offers Undo for a few seconds before it is gone for good. The row is held, not taken out of the page.

Expected:

  • Press Delete: the job disappears, the count drops by one, and a line offers Undo. Search and sort while it waits: the job stays hidden.
  • Press Undo: the job is back in its place and the count is as before. Let the time run out and the job is gone.

Formal: a held job is nowhere: not in the rows, not in the count, not in a search.
