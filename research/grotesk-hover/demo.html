Grotesk: which hover for a coloured button?

Decided on 2026-09-14: B ships. Grotesk's primary and destructive buttons invert on hover and on keyboard focus, and while one is pressed its label turns black (--foreground) on the grey press ground — the white label read 1.66:1 there, the black one reads 11.31:1. E stays on this page as a demo option that was not taken. The question, measured on 2026-09-13: in grotesk a --primary, --destructive or bare --mirror button changed nothing on hover, while the approved grotesk demo inverted a coloured button (fill becomes the page ground, the label takes the colour) only when it also carried --mirror. The five columns below are the same six buttons under five rules. Because the register now ships B, the black pressed label shows in every column when you hold the mouse down. This page stays in grotesk; the theme menu is there if you want to see the others.

Five options, side by side

Look at: hover each button in each column, and Tab through them — focus shows the same change as hover; then hold the mouse down on Save and Delete and check the black label on the grey. A was the package before scope-63. B, what ships, gives every coloured button the demo's invert. C gives every variant the grey wash of the default button. D deepens the red to the theme's own hover value (--primary-hover hsl(0 100% 40%), --destructive-hover hsl(0 100% 35%)). E inverts every variant, not only the red ones: a coloured button turns white with its red on the label and the edge, and the default, --secondary, ghost and bare mirror buttons turn black with a white label and a black edge — shown, not taken. The choice was made by which one still reads as grotesk: one red, black, white, and identity by letter rather than by effect.

A · before scope-63

Coloured buttons and a bare mirror did nothing on hover. The register now ships B, so this column shows B too.

B · the demo's invert (ships)

A coloured button turns white with a red label; pressed, it goes grey with a black label; a bare mirror takes the grey wash.

C · grey wash everywhere

Every variant goes grey with a black label and edge while hovered.

D · a deeper red

A coloured button keeps its fill, one step darker; a bare mirror takes the grey wash.

E · everything inverts (not taken)

Every variant swaps ground and ink while hovered: red buttons go white with a red label, the rest go black with a white label.
