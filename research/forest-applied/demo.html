Every block below is the package itself in forest, as an app gets it: the real components, the real stylesheets and the real scripts, nothing drawn for this page. Each heading says in one sentence what the component did before and what it does now. Point at the parts, press them, Tab onto them and open what opens; where a state needs a button (loading, right to left, play again), the button is in the block.

Approve or not per block in the review dialog ("Review in a dialog" at the top), with a note where something is wrong. What this page found broken while it was built is listed in research/forest-applied/README.md; nothing in the package was changed by this page. Inside the review dialog, Escape closes the review dialog itself: close what you opened there with its own button or a click outside it.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page grows or withers: every part shows its finished pose. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the openings and closes.

1 · The bar

The bar's dropdown and mega menu used to fade in and out, the phone menu appeared at once and the call to action and the toggle did not answer the pointer; now each menu grows down out of its link and closes as that growth backwards, and the call to action and the toggle take the dashed blaze.

Hover Readings (or Tab onto it): its dropdown grows down out of the link, far end first, clipped at the bar's line, and withers back up when you leave. Press Equipment for the mega menu, the same growth across the bar. Hover Request access and press it: the blaze is laid and closes in. In the narrow frame press the three-bar toggle: the links grow down out of the bar and go back into it.

2 · Side navigation and its groups

The side navigation used to slide in over 200 ms and its groups unfolded in 200 ms with grey rows; now the panel grows out of its edge in one growth (1000 ms) with its backdrop fading on the growth curve, its groups unfold in the accordion's 1000 ms, and its rows answer as menu entries with the blaze and the leaf corner.

Point at the rows of the panel: kraft ground and the blaze laid inside. Press Maintenance and Readings: the groups unfold and fold in one growth. Press Open the panel: it grows out of the window's left edge, far side first, the backdrop fades in with it; close it with Close, Escape or the backdrop (the backdrop still goes at once on close, a known finding).

3 · Date picker

The date picker's panel opened and closed at once and its days took a grey ground with rounded corners; now the panel grows down out of its field like a menu, and each day takes the blaze and the leaf corner, with the two-channel focus ring winning over the blaze.

Press the calendar button: the panel grows down out of the field. Point at the days, then use the arrow keys: the focused day shows the two-channel ring and no blaze. Pick a day or press Escape: the panel closes smoothly as the exact reverse of its opening, and switching month or year preserves the open panel without replaying the opening animation.

4 · Tour

The tour's card faded in over 160 ms and went at once; now it grows up out of its own line in one growth, and when the tour ends that growth plays backwards.

Press Start the tour: the card grows up beside each part in turn. Press Next, Back, then Skip or Escape: the card withers back into its line.
