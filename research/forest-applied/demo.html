Forest, as applied: what changed

Kenny, 2026-10-09: "Can you show in a demo which forest components you changed? Just a showcase where I can approve everything." Every block below is the package itself in forest, as an app gets it: the real components, the real stylesheets and the real scripts, nothing drawn for this page. Each heading says in one sentence what the component did before and what it does now. Point at the parts, press them, Tab onto them and open what opens; where a state needs a button (loading, right to left, play again), the button is in the block.

Approve or not per block in the review dialog ("Review in a dialog" at the top), with a note where something is wrong. What this page found broken while it was built is listed in research/forest-applied/README.md; nothing in the package was changed by this page. Inside the review dialog, Escape closes the review dialog itself: close what you opened there with its own button or a click outside it.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page grows or withers: every part shows its finished pose. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the openings and closes.

1 · The bar

The bar's dropdown and mega menu used to fade in and out, the phone menu appeared at once and the call to action and the toggle did not answer the pointer; now each menu grows down out of its link and closes as that growth backwards, and the call to action and the toggle take the dashed blaze.

Hover Readings (or Tab onto it): its dropdown grows down out of the link, far end first, clipped at the bar's line, and withers back up when you leave. Press Equipment for the mega menu, the same growth across the bar. Hover Request access and press it: the blaze is laid and closes in. In the narrow frame press the three-bar toggle: the links grow down out of the bar and go back into it.

3 · Side navigation and its groups

Point at the rows of the panel: kraft ground and the blaze laid inside. Press Maintenance and Readings: the groups unfold and fold in one growth. The command palette opened and closed at once and its options did not answer the pointer; now it grows up out of its base like the dialog with its backdrop fading in, and its options take the blaze laid inside on the kraft ground.

Press Open the commands: the palette grows up out of its base line. Point at the options, type "lock" to filter. Escape closes it: in Chromium as the growth played backwards, in Firefox at once (a known finding: the palette's close still needs js/motion.js).

5 · Date picker

The date picker's panel opened and closed at once and its days took a grey ground with rounded corners; now the panel grows down out of its field like a menu, and each day takes the blaze and the leaf corner, with the two-channel focus ring winning over the blaze.

Press the calendar button: the panel grows down out of the field. Point at the days, then use the arrow keys: the focused day shows the two-channel ring and no blaze. Pick a day or press Escape: the panel still goes at once (a known finding, the close needs a swap of two lines in js/datepicker.js).

6 · Theme menu

The theme menu's options took an 8 % wash, the React list opened at once, and an option's focus was one pale channel; now the options take the blaze laid inside with the leaf corner, the React list grows down out of its button, and focus is the two-channel ring.

Press the palette icon: the menu grows down out of it and closes as that growth backwards. Point at the options, then Tab through them: the ring of two channels, no blaze. The second button is the React shape: its list grows on opening and still goes at once on closing (a known finding). Choosing a theme here does not switch this page, which judges forest only.

7 · Combobox

9 · Dialog, drawer and every backdrop

Open the dialog: the plate grows up out of its base and the backdrop darkens with it on the same curve. Close it with either button or Escape: both go back together. The same for the drawer, which grows out of its edge.

    Who may reopen a locked-out line?
    Only the shift lead who signed the lock-out, or the site manager when that person has left the site.
    What counts as a pressure drop?
    A fall of more than one bar within thirty minutes on any line.
    Which reading do we believe when the gauge and the sensor disagree?
    Neither, on its own: close the line and log both readings at the same minute.

    17 · Buttons

    A pressed primary or destructive button turned pale under its light words (1.8:1); now it keeps its own plate while pressed and only the blaze closes in, the plain button alone turning to its pressed ground, and every other variant answers as before.

    Press and hold each button. The primary and the destructive keep their plate and their words stay readable; the plain one takes its pressed ground. Point at the ghost and the icon button: the blaze over a faint wash (kept on purpose, it shows a ghost is a button). Tab through them all: the focus ring wins over the blaze.

    18 · Links, headline, rule, alert edge, meter, back to top

    A link in running text changed at once, the headline slid in, and the section rule, the meter's wipe and the alert's edge always ran left to right; now the link answers in 200 ms, the headline grows up out of its line, the rule, the wipe and the edge start from the reading side in a right-to-left page too, and back to top withers out.

    Point at the link in the text. Press Play again for the headline, the rule and the meters. Press Right to left and play again: the rule and the wipes start at the right, the alerts' edge stands at the right. Press Show back to top twice: it fades in and withers out.

    19 · Skeletons, spinner, busy button, loading meter

    The busy bar breathed in 6600 ms while the skeleton block and circle looped in 3200 ms; now the bar, the planting strips, the skeleton lines, block and circle and the measuring meter all breathe as one in 6600 ms (3000 in, 600 full, 3000 out), and the spinner keeps its tree every 1600 ms.

    Watch a whole breath: every row fills start to end, holds, and empties end to start, together. Press Start together to set them all off on the same frame. The spinner plants one tree in three stages; the busy button and the loading meter show the planting strip.

    20 · Popover menus

    A menu in a popover showed two frames and two shadows, and while it grew it was drawn up to 3 rem over its own button; now it is one plate with the leaf corner, clipped at its button's edge for the whole growth.

    Press Row actions: one plate grows down out of the button and never covers it; Escape or a click outside closes it as that growth backwards. Press More ▾ for the menu of every other action, the same growth, its entries with the blaze laid inside. The menu opens right under its button (found while building this page, fixed in js/top-layer.js).
