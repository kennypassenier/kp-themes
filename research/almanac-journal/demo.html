Almanac status page: the Journal and Sources sections

The two sections Almanac builds itself on the status page, drawn with kp-themes 11.0.0 parts. Four sections, formal only. The numbers on the first card start from the live service on 19/10/2026 (version 4.1.7, up 10 days 3 h, 3 events accepted, 3 delivered, 0 failed, nothing pending). Anything beyond that is sample data and says so on the page.

Journal at a glance

The one number that matters (events waiting to be delivered), whether the journal and the store are healthy, and how the events since the service started ended.

Expected:

  • Four key figures of one height: Waiting to be delivered 0, Delivered since start 3, Failed since start 0 and a trend tile Deliveries per hour with its 24-hour line (sample data). Press Delivered or Failed: they are toggles, and the bar below shows only that outcome (press again to leave it). Press An event arrives: the waiting figure counts up to 1 (it ticks, it does not jump), the tile turns to the warning tone and the journal dot turns amber with its reason.
  • It is delivered counts the waiting figure back down and the delivered figure up; A delivery fails puts one in the red part of the bar. Every figure is the exact number.
  • Loading shows the busy card over the whole card, failed shows one plate with a normal Retry; both keep the filled height. The How the outcome bar reads set in the dialog switches the bar between its two looks.

The waiting figure ticks when it changes, the two dots say in words what they mean, and the bar and the figures agree. The created / updated split of the bar is sample data, as the note under the card says.

Recent deliveries

What Almanac wrote to the calendars lately: when, from which source and how it ended. Filter by result; open a row to read what went wrong.

Expected:

  • Three columns, When (relative: 4 min ago), Source (a tag) and Result (Created green, Updated blue, Retrying amber, Failed red), sortable, with the toggle in its own column. The chips above show exact counts: All 13, Created 6, Updated 4, Retrying 1, Failed 2.
  • Press Failed: two rows stay and the chip is the only one on. Open one: a plain sentence and the error as a code block. A row that went well says where it was written.
  • Loading shows the busy card over the whole table, Empty the plate Nothing delivered yet this run, Failed one plate with Retry. All three keep the filled height. A filter that matches nothing shows the table’s own “nothing matches” plate.

The counts on the chips add up to the rows, a failed row opens onto its error in a code block, and the three states are a plate each in the card’s footprint with a normal-size Retry.

Staying live

The deliveries table keeps itself current: you choose how often, a line says how fresh it is, a banner appears when the service cannot be reached, and a new delivery arrives in its place.

Expected:

  • The header is one row: the title Journal, then the refresh control with its freshness line (updated 20 s ago) and Refresh now. It opens paused; pick 15 s and a thin rule runs down to the next refresh.
  • Simulate a delivery: a new row is added at the top of the table without the table redrawing, the chip counts change by one, and nothing else moves. Sorting, an open row and a chip you pressed all survive it.
  • Reconnecting or Refused shows the banner above the card with the reason and a Retry; while it shows, Refresh now fails with its own warning, and Online brings the table back with Connected again.

The freshness line sits right after the title, a simulated delivery lands in place with its count, and the banner reads at a glance without covering the table.

Sources

Which source writes to which calendar, and whether its profile file is being served. Reloading re-reads the files from disk without leaving the page.

Expected:

  • Four sources: home-assistant, homelab, kyu, claude-sessions, each with its calendar and a health mark; one source warns and says what to do about it. Rows and cards show the same four sources.
  • Press Reload profiles from disk: the button shows it is busy, says Done and goes back to its words; a toast says 4 profiles reloaded from disk, the warning row turns healthy, and the page does not jump or scroll.
  • Loading shows the busy card over the list, empty a plate with No profile files on disk, failed one plate with Retry; all keep the filled height.

Rows and cards show the same data; the reload button flashes busy then done, the toast carries the number, and the list updates in place.
