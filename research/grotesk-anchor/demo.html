The colours are already right everywhere and stay the shared base. For grotesk the candidate so far is the loading Out of register; you asked for more options.

One question, five bold candidates, each drawn only from what grotesk's world already owns (the International Typographic Style: the twelve-column grid, flush-left type on its baseline, red on paper, the rule, the register mark, the colour bands, the timetable that runs on time). Each one is shown the same three ways, on one clock: in its own scene, as a progress bar and as a button press, so you see whether it carries the theme.
