The activity feed

A new component for the lists that say who did what and when: the homelab dashboard’s History tab and Activity page drew it by hand, and it was the last thing they drew themselves. It is made from parts the package already has: the status marks, the identity chip, the ticking time, the timeline’s rule, the thread’s day divider and the empty and failed plates. Five sections, each with its own controls above the feed; formal only, the other themes follow once you approve it here. The data is mock (stacks such as jellyfin, sonarr and traefik), and its times are relative to the moment you open the page.

What is asked: A new component, built because the admin dashboard drew this row by hand twice (a stack’s History tab and the Activity page). Does it read as one tidy list — a mark you recognise by its shape, the chip of whoever started it, the time on the right, one rule joining the marks of a day — and are the five states (ready, loading, empty, failed, stale) what you would want to see when the dashboard cannot read its log? Press Grouping to see the same rows as one list.

Who did what, day by day

One feed of a homelab: every row has its outcome, what happened, who started it and when, and the rows sit under a heading per day with that day’s count.

Expected:

  • Each day has a heading: Today, Yesterday, then the weekday, then the date as dd/mm/yyyy, with that day’s exact count and N failed (or all ok) at the end.
  • A row: a round mark (tick, cross, exclamation, spinner or i), the title in bold, a muted detail line, a chip with the actor, and the time. The chips and the times stand in one column each, whichever row is longer.
  • The time says 12 min ago for the first 24 hours and ticks by itself; after that it is the moment, dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm. Pointing at it gives the full moment.
  • Loading keeps the feed’s height with the theme’s busy card over a skeleton of rows; Empty and Failed fill the same height with a large centred plate (Failed has a Retry that reads the log again); Stale keeps every row and says in the head line why the feed was not refreshed, with a button to read it again.
  • Nothing moves sideways or jumps in height between the states. Labels are whole, never cut.

Formal is a paper plate: quiet ink, square-ish corners, one border weight. Check that the marks are legible by shape alone, that a failed row stands out without shouting, that text keeps 8 px from every edge, and that the chip column lines up from the first day to the last.

What is asked: Each of these is a setting of the component with a default (time auto, actor chip, rule on), switchable at run time. Press them and say which default is right, and whether the others are worth keeping as options. The rows reach back three days so the time options differ.

The time, the actor and the rule

Three things a page may want different: how the time reads, whether the actor is a chip, a mark or left out, and whether the timeline’s rule joins the marks.

Expected:

  • Auto (default): 12 min ago for 24 hours, then dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm. Ago line: always 1 day 20 h ago and so on. Clock: the ago line, then only HH:mm under its day heading. Moment: always dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm.
  • Chip (default): mark and name. Mark only: the round or square mark with the name as its title (a screen reader still hears it). No actor: the column is gone and the title gets the room.
  • Rule: a thin line joins one mark to the next inside a day and stops at the day heading; off, the marks stand alone.
  • Every press changes the feed at once, and the columns stay aligned.

Compare Auto with Clock: the older rows lose their date and keep only the hour. Compare Chip with Mark only: the name leaves but the mark stays recognisable by its colour and pattern. Check that the title uses the room when the actor is off.

What is asked: In the dashboard a row opens an inline detail. Here the title is the button (a chevron says so), the lines come in the mono face on the muted plate, and a row that has both lines and a page shows Open inside. Opening is a setting: several rows at once (default), one at a time, or not at all.

Open a row to read its log

A row that has lines (a log excerpt) opens under its title; a row that only has a place to go is a link; the rows keep their state when the feed refreshes.

Expected:

  • Press a title with a chevron: its lines open under it, the chevron turns, and the row below moves down by exactly the lines’ height. Press again: it closes.
  • One at a time: opening a row closes the others (and if several are open when you switch to it, the one opened last stays). Not openable: no chevrons, the titles are plain text or links.
  • The arrow keys move from one title to the next. Open all and Close all act on the rows; Refresh reads the feed again and the rows you opened stay open, with the focus where it was.
  • The failed row’s lines show the real error; the running row shows the pull as it goes.

Formal keeps the lines on a quiet muted plate with a hairline border, in the mono face. Check the lines never touch the plate’s edge, that long lines wrap instead of being cut, and that pressing Refresh does not close what you opened.

What is asked: The foot keeps the same room in all its states, so nothing moves when it changes. The button here is real: press it and two older days come in (after a short wait), then the foot says there is nothing earlier. The state buttons show each foot without waiting.

Reach further back

A feed shows the newest rows first; at its foot a button loads the earlier ones, says how far back it has got, and says plainly when there is nothing more.

Expected:

  • Load earlier: a normal button with the note Showing 6 of 30, back to dd/mm/yyyy (exact numbers).
  • Loading: the same button, disabled, with the spinner and Loading earlier…; the rows stay on screen. Failed: the sentence and Try again. End: Nothing earlier: all 30 are shown, back to …. Hidden: no foot and no room for it.
  • Pressing the real button: the rows grow downward, the day headings are right, the count in the head line rises (6 of 30, 12 of 30, then 30 operations).
  • Start over puts the first six rows back.

Formal: a plain button centred under the list with a quiet note. Check that the button does not change width between Load earlier and Loading, that the foot keeps its height from state to state, and that the head count is the exact number, never rounded.

What is asked: The layout reads its own width, not the window’s, so it holds in a side panel as well as on a phone. Scrolls inside is the setting data-kp-feed-max: the list scrolls in a box of 26 rem and the day headings stick.

A phone-wide column

The same feed in a column as wide as a phone: the actor and the time move under the text, and the list can scroll inside a fixed height while the day heading stays at its top.

Expected:

  • Each row: the mark and the title on the first line, the detail under it, then the chip on the left and the time on the right on a line of their own. Nothing is cut and nothing runs sideways.
  • Scrolls inside: the list is 26 rem high and scrolls; scroll it and the heading of the day you are in stays at the top, on the card’s ground.
  • The states keep the same footprint: Loading, Empty and Failed fill the box the rows had.

Formal: check at this width that the heading of each day fits on one line or wraps cleanly, that the chip and the time are never closer than 8 px, and that the sticky heading is opaque (no row shows through).
