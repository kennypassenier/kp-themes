What is deco's anchor element

Kenny, 2026-10-07 18:13: every theme gets one recognisable anchor element, the one thing every later decision of the theme departs from (forest: the tree progress bar, grotesk: the red plate that falls into register, synthwave: the page horizon, blueprint: the tracing pen). The colours are already right everywhere and stay the shared base. Deco has no anchor yet: its register has the fan of rays on every hovered button, the sun that rises on the dialog, the fluted pennant bar and the lobby's lamps on the meter, but they are a handful of fine devices, and nothing says which one every later decision departs from.

One question, seven bold candidates, each drawn only from what deco's world already owns (the lobby of the skyscraper: the fan that opens, the lift that ascends, the setbacks that rise, the curtain that rises, the jewel that is set, the glint that runs the gilt, the marquee bulbs that chase). Each one is shown the same three ways, on one clock: in its own scene, as a progress bar and as a button press, so you see whether it carries the theme.
