Toggle button

Toggle button: one, filters and a one-of switch

<button class="kp-button" data-kp-toggle> (js/toggle.js): a button that stays pressed until it is pressed again. The words never change; the state is aria-pressed. Press things; nothing here is stored.

Expected:

  • One toggle: Only errors is a plain button. Press it: the face turns dark, the words stay the same, and the line Showing errors only appears under the words (the row is there all the time, so the button keeps its height). Press again: back to plain. The pin beside it is the icon look, a square.
  • Filters: four pills All 138, Error 12, Warning 31, Info 95. All starts on. Press Error: All goes off and Error goes on; press Warning as well: both are on and the list below says what is shown. Press All (or Clear filters (2) at the end of the row): everything else goes off and All is on again. The Clear button keeps its place when nothing is pressed (it is only invisible), so the row never moves. The key W presses Warning when the focus is not in a field.
  • A one-of switch: Day, Week, Month; exactly one is on, pressing the one that is on does nothing, and the arrow keys walk along the three (Tab enters the group once). Single or none lets the on one be pressed off.
  • Every press says one polite line below the row (read by a screen reader, shown here in the grey box). The count is the real number, never “9+”; the next section feeds a count past 99 and a count that is still being counted.
  • Not new, and what is new: the package already has the segmented control (js/segmented.js), shown under the three cases above with the same filters and the same one-of switch. A group of choices is that part’s job, and a toggle group looks and answers the same way. What the toggle adds is what that part cannot do: a single button that stays pressed (a pin, “Only errors”; the segmented control is always a group), a half-on state, a line under the words and a tick, Clear filters (N), a one-of switch that can be off, a key that presses it, and an app that answers after a wait (busy, then rolled back with a word if refused).
  • The choice (tick it in the dialog): whether a pressed toggle also shows a tick. The buttons below flip it too.

A pressed toggle is formal’s ink with the paper-coloured words and the double rule drawn inside, as a held press looks in formal; the pills are formal’s own, with its soft corners, and the segmented chips beside them are the same pills. Look at the words next to the numbers (a gap of at least a few pixels), at the tick appearing without moving a word, and at the Clear button leaving no hole in the row.

                        or press a query below.

  • 
                            Nothing typed: not the whole list but a short start: the two Inbox items, what is featured (Deploy all changes, New stack, Overview, Backups) and what is about the stack that is open, jellyfin (its
                            Update, Restart and logs first in Do). The status line says how many commands are shown (9).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            jellyfin upd, upd jellyfin and update jelly all show Update jellyfin first, the typed words

Entity card: one fixed card per stack

                    

                        <div class="kp-entity-grid" data-kp-entity-grid> (js/entity-card.js): the same five rows on every card,
                        kept whether a row has anything or not. Switch the options below; the cards draw again.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            Six cards in a grid (two columns here, one on a phone), every card exactly as high as the next: media has a flag,
                            vault has none and keeps its flag row empty, gateway has five flags (three show and +2 opens the rest
                            in the package’s menu). Each card: the mark made from the name, the name (the card’s one link), the id, the
                            state in words; the figures with exact numbers; two meters with their number always beside the bar (CPU 34%,
                            RAM 5.6 of 8 GB; a full one turns warning or destructive); then, with the data below, Restart, Logs,
                            Update in three fixed columns and More in the fourth.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            The buttons come only from the data. Actions in the data: None and the cards have no button row at all and
                            no empty space where it was (they are shorter, still level with each other). Two shows two buttons,
                            Four three buttons and More; Mixed gives each card its own number (one has none): the columns are the same
                            on every card, so Restart stands in the first column everywhere, and a card with none leaves the room empty below
                            its meters because the cards in a row share one height. Between the last meter and the buttons there is always one clear
                            gap (Meters 1, 2 and 3): the buttons never touch the line above them.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            More and +2 open the package’s own menu (the buttons below open them one at a time, each beside the
                            package’s own menu button under the cards, so the plates can be compared); the arrow keys move in it and Esc closes
                            it. Remove in More is destructive. A live refresh changes the numbers and a flag in place: the cards are the
                            same elements, so nothing under the pointer moves.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Selectable adds a tick on each card: tick one (or press j to move the ring, x to tick) and its edge

                            a mix), One flag, Compact (the figures go),
