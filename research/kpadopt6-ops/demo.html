Operations parts, round six: seven more

Seven parts the admin dashboard draws by hand today, built in the package: a button that stays pressed, a command palette that finds words in any order, the chip for a version jump, the tick that must be set before an update may start, the list of what depends on what, the path one connection takes, and the fixed card for one stack. This page asks formal only; the other themes follow once formal is approved.

Judge in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top): one topic per part, one step each in formal. Every section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. Everything with a kp- class and every script under js/ is the package; the sample data, the frames marked “phone width” (390 px boxes standing in for a phone), the event readouts and the buttons that drive a state are this page’s. Nothing moves until you press a button.

Toggle button

Toggle button: one, filters and a one-of switch

<button class="kp-button" data-kp-toggle> (js/toggle.js): a button that stays pressed until it is pressed again. The words never change; the state is aria-pressed. Press things; nothing here is stored.

Expected:

  • One toggle: Only errors is a plain button. Press it: the face turns dark, the words stay the same, and the line Showing errors only appears under the words (the row is there all the time, so the button keeps its height). Press again: back to plain. The pin beside it is the icon look, a square.
  • Filters: four pills All 138, Error 12, Warning 31, Info 95. All starts on. Press Error: All goes off and Error goes on; press Warning as well: both are on and the list below says what is shown. Press All (or Clear filters (2) at the end of the row): everything else goes off and All is on again. The Clear button keeps its place when nothing is pressed (it is only invisible), so the row never moves. The key W presses Warning when the focus is not in a field.
  • A one-of switch: Day, Week, Month; exactly one is on, pressing the one that is on does nothing, and the arrow keys walk along the three (Tab enters the group once). Single or none lets the on one be pressed off.
  • Every press says one polite line below the row (read by a screen reader, shown here in the grey box). The count is the real number, never “9+”; the next section feeds a count past 99 and a count that is still being counted.
  • The choice (tick it in the dialog): whether a pressed toggle also shows a tick. The buttons below flip it too.

A pressed toggle is formal’s ink with the paper-coloured words and the double rule drawn inside, as a held press looks in formal; the pills are formal’s own, with its soft corners. Look at the words next to the numbers (a gap of at least a few pixels), at the tick appearing without moving a word, and at the Clear button leaving no hole in the row.

Palette: words in any order, best answer first

                    

                        <dialog class="kp-palette" data-kp-palette> (js/palette.js with js/intent.js): the default match of every
                        palette now. Every typed word has to start a word of the command, in any order. The palette is shown open in place; type in it
                        or press a query below.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            Nothing typed: not the whole list but a short start: the two Inbox items, what is featured (Deploy all changes, New stack, Overview, Backups) and what is about the stack that is open, jellyfin (its
                            Update, Restart and logs first in Do). The status line says how many commands are shown (9).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            jellyfin upd, upd jellyfin and update jelly all show Update jellyfin first, the typed words
                            ruled under. logs gateway and gateway logs find Show the logs of gateway. reboot gate finds
                            Restart gateway by a hidden word (it is never shown). cafe finds the stack café-kiosk (accents do not
                            matter) and kiosk too (a hyphenated name is found by its parts).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            A word has to start a word: read does not find Restart, ate finds nothing. The matches in the
                            description count less than the ones in the label (newest finds the commands that say it in their description,
                            after any whose name says it).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Enter runs the first row (the readout under the palette says which); the arrow keys walk the rows in the order they
                            stand in; the commands stay in their groups, the groups in the order Inbox, Do, Go to.
                        
    • 
                        
  • The choice (tick it in the dialog): how the matched words are marked. The buttons below flip it too.
    • 
                    

                

                

                    

                        Formal’s palette is the card with a strong boundary; a matched word is ruled under in navy, as formal rules what it
                        points at, and the active row is the soft wash of the ink. Look at the rule under the words (a little air under the letters),
                        at the order of the rows changing as you type, and at the status line staying exact.

Palette: no match, a cap, loading, failed and phone width

                    

                        The same palette when nothing answers, when more match than are shown, while its commands are still arriving and when they
                        could not be read; and a palette at the width of a phone.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            No match (zzz): the list gives way to the package’s empty plate, as high as the list was, the words
                            Nothing matches centred at heading size and a hint under them. update zzz knows its verb:
                            There is no stack called “zzz”. Update works on: jellyfin, gateway, paperless, immich, vault,
                                café-kiosk.
                            The status line says No commands.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Cap at 5 with e typed: five rows show and the status line says 5 of N shown, type more words (the
                            real number, never “many”); one more letter narrows it.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Loading: the theme’s busy card (spinner and words) sits in the middle of the list, which keeps its size; the
                            input stays usable. Failed: a red plate “The commands could not be read” with a normal-size
                            Retry above the places (Overview, Backups, Host and the rest of Go to), which still
                            work; Retry puts the palette back to ready.
                        
    • 
                        
  • Phone width: the same palette in a 390 px box; long commands wrap and nothing is cut, no word touches an edge.
    • 
                    

                

                

                    

                        The empty and the failed plate are formal’s own (the dashed paper plate, the red plate with its rule); the busy card is
                        formal’s ruled ring. Check the text’s centre against the plate’s centre and that nothing changes the
                        palette’s height when a state is switched.
