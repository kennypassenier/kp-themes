Operations parts, round six: seven more

Seven parts the admin dashboard draws by hand today, built in the package: a button that stays pressed, a command palette that finds words in any order, the chip for a version jump, the tick that must be set before an update may start, the list of what depends on what, the path one connection takes, and the fixed card for one stack. This page asks formal only; the other themes follow once formal is approved.

Judge in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top): one topic per part, one step each in formal. Every section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. Everything with a kp- class and every script under js/ is the package; the sample data, the frames marked “phone width” (390 px boxes standing in for a phone), the event readouts and the buttons that drive a state are this page’s. Toggle button

Toggle button: one, filters and a one-of switch

<button class="kp-button" data-kp-toggle> (js/toggle.js): a button that stays pressed until it is pressed again. The words never change; the state is aria-pressed. Press things; nothing here is stored.

Expected:

  • One toggle: Only errors is a plain button. Press it: the face turns dark, the words stay the same, and the line Showing errors only appears under the words (the row is there all the time, so the button keeps its height). Press again: back to plain. The pin beside it is the icon look, a square.
  • Filters: four pills All 138, Error 12, Warning 31, Info 95. All starts on. Press Error: All goes off and Error goes on; press Warning as well: both are on and the list below says what is shown. Press All (or Clear filters (2) at the end of the row): everything else goes off and All is on again. The Clear button keeps its place when nothing is pressed (it is only invisible), so the row never moves. The key W presses Warning when the focus is not in a field.
  • A one-of switch: Day, Week, Month; exactly one is on, pressing the one that is on does nothing, and the arrow keys walk along the three (Tab enters the group once). Single or none lets the on one be pressed off.
  • Every press says one polite line below the row (read by a screen reader, shown here in the grey box). The count is the real number, never “9+”; the next section feeds a count past 99 and a count that is still being counted.
  • The choice (tick it in the dialog): whether a pressed toggle also shows a tick. The buttons below flip it too.

A pressed toggle is formal’s ink with the paper-coloured words and the double rule drawn inside, as a held press looks in formal; the pills are formal’s own, with its soft corners. Look at the words next to the numbers (a gap of at least a few pixels), at the tick appearing without moving a word, and at the Clear button leaving no hole in the row.
