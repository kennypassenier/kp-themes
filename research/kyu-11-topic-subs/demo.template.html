kyu on kp-themes 11: the Topic page, top half

How the top of kyu’s Topic page (/t/notify.kenny/dashboard) looks when it uses the new components to the full: the page header, the commands to send and receive, the warning for a topic nobody reads yet, and the table of subscriptions with its menu, Archive and Delete. Everything here is mock data in kyu’s real shape (the topics notify.kenny, ha.events, almanac.sync, jobtracker.mail and newsflash.items; the subscriptions desktop, phone, kyu-runner, almanac and archive); nothing is sent to a server and the token is made up.

Seven sections, formal only. Each says under Expected what to see and what each control does. Every setting a component offers is a button above it and a choice in the dialog that only asks for the default.

Page header: mark, title, freshness and one sentence

Says which topic this is and how fresh it is: a coloured mark that is the same wherever the topic appears, the name, a badge for the dead letters, a line that ticks (“updated 12 s ago”) and one sentence on what the page is for.

Expected:

  • Filled: the mark NK in front of notify.kenny, a red 1 dead badge, and updated 12 s ago that counts on by itself without moving anything beside it. Point at it: the title shows 19/10/2026 14:00-style time (dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm). Under the title the sentence, then 1,284 messages retained · backlog 112 · 5 subscriptions (exact numbers).
  • Quiet topic turns the line into last message 3 days ago on a warning plate, without shifting the title. Loading shows the same header as grey shapes of the same size, animated, and nothing jumps back when it fills.
  • Topic mark: Letters, Pattern (a 5 by 5 picture made from the name) or Glyph (a small corner shape). Sentence under the title: Whole, or Folded after the first sentence with a More button that opens it. Copy address says Copied for a moment.
  • At 390 px the actions go under the sentence and the title wraps under its mark.

Formal is the reference look: white plates, hairline borders, a quiet blue and gentle 180 ms motion. Check that the mark, title, badge and freshness sit on one line and wrap together, that the freshness line keeps its width while it ticks, and that nothing moves between Filled, Quiet and Loading.

Send and receive: three steps, four commands, a hidden token

Publish a message, receive it, acknowledge it: each command is shown to read, and the token that makes it work stays hidden until you ask. Copy puts the whole working command on your clipboard without ever showing it.

Expected:

  • A path of three steps, 1. Publish, 2. Receive, 3. Acknowledge, and under each its command(s) with the terminal’s prompt and its own Copy. Receive has two: the raw payload and the JSON envelope. The token appears in them only as $KYU_TOKEN.
  • Under each command a field Working command with the token shows ●●●● dots. Reveal shows the whole command for the chosen time with a draining rule and the seconds left (it pauses while you select the text); Copy there copies the working command and never shows it.
  • Copy a command and its step turns green with the time; the next step becomes the current one. Start over resets the path.
  • Token: Being read (a spinner with “Reading the token”), Cannot be read (the reason and a Try again in the Reveal button), Locked (both buttons off, the sentence under them says why), Hidden (back to normal). Section: Loading shows the shared loading card over the dimmed commands; Failed the red alert with a normal Try again. Reveal shows for and Reveal works by change the next Reveal.
  • At 390 px the steps stack, the path becomes a column, and nothing scrolls sideways.

Check that the three steps read as one path with the commands under their own step, that the prompt is muted and never copied, that Reveal and Copy are the same size in every command, and that the countdown rule drains without moving the buttons.

Commands for notify.kenny

The commands carry this hub’s login token (mock), hidden. Reveal shows it for 10 seconds. A command with an app’s own token is on the Apps page.

  1. 1. Publish Send a message to the topic
  2. 2. Receive Ask for the next message
  3. 3. Acknowledge Say it arrived, so it is not sent again

Publish a message:

curl -s -H "authorization: Bearer $KYU_TOKEN" \
     -H 'content-type: application/json' \
     -d '{"title":"eerste echte bericht","body":"hello from HA"}' \
     https://kyu.example/t/notify.kenny

Receive it: the payload is the body, the id comes back in a header:

curl -s -H "authorization: Bearer $KYU_TOKEN" -D- -o message.body "https://kyu.example/t/notify.kenny/next?as=desktop"

Or ask for one JSON document instead:

curl -s -H "authorization: Bearer $KYU_TOKEN" "https://kyu.example/t/notify.kenny/next?as=desktop&envelope=json"

Then acknowledge it, so it is not delivered again:

curl -s -X POST -H "authorization: Bearer $KYU_TOKEN" "https://kyu.example/t/notify.kenny/ack/<id>?as=desktop"

Warning: nobody has polled this topic yet

A subscription only exists once it has polled, so on a brand-new topic (here newsflash.items) the page warns that a message published now will not be seen, and says what to do first.

Expected:

  • Shown: the theme's warning plate with three lines: what is wrong (Nobody has polled newsflash.items yet), why, and What now with the command to run once, in code, with its own Copy.
  • Loading: the shared loading card over the dimmed warning. Failed: the red alert “Could not check who reads this topic” with a normal Try again. A reader exists: the warning is gone and the card says so, at the same size, so the page below does not jump.

Check that the warning is the theme’s warning plate, that what / why / what now read as three lines, and that the card keeps its height in all four states.

Subscriptions: filter, sort, Archive and Delete

Every reader of notify.kenny in one table: narrow it by state with the filter above it, sort by any column, and archive or delete a reader on the row, with the same buttons in the same place on every row.

Expected:

  • Above the table All 5 · active 3 · flagged 1 · archived 1 with the exact numbers. A plain click turns a state on or off (active and flagged together is allowed); nothing on, or All, shows everything. A pill under the bar names the filter and removes it. The table’s own line says how many rows are shown.
  • Each row: the name with its mark, a state badge (desktop is flagged because a message died on it), backlog (112 on kyu-runner), in flight, dead, lapsed, the oldest unacked message, and the policy with (default) muted. Click a header to sort; Shift-click adds a second sort key and the headers number the keys.
  • The buttons stand in fixed columns: Archive, Delete and More. Delete is off until a subscription is archived; point at it for why. Archive on almanac turns the row to archived at once, the counts follow, Delete on that row turns on, and a toast offers Undo. Delete on archive asks for the name; with the server set to Refuses (section 7) the row stays and the refusal is said.
  • Filled / Loading / Empty / Failed keep the table’s footprint: the loading card over the dimmed rows, the empty note “Nothing has polled this topic yet”, the alert with Try again. Rows, Filter look and Filter picks are the table’s and the filter’s own options. At 390 px every row is a card and the buttons keep their order.

Check that the action buttons line up in three columns through all five rows (a row without Archive or Delete still leaves its slot), that numbers are right-aligned and exact, and that the filter counts change when a row is archived or deleted.

Archive without asking, with Undo and a clock

Archiving stops delivery but keeps the history, so kyu does not ask first: it does it, says so, and offers Undo for a few seconds with a visible clock.

Expected:

  • Press Archive on a row: the row turns to archived at once and a toast almanac archived appears in the box under the list with Undo and the clock (a ring by default). The toast stops its clock while the pointer is on it. Undo, or Ctrl+Z, puts the row back and the toast says Restored.
  • Let the clock run out: the toast leaves and the archive is committed. With the server on Refuses the toast turns into “Not done: …” and the row comes back by itself.
  • Clock (ring, rule, seconds in words, none), Undo is offered for (3, 6, 10 s) and The archive happens (after the window, or at once with Undo reversing it) apply to the next toast. Put back returns the rows.

Check that the toast is the theme’s plate, that the clock empties smoothly without moving Undo, and that the row and the toast agree at every moment (archived while counting, back after Undo).

Delete: type the name first

Deleting an archived subscription removes its delivery history for good, so the button stays off until its exact name is typed, and the server can still refuse.

Expected:

  • The card names what will be lost (history of archive), has a field and a line that says whether the name matches; Delete subscription stays off until you type archive. A wrong name (“Archive”) keeps it off and says why; Any capitals lets it pass.
  • Press Delete with the right name: the subscription disappears from the list at once and the toast says so. With the server on Refuses the list keeps it and a red toast and the line under the card give the reason in plain words.
  • As a dialog: Shown opens the same question the table’s Delete button opens, in a dialog whose field has the focus; Cancel or Esc closes it.

Check that the match line changes the moment the typed text equals the name, that the off button is plainly off with the reason in reach, and that the dialog stands on an opaque plate.
