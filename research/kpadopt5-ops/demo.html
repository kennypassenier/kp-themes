The offline and reconnecting banner

.kp-connection with js/connection.js: a band that says the connection is gone, how many changes wait, that it is reconnecting, and that it is back. It is not “the data is old”.

Expected: changed since round 1: the banner stands at the foot of its window by default; “under the navigation bar” is the option data-kp-placement="top". Both are shown here.

  • Four mock pages: the first two stand at the foot (the default), the last two under the navigation bar (the option); each pair has one at full width and one at 390 px. Online, the banner takes no room in any of them.
  • Lose the connection (after 1.5 s): at the foot the band slides in over the last lines of its page, under the bar it pushes the content down. It says You are offline, the exact count of waiting changes and “next try in 3 s”, with Retry now; then Reconnecting…, Still offline, Server refuses, and Come back (a green Connected again for 3 s, then it leaves). A one-second drop shows nothing.

The theme's own alert band: a red-ink entry for offline and refused (paper ground, a rule along the start edge), a ruled-in arrival and a ruled-off leave, the spinner formal's double ring.

A transfer with its speed and the time left

.kp-progress-group--transfer with js/transfer.js: a bar with the file name, how much is done, the speed and the time left. The app reports bytes and moments; the package works out the rest.

Expected: changed since round 1: the second bar (the one with no known size) no longer loops from the start and then jumps to full; it is one full band that breathes slowly in place, and when its size is known it settles into the finished bar without a jump. The first and third rows are as before.

  • Press Play once (about 12 s, the clock runs at five times speed): the first fills at about 12 MB/s, the second says “so far” and breathes, finishes on its own at about 6 s, the third stalls and fails with Retry. Reset clears all three.

The bar is formal's navy rule ruled to the share (the second rule only at 100 %); numbers are Instrument Sans tabular; the stalled speed is a warning plate, the finished time a green plate, the failure a red-ink entry along its start edge.

The notification centre

.kp-notices with js/notices.js: a counter with the exact number, a panel with the newest notices and the same list as an Inbox page, with a switch per source and a quiet time in plain words.

Expected: changed since round 1: Mark read / Mark unread is the first button of every row, in one column under each other whether the notice has a Fix button or not; Fix is the second. The rest is as before.

  • Press Inbox (the panel) and look at the Inbox page below: the Mark buttons stand at the same distance from the left of the text in every row. Press one: the row turns read (the button says “Mark unread”) and the button does not move.

Formal's raised paper plate for the panel, the red-ink mark for a critical notice, navy dot for unread, the badge a small engraved plate; the empty plate with its ledger lines and the failed plate a red-ink entry, both large and centred.
