The fifth adoption round: the operations components

Seven parts a dashboard needs and today draws itself: a page frame, a list and its detail, an automatic refresh, a connection banner, a transfer with its speed, a question that waits for an answer and a notification centre. Formal only, one topic per component in the review dialog.

Every section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. Nothing moves until you press a button.

The page frame of a dashboard

.kp-ops, CSS only: the header, the key figures, the attention band, the toolbar and a grid of cards in one fixed order, with the spacing and the 12-column spans written once.

Expected:

  • One page in a frame that scrolls: the header (the title Pump houses, the freshness line “updated … ago” right after it, a one-sentence description, the buttons at the right), a strip of four key figures of one height, one warning in the attention band, the toolbar (search and filter chips) and a grid of cards: 8 + 4, 6 + 6, 4 + 4 + 4 and 3 + 3 + 3 + 3. Cards in a row share one height; the gaps are the same everywhere.
  • Scroll inside the frame: the toolbar stays under the top edge (the default). Sticky part moves that to the header or turns it off. Attention band shows the warning or takes it away: with none, the band takes no room at all, no “all well” line.
  • Loading shows the whole page waiting at its final size from the first frame: the key figures as ruled lines, every card with formal’s busy card in its middle. Page failed puts one red entry in the attention band with Retry and the cards keep their place and their data. Ready brings it all back.
  • Page width switches the rule between 80 % of the window (never under 64 rem, the default), the whole width and a reading measure (64 rem). The frame is narrower than every one of them, so the readout beside the buttons says the widest the page may be at this window. Density tightens the gaps.
  • The phone frame (390 px) is the same page folded: every card on its own row, the key figures two to a row, only the primary button in the header and the rest behind More.

Ledger paper: cards ruled with hairlines, the toolbar a ruled band that stays put, the loading as ledger lines ruled across the key figures and the busy card in each card, the failed page one red-ink entry along its start edge.

A list and its detail that folds on a phone

.kp-split--panes with js/master-detail.js: a list, a detail and an optional side panel next to each other; on a narrow screen one pane at a time, with a Back button.

Expected:

  • Three panes side by side in a ruled frame: the list of secrets (17 rem), the detail (the rest) and, when the page is wide enough, a History panel (18 rem). Nothing is picked yet, so the detail is an empty plate that says Pick an item on the left large, in the middle of the whole pane, with the list still usable.
  • Press an item, or move with the up and down arrow keys (the focus stays in the list, Home and End jump): the detail fills for the item you are on after a short load. The picked row has a navy bar along its start. A long name wraps, it is never cut.
  • Loading lays formal's busy card over the detail pane only; the list stays usable. Failed fills the pane with the red-ink plate, the sentence large, and a normal-size Retry that loads the item again. Nothing picked brings the empty plate back. The pane keeps its size in every state.
  • The phone frame (390 px) shows the same layout folded: the list alone. Press an item: the detail arrives (the theme's way) with Back to the list at the top and the focus on the heading. Back, the browser's Back, or Esc returns to the list at the same scroll with the same row picked and the focus on it. What a phone shows switches between one pane (the default), a sheet that slides over the list and everything stacked.
  • The log shows each select, open, close and retry event.

Ledger paper with the faint rules between the panes; the picked row carries the navy rule along its start, the empty and failed plates are the theme's own (dashed paper, red-ink entry) set large in the middle.
