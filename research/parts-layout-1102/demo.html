The More menu and the coupled wheels, round 2

Round 2 of the layout options and new parts from the apps: the two you sent back. Kitchen’s More menu now opens above the second bar at full width, and the side dish wheel no longer turns with the main course: it waits, locked, until the main course has stopped, and the app decides whether it then waits for a press (the default) or starts by itself. The other six sections are approved and leave this page. Formal only, on the apps’ own data (names are mock). 2 sections, 2 pairs.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. The width control shows the stage at a phone’s 390 px.

Kitchen and Medicine Cabinet

Kitchen’s bar: five daily places and More. Medicine Cabinet’s bar: Stock opens a menu.

What is asked (nav-more, Kitchen (chassis-rs 25) and Medicine Cabinet 28): Kitchen built its own More item for six pages that always live there; Medicine Cabinet dressed a menu button as a bar link by hand. data-kp-nav-more on a list item pins it behind the folding bar’s More whatever the width (a value names its group, data-kp-nav-hint its hint); a .kp-menu-button in .kp-nav__links is now a documented bar link with a rich menu.

Expected:

  • Changed since round 1: At full width the More menu opened under Medicine Cabinet’s bar, which painted over Wheel and Timers. Every bar is a layer of its own (z-index: 30), so the later bar won whatever the menu’s own z-index. A menu button’s menu inside such a layer (a positioned box with a z-index, or a sticky or fixed one) now opens on the top layer, like the date picker and the time list: above every bar and sticky element, on any page. Measured at 1280 px: 4 of 6 entries reachable before, 6 of 6 now.
  • Open More at full width: the whole menu lies over Medicine Cabinet’s bar, every entry can be pressed, and it still hangs under More. The same for Stock.
  • Kitchen shows Today, Week, Shopping list 7, Recipes, Pantry and More, at every width where the bar is a row. More opens Wheel, Timers, Freezer stock and Saved menus, each with its hint, then “SETTINGS” over Household and Settings. Current: Timers: More reads as the current link and Timers is marked in the menu. A pick moves the current page; the line names it.
  • Medicine Cabinet’s Stock is a bar link with a caret; it opens the rich menu under it. Phone: both bars fold into their toggle; Kitchen’s open list holds all eleven links.

Formal: the bar’s links and the menu’s plate. Check that More and Stock look like the other links and that the menus hang under them.

What do we eat tonight

Six main courses; the side dishes that fit the main course chosen.

What is asked (coupled-wheels, Kitchen 15): Kitchen spun the side dish from the main course’s spin event by hand. data-kp-wheel-follows="<id>" on a wheel makes it follow the wheel it names; data-kp-wheel-follows-start lets the app say how.

Expected:

  • Changed since round 1: You sent back the side dish turning with the main course, because it spoiled the outcome. Now the app decides, and the default waits: the side dish is locked, its Spin dimmed and “Spins after the main course” where its answer goes, until the main course has stopped. Then it hears the main course’s pick (Kitchen gives it the sides that go with it) and, by the option, waits for its own Spin (the default) or starts by itself. Spinning together stays as a third option for two picks that do not depend on each other.
  • Before the main course: the side dish is dimmed, its Spin does nothing and its line says “Spins after the main course”. Spin the main course: the side dish stays still until the main course stops; then its disc shows only the sides that fit (Lasagne: Green salad and Bread) and the line names the pick.
  • Press (the default): the side dish waits for its own Spin. Auto: it starts by itself right after the main course stops, never before. Together: the earlier behaviour, both turn and settle together. Main course again locks the side dish again and clears its answer. Phone: the wheels stack, both whole.

Formal: the package’s wheels. Check that the side dish reads as waiting, not broken, and that it never moves before the main course has stopped.
