Dashboard tiles of their own, per theme: one pick per aspect

The character round's next component, with one choice per aspect from the start (Kenny, 2026-10-05: "keep these things separate so I could choose one over another … it should be like this in the future"). Per theme you pick the shape, the loading picture, how the tiles arrive, the tone, the pointer and the keyboard focus, and the live update, each from three options and each on its own. Nothing is ticked for you.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: every loading picture shows its still frame and the tiles arrive at once. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Every row holds two tiles of a water board: pump house 1 and reservoir North. The tiles are the package's own (.kp-tiles of .kp-card, one board per row through attachTileSets() in js/tiles.js); an option only restyles them from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (tiles.css), one attribute per aspect, and keeps every tile's height in every state.
