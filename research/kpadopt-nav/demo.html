The folding top bar in phantom, still open

Round 5. Kenny approved the folding bar in cyberpunk and grotesk and the shell in grotesk, so only phantom’s folding bar is left. Its opened More menu had no plate in the review dialog: phantom makes a list inside a dialog bare, and the review dialog is a dialog. The More menu now keeps phantom’s own plate there too: the dark ground, the red edge, the cut corner and its red fold, the same as phantom’s menus everywhere else. Everything inside the frames is the package’s own (kp-nav with data-kp-nav-fold, the menu button); the frames and the slider are this demo’s. Judge it in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top).

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the marks and menus appear without moving. Turn it off to judge the movement.

1 · Top bar

What does not fit goes behind More, and the bar never changes height.

data-kp-nav-fold on the nav. Nothing else is written by hand: the More button and its menu are added by the script, and the menu is the package’s menu button.

Expected:

  • Drag the slider above the first bar: the links that do not fit leave the bar from the end and are listed behind a More button; the bar stays one row tall at every width (at 18rem the brand gives way to an ellipsis before anything wraps), and nothing jumps when a link folds. The More button is always inside the bar, after the links that stay and before Search.
  • Press More (or the button under the “More opened” bar): a menu hangs from its left edge (its right edge in the right-to-left bar), straight under the button, wherever it is on the page, with the folded links; picking an entry follows its link, Escape closes the menu and the focus is back on More.
  • The opened menu stands on phantom’s own plate, on the page and in the review dialog alike: the dark ground, the red edge on its left, the cut corner and its red fold, a degree and a half askew; nothing of the page reads through it.
  • The three-link bar at 26rem has no More at all. The bar that keeps Settings (data-kp-nav-keep) keeps it however narrow it gets.
  • Tab through a bar: the links that stay, then More, then Search. Folded links are skipped.
