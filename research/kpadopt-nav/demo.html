A folding top bar, a bottom tab bar and the running-job button

Three things the homelab dashboard still draws by hand, built into the package so it can draw nothing itself. Everything inside the frames is the package’s own classes and scripts (kp-nav with data-kp-nav-fold, kp-tabbar, kp-button--live, the menu button and the drawer); the frames, the slider, the mock page behind the phones and the buttons that play a state are this demo’s. Judge them in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top), one theme at a time.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the marks and menus appear without moving. Turn it off to judge the movement.

2 · Bottom tab bar

Four areas within thumb reach, then More.

nav.kp-tabbar of .kp-tabbar__tabs (icon over label, a count at the icon’s corner). The More tab is a data-kp-dialog button that opens the package’s drawer. Shown here inside phone frames with kp-tabbar--always kp-tabbar--static; the section below shows the real, fixed bar in a window of its own.

Expected:

  • Five tabs sit at the foot of each phone: Home, Needs you, Overview, Activity and More, each with an icon over its label. The current tab is marked the way the theme marks it (see the look-at line).
  • Press Home, Needs you, Overview or Activity in the first phone: the mark moves to the tab you pressed. (A busy or disabled tab does not take the mark.)
  • The buttons under the phones act on the first phone: Add a count raises the number on Needs you by one; Clear the count removes it; Toggle busy swaps the icon of Overview for the theme’s spinner and back, the tab keeps its size; Show keyboard focus draws the focus ring on Activity.

  • The panel closes four ways: the ×, Escape, a press on the dimmed strip, or a press on one of its entries (Backups and System also follow their link). Open it again and the same four entries are there. After it closes the focus is back on the More tab.
