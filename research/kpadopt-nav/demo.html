The folding top bar and the shell, still open

Round 4. Kenny approved the bottom tab bar and the running-job button in every theme, and the folding bar and the shell in every other theme, so only what is still open is on this page: the folding bar in cyberpunk, phantom and grotesk, and the shell in three windows in grotesk. Everything inside the frames is the package’s own classes and scripts (kp-nav with data-kp-nav-fold, the tab bar and running-job button inside the shell windows, the menu button and the drawer); the frames, the slider and the mock page are this demo’s. Judge the four in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top).

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the marks and menus appear without moving. Turn it off to judge the movement.

1 · Top bar

What does not fit goes behind More, and the bar never changes height.

data-kp-nav-fold on the nav. Nothing else is written by hand: the More button and its menu are added by the script, and the menu is the package’s menu button.

Expected:

  • Drag the slider above the first bar: the links that do not fit leave the bar from the end and are listed behind a More button; the bar stays one row tall at every width (at 18rem the brand gives way to an ellipsis before anything wraps), and nothing jumps when a link folds. The More button is always inside the bar, after the links that stay and before Search.
  • Press More (or the button under the “More opened” bar): a menu hangs from its left edge (its right edge in the right-to-left bar), straight under the button, wherever it is on the page, with the folded links; picking an entry follows its link, Escape closes the menu and the focus is back on More.
  • The three-link bar at 26rem has no More at all. The bar that keeps Settings (data-kp-nav-keep) keeps it however narrow it gets.
  • Tab through a bar: the links that stay, then More, then Search. Folded links are skipped.

2 · Together

The dashboard’s shell in three real windows.

Three pages in frames, each a window of its own width: the media queries answer to the frame, so this is what a phone, a tablet and a desktop get. The mock page behind is the demo’s; the top bar, the tab bar, the running-job button, the menu button and the drawer are the package’s.

Expected:

  • Three real pages in three windows. Phone (22.5rem): brand, search, the job button (“Running”) at the top, the four areas and More in a tab bar at the foot, the last line of the page clear of the bar. Tablet (48rem): the same tab bar. Desktop (70rem): all links in the top bar, no tab bar.
  • Press More in the phone or tablet window: its panel opens inside that window (not over this page) and closes with its ×, Escape, a press on the strip beside it or a press on an entry.
  • Nothing in the three windows should overlap, cut or shift when you press the controls.
