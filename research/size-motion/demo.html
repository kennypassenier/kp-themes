Dialog close

The dialog leaves the way it came: it sinks the same 14 px it rose and fades, a little faster than it opened.

Open it and close it three ways: Cancel, Escape, and a click on the backdrop. Each close should play the same short exit, and the page behind should never flash. Then compare with Package today, where it vanishes at once.

Dialog that grows and shrinks

The admin dashboard's case: the dialog opens, then rows arrive and the box jumps. In the proposal the box eases to its new height, whether rows arrive or leave, one at a time or five at once.

Open the dialog, then use Load five rows, Add a row, Remove a row and Clear inside it. The box should glide to each new height in both directions, growing out from its middle as a centred dialog does, and a second click during a glide should continue from where the box is, not jump.

A card whose content changes

Any box on a page: an alert that appears, a list that fills after loading, a form that shows its errors. The card eases to its new height, and what sits under it slides along instead of jumping.

Use the buttons on both cards. The left card follows the proposal, the right one is the package today. Watch the line under each card: on the left it should slide, on the right it jumps.

            Where else this applies

            Searched the package's scripts for every place that changes a box after it is drawn (.hidden =, .remove(), replaceChildren, append( in js/*.js). js/motion.js eases these too, wherever js/auto.js runs, and any box marked data-kp-size-motion:

            • Data table: rows arriving after the busy state, a row expanding to its detail, a filter emptying the list.
            • Toasts: the stack closing up when one toast leaves.
            • Combobox and select list: the list shrinking as you type a filter.
            • Upload: the file list growing per file and shrinking on remove.
            • Field errors: the error line appearing under a field and leaving again.
            • Tree: a branch folding open and closed.
            • Wizard: one step swapped for the next, of another height.

            Not included: menus and tooltips, which open in one piece and already have their own entrance.
