One register at a time

The shared stylesheets are linked once. The register of the active theme arrives when the theme does, and nothing another theme needs is fetched. The scripts follow the same rule: a module comes only when the page has something for it.

Pick a theme

Each choice fetches that theme's register once and holds the switch until it has arrived; a theme already fetched costs nothing more. The choice is stored, so a reload paints it before anything else.
