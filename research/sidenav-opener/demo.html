The side navigation chooses its opener

One attribute on the panel, data-kp-sidenav-opener="launcher" | "tab" | "both", says whether an over or a push panel (with or without a gutter) is opened from the fixed launcher, from the edge tab, or from both, as it is today. The page below holds both elements in every window; the left window asks for the launcher, the right one for the tab. The element that is not asked for is not drawn and not reachable by keyboard, and the panel’s header and the page title take back the room kept for the launcher. A side panel and the collapsed rail ignore the attribute. Nothing plays until "Play once" is pressed; each theme keeps its own opening and closing, the close being the opening backwards. Judge them in the review dialog ("Review in a dialog" at the top), one theme at a time.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the panels appear and disappear without their effects. Turn it off to judge the movement.
