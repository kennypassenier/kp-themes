HTTPSwitchboard: the profiles view

HTTPSwitchboard takes a message in one shape and delivers it in another. Each profile is one such route: where the message comes from, how it is rewritten, where it goes. This page shows how the profiles part of its status page would look with kp-themes 11.0.0: the main view (a map or cards, you set the default), the path of one profile, and the card that opens on a profile name.

Mock on this page: the sample data. The profile alertmanager follows the real configuration (kyu topic alerts.raw, subscription switchboard, Home Assistant as the destination); uptime-kuma is an invented second profile. The webhook address is a credential and is never shown. Judge in the review dialog; nothing moves until you press a button.

The profiles view: a map or cards

Shows every profile and whether it is working, as a map of what feeds what or as one card per profile; the same data, two looks.

Expected:

  • Cards (.kp-entity-grid): alertmanager and uptime-kuma, each with its state in words, the figures Delivered, Failed and Average, flags for the source kind and the destination kind (and the problem, when there is one), a meter with the share delivered, and the buttons Recheck this profile and Copy test command. Both cards are the same height in every health state.
  • Map (.kp-uses): the sources on the left (kyu topic alerts.raw, POST /uptime-kuma), the destinations on the right, one line per profile. A profile still starting has a dashed line; a node that is refused or failing is toned. Hover or focus a node: its line lights and the rest dims; press it to pin.
  • Health (alertmanager; uptime-kuma has no hub, so it only follows Starting and Failing): Working, Starting (nothing tried yet), Failing (deliveries fail, messages wait on the hub), Denied (the hub refused our credentials), Hub down (the hub cannot be reached). The state word, the flags and the tones change; the cards do not change size.
  • Data: Loading lays the busy card over the whole view; Empty and Error fill the same footprint with a plate and a normal-size action (Reload profiles, Retry).
  • Recheck this profile puts that one profile back to Starting (the page's own log under the cards says what was pressed). Pressing a Health button clears it again.
  • The choice (tick in the dialog): which view opens first. Both are real options, the buttons below flip them, and nothing is required.

White cards with a hairline, navy for the primary and the pinned line; state words in plain ink with a coloured mark, never colour alone. Look at the figures (a gap between the number and its label), the flags (no text touching the edge of its plate), the meter, and that the two buttons stand in the same place on both cards. On the map: the dashed line of a starting profile, the toned node where it stops, and the long sub lines wrapping inside their nodes.

The path of one profile, and where it stops

Follows one message from its sender to Home Assistant and stops at the first hop that refuses it, saying which one and why.

Expected:

  • Four hops in a row: the sender, the door (the kyu topic for alertmanager, POST /uptime-kuma for uptime-kuma), the translation, and Home Assistant. A summary line above them says the outcome in a sentence.
  • Working: every hop is allowed. Starting: nothing is measured yet, every hop says “Not measured”. Failing: Home Assistant refuses (the receiver fails), the summary names it. Denied: the hub refused our credentials, so the kyu hop fails and the two after it read “Not reached”. Hub down: the same place, another reason. uptime-kuma has no hub, so Denied and Hub down leave it working.
  • The refusing rule is a chip you can press (the line below the trace says what was pressed); Copy copies the trace as text.
  • Loading lays the busy card over placeholder hops; Empty and Error fill the same height with a plate; Retry loads it again.

The allowed hops carry a check, the refused one a cross, the skipped ones are dimmed; the mark and the word are both there. Look at the gap between the hop's note and the edge of its plate and that the trace keeps its height in every state.

The card on a profile name

Opens the full shape of a profile (where it comes from, where it goes, what it carries) when you rest on its name, without leaving the page.

Expected:

  • Rest the pointer on a profile name (here, and on the card names in the section above): after a short pause a card opens under it with Source, Destination (the host only, never the webhook path), Content type, Template, Received, Delivered, Failed and Last success (dd/mm/yyyy HH:MM). Focus with the keyboard opens it at once; Escape closes it; the pointer may rest on the card.
  • The buttons Show the card open it without a pointer, so the opened state is on screen. The card is the opaque plate of the theme, readable wherever it stands.
  • Health changes the status row and the counters in the card the next time it opens.

An opaque white plate with a hairline and formal's soft shadow, the labels in one column as wide as the longest label, and no text against the plate's edge. The name's row keeps its place with the card open or closed.
