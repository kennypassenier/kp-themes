Week agenda: loading, empty, error, focus

The three states of the whole agenda, each at the agenda’s final size, and a pill that has the keyboard focus.

Expected:

  • Loading: the week loads as the data table loads. The days, the hours and the lines are all there and stay empty; in the middle of the days stands the same card as a loading table shows: formal’s large ring spinner, the words “Reading the week” and the navy rules ruled under them. Nothing else is drawn in the days, and the card covers neither the day heads nor the hours. It is the card of the data table’s “Loading, with a layer over the rows”.
  • Empty: the same grid, the sentence “Nothing is planned in this week.” over it.
  • Error: the grid, with the page’s own words in the destructive colour over it.
  • Focus: the third agenda has its first pill focused as a keyboard user would see it; the ring must be whole, not cut by the pill’s corners, and clearly visible against the neighbours.
  • Make it load, then arrive (above the first agenda): the button shows the theme’s spinner and says the items arrive in 2 s; the first agenda’s items leave at once and the loading card arrives in the middle as it does over a table. After two seconds the card is ruled off first, then the items arrive the theme’s way, one after the other (formal rules them in); no item is ever drawn under the card. No column changes height and no head moves, here and in the 390 px view (where a day keeps its height while it loads again); the button can play it again, as often as you like.

Press Make it load, then arrive three times in a row and watch the first agenda: an empty week with the table’s loading card in the middle, then its items, never both. Ledger entries: a heavy 4px rule on the left of each pill, 2px corners, the weekday in small capitals, dotted dividers between the days like leaders.
