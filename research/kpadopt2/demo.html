Round 5 of the dashboard adoption: the eight components in every theme

Review round 5. Kenny approved the eight components in formal (rounds 1 to 4): the action tiles, the status rows, the facet list, the heat grid, the matrix, the bar in a table cell, the week agenda with its states and the run timeline. This round asks the fifteen other themes, each built from that theme’s own approved parts: its buttons, segmented track, menus, loading picture, arrival and signature motion. Formal is asked again only for the heat grid, the matrix, the bar cell and the run timeline, each with one repair to judge. Every section says what you should see under Expected, and the review dialog’s “Look at” line names what is this theme’s own. Everything with a kp- class and every script under js/ is the package; the sample data and the buttons that drive a demo are this page’s.

Hover, focus and press rows are drawn by copying the package’s own rules (the page reads the stylesheets and re-writes :hover as a class), so they show the theme’s real look. That needs the page to be served over http (as the catalogue does); opened as a file, those rows show only the plain tile.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so hover and press change the look but nothing moves or fades.

1 · Action tiles

A button or a link drawn as a tile: a title and the one sentence that says what it does, in a grid whose tiles on one row are all as tall as the tallest. Use it where a short list of things to do (or places to go) needs a sentence each, on a landing page or in a drawer. A button tile does something; a link tile goes somewhere (and shows a chevron).

Light’s card: the hairline, the small shadow, a 0.5 rem corner. Point at a tile: it settles 2 px toward the paper and its shadow tightens; a press lands it flat and flashes it bright for a moment. The destructive tile carries a 3 px red band along its top and a red title, no red frame and no side bar. Busy shows light’s bead spinner.

2 · Status rows

One row per item that is read or checked one by one: a mark, the item’s name, a short detail and, when it failed, a Retry that tries that one item again. The four states are each a shape and a word as well as a colour: a disc with a tick (ok), the theme’s spinner (loading), a disc with a cross (failed, with its reason and a Retry), a dashed ring (empty, with the reason there is nothing).

Light’s cards in a grid. A failed row is a white card with a 3 px red band along its top and Retry as light’s pill, no red frame; an empty row is the bare hairline on the paper, no shadow; loading shows light’s bead spinner.

Machined rows on the small cut, square marks, the detail in the ticker mono. A failed row is the coral edge; a loading row is crossed by dark’s comet (the line and its film tail), again and again.

Square read-outs cut at the end corner, the detail in mono. A failed row is locked as a target: red corner brackets that twitch twice and hold. Loading shows cyberpunk’s signal bars.

Square tape rows with a 2 px stripe on top; the detail in cyan OSD letters. A failed row has a red stripe and the stop square before its name; a loading row’s top edge is a marquee, a lit bulb chasing along it.

White paper rows with the sky second pass beside them. A failed row’s reason is a red sticker with its peel; an empty row is a dashed outline without a pass; marks stand upright. Loading shows the caterpillar circling its day sticker.

Log lines, all mono, a dashed rule at the start: + ok, x failed, - empty. A failed row says [fail] in red before its name; a loading row has the braille spinner and the block cursor waiting after its reading.

Kraft rows with the small leaf corner, italic notes under the names. A failed row’s reason is a field note, a square red tag; an empty row is dashed; a loading row plants a tree in its mark.

Rows lit faintly from below. A failed row’s sky reddens; an empty row is dashed and unlit; a loading row has the sun’s arc in its mark and a small sun crossing along its foot.

Slabs on hard shadows, square marks with a heavy ring, names in bold capitals. A failed row is taped off along its foot; a loading row has the muted ground and the tipping block; an empty row is a dashed line without a shadow.

Cut-paper rows with a heavy edge, names in condensed italic capitals. A failed row’s name is stamped on the red plate and its edge is red; a loading row has the red edge and the snapping star.

Lines of a timetable under a 2 px rule, no box, bold names in sentence case. A failed row is indexed by the red bar down its start; an empty row’s rule is dashed. No row wraps where one line fits.

3 · Facet list

Checkbox rows that narrow a list by one property, each with how many items it would show, and an only button that selects just that row. “Everything” turns every row on. The checkboxes are the theme’s own; only is the theme’s ghost button, a sibling of the label (never inside it), shown when the pointer is on the row or focus is in it, and always on a touch screen.

Pill rows that take the menu’s muted wash. Press “Counts are loading”: each count is a soft pill out of focus, breathing between dim and dazzling (light’s loading), and comes back sharp with “Counts arrive”.
