Entity card: a trend line and an app tile

The admin dashboard still draws two things next to the entity card by hand: the 24-hour CPU line on each stack card, and the tile of the Apps page (one link, a favourite star, a description, a few lines). Both are now options of the entity card, shown here in formal only. Judge them in the review dialog: three sections, one step each. The stacks, the readings and the log lines are sample data of this page (mock); the cards, the line, the star and every state are the package.

Stack card with a trend line

Each stack card gets one more row under its meters: a label, the last 24 hours as a line, and the current value. It is the same line the key-figure tiles draw. The row is there on every card as soon as one card has readings, so the cards stay level.

Expected:

  • Four stack cards (jellyfin, sonarr, traefik, paperless), each with RAM and disk meters and, under them, CPU 24 h with a line over a soft area and its value (34%) at the end of the row, on the same columns as the meters. All four cards are exactly as high as each other.
  • Trend line: Hidden takes the row away on every card at once; A full day brings it back. Sonarr has one reading keeps sonarr’s row and says no line yet in the same box, since a line needs two readings. Live: Add a reading draws every line again from one more number; the cards are the same elements, so nothing moves.
  • State: Loading shows skeleton cards under the busy card, Not measured says so in the figures and the trend row, Failed turns each card destructive with a Retry where the buttons were. The card keeps its height in each state.

Formal’s card is a page of the ledger: the identity row is ruled off, the rows under it share their columns. Look at the line: does it read as a quiet 24-hour shape beside the meters, is its value legible at the row end, and does the row sit on the meters’ columns without making the card feel crowded?

App tile: one link, a favourite star

The Apps page as a board of tiles. The whole tile opens the app; the star in its corner marks a favourite and never opens the link. A tile holds the mark, the name, the state in a dot and a word, a description and a few facts at the foot.

Expected:

  • Eight tiles, favourites first (Jellyfin and Traefik start starred), every tile exactly as high as the tallest. Each has the mark made from its name, the name, a hollow star (a filled star in a dark square once pressed), the state as a dot and a word (up, degraded, starting, down), the description, and under it the lines: a count and the last log line. The lines of every tile in a row stand on one line at the foot.
  • Click anywhere on a tile (not the star): the readout says the app opened and the page stays (this page is a mock, it does not leave). Press the star, or the key S while the tile has the focus: the star flips, the readout says so, and the link did not open. Tab goes tile by tile (one stop each) and then to the next star.
  • Opens in: A new tab adds a small ↗ before the star and tells a screen reader. Favourites sets the stars in the data (the board is drawn again in place). Star button: Hidden takes the star away from every tile.

Formal reads as a register of services. Look at the tile as one object: is it clear that all of it is a link, is the star clearly separate and clearly pressed or not, and do the lines at the foot make the board feel like a grid? The hovered tile and the tile with the focus each show their own edge.

App tile: states and width

What the board does while the apps are read, when nothing is known, when one app cannot be read and when all of them fail, and how it folds on a phone.

Expected:

  • Loading: six skeleton tiles with the final geometry, under the busy card. Not measured: every tile keeps its name, state and description, and its lines row says so. One tile failed: Sonarr’s state turns to a red dot and failed, and its description and lines are replaced by reading failed and a Retry of normal size; the board does not change height. The mock log line of the failure sits in the Retry’s tooltip.
  • Grid failed and No apps are the package’s state plates, centred and at heading size, filling the board’s footprint, with a normal Retry or Add button.
  • Width: Phone narrows the board to one column of 20 rem: nothing is cut, long words wrap, the star stays reachable.

Check each state against the loaded board: same frame, same height, nothing jumping. In the failed tile the Retry must be a normal button and the sentence must be legible, with air on every side of its text.
