A network graph of its own, per theme

Kenny, form v18 (2026-10-05): the character round, one component at a time, all 22 themes in one demo. After the meter, the time chart and the month heatmap comes the network graph. Today .kp-graph has the same shape in every theme (a hub in the middle, the other sites on a ring, round nodes with a ring and a core in their own hue, links by kind in a colour and a dash, labels with a halo, the kinds as pills above the picture), drawn in the theme's colours only. Here every theme gets two graphs of its own, drawn in the theme's own material, beside the plain graph as it is today. Pick one of the three per theme.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: the loading picture is a still frame. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Each column is the northern water network: a control centre, six pump houses (number 6 still being built, number 5 set differently on site than in the plan), two reservoirs, a treatment plant, and two addresses outside the network (the weather service and the energy supplier). Telemetry, remote control, the radio spare path and the planned links each have their kind. Every graph is the package's own (js/graph.js draws it, so hovering, picking, the arrow keys, the kinds and Show all work); a character only restyles it from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (graphs.css), and keeps the picture's height in every state.
