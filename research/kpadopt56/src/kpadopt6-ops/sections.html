  • Filters: four pills All 138, Error 12, Warning 31, Info 95. All starts on. Press Error: All goes off and Error goes on; press Warning as well: both are on and the list below says what is shown. Press All (or Clear filters (2) at the end of the row): everything else goes off and All is on again.   • A one-of switch: Day, Week, Month; exactly one is on, pressing the one that is on does nothing, and the arrow keys walk along the three (Tab enters the group once). Single or none lets the on one be pressed off.
  • Every press says one polite line below the row (read by a screen reader, shown here in the grey box). The count is the real number, never “9+”; the next section feeds a count past 99 and a count that is still being counted.
  • Not new, and what is new: the package already has the segmented control (js/segmented.js), shown under the three cases above with the same filters and the same one-of switch. A group of choices is that part’s job, and a toggle group looks and answers the same way.   • The choice (tick it in the dialog): whether a pressed toggle also shows a tick. The buttons below flip it too.

A pressed toggle is formal’s ink with the paper-coloured words and the double rule drawn inside, as a held press looks in formal; the pills are formal’s own, with its soft corners, and the segmented chips beside them are the same pills.
