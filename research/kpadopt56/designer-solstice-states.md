state: component height, the state plate's size and centre against the component's centre, the plate's heading size, the
Retry / action button's size, the busy card (present, centred, animating). Screenshots of every state were read per theme.

7. **Inline loading without a busy card** (slider, time-field, version badge, sentence, ack): a skeleton of its own, no busy card
   (`busy 0`); by design of those parts, listed for completeness.
8. **Tall components of their own at 390:** the claim-row list at 1621 px, ops-page 3226 px, payload-cell 1874 px: the states cannot
   fill that without a `min-block-size` from the loaded state (see 1).
9. Theme-side content differences between themes in the loaded heights (brutalism claim-row 1712 against 1621 in formal, deco
   ops-page 3269) come from each register's own row padding and are not faults.
