Feedback components for the admin dashboard

Three things the dashboard still draws itself: a toast that waits while it is being read, a live list of the steps of a run, and the @@ header of a diff hunk. All three are the package's own now: toast() in js/overlays.js (new options), .kp-steplist with js/steplist.js (new), and .kp-diff__hunk in css/components.css (new row).

Mock on this page: everything prefixed kf-, the sample data (a water company's pump houses and an update of its dashboard) and the scripted run. Package: every kp- class and the three scripts. Each section also shows the component in a 360 px column.

A toast that waits while it is read

toast(content, { pause, linger, close, sticky }). While the pointer is on a toast or focus is in it the clock stops ( data-kp-paused, event kp-toast-pause); linger ms after both are gone (default 2 s) it leaves. close: true adds the close button; sticky: true is "stay until closed" (no clock, close button). Esc on a focused toast closes it. A touch does not count as hovering. Without the new options a toast behaves as before, except that it now waits (set pause: false for the old timer).

The readout under the buttons says when the clock stopped and started. The steps of a run, live

                    

                        .kp-steplist is a new component, not a variant of the timeline (which is what happened, in the past, and never
                        changes) or of the wizard steps (where a person is in a form, and they move it). Each .kp-steplist__item has
                        data-state of pending, running, done, failed or skipped; js/steplist.js adds the spinner, the spoken
                        state and the status region. setStep(list, 'migrate', { state: 'done', time: '3.1 s' }) changes a step; setting
                        data-state by hand is followed too. Event: kp-steplist-step.

                        and over it from the second hunk on.
