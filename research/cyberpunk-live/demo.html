Cyberpunk’s live update: the chromatic split, six ways

Kenny, 2026-10-07 01:32, on cyberpunk’s live update in research/families: “The glitch effect where one colour goes a bit to the right and up/down and another colour goes to the right and the opposite of the other, with neon colours”. Every option below is that split: the new value is written at once and stays on top, sharp and readable the whole time; behind it two neon copies of it jump to the right, one up and the other down, and come back.

The six differ in what you can see at a glance: how far the copies go, whether they move in hard frames, in slices or in one glide, whether a scan line runs through, which two of cyberpunk’s own neons (cyan, violet, yellow, red: tokens, no new colour), how long, and how it settles. Each option shows every place a value updates live on a dashboard: a key figure and its change, a trend tile, a tile, two columns of a key-figure strip, a state word and a table cell. Only the values that changed split; the others stay still. The first option is the recommendation.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so every option shows its still version: the copies stand two pixels out for a moment and go, and nothing moves. Turn it off in the system settings to see the motion.
