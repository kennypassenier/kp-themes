Six components on a shared line

The new alignment check measures whether the parts of a composition stand on one grid: cells of one role start and end on one edge, and the first line of every cell is on the label’s line. It found six approved components off by 2 to 56 px. 6 pairs, all in formal (none of the faults depends on the theme). Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was off and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: The chip of whoever started a row, and the time at the end of it, stood 2 px higher than the title they belong to: each was centred in its own, smaller line, while the title is set in the larger body size. They now share the title’s baseline, the way a caption stands beside its heading, and the mark-only chip stands in a cell one title line high. The marks, the rail, the day headings, the states and the sizes are as you approved them.

Activity feed: the chip and the time on the title’s line

A day of a homelab’s activity: a mark, the title, who started it and when. Below it the same rows with the actor as a mark only.

Expected:

  • In every row the actor’s name and the time sit on the same line as the title: the bottoms of the letters meet on one line.
  • The chips stand in one column and the times in one column; the second feed shows only the round or square mark, centred on the title’s line.

Formal: the name, the time and the title read on one line in every row; nothing hangs above it.

What changed since you approved it: The row’s button was centred on the whole row, so beside a title with a sentence under it the button hung 21 px below the badge it belongs to. Now the badge, the title’s first line and the buttons stand on one first line, as high as a button, and the sentence runs on below. A row of one line is as tall as before. The badges, the meta line, the rows’ rules and the narrow layout (buttons centred under the text) are as you approved them.

List with a lead: the buttons on the first line

A list whose rows start with a badge, have a title with a sentence under it, and a button at the end.

Expected:

  • In each row the badge, the bold title and the button are on one line; the grey sentence and the meta line are under the title.
  • The buttons stand in one column, one width; at phone width they sit centred under the text as before.

Formal: the badge and the button share one centre line in every row, and the title starts on it.
