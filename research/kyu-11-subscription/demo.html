kyu on kp-themes 11.0.0: the Subscription page

How kyu’s page for one subscription (/t/notify.kenny/dashboard/subs/desktop) looks when it uses the new components to the full: a header with a mark, a state and a freshness line, the policy as a grid, two gauges and a progress bar, the backlog as a data table with bulk delete and Undo, and three new use cases that need kyu backend work.

Everything here is mock data in kyu’s real shape (topic notify.kenny, subscription desktop); nothing is read from a server. Sections marked New: are use cases kyu does not have yet.

The page header: who this is, how it is, how fresh

The title, the subscription’s coloured mark (the same mark as everywhere in kyu), its state as a badge and how long ago it last read a message. .kp-page-header with its heading line, idmark, freshness, the lede and the breadcrumb.

Expected:

  • The mark beside the title is built from the name “desktop”; the small mark in the breadcrumb for “notify.kenny” has a different colour (the two never collide). The badge says active in the success tone; the line “last read … ago” ticks by itself and shows dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm when you hover it.
  • State changes the badge: flagged is warning, archived is plain. Last read “Quiet for 3 days” turns the line into a warning plate without moving anything beside it. Dead letters adds the destructive 1 dead badge to the counts line.
  • The choice “The mark” shows the same mark as a plate (default), a glyph or a pattern. 390 px: the title, badge and freshness wrap under the mark, never under the title’s own words.

Formal is a ledger: ruled lines and navy ink. Check the mark sits on the title’s line, the badge and the freshness line keep their place when the state changes, and no text touches the mark or the edge.

The policy: how this subscription treats a message

Label and value in one grid, with a quiet “(default)” beside every value kyu chose itself. .kp-kv in a .kp-card with its four states.

Expected:

  • Five rows. The labels share one column as wide as the longest label; the values start in the same place. Defaults say (default) in the muted ink; a value somebody set has no aside.
  • Policy “Customised”: lease 60 s and time to live 7 days lose their aside, the others keep it. State Loading: the card’s busy card stands over the grid; Failed: one destructive plate fills the same height with Try again. There is no Empty: every subscription has a policy, even if all of it is default.
  • The choice “Grid layout” sets the lines (default), the stacked or the two-column form.

Check the rule lines between rows are the ledger’s hairlines, the labels are muted and the values navy, and the card keeps its height in all three states.

Is it keeping up? Age against the lease, attempts against the limit, the backlog draining

Two lists of bars with a tick at the limit (bullets) and one progress bar (progressbar), so a glance says which message waits too long, which ones are close to dying and how fast the backlog goes down.

Expected:

  • Oldest unacked message: one bar against the 30 s lease. Healthy is 6 s (no tone); “Over the lease” is 41 s (warning, the figure on the warning plate); “Stuck” is 95 s (destructive, and the ▸ says the bar ran past its end). Attempts: one bar per message against the 5 allowed; the one at 5 of 5 is destructive.
  • List Loading shows the waiting rows under the busy card; Empty says nothing is held; Failed fills the list’s height with Retry. Backlog “Draining” counts the bar down from 312 to 0 once (press it again to restart); “Counting” is the unknown-amount stripe; “Empty” is a full bar with 312 of 312.
  • Choices: the size of the bar (default middle), and whether the rows are sorted worst first (default) or as given.

Check every bar begins and ends in the same place, the tick stands at the limit, tones are never the only carrier (the figure changes plate too) and the progress bar’s head is the double rule’s navy.

The backlog: what this subscription still holds, with bulk delete and Undo

New New: kyu would need a bulk delete endpoint for this (today a message is deleted one at a time). The kp data table with a tick column, a selection bar, a payload cell that lays JSON out, state badges and a toast with Undo.

Expected: New: kyu would need one endpoint that deletes many deliveries of one subscription in one call.

  • Twelve of 312 messages are shown. Payloads are code cells: JSON gets Pretty and Raw, the long one is folded to three lines with Show all 38 lines, binary data says binary, 2.1 kB, a cut one says how much was kept. States are pending (plain) or claimed (warning). Sort by a header; Shift-click adds a second sort.
  • Tick rows (or j/k to move, x to tick, Shift-click for a range): the bar says “3 selected” and offers Select all 312 that match once every row shown is ticked. Delete removes the rows at once and a toast offers Undo for 6 s with a clock; Undo puts them back. “The server” Refuses: the toast turns into the reason and the rows come back by themselves.
  • The row’s own Delete button stands in the last column of every row and does the same for one message. 390 px: the table becomes cards.

Check the payload cell reads as set type in the mono face on the muted ground, keys in navy; the selection bar is a ruled slab that keeps its place whether or not a row is ticked; the table’s states fill the table’s height.

Why did this message die? Its delivery attempts on a timeline

New New: kyu would need to keep an attempt history for this (today it keeps only the count). One lane per attempt: the bar is the time the message was claimed, the mark at its end says how it ended: lease expired, nack, dead or acked. gantt with runs, points and zones.

Expected: New: kyu would need to store, per attempt, when it was claimed, by which app, and how it ended.

  • Dead after 5 attempts (the default): five lanes; attempts 1 and 4 end in a ring (lease expired, after 30 s, with the lease drawn as a thin band under the bar), attempts 2, 3 and 5 in a triangle (nack), and a square dead closes attempt 5. Recovered ends in a diamond (acked); In flight has a striped bar still growing; Not tried yet is the empty state with its own sentence.
  • Hover or focus a bar for its times and reason. The range buttons (2 min, 5 min by default, 15 min), Live, and dragging across the plot to zoom all work. Loading shows the lanes as waiting under the busy card; Failed fills the same height with the sentence.
  • The choice “The lease band” shows the band under each claim (default) or leaves it out.

Check a failed bar is taller and capped (shape, not only colour), the marks at the bar ends keep clear of the cap, lane names sit in one column and the frame keeps its height from loading to loaded.

The same attempts as a list you can open: what the app said to kyu

New New: kyu would need the same attempt history, with the app’s reason (the text it sent with a nack). One foldable block per attempt; the failed ones are open. request-inspector.

Expected: New: kyu would need to store each attempt with the app that took it and the nack reason. The status is the outcome written as the HTTP answer kyu would give a late ack: 200 acked, 409 lease expired, 422 nacked, 410 dead-lettered (mock mapping).

  • Dead after 5: five blocks, newest first; every one is a failure and starts open (the default failed), with headers (the app’s token masked), the attempt as JSON and Copy as curl, JSON or body. Recovered has one closed block (200) and one open (409).
  • The choices: which blocks start open (failed by default, none, or the first), and whether a copy carries the token masked (default, with a separate “Copy curl with secrets”), masked only, or real. Empty has Send test left out; Failed fills the same height with Try again.

Check each block is a ledger line (method, path, status in words, time) that opens to ruled rows, the masked token reads ********, and the open and closed blocks align their columns.

The last 90 days: delivered against dead

New New: kyu would need daily statistics for this (a count per subscription per day of messages delivered and dead-lettered; today it keeps neither). One cell per day, toned by how the day went. uptime.

Expected: New: kyu would need to keep two numbers per subscription per day.

  • 90 cells, oldest left. A day is up (everything delivered), degraded (some dead letters) or down (most died); a day with no messages is empty. The title carries the window’s summary in exact figures (for example “99.4% delivered, 11 dead”); the first and the last day are named under the strip.
  • Hover or focus a cell for the day’s figures; press it (or Enter) to open the quick info with that day’s numbers and a link to the day. The arrows, Home and End move along the strip. 390 px: the latest days that fit, and it says “55 of 90 shown”; the choice “When the strip is narrow” can scroll instead.
  • Loading, Empty and Failed keep the strip’s height; History “New subscription” has only 40 days of data.

Check the three tones differ by more than colour (the cell’s height or fill), the summary uses a cut, never rounded-up, percentage, and the first and last day are readable at both ends.
