Three gaps the admin dashboard found

The homelab admin dashboard drew three small things for itself because the package had none: the number on a tab, the arrow keys over a board of tiles, and the key under a heat grid. Each is now a part of the package. Four sections, in formal only: the count on a tab, the arrow keys over a board of app tiles, the legend of the backup grid, and the legend a calm grid draws by itself. The stacks, tiles and log lines are made-up samples of the dashboard’s data.

A count on a tab: Apps 7, Logs 12

A tab can carry the exact number of what is behind it, in a colour that says how urgent it is, and it can say that it is still counting. The Logs tab below is the one the buttons drive; Apps and History show the same count with their own tone and word.

Expected:

  • The number is the whole number: 1534 reads 1534, never “1.5k” or “99+”. A number with no count (Not counted yet) and a 0 draw nothing; 0, shown draws the 0, as History does for “0 incidents”.
  • Tone changes the colour of the count only. Counting turns the count into a pulsing bar as wide as the number was, and Could not count into a dash; the tabs beside it do not move in either.
  • Number and word writes the unit after the number (“12 errors”, and “1 error” for one). The word is always there for a screen reader, which hears “Logs, 12 errors”.
  • Select another tab: its count stays readable on the selected tab, and no tab changes width when it is selected.

Formal is ruled paper: an opaque plate in a strong hairline, a 3 px navy rule, nothing fades. Check that the count sits on the tab like a small stamp on the same baseline as the label, that its colours are the status plates the badges already have, and that there is clear space between the label and the count and between the count and the tab’s edge.

Arrow keys over a board of tiles

A board of app tiles is one place in the tab order: Tab enters it once, the arrow keys move between the tiles by where they are drawn, and Tab leaves it. Click a tile (or use Put the focus on the board) and press the arrow keys.

Expected:

  • Keys on: the readings under the board say one tab stop for all the tiles. Right and Left walk a row and go on to the next row; Up and Down go to the tile above or below in the same column, and from the last tile of a short row to the last tile of the next row, never skipping a row. Home and End go to the first and last tile. Up on the first row lands in the search box.
  • Keys off gives the ordinary tab order back: every tile is a tab stop (the readings say so) and the arrow keys do nothing.
  • Board width changes how many tiles stand in a row (four, three, two, one); the keys follow whatever is drawn. Folded and Only apps with a problem take tiles out; the keys skip them.
  • Press S on a tile to star it; the star is in the key line because it is not a tab stop of its own.

Formal is ruled paper. Check that the focused tile shows the package’s focus ring with clear space round the tile, that the ring moves with the key at once, and that the tiles keep one height in every width.

The legend under a heat grid

A heat grid can write its own legend: a swatch and its words per state, a scale from less to more for a grid of values, and at the end the line of keys. The legend keeps its place while the grid loads or fails.

Expected:

  • Nightly backups: five states in words (backed up, some missed, missed, before any history, keeps no data by design) and the key line at the end. Requests per hour swaps the grid for values, and the legend for a scale from fewer to more requests.
  • Words only drops the key line, Hidden removes the legend; neither moves the grid.
  • Loading and Could not be read leave the legend where it is, under a grid that keeps its size.

Formal is ruled paper. Check that every swatch is the same colour and pattern as the cell it describes, that the words have clear space from the swatch and from each other, and that the key line stands at the end and wraps whole when it does not fit.

A calm grid draws its own legend

A tracker where a day without an entry is simply empty never shows red. A heat grid in the calm vocabulary now says what its three tones mean by itself: Logged, Nothing logged, Paused.

Expected:

  • The legend appears with the grid and names the three tones, in words that never say “missed”.
  • Hidden takes the legend away and Automatic brings it back, without the grid being drawn again.
  • Paused for 3 days turns the middle of the week hatched, and the legend already names that tone.
  • Loading keeps the legend where it is.

Formal is ruled paper. Check that the three swatches match the cells (filled, empty, hatched) and that nothing in the grid or the legend reads as a failure.
