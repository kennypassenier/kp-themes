A page header of its own, per theme: one pick per aspect

Phase 1 (Kenny, 2026-10-05): the page header (.kp-page-header): the title and its one-sentence description on the left, the actions — secondary buttons, one primary and an overflow menu — on the right. Per theme you pick the shape, how the overflow menu opens and closes, and hover/focus/press, each from three options and each on its own; any combination composes.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: the overflow menu appears and disappears at once. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

The header below is a page's own: a title, a one-sentence description, two secondary actions, one primary action and an overflow menu with a destructive item last. An option only restyles .kp-page-header from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (header.css, and the four group files for the other 20 themes), one attribute per aspect, and keeps the header's layout — including how it stacks below 40rem — exactly as the package's own.
