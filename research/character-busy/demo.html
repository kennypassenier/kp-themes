A data table's busy overlay, of its own, per theme: one pick per aspect

Phase 1 (Kenny, 2026-10-05): the panel that covers a data table while it loads (.kp-datatable__busy-overlay, data-kp-busy-overlay or busy({ overlay: true }), fix-80 to fix-85) — a large spinner, the app's words, a live busy counter, and the failed alert that can stand in its place when fail() is called. Per theme you pick the shape of the panel, the loading picture, how the panel arrives (and leaves), the failed state, and the flat layout on a phone, each from three options and each on its own.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: every loading picture shows its still frame. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Every table is the package's own (js/datatable.js's attachDataTables() draws the overlay, the live counter and the failed alert; it is not changed here); an option only restyles the overlay and the panel from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (busy.css), one attribute per aspect, and keeps the table's own rows untouched.
