Notes on the form grid

The chassis-rs and homelab threads, laying their settings out on the form grid, found three places where it fell short and two where the alignment check was wrong. Five sections, all in formal: the form grid gap, the JSON field’s action, the refresh with a label, and the check’s reading of a segmented control and of buttons. Each says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: Formal drew its own 1 rem gap on every form (.kp-form). When that form was a block of a form grid, its rows are rows of the grid (the form is a subgrid of it), but this gap, being more specific than the grid’s, overrode the grid’s: the two rows inside the form stood further apart than the rows around them, and a page that set --kp-formgrid-gap got its setting everywhere except inside the form. Now the form takes the grid’s gap, and a form on its own page keeps formal’s gap. The rows, the columns and everything else of the grid are as you approved them.

Form grid: a form between rows keeps the grid’s gap

One form grid with a row, then a form (two rows of its own), then a row again. The rows are spaced by the grid’s row gap wherever they stand.

Expected:

  • The distance from Name to Region, from Region to Retention (both inside the form), and from Retention to Contact is the same in all four steps.
  • The labels, the fields and the Save button keep their columns from row to row, in and out of the form.

Formal: four rows with one and the same space between them, the form’s two rows not further apart than the rest.

What changed since you approved it: A JSON field’s first line is its tool row, and its label already stood on that line. The row’s own action in the actions column had no way to: it stood at the top of the cell, level with the field’s top edge, so “Send test” was a few pixels off the line of Format and Minify. Now, in a row that holds a JSON field, the actions cell is exactly one tool row high and centres its button on it, so Send test stands on the line of the tools and in the column of every other row action. Stacked on a phone the action falls below the field as before. The JSON field itself is as you approved it.

What changed since you approved it: The refresh bar of a form grid is a subgrid of it, which left no cell for a label: the label had to go in a row of its own, or the page drew its own nested grid. Now the refresh takes data-kp-refresh-label="Auto-refresh" and draws that word as the first cell of its bar, in the label column, with the chooser in the control column, the freshness line in the value column and Refresh now in the actions column, all on one first baseline. The chooser also started 4 px left of every other control (the refresh kept its own column gap instead of the grid’s); it now starts on the same edge. Outside a form grid the label is a line above the bar. The chooser, the countdown, the freshness line and Refresh now are as you approved them.

A segmented control is one box, drawn with its own padding: that box is its edge. The check now reads the box. A filter whose box is off the columns is still reported, as the proof below shows. Nothing on this page, and no component, looks different.
