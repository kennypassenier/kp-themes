Every theme: what changed so that a close is its open backwards

Kenny, 2026-10-12: "Cyberpunk lijkt af, zie dat dezelfde fouten nu niet terugkomen in andere thema's". Every theme but forest and cyberpunk was measured in Firefox and mended: each part that opens or arrives now uses the theme's own effect, and each close is its open played backwards. This page shows only what is new to look at: a part that borrowed an effect the theme had not decided for it, a timing that moved, or a question. Each block is asked only in the themes where it changed.

Judge it in the review dialog ("Review in a dialog" at the top). Measurements and reasons per theme are in research/all-themes/reports/ and research/all-themes/README.md.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves. Turn it off to judge the openings and closes.

The bar: dropdown, mega menu and phone panel open with the theme's own effect and close as it backwards.

terminal: The dropdown, mega menu and phone panel are printed in the menu's four lines and cleared backwards. Borrowed from the menu's print (G3).

grotesk: The dropdown, mega menu and phone panel take the plate's fall (960 ms) and fall back. Round 2: on the phone the links stand in one column from the start edge under the brand (they fell into the grid's columns under the toggle).

nostromo: The dropdown, mega menu and phone panel are drawn by the raster with the beam and erased with the same frames backwards. Borrowed from the popover's raster.
