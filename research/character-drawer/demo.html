A drawer and its guided tour, each of its own, per theme

One pick per aspect, as the meter and the trend tile. Per theme you pick the drawer's shape, how it opens and closes (a theme's close is its open played backwards), the tour's highlight, the tour's step card, and what moving to the next step does, each from three options and each on its own. Nothing is ticked for you.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: the drawer shows its open or closed frame at once and the tour's card shows its new step at once. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Every stage is the package's own markup for dialog.kp-drawer (a head, a scrolling body, a foot) and the tour's card .kp-tour (js/tour.js): a short tour of three steps over a help drawer about a water network's control room, the second step pointing at the area list, the third at the drawer's own close button. An option only restyles it from the theme's tokens, in the theme's kp.signature layer (drawer.css and its four theme-group files), one attribute per aspect, and keeps the drawer's and the card's shape in every state.
