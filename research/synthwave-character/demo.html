What synthwave is

Synthwave is the drive into the sunset, 1984: a neon horizon on the night, the grid floor running to it, the striped sun sinking behind it, an arcade marquee and a VCR's on-screen display. Pink signals, cyan reads, laser yellow warns; the light is neon tubes, a near-white core with the colour in its glow. "Cyberpunk is diagonal and day; synthwave is horizontal and night" (the anatomy, 2026-09-08).

So everything that happens, happens on the horizon: what opens or arrives rises over its horizon, cut by the sun's stripes, and what leaves sets behind it; while something waits the marquee chases; a change is marked by a laser drawn under it; what you point at turns its tube up; a warning carries a neon ring. Bodies glide on a slow sunrise curve; light switches in hard steps, as bulbs and tubes do. Nothing tears, falls, bounces or runs diagonal.
