Medicine Cabinet: a popover with room for a form, and a refresh that keeps the row you type in

Two options built from Medicine Cabinet’s gaps of 01/11/2026: its “Add stock” popover with room inside and its edge under the button, and its medicines table, whose refresh no longer takes the row you are typing in. Both are opt-in; the third gap (the tab bar’s More drawer that stayed full-screen) was a fault, fixed without a new look, so it has no section. Formal only (names, counts and stock are mock). 2 sections, 2 pairs.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does.

Medicine Cabinet, stock

Three medicines with what is left. Add stock opens a small form under its button: how many packs, and Add.

What changed (Medicine Cabinet gap 25): Medicine Cabinet’s “Add stock” popover put its sentence 5 px from the plate’s border, and from a button at the end of a row it opened toward the end. Two opt-in classes: kp-popover--panel gives the plate room inside (12 px, knob --kp-popover-panel-padding) and its own width (up to 22rem); kp-popover--end lines its end edge up with the button’s. The plain plate stays the default for menus.

Expected:

  • Press Add stock on Ibuprofen (or Open): the form hangs 4 px under the button, its right edge on the button’s right edge, the sentence 13 px from the border on every side. Escape or a press outside closes it.
  • Look: “Plain plate (as before)” puts the sentence 5 px from the border, the field and Add tight against it. Edge: “Start edge (default)” starts the form at the button’s left edge; at a row’s end it flips back so it never leaves the screen.
  • Add with 3 packs: the line under the list says “3 packs added to Ibuprofen 400 mg” and the form closes. Phone: the form stays whole on the screen, 8 px or more from each side.

Formal: the raised plate with its fine border; check that no text touches the border and that the form stands under its own button.

Medicine Cabinet, medicines

Five medicines with how many were taken today and the stock. The page asks for fresh rows every 3 seconds once Refresh runs.

What changed (Medicine Cabinet gap 27): Medicine Cabinet redraws its table’s rows when the data changes; a redraw took the row under the stock field you were typing in, so the app skipped every refresh while a field had the focus. With data-kp-keep-focused-row on the table, the row that holds the focus stays, with its field, what you typed, the caret and the focus; every other row is redrawn. Off by default.

Expected:

  • Set Refresh to “Every 3 s”: the line under the table counts the refreshes and “Taken today” moves. Click a Stock field and type: the row stays with what you typed and the caret where it was; the line says the row was kept; the other rows go on changing.
  • Leave the field (Tab or a click elsewhere): what you typed is saved, and the next refresh draws that row fresh too. Keep the focused row “Off (as before)”: the next refresh takes the field away while you type and the focus is lost.
  • Phone: the rows are cards and the same holds. Refresh is paused when the page opens.

Formal: the plain table and its fields; check that the field you type in never blinks, loses a letter or loses the focus while the other rows change.
