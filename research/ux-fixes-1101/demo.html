Eight UX fixes from the app reviews

Kenny’s review of the apps (kyu, Almanac, homelab, HTTPSwitchboard, Medicine Cabinet, Kitchen) found faults in the package; you approved the fixes. Each section shows one visible change on the app’s own shape, and where it replaces something the earlier look stays beside it as “before”. Formal only (the names, counts, dates and times are mock). 8 sections, 8 pairs.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. A width control shows the stage at a phone’s 390 px; a touch screen’s behaviour is shown with a switch, because a mouse cannot be a finger.

What is asked (your answer to the review of the apps, 2026-11-01: “table-filters: Popover met labels”): kyu’s Topics table opened its Filters as a panel inside the table that pushed everything below it down by 95 px, and the Min and Max boxes of the message count had no name. The panel is now a popover under its button; every input has a visible label.

Topics: filter on messages, last seen and state

kyu’s Topics (sample data). Press Filters: the panel opens under the button, over the table. The line under the table does not move; the line under it says where it stood before and after.

Expected:

  • Popover: the panel hangs under the Filters button, over the table’s rows; the line under the table stays at the same height (the readout under the table says so in pixels). Escape or a press outside closes it.
  • Labels: State, Messages (Min and Max) and Last seen (From and To) each have their own words over the box. Type a Min of 100 and the rows narrow.
  • Inline (before): the older panel, in the flow: the line under the table moves down when it opens. It stays as the choice data-kp-filters="inline".

Formal is a paper plate: the panel is a plate with the theme’s border and opaque ground, never see-through over the rows. Check the labels are as readable as the table text and nothing touches an edge.

What is asked (your answer to the review of the apps, 2026-11-01: “table-states: Opnemen”): An empty table kept about 550 px of blank body with only “Showing 0 of 0”, and a failed one stood inside the full table chrome (“Not sorted.”). Now the plate fills the table’s footprint and the count line, the pager and the sort summary leave. A pager with one page hides.

Dead letters: rows, none, or could not be read

kyu’s Dead letters table (sample data). Switch it to Empty and Failed and back: the plate takes the height the rows had and nothing around the table moves.

Expected:

  • Empty: the package’s empty plate, centred, as high as the table was; no “Showing 0 of 0”, no pager, no sort line. The search stays so a search can be cleared.
  • Failed: the failed plate with Try again replaces the footprint; the same chrome is gone. Press Try again: the rows come back.
  • Rows: nine rows fit on one page, so no pager is drawn; the line says “Showing 1–9 of 9”. A page size of 5 (press Pager) brings it back with two pages.

Formal’s plates are quiet paper with a heading-size line: check the heading is centred in the table’s old footprint and the Try again button has a normal size.

Publish a message: topic, payload and days

kyu’s Publish form: it opens clean; the control shows it after leaving the payload and after Publish.

Expected:

Last 48 hours of two profiles
