**Why.** Kenny decided the anchor of dark on 2026-10-08 in [research/dark-anchor](../dark-anchor/README.md), update 1: **The line lays the film down, turning**. **How.**

- `aspects.js` holds the nineteen questions as data (the designer's text, verbatim: question, reason, rule, kind, scene key, options with what you see and the verdict). `demo.js` imports it, adds each question's scene (`SCENES`, by the scene key), builds the rows (`data-dk-aspect`, option cells `data-dk-option`) and writes `data-review-choices` from the same data, so the page and the dialog cannot disagree.
- `grammar.css` is the grammar as tokens on `.dk-page, .dk-scene` (the settle curve and its inverse, the durations, the colours by role, the line's glow, the cuts, the ruled ground) and the keyframes of the recommended options: the pass (`dk-clip`, `dk-line-t`, `dk-line-o`, each with its `-out`), the film's turn (`dk-turn`), the figure taking the film through its letters (`dk-fig`, `dk-fig-live`), a slot swept while it waits (`dk-wash`, `dk-wait-line`).
- `options.css` (in `@layer kp.signature`) draws every option, scoped by `data-dk-<question>="<key>"` on the scene. Colours are tokens only; every motion sits under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; the reduced pose is the part laid, the film settled, cyan at the top. The film is the register's `--kp-iris` written on a registered angle (`--dk-angle`) on every element of a scene, so a turn of the angle turns every edge that wears it.
- A laid part is a **lay**: the body clipped to what the line has passed and the line itself, a 2 px slice of the film with the one glow the theme allows (10 px of `--chart-1` at 40 %), crossing the part and the reach of its halo, so the clip's edge and the line are one thing. A group (a week of days) is crossed by one line, a stride a part.
- One clock in `demo.js` plays every scene that arrives, opens, updates or leaves (`data-dk-phase`: gap 0.5 s, in 1.5 s, hold 1.5 s, out 1.5 s): the options of a row start together. Replay restarts it, the speed buttons stretch every duration by 2 or 4, the dialog's Pause (Space) stops it. Loops (loading, the busy bar, the spinner) run in CSS; the press row is looped by the same script (pressing is at once, letting go eases back).
- Every open is a pair of keyframes (`dk-x`, `dk-x-out`): the close is the open reversed. The timing function is written out inside the keyframes, an eased open closes on the inverse curve (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` against `cubic-bezier(0.64, 0, 0.78, 0)`, the register's `cubic-bezier(0.16, 0.84, 0.28, 1)` against `cubic-bezier(0.72, 0, 0.84, 0.16)`; `steps(n, jump-end)` against `steps(n, jump-start)`), and a part's delay is mirrored (window less delay less length: what came last goes first). The window is 1000 ms (1500 ms for the unhurried durations).
- State (Rest, Hover, Focus, Press) gives every button, entry and link of question 14 the class the register's pointer, keyboard and press would give it; the hover, focus and press rows draw their own state (`dk-pointed`, `dk-focused`, a looped `dk-st-press`), which the dialog also plays by itself.
- The gallery of decided components loads the character demos through the review kit's embed mode (`?embed=…&theme=dark`) only when it is opened.

**Findings while building.**

- The register's badge and tag keep the package's pill radius (`border-radius: var(--kp-radius-pill, 999px)` in `kp.components`; the register only adds the chamfer's `clip-path`), so a dark badge is a pill with its corners nicked. G6 says radius 0; the scenes set 0 on badges and tags. The register's own fix would be one line in `css/dark-register.css`.
- The register's chamfer cuts the top-right and the bottom-left corners (`polygon(0 0, calc(100% - c) 0, 100% c, 100% 100%, c 100%, 0 calc(100% - c))`); the text of question 6 says top-left and bottom-right. The scenes follow the register.
- The key-figure tile (`.kp-kpi`) is a plain rectangle in the register (no chamfer, no film edge); the scenes draw it as a panel (the panel cut, the film along its edge), as G6 and G7 describe a panel.
- The ring of question 16 is the register's: the ink (`--focus-ring`) along the edge, the void (`--focus-ring-contrast`) inside it; the text says "the ring colour (cyan) inside", but cyan is `--ring`, which the register's focus does not use.
- The inverse of `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` is `cubic-bezier(0.64, 0, 0.78, 0)` (G1 and the brief write `0.78, 1`, a typo: a curve's inverse is its point reflection, and the mirror measurement fails with the typo).
- The register's spinner turns in 1400 ms (`--kp-sig-dur`); the text of question 12's "today" says 1.2 s. The scene uses 1400 ms.
- The menu button's "powered up" pick (`mb-dark-power`) is a three-step brightness ramp (0.4, 0.65, 0.85, 1), not the tube's dark-flash-dark-on that question 13's text describes (the tube is the tiles' arrival, used for question 2's "switched on"); the option draws the pick's own keyframes.
