How a dialog opens, per theme

Kenny, 2026-10-03, on the signature-elements round: "was het de bedoeling dat die een bepaalde animatie ofzo toonden als de dialog opende? want dat heb ik niet kunnen zien? maak een aparte demo pagina hiervoor". There the dialog stood open in the page, so its entrance played once, on load. Here it opens when you ask: Open the dialog plays the theme's entrance over a screen-sized backdrop, Slow motion plays it at a quarter of its speed, and Package today shows what the package does now, for comparison. Close it with Escape, a button, or a click on the backdrop. In the review dialog each theme opens it once by itself.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so every entrance is switched off and the dialog simply appears. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.
