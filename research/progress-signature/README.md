# A progress bar of its own, per theme

**Decided (scope-140: all 22 bars approved on 2026-10-03, forest after its rewrite; they replace `.kp-progress` in 9.0.0).**

`demo.html` shows the signature progress bar in the theme on screen (`?theme=<name>`, `?still` for a frozen frame); `base.css` holds the shared contract and `themes/<theme>.css` each theme's drawing.
