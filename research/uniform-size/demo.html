Can every element keep its size when the theme changes?

Kenny's question for scope-79: not one control's height, but whether all elements can have a uniform size, so that switching theme leaves the layout and the size of everything nearly where it was. Below: today's measured size of all 22 themes, and four ways to make them agree, each shown in the largest, the median and the smallest theme side by side.

What is mock and what is the package. Inside every column, all of it is the package: .kp-card, .kp-field, .kp-combobox, .kp-switch, .kp-button and .kp-button--sm, .kp-nav, .kp-sidenav, .kp-tabs, .kp-alert, .kp-table, .kp-badge, .kp-pagination, .kp-dialog, with all 22 registers linked. Mock: the column page itself (frame.html), the four option stylesheets (option-b.css to option-e.css; option A adds nothing), and everything on this outer page prefixed demo- — the rulers, the height readouts, the layout.

Numbers in the tables were measured in firefox at a 1400px viewport with node research/uniform-size/measure.mjs and node research/uniform-size/analyze.mjs. The readouts under each column are measured live by your own browser and can differ by a pixel or two.

The size index of all 22 themes

Look at: the first column is today. The index is the geometric mean, over 19 elements (button, small button, text field, select, combobox, checkbox row, switch, tab, badge, alert, table header row, table row, nav bar, nav link, side navigation item, pagination link, dialog title, card title, field label), of the element's height in that theme divided by the median height of that element across the 22 themes. 1.00 is the typical theme; 1.25 is a quarter taller. The three themes in bold are the columns below. The right-hand table is the spread (tallest ÷ shortest) of each composite, per option, across all 22 and across the three.

A · today, no change (the control)

Look at: the bottom edges against the red rulers, which sit at the median theme's edges. Brutalism's pinned 48px controls and thick lines push its form card down; its nav bar and dialog title wrap to two lines because Archivo Black in capitals does not fit. Blueprint stays within 3% of the median in every composite. Nothing on the package side is changed here.

B · one package-wide box scale; registers keep colour, border, face

Look at: the controls, tabs, rows and labels now line up to the pixel across the three; brutalism keeps its black 3px lines and offset shadows, but they are drawn inside a 36px control instead of a 48px one, and its card title is 16px instead of 24px. What still differs is text that wraps: a face or letter spacing that makes a line longer still adds a line. Mock: option-b.css, unlayered, restating the defaults already in css/components.css.

C · B, plus font-size-adjust to normalise each face

Look at: whether the words look the same size across the columns compared with B. For these three the change is small, because their faces already have an x-height near 0.5; switch the all-22 view below to C and look at deco, whose small-x-height face grows by about a third, so its checkbox label wraps to two lines under the box (row height 25.6px today, 67.4px in C). Mock: option-b.css plus option-c.css.

D · each theme keeps its sizes, clamped to ±8% of the median

Look at: brutalism's buttons come down from 48 to about 39px and its fields to 43, but its nav bar, dialog title and table rows barely move: a clamp on padding and font size cannot touch the height a face's own line spacing and wrapping give. Mock: option-d.css, generated by build-d.mjs from today's measurements, one clamped value per theme where the package would write clamp() around a size token.

E · only the line boxes fixed; padding, type size and containers stay free

Look at: the form card and the table agree nearly as well as in B, because what makes them tall is controls and rows; the dialog does not, because its title keeps each theme's own size and wraps. Brutalism keeps its 24px card title and its chunky nav links, now in 36px rows. Mock: option-e.css.
