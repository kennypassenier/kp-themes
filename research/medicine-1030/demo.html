Medicine Cabinet: thirteen things the app needed from the package

The Medicine Cabinet web app found thirteen places where it had to draw its own glue; you approved building them in the package on 2026-10-30. Eight are new parts or options (a list of rows, a quantity that fills its cell, a repeating row editor, an attention band with a maximum, a run of badges, dialog widths and a rich confirmation, a table’s actions column and card width, pills with nothing chosen tied to a tag input) and five are fixes (typed text at the start edge, air under a card’s sentence, an error line that moves nothing, a switch without a state word, a freshness line after the title); each fix shows Before and After. Formal only, on the app’s own shapes (the names, counts and times are mock). Nothing here is on by default except the five fixes and the narrow confirmation, which say so. 13 sections, 13 pairs.

Every section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. The width control shows the stage at a phone’s 390 px.

What is asked (item 1 · Medicine Cabinet gap 1): A list where every row holds several controls has no component: a data table is the wrong tool. .kp-list with .kp-listrow is one grid of four named columns and every row a subgrid of it, so the columns continue across rows, and a row is a card on a phone. Is this the row the Cabinet, Today and Symptoms lists should use?

The Cabinet: six medicines

Each row: the medicine and what it is, its status badges, a stepper for the stock and a switch for whether it is in use. The list sits in the card’s data area, so loading, empty and failed are the card’s own.

Expected:

  • Full width: the names, the badges, the steppers and the switches stand on four edges that run down all six rows. A stepper is as wide as its column, so every stepper ends on one edge. The inactive medicine is quieter, its controls are not.
  • Phone: each row is a card: the name and what it is, the badges on their own line, the stepper filling its line, the switch beside it when the line has room for both and under it when not. Loading, Empty and Failed fill the list’s whole footprint with the card’s plate and a normal Retry.

Formal is a paper plate: rows ruled by thin lines, nothing in a row drawn on a card of its own. Check the columns line up whatever a row holds, and that the badge column never pushes a stepper.
