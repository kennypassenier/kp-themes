Four fixes on the grid

The project threads found four places where an approved component did not hold its place: the remind-me picker had no width of its own, a Copy button broke in the middle of its word in a narrow cell, a checklist that could not be read had no Retry, and the timer list had no loading or failed state and a too-low empty line. 4 sections, formal only (every fix is the theme’s own plate, button and loading card). Each section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: The picker had no width of its own: wherever its cell, row or bar sized itself from what it held, the picker came out as wide as nothing, so “Not snoozed” broke letter by letter and each page had to give it a fixed width. Now the picker is as wide as its button and its line, and fills the room it is given: in a column of a grid it takes the column, in a bar it sits among the other buttons, and in a narrow cell End snooze goes under the button. The choices, the menu, the date panel and the snoozed line are as you approved them.
