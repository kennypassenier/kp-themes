Opening as the reverse of closing

Kenny, 2026-10-05, on the catalogue's leave options: "can we also see the opposite of close() with the reverse animation of close? So when it grows it's the opposite? give a demo with formal, cyberpunk and titanium". Each section below opens the same element two ways, side by side: on the left it opens as its theme's close played backwards, frame for frame and in the close's own time; on the right it opens the way the package opens it today. Both close the same way, with the theme's close.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing moves on this page: both sides open and close at once. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Nothing here has a speed of its own. A dialog closes as its entrance backwards, in two thirds of the entrance's time (at most 600 ms), so the reverse of its close is the entrance played forwards in the close's time. A card closes with its theme's leave (leave(): the register's exit on [data-kp-leaving] while its space folds shut), so the reverse of its close is that exit played backwards while the space unfolds, on the same timeline turned around.
