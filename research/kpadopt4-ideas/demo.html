Seven new components, in formal

The seven you marked “Onmisbaar” on 16/10/2026, each built from the package’s own parts (the buttons, the segmented control, the date picker, the hover card, the busy card, the badge, the dialog) and shown here in formal only, in every state. Every section says what you should see under Expected. Everything with a kp- class and every script under js/ is the package; the sample data, the frames, the event readouts and the controls marked “demo” are this page’s.

1 · Uptime strip: 90 days and 48 hours

.kp-uptime[data-kp-uptime] with setUptime(el, { label, periods, from, to }) (js/uptime.js): one cell per period, toned up, degraded, down or no data, the window’s summary in exact figures over it. For the homelab Health page per service and for kyu’s delivery per hour.

Expected:

  • Four strips, each one line high: the name on the left, the summary on the right (100% · 0 incidents for jellyfin; kyu’s share is cut, never rounded up, and its incident count is the exact number). The cells never wrap.
  • Up is a soft green bar, degraded a lighter amber bar with fine horizontal rules, down a red bar with diagonal stripes, no data an empty frame (almanac’s first 26 days). So every state is a shape as well as a colour.
  • Point at a cell: it grows a little upwards (nothing beside it moves) and the hover card opens under it with the day, its state and its figures. Tab into a strip: one stop, on today’s cell, with the card open; the arrow keys walk the days, Home and End jump to the first and today. Enter or a click writes the pick in the grey box below.
  • Under each strip: “90 days ago” at the start and “Today” at the end (“48 hours ago” and “This hour” for the hours).

The strips in formal’s ink: the title in its body face at semibold, the share in bold tabular figures, the cells thin with a 1 px corner, and the hover card is formal’s own tooltip plate.
