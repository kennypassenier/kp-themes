Control heights: one row, four rules

Measured on 2026-09-13 in firefox: in 13 themes a button, a field, a select, a combobox and a date field already share one height (brutalism at 48 px, the rest at 36). In nine they do not — nostromo, phantom, synthwave, pastel, deco, terminal, lapis, shade-dark and shade-light. The page opens in nostromo; use the theme menu to see the others. Under each row the page measures what it drew.

Four options, one above the other

Look at: the bottom edges of each row. In A they step wherever the heights differ. B gives every control the field's height in this theme, so each theme keeps its own size. C gives every control 36 px in every theme. D only makes the combobox follow the field. Switch to phantom and synthwave to see the biggest differences, and to brutalism to see what C takes away from a theme that is large on purpose. The forced heights here are drawn with block-size for the demo; the package would keep min-height so enlarged text still grows.
