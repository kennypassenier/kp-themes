Seven things the app review of 2026-11-01 found missing in the package, built as you answered: three new parts (the popover form, the date navigation, Delete with Undo), the three-way chip’s state as a word, and three options whose default you set here (chips on whole tracks, the empty plate without lines, the refresh control without its own line). Every option shows the approved look and the new one under each other; the approved look stays the default until you choose. Formal only, on the apps’ own data (Medicine Cabinet, Kitchen, kyu, Almanac; names, counts and days are mock). 7 sections, 7 pairs.

Each section says under Expected what you should see and what each control does. The width control shows the stage at a phone’s 390 px.
