Six calm components for a recipe app

The recipe app (parked, its concept still open) is for someone with AuDHD: calm, predictable, little to hold in mind, big targets, no motion that surprises, every state said in words and shapes as well as colour. It asked for six things the package did not have. Each is built here as a general kp component, useful beyond recipes, formal only until you approve it. Every section says what you should see under Expected. Everything with a kp- class and every script under js/ is the package; the sample data, the frames, the event logs and the buttons that drive a section are this page’s.

4 · Quantity with minus and plus

.kp-quantity with js/quantity.js: a number between the theme’s own minus and plus buttons, its unit after it, for portions and shopping amounts. The number can also be typed.

Expected:

  • Each control is three parts on one line: a square minus button, one field box holding the number and its unit, a square plus button. Buttons are formal’s secondary buttons (inner rule on hover, double rule on press), at least 44 px square; on a phone 48 px.
  • Portions starts at 2. Press plus: 3 portions … 12 portions; at 12 the plus dims and does nothing, at 1 the minus dims and the unit says “portion”. Nothing moves while the number changes, not even from 9 to 10, and the unit keeps its place between “portion” and “portions”.
  • Hold a button down: one step at once, then after a short pause it repeats, slowly first and then faster; let go and it stops. Keyboard: Tab into the number, ArrowUp and ArrowDown step, PageUp and PageDown step ten, Home and End go to the bounds; Enter on a focused button steps once.
  • Type in a field: the Dutch one reads 1,5 and writes a comma, the English one a point; both accept either mark. Leaving the field keeps the value inside the bounds and on its step (type 7 in grams: it becomes 0 or 50 … the nearest step). “Type a word” puts “a lot” in the field: its edge turns red while it stands; leaving it brings the last value back.
  • The large one is for cook mode at arm’s length. The disabled one is dimmed and out of reach. The long unit stays on one line beside its number, with room on both sides. No loading state: a quantity has nothing to wait for.
  • The log shows each kp-quantity-change with its value, the previous value and what changed it.

Hold plus on Portions until it stops at 12, then type 1,5 in the Dutch field and Tab away.

5 · Checklist whose ticked items sink

                    

                        .kp-checklist with js/checklist.js: a shopping list by shop. What you still need stays on top, what
                        is in the basket sinks, struck through. The component stores nothing; every change is an event with the whole list.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            Three shops, each a heading with its count at the end (“Colruyt … 1 of 6”). Rows are a fingertip high and separated by
                            hairlines; the amount stands in its own column at the end, so amounts line up. Bread and butter are already ticked and
                            stand last in their group, struck through in grey.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Click anywhere on a row (not only the box): it is ticked, struck through, and glides to the end of its group in formal’s
                            time, so you see where it went; the count goes up. Click it again: it glides back to its own place among the open ones. A
                            shop whose every row is ticked shows formal’s check before its count.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Keyboard: Tab to the first row and press Space: the row sinks and the focus is already on the next open row, so Space,
                            Space, Space ticks down the list without moving your eyes.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Clear checked takes the ticked rows away; the bar’s line becomes “3 cleared” with an Undo button in the same
                            place (6 s), which puts them back where they were. Check all ticks everything and dims while all is done.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            The buttons below the list: Load again shows formal’s busy card in the middle of the list (2.5 s, the spinner
                            turning) and then the list; Fail shows a red-edged sentence where the rows were; Empty the dashed empty
                            line. The second list keeps ticked rows in place (data-kp-checklist-sink="false"). The 360 px frame wraps the
                            long name onto a second line, with room around it.
                        
    • 
                        
  • Reduced motion: rows move at once, without the glide. The log shows every event.
    •

6 · Three-way filter chips: with, without, either way

                    

                        An option of the segmented chips you approved, not a new component: data-kp-segmented="tri" on a
                        kp-segmented--chips group. Each chip is either way, must be in, or must not be in (“with chicken, without
                        mushrooms”). The facet list stays a list of checkboxes; a third state there would turn a checkbox into a riddle.
                    

                

                

                    
Expected:

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            Every chip starts with a mark at its start: a hollow ring means either way. Chicken is already in: the chip
                            filled in formal’s navy ink, the ring filled with a check. Mushrooms is out: a red edge, the ring a red plate with
                            a cross, the word struck through in red. Shape, word and colour each say it on their own.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Click a chip: either way → in → out → either way. The chip never changes width and nothing beside it moves. The line under
                            the group says what is chosen (“With chicken, without mushrooms”); with nothing chosen it says how it works.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Clear all at the end lets every chip go; it is dimmed and does nothing while nothing is chosen. The button under
                            the group does the same from outside (resetSegmented()).
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            Keyboard: Tab reaches the group once; the arrows move between chips; Space or Enter turns the focused chip; Delete or
                            Backspace lets it go at once. A screen reader hears “Chicken, required, button” and, after a press, “Chicken: left out”.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            The long chip is cut with an ellipsis (its full name is its tooltip). The 360 px frame wraps the chips onto more lines.
                            The disabled group and the disabled chip are dimmed and cannot be pressed. In the loading group the counts are quiet bars
                            that breathe until the numbers arrive (2.4 s), and nothing moves when they do.
                        
    • 
                        
  • The log shows each kp-segmented-change with its include and exclude lists.
    • 
                    

                

                

                    
Turn one chip through all three states and watch that it keeps its width; then try the keys.
