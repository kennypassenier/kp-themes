People, round 2: the conversation

Formal only. Only the conversation is back. Your note is built in: long messages and order are documented options of the component (attributes you can change at any time, the thread redraws by itself), and “yours on a wash of the primary colour” is the default look. The people and messages are made up.

Conversation

Conversation in bubbles

Messages as bubbles: theirs on the start side, yours on the end side, status changes as centred lines, a day divider between days, attachments as tags. Only the newest message is shown whole; older long ones fold to three lines with “Show all N lines” (configurable).

Bubbles are soft plates with a small corner where the sender’s side begins; the sender’s mark stands beside the first bubble of a run. Look at the folded older mails fading out, the day dividers, the failed message with its Retry, and at the box that keeps its height in loading, empty and failed.

Expected: changed since round 1: every setting below is a documented option of the component with a default (shown pressed) and switches live by changing an attribute; the look is a fifth option whose default is yours on a wash of the primary colour.

  • Long messages (data-kp-thread-fold: newest, a number, full) and Order (data-kp-thread-order: oldest, newest) redraw the conversation when pressed; so do Look (data-kp-thread-bubble: tinted, plain, filled), Marks and Days. The line under the box names the attribute that was just set.
  • On load: seven messages over three days, oldest first, only the newest whole, your bubbles on a light wash of the primary colour with a primary edge. Send a reply and Send one that fails still work; the states Loading, Empty and Failed fill the same box.
