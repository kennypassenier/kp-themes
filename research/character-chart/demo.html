A time chart of its own, per theme: one pick per aspect

Round 2 (Kenny, 2026-10-05 20:03): nineteen themes kept the character they picked in round 1, but not its loading screen, and every aspect is its own pick now. Per theme you pick the shape, the loading picture, how the series arrives, how a new reading shows, the event dots and the pinned tooltip, each from three options. Where round 1 already decided (the shape, the arrival, the update and the tooltip of the character you picked) option 1 is that and it is ticked for you; the loading picture (three new ones per theme, every one of them moving) and the event dots are open everywhere. High-contrast and brutalism get three new shapes; solstice's shape 1 is the low sun with the riveted plate as tooltip 1. In those three themes every aspect is open.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so nothing on this page moves: every loading chart shows its still frame and the series arrive at once. Turn it off in the system settings to judge the motion.

Every chart is the package's own (js/chart.js draws it, so hover, drag to zoom, click to pin, the keys and the legend all work): the pressure of the pump houses over the last 24 hours, the alarm level dashed, three events above the plot. Every option keeps the plot's height in every state, takes its colours from the theme's tokens only, and lives in the theme's kp.signature layer, one attribute per aspect (charts.css, round2.css, aspects.css).
