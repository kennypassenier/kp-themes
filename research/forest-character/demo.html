What forest is

Forest is a ranger's field kit. The ground is a map on kraft paper: contour lines, the trail with its painted blazes and its trig points. What stands on that ground is the wood the map describes: trees, wood grain, rings.

So everything that happens on screen is something that happens in a wood: it is planted (loading, the progress bar below), it grows up from its own ground line (arriving), it is walked from start to end (a line, a pass), it is blazed where you point, it gets a new growth ring when it changes, it withers when it leaves. Nothing is cut, stamped, glitched or bounced, and nothing falls.

Already decided by you: arrival = Growing (the key-figure strip), live = Growth ring (the trend tile), hover = Blazed (the network graph), tone = the field note (the strip), shape = the wooden gauge (the meter), loading = the trees being planted (the progress bar), and the network graph itself changes in no theme. The questions below turn those picks into rules for every other component. The full analysis (every decided pick measured, the families, the twenty outliers and the proposals) is in themes/forest/CHARACTER.md.
