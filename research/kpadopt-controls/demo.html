Segmented control, list toolbar and key line

Three parts Kenny’s admin dashboard still draws itself because kp-themes 10.1.1 has no component for them (its toggleGroup, segSwitch, toolbar, filterChip and keyRow). Each is built here on the package: the stylesheet rules are in css/components.css and every register, the behaviour in js/segmented.js, js/toolbar.js and js/keys.js. Everything on this page is the package; only the list of containers, the filtering of it and the “hover” and “focus” rows are this page’s (stand-ins, named where they appear). Judge in the review dialog (“Review in a dialog” at the top): sixteen themes, three sections each.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the plate under a chosen segment appears in place instead of gliding. Turn it off to judge the movement.

1 · Segmented control and filter chips

One component, two looks. The segmented look is a track with a plate under the chosen value; the chip look is the same buttons as separate pills. A group chooses one value (a radio group: one tab stop, the arrow keys move and choose) or several (toggle buttons: arrows move, Space or Enter turns the value; none on means All, turning every value on is the same as none). Try it with the keyboard: Tab into a control, then the arrow keys, Home and End.

The chosen segment is a card on a ledger track with a navy rule under its word (the quill underline); chips are square-cornered and fill navy. The label keeps one weight, so nothing shifts when you press.

A white pill floats on a pale track with a soft lift and glides to the next value on the theme’s easing. Chips are round pills that fill indigo.

A machined slot: the chosen segment is a lit slot with a bright top edge, small capitals, square ends. The glide is quick and square.

Neon trace: the chosen word is lit inside a thin yellow frame with a glow; after a press the word splits into cyan and red for three steps. It must not play on load.

A VCR display: spaced capitals, the plate a pink-to-cyan gradient with a glow. Check the dark word reads on both ends of the gradient.

A sticker on washi tape (dashed track): the chosen plate is a raspberry sticker with a darker peel edge beneath, and it springs a little past the next value before it settles. Chips peel the same way.

Reverse video: the chosen segment is a solid green block with black type and it jumps in one step, no glide. Monospace throughout.

The chosen segment is a leaf: two rounded corners, two nearly square, with a darker edge under it. Chips take the same leaf when they are on.

Drafting hatch: a cyan-hatched box with a thin cyan frame on an open track, mono capitals; the plate moves in drafting steps.

Embers: the plate is an orange-to-red gradient with a warm halo; chips glow when on. The glow must not bleed over the neighbouring segment.

A slab: thick black frame with a hard drop, bold capitals; the chosen segment is a yellow block with a thick black inset frame, and it slams across in a blink.

A gilt frame: a gold hairline plate with a double inlay rule inside it, spaced capitals. The inlay must stay inside the plate while it moves.

Cut paper: a red plate with the top-start and bottom-end corners snipped, condensed capitals. Chips are flat red with a deep second plate behind. Tab to a chosen chip: the focus ring must stay whole.

A transit board: no box; the segments hang from one black rule and the chosen one carries a thick red bar under its word, which runs to the next value. Chips are plain frames that invert when on.

A CRT panel: a tan track, the chosen segment a dark block with faint scanlines in monospace capitals; the movement is stepped like a refresh.

An anodised badge: the chosen plate is a chamfered, knurled block in the primary colour and moves in one linear glide (metal does not ease). Check the chamfer clips cleanly at both ends.

Press / anywhere outside a field to jump to the search; Escape in the search clears it.

Search box (the theme’s own field), then the filter group labels (small capitals with air between the letters), the chips, and the count at the right edge. Type a word, turn a status on: the “Showing only” row appears with removable tags, the same tags as the data table’s. 3 · Inline key line

A row of key caps with what each does, under a list or beside a control. It is not the Help sheet: no box, no dialog, one line that wraps by whole shortcuts. The cap (kbd.kp-kbd) also stands alone: in a button, a hint, a sentence.

Engraved plate caps in the body face, a thin foot. The whole line stays one row of one height; the words follow their caps; two caps for one action (↑ ↓) sit closer than two actions. The last line wraps by whole shortcuts, never mid-shortcut. Ctrl K shows ⌘ on a Mac.
