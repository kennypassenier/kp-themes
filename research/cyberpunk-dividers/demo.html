A new divider for cyberpunk: six options

Kenny, judging catalogue/page-effects.html#dividers in cyberpunk: a new section divider for this theme only, with a few options that fit it. The first block is the razor tear as it ships. Each block after it is one option, drawn with the same markup as the catalogue block: a hero surface, data-kp-divider, an app surface, data-kp-divider="alt", the footer. The surfaces, the footer and the tear are package. Every option is mock: rules in this page's <style>, written as [data-theme='cyberpunk'] .cd-<option> [data-kp-divider] so they go into css/cyberpunk-register.css by dropping the wrapper class. No knob, no attribute, nothing outside cyberpunk. Each option block is judged in cyberpunk whatever the menu says; the last block follows the menu, to show the other themes are untouched. Loaded registers: cyberpunk, synthwave, nostromo.

2 · Circuit bus mock

Look at: a printed-circuit edge: one cyan bus line across the full width, with short 45-degree stubs that end in ring pads, one above and one below per 90px, and a solid via on the bus. The bus has no break anywhere, also where two stub groups meet. The footer seam draws it in yellow with the stubs flipped. With motion on, a brighter pulse runs along the bus once every four seconds; with reduced motion it is absent.

3 · Data stream mock

Look at: three rows of dashes under a scan hairline, muted yellow with a few bright cyan packets, like a readout scrolling past. The yellow pattern repeats every 144px and the cyan one every 216px, so the eye finds no short period. With motion on, the two layers drift right at two speeds and loop without a jump; the footer seam flows left. With reduced motion both stand still.

4 · Glitch slice mock

Look at: the bottom edge of the surface above, broken into horizontal slices that sit a few pixels too high or too low, with a cyan hairline tracing the broken edge and thin cyan, red and yellow ghost slices below it: the headline's slice glitch, applied to a seam. It is the closest in spirit to the tear, but in straight cuts. One shape stretched to the width, so there is no repeat to see. With motion on, the ghost slices jump sideways by 6 to 10px for a quarter of a second every five seconds, the footer seam half a cycle later.
