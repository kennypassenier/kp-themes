Fixes and options from the project threads, each in the package. The remind-me picker's width, the Copy button in a key-value cell, the checklist and timer list when loading, empty or failed; three options (action tile size, a calm freshness line counted in days, a computed field in a sentence form); the hover card on touch and the wheel's answer, kiosk and resting states; and the form grid (a form block's gap, a JSON field's action, the refresh bar's label, two review-kit measures). 16 pairs, all in formal. Every section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: The picker had no width of its own: wherever its cell, row or bar sized itself from what it held, the picker came out as wide as nothing, so “Not snoozed” broke letter by letter and each page had to give it a fixed width. Now the picker is as wide as its button and its line, and fills the room it is given: in a column of a grid it takes the column, in a bar it sits among the other buttons, and in a narrow cell End snooze goes under the button. What changed since you approved it: Action tiles had one size in every place they were used, so a page with many small actions and a page with three important ones looked the same. The board, one tile, or any element above them can now say data-kp-action-size="compact" or "large". The tile’s padding, title, sentence and mark scale together, and so does the width a column may shrink to, so a compact board fits more tiles on a line and a large one fewer. With no attribute nothing changes. The option follows the attribute at runtime, in every theme.

What changed since you approved it: Every field in a sentence form was something the person typed or chose. A sentence can now hold a read-only computed field: <output class="kp-sentence__computed" data-kp-sentence-computed="ready"> inside a .kp-sentence__field, with a function that gets the value of the other fields and returns its words (attachSentences(root, { computed }), sentence(form).compute(name, fn), or setSentenceComputed(form, name, text) to push the text in). It is drawn on a quiet plate with no underline in the primary colour, so it is not mistaken for something to fill in; it is not a tab stop, is not sent with the form, and is part of the spoken summary. With nothing to say it shows a dash.
