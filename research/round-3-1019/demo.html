Fixes from the project threads, round 3

Fixes and options from the project threads, each in the package. The remind-me picker's width, the Copy button in a key-value cell, the checklist and timer list when loading, empty or failed; three options (action tile size, a calm freshness line counted in days, a computed field in a sentence form); the hover card on touch and the wheel's answer, kiosk and resting states; and the form grid (a form block's gap, a JSON field's action, the refresh bar's label, two review-kit measures). 16 pairs, all in formal. Every section says under What changed since you approved it what was wrong and what is different now.

What changed since you approved it: The picker had no width of its own, and the first fix still left it loose: buttons were sized by their words, so End snooze stood at 116 px under a button in a 208 px cell (the rest of the line empty), beside a differently wide button in a table row, and the status line stood apart from both. Now the picker is one grid: the button and End snooze are equal tracks that fill the whole width together, End snooze goes under the button at the full width when two do not fit, a lone button takes the whole width, and the status line spans every track. The table, its selection bar and the form row are laid out on tracks too (name, picker and action fill the plate; Open, Mark read and Delete fill their cells). No button overlaps another and nothing is taller than its content. The choices, the menu, the date panel and the snoozed line are as you approved them.
