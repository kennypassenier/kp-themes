# Brief: phantom, as it stands on 2026-10-08

Sources read: css/phantom-register.css (all 2878 lines), themes/phantom/tokens.json, themes/phantom/anatomy.md,
the `[data-theme='phantom']` blocks in css/themes.css, research/THEME_PROFILES.md (its phantom section is only the

## 1. The world

Persona 5 menus and the "Calling Card": black, white and one violent red, cut paper, a halftone screen, condensed
italic capitals. "Cyberpunk emits light; phantom is print" (register header). Energy without light emission:
shapes are transforms and clip-paths, shadows are hard and offset, nothing glows, nothing loops except loaders.
The red is always a plate with black ink and never a word or text colour (red on black is 4.12:1); white on red
is never used. The sidebar is the one loud sidebar in the set (deep red).

- Fonts: display `'Barlow Condensed'` 900 italic UPPERCASE (headings, brand, labels, buttons, tabs, toast text);
  body `'Barlow'`; mono `ui-monospace, 'SFMono-Regular', Menlo, Consolas` for ids, help, errors, microlabels, spec.
- Radius: `0rem`. Everything is a rectangle, a parallelogram (`--kp-skew: -8deg`) or a clip-path polygon.
- Colours: background `hsl(0 0% 4%)` ~#0A0A0A; foreground `hsl(0 0% 98%)` ~#FAFAFA; card 9% (#171717); popover 13%;
  primary/destructive/ring/selected `hsl(355 100% 58%)` ~#FF2E3C (lifted from the demo's #E60012 so black ink reads);
  the deep red "second plate" `--kp-red-deep` = `--sidebar-background` `hsl(355 100% 45%)` ~#E60012; accent plate
  `hsl(0 0% 86%)` with black ink; border 22%, border-strong 60%; signal/overprint `hsl(45 100% 55%)` (yellow, used
  for warnings and invalid fields only). Status plates: draft grey, sent 90%, screening yellow, offer pure white,
  rejected deep red. Chart: red, 90% white, 55% grey, deep red, yellow.
- Texture (static, never moves): `--fx-texture` = a 7px halftone dot grid (white dots, 0.9px) plus a 1px/3px scan
  line, `--fx-texture-opacity: 0.14` on the page (register overrides themes.css's 0.05); `body::before` adds a
  desaturated fractal-noise grain, soft-light, 0.1; the hero carries a denser dot grid (0.55) and a red slash
  silhouette `clip-path: polygon(58% 0, 100% 0, 100% 100%, 40% 100%, 52% 62%, 45% 60%, 60% 24%, 51% 22%)` that is
  `linear-gradient(160deg, primary 0 62%, red-deep 62% 100%)`. Knobs: `--fx-ease: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.25, 1)`,
  `--fx-duration: 120ms`, `--kp-cut: cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.3, 1)`, `--kp-out: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`,
  `--kp-shove: cubic-bezier(0.81, -0.01, 0, 1)`, `--kp-paperclip: polygon(0 6%, 3% 0, 100% 0, 100% 88%, 97% 100%, 0 100%)`.

## 4. Recurring motifs

1. The skew: `--kp-skew: -8deg` on buttons, badges, tags, icon buttons, switch, checkbox, meter, pagination; `-16deg`
   on the red hover bars; `-24deg` on the rail and wizard separators; `-10deg` on the intro word.
2. The red plate: red as a solid block with black ink, sliding out from behind text (nav, tab, footer, button fill, sidenav).
3. The deep-red second plate / misregistration: a darker red copy offset a few pixels (progress bar `::after`, meter,
   chart "off-register plates", hard shadows `5px 5px 0 var(--kp-red-deep)`).
4. Hard offset shadows with zero blur: headings (black 4px + red 9px), toast 6px, dialog 10px, tooltip 4px, laurels, checkbox 3px.
5. Halftone dots: page, hero, progress track, meter, skeleton, empty state, dialog backdrop, loading shuffles.
6. Cut paper: `--kp-paperclip` clipped corners + 4-5px red left edge on cards, popovers, menus, wizard, spec; torn divider.
7. Slightly rotated objects: dialog -1.5deg, tooltip -3deg, alarm title -3deg, stamp label -6deg, laurels +-2deg, wizard numerals.
8. The five-point star (spinner, radio) and the slash (skeleton, hero, calendar red cut, corner slash on cards).
9. Stamp / pin / string / evidence-board vocabulary in the picks: red string, stamped ring, ransom note, target, bullet holes, dossier, redaction bar.
10. Hard stepped or snapping motion: `steps(3)`, 5-turn snap, jolts, "hard jumps"; the `--kp-cut` / `--kp-shove` curves.
11. Mono + `▮` red tick before microlabels, field labels and platforms.

## 5. Anchor candidates already present

Strongest, recognisable at a glance:

- The red bar that grows behind an item as a skewed parallelogram (nav link, tab, footer link, button fill, sidenav):
  widest reach, one idea, uniquely phantom. It is used on hover, so it is a "state", not an element.
- The skewed key-cap button with its hard deep-red drop shadow and press that drops it onto the shadow (3px, 5px).
- The progress bar: skewed parallelogram track, diagonal red cut with a deep-red plate off register and a white shard
  head; busy form is the 1500ms shove. It is the most fully drawn single element and already carries all motifs.
- The Calling Card itself: intro `.kp-boot` (word + running red bar, shoved off left) and the card-shaped surfaces.
- The torn-paper divider and the hero's red slash silhouette (static decorations, strong but not a UI element).
- The star spinner and the stamp-slam check mark.

Weak or generic:

- `[data-kp-leaving]` leave: ghostly waver + blur + red glow, ease-in 650ms. Soft, blurred, and the only glow in
  the register; reads as "ghost fade" rather than print. Contradicts anatomy ("no glow, no blur").
- The register's `kp-cut-in` (dead: overridden), `kp-slide-in` badge (a plain slide), tooltip pop (scale .4 to 1 with
  `--fx-ease`), the dialog (scale 1.25 + rotate), the tooltip/toast easings (`var(--fx-ease)`, a generic out-curve).
- Update in place: no `--kp-update`, chart update "none", trend live "Redrawn", tiles/columns small jolts.
- The whole drawer character (spectral, vapour, fog, halo) is ghost-themed, not print-themed.

## 6. Overlaps

- grotesk: same red/black/white triad and almost the same red (hsl(355,95%,45%) vs phantom's 355 100% 58% / 45%);
  zero radius; a stranger glancing at the red could mix them. Grotesk is white-ground Swiss and flat (no skew, no halftone).
- cyberpunk: condensed caps display (Big Shoulders vs Barlow Condensed), black ground, hard shadows, `steps()` cuts,
  glitch/jolt motions. Cyberpunk is yellow-on-violet-black with glow; phantom is print without glow.
- brutalism: zero radius, hard offset shadows, black borders. Brutalism is cream-ground with no skew.
- synthwave: slanted italic display and a hot pink/red, skewed bars; synthwave glows and has gradients.
- retro and pastel: hard offset shadows (retro `steps()`), halftone (pastel mentions it); neither is skewed.
- Internal: the "ghost" reading of the name (drawer picks, wavering leave, blur) versus the Persona-5 print metaphor
  in everything else.
