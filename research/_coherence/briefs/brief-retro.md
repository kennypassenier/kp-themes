## 1. The world

- The Windows 95 desktop. Register header: "the 1995 desktop"; tokens comment: "grey chrome, a navy title bar, bevels around a gated boundary. Nothing blinks." The approved concept demo is "Bevel 95" (2026-09-08), reference 98.css.
- The root is the teal desktop; `body` is the one application window on it (max 84rem, raised bevel, hard 4px drop). Everything inside is a window part: title bar, menu strip, status bar with resize grip, sunken wells, raised buttons, groove dividers, a 16px two-arrow checkerboard scrollbar drawn by hand.
- The bevel is decoration INSIDE a 3:1 boundary (`--border-strong`), never the boundary (original bevel measured 2.17:1). No dotted focus rectangle (DI2); the ring is navy outside, ink inside.
- Time model: `--fx-duration: 0ms`, `--fx-ease: linear`. Everything that moves moves in hard `steps()`; 1995 "did not ease". Anatomy says "nothing loops", but the 2026-10-03 signature layer added four infinite loops (hourglass, skeleton dither, meter handshake). The anatomy text is stale on that point.
- Fonts: display `'Pixelify Sans', 'Silkscreen', 'Courier New'` (headings only, h1-h3, card/dialog titles, brand); body `'Instrument Sans', 'Segoe UI', Tahoma` (also the alarm headline, scope-108, Kenny rejected the pixel face as "te grof"); DOS voice `--kp-dos: 'VT323'` (labels, help, status, badges, tags, ids, tooltips, spec sheet, boot); mono `ui-monospace`.
- Radius `0rem`, `--fx-notch 0px`, `--fx-lift 0px`, `--fx-shadow-offset 0px`, chart patterns all `none`.
- Colours (all hsl in tokens): chrome/background `hsl(0,0%,75%)` #bfbfbf; card #d6d6d6; popover/window #e6e6e6; ink #1a1a1a; primary (title-bar navy) `hsl(240,100%,25%)` #000080; accent/signal teal `hsl(180,100%,20%)` #006666; teal ink for labels `--kp-teal-ink` 15% = #004c4c; desktop `--kp-desktop` = accent at 25% = #008080; ramp end `--sidebar-accent` #0d61ab; border #808080, border-strong #616161; destructive #800000; success #006600; warning #ffd400; info #004c99; chart-4 purple #80007f. `--fx-hot-alarm` #990000. Light theme (`color-scheme: light`).
- Texture: `--fx-texture` = 2px-cell checkerboard (`repeating-conic-gradient`, 4x4 tile) at opacity 0.04 ("the dither every gradient was"). The 50% dither brush `--kp-brush` (2x2 conic checker) is the register's recurring fill; `--kp-dd-*` / `--kp-dm-*` are four dither densities (100/75/50/25) as ground and as mask.
- Bevel stacks (custom properties): `--kp-raised`, `--kp-pressed`, `--kp-sunken`, `--kp-raised-navy`, `--kp-pressed-navy`; `--kp-groove-line`; `--kp-ramp` (navy to #0d61ab, 90deg); `--kp-drop` 3px hard 30%; `--kp-drop-hard` 4px hard 35%.

## 2. The register's signature today

Progress uses `--kp-rp-block/-gap/-dot/-pitch/-run`. `--kp-open: reverse-close` (line 3064). There is NO `--kp-update` in retro (updates in place do nothing; js/update.js treats empty as no animation). Layers: register = `kp.register`, signature = `kp.signature` (draws over the register).

## 4. Recurring motifs

1. **The four-inset bevel** (raised / pressed / sunken, white-top-left, ink-bottom-right) on almost every surface; sunken wells for any value or load, raised for anything pressable.
2. **The navy title-bar ramp** (#000080 to #0d61ab, 90deg) on brand, dialog title, toast strip, card header, spec sheet, tiles/kpi/trend shapes.
3. **The navy selection bar with white text**: hover on links/menu/nav, mark, active combobox option, picked day, Start menu pick. "Every hover is the selection bar, instantly."
4. **The 50% dither brush / checkerboard** (`--kp-brush`, 2x2 conic): disabled-control brush, skeleton, redactions, busy bar, empty state icon, hourglass sand, dissolves, scrollbar track, page texture.
5. **Navy progress blocks** (segmented bar, 10px blocks with 3px gaps / 0.5rem + 2px): progressbar, boot POST, and the picked "progress blocks" loading in trend, columns, menu, tiles, busy, calendar, chart.
6. **Hard step motion**: `steps(n)` with no easing, one-way only; reveals are discrete densities (4), selection (8), groove (12); knock/shake in 2 and 10.
7. **The hard offset drop shadow** (3-4px, no blur) under windows, menus; zero blur anywhere.
8. **The DOS voice** (VT323): labels, help, ids, tags, tooltip, boot; `> ` prompt with a static block cursor on microlabels.
9. **The black-well LED / "performance monitor" panel**: meter, state "scope", the monitor shape options (only the meter's is picked in the register).
10. **Marching ants / dotted focus lines / the hourglass**: Win95's busy and selection vocabulary (spinner, graph loading, calendar pick, Start menu focus).

## 5. Anchor candidates already present

**Strongest, most recognisable (could be THE anchor):**

- The **hourglass spinner** (`.kp-spinner`, `kp-sig-retro-turn` + `kp-sig-retro-sand`): bespoke clip-path draw, the one object everyone remembers from 1995, already animated with sand that drains and a flip. Only retro-exclusive "character" element that moves.
- The **boot POST** (`.kp-boot`): complete, once-per-session, with BIOS lines, memory count, segmented bar and a four-density dither exit. Strongest arrival; but it is an intro (once per session), not a recurring anchor, and the headline dissolve/groove-in reuse its dither.
- The **window frame itself** (`body` as window on the teal root, drawn window controls on `.kp-nav::after`, resize grip on `.kp-footer::after`): the "page is an application window" is the one idea that makes the theme read 1995, but it is static.

**Real but secondary:** the segmented navy progress blocks (signature progress bar, share jumps whole blocks, busy = still dither); the selection bar drag (`mark`, 8 steps); the press that moves only the label by 1px (`translate` + `::before` repaint); the hand-drawn scrollbar; the accelerator underline (the theme's quirk, scope-80).

**Weak / generic:**

- Dialog open (`kp-sig-retro-zoom`, scale .25 to 1 in 4 steps) and the leave (`scale(0.1) + opacity`, 4 steps): a stepped scale-and-fade, the same shape as many zoom entrances; only the stepping is retro.
- Toast drop (`translateY(-12px)` in 3 steps): a stepped slide, generic.
- Tooltip (`step-end` delay): just a 500ms wait, no picture.
- Skeleton dither pulse (opacity .15-.6, 4 steps): a stepped pulse.
- Button hover: nothing visible except the accelerator underline; no hover picture of its own.
- No `--kp-update`, no drawer/tour/chart register CSS: updates are "redrawn" (no motion).
- The retro buttons and cards: bevels are a static skin, not a motion.

Gaps a designer could fill: an update-in-place picture; a button/hover signature beyond the selection bar; a closing that is the exact reverse of the zoom (0.25 vs 0.1 mismatch); the tooltip and toast open/close asymmetries logged in research/PACKAGE_FINDINGS.md line 39 ("Tooltips of high-contrast (140 ms) and retro (500 ms): open = wait, then appear at once; close = stay, then vanish at once. The close must be the open reversed").

## 6. Overlaps (what an outsider could confuse)

- **terminal**: VT323 DOS voice, `> ` prompt with block cursor (`.microlabel::before/::after`), square corners, hard steps. Terminal is green on near-black monospace (primary hsl(120,90%,50%)) and uses ~27 `steps(`; retro's mono voice is only the label layer. The meter's black-well green LED panel and "scope" state/header monitor look phosphor-like.
- **synthwave**: shares the VT323 file ("shipped with synthwave") for its OSD data voice, so the same typeface appears in two registers.
- **nostromo**: black-well LED meter, indicator lamps, steps-heavy (~40 `steps(`); a CRT/amber console world. The DRAWER picks for retro (CRT cabinet, woodgrain, scanline wipe, tube-glow halo, hi-fi dials) are nostromo/70s-console in spirit and contradict Win95; they are not implemented, but as decided picks they are an inconsistency.
- **brutalism**: hard offset shadow (no blur), square corners, hazard stripes in the alarm; brutalism's are loud poster colours, retro's are 3-4px grey-ink shadows. Also both ship a yellow tooltip-like warning plate (retro's tooltip mixes `--warning` 30% into popover).
- **high-contrast**: the alarm's hazard stripes (high-contrast uses black bars; retro uses `--warning` yellow); both have a "Windows-ish" system feel (high-contrast body `Instrument Sans` = retro's body face, so body type is identical).
- **formal**: also uses `Instrument Sans` body and a navy primary (hsl(218,45%,24%)), but with serif display and soft grey-cream ground; retro's navy is pure #000080.
- **blueprint**: navy ground (not primary) and 2 `steps(`; different visual world, little real overlap.
- Internal seams: calendar shape (floppy label) vs tone/select/loading (tear-off pad); busy and kpi both "floppy label"/"title bar" shapes reused across components; trend/tiles/kpi all use the title-bar ramp (consistent, but means no component has its own unique shape).
