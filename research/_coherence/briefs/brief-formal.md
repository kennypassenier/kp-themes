## 1. The world

- Paper and ink. A serious editorial document that expects to be read at length: warm stock, near-black ink with a blue cast, one navy that carries every interactive thing. Bronze is the second voice (emphasis, never a control); gold `--fx-signal` is "wet ink" / a signal, never an action.
- Formal "stays still while the others move". Motion "commits, it does not perform": a fade, a rise, a rule that draws once, a stamp that lands. Never glitches, never decipher/type effects on text, nothing loops on text. (The only loops are small: spinner orbit, skeleton ink, meter busy bar.)
- Vocabulary Kenny has chosen for it in every demo: ledger, engraved plate, certificate, annual report, docket, seal / wax seal, stamp, red ink (accountant's), dotted leader, double rule, small capitals, guilloche, quill/pen, minute book, letterhead.
- Anatomy rules ("what this theme may not do"): no pure white or pure black, no bronze/gold action, no visible texture beyond the 3.5% grain, no serif body text, never manipulate the headline's characters, no glow, no loop of a reveal.
- Fonts: display `'Fraunces', Georgia, serif` on h1-h3, card titles, wizard numerals, empty-state title (opsz 144 on a surface h1, weight 560/600; italic for `.kp-empty__title`). Body `'Instrument Sans'`. Mono is only `ui-monospace` (formal has "no mono voice" in the register, though the chart/calendar ledger picks use mono figures in the demos).
- Radius: `--radius: 0.375rem`; small things radius/2; status dot, timeline marker, datepicker day, switch pill are round. Meter and progress bar are square (1px / 0).
- Main colours (hsl in tokens, hex computed):
    - paper `--background` hsl(40,25%,97%) #f9f8f5; card stock `--card` hsl(40,30%,99%) #fdfdfc; dossier/footer `--muted` #ebe9e5
    - ink `--foreground` hsl(220,30%,13%) #171e2b; `--muted-foreground` #515867
    - navy `--primary` hsl(218,45%,24%) #223659 (the one acting hue); ring hsl(218,45%,40%)
    - bronze `--accent` hsl(30,40%,88%) #ede0d4 (mark wash), `--accent-foreground` hsl(25,45%,22%)
    - gold signal `--fx-signal` hsl(38,55%,44%) #ae8132 (mirror button offset, laurels, ribbon on the meter)
    - brick red `--destructive` hsl(0,60%,40%) #a32929 (stamp, red-ink entries, voided stamps)
    - rules: `--border` hairline #dedbd3 (1.31:1), `--border-strong`/`--input` hsl(220,14%,56%) #7f8a9f (3.3:1)
    - status: success #276d3e, warning #835a21, info #335e8e (ink on tinted plates). Sidebar is dark navy hsl(220,32%,13%) with gold hsl(38,55%,62%).
- Texture: T5 "Gravure": laid-paper fractal-noise grain, `--fx-texture-opacity: 0.035`, 240px tile. Felt, not seen (DI9 cap 0.06). That is the only page texture.
- Global motion tokens: `--fx-duration: 180ms`, `--fx-ease: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` (a fast-out, long settle; "commits"). `--fx-lift: 0px`, `--fx-shadow-offset: 0px`, `--fx-notch: 0px` (no lift, no hard shadow, no cut corners).

## 4. Recurring motifs

1. **The double rule** (thin line inside a thicker one, "the way a diploma or letterhead frames what matters"). Everywhere: button hover/focus inner rule, alarm panel, dialog mat + hairline, switch ring, link hover (`text-decoration-style: double`), 3px double skeleton/empty-state top rule, divider/progress "docket cut", and the decided engraved plates, certificates, today-in-calendar, the closed-account line on the progress bar.
2. **The docket cut / ledger tick**: a strong hairline with a short (3rem or 4px band) navy tick under it. Divider, progress bar, alarm title.
3. **A rule that draws once, left to right** (scaleX / clip-path from the left). Section rule, toast top rule, checkbox pen tick, size-ink, meter wipe, trend/tiles "engraved", skeleton ink. This is the default verb of the whole theme.
4. **The stamp / seal** (wax seal, rotated stamp frame, press-and-lift). Dossier "label" stamp (-3deg, brick), update-stamp (frame lands askew then soaks in), and decided: seal is pressed (busy, kpi), received stamp (chart), clerk's stamp (columns), re-sealed (state), voided stamp, wax seal ring/seal is broken (drawer), raised seal (tiles).
5. **Red ink** (accountant's): brick `--destructive` only, as a left-edge rule, a tick, a voided stamp, the dossier stamp. The one non-navy "act" colour, used for meaning not for action.
6. **The dotted leader** ("to be entered"): progress busy dots, and the decided loading for busy, kpi, tiles, columns, menu, calendar. Static or written dot by dot.
7. **Small capitals / tracked caps + Fraunces display**: nav strapline, microlabel, tooltip, marquee, menu captions, footer headings, and all decided shape plates (engraved plate = small-caps title in a double frame).
8. **Ruled ledger lines / paper**: empty-state ruled lines, tooltip ledger slip, book fore-edge lines on the meter, 4 ledger rules in trend, three-stock paper (page 97 / card 99 / muted 91).

11. **Guilloche** (share-certificate fine rings): not in the register yet; appears in decided kpi hover, graph border, header menu ideas, and the anatomy mentions it.

## 6. Overlaps (what an outsider might confuse)

- **light**: closest neighbour. Anatomy says "move the ground to pure white and formal becomes light". Same Instrument Sans/Fraunces-ish restraint, same hairline cards, "one colour acts". Anything formal does with plain fade/rise or a soft card will read as light.
- **sepia**: shares the paper/ink/typography world and much of the vocabulary: its register has a double gold rule / double warm-brown divider, a dossier stamp and redactions, and its decided picks are letterpress, quill stroke, rubber stamp, wax-seal beads, "The seal" (graph live), index card, drawer pushed in. Stamp, seal, quill, ledger-like rules and "written by hand" are therefore NOT unique to formal.
- **deco**: gilt/gold double rules, certificate-like framed plates, fan/skyline arrival; formal's gold ribbon, guilloche and double frame can look deco if gold is pushed. Formal stays navy-led and gold-light.
- **titanium**: the twin in the character rounds (formal and titanium were built first, their grammar is symmetric: engraved plate vs milled plate, wax seal vs machined tick). Engraved/etched/plate/rivet language overlaps (chart "Etched in", "engraved dial face").
- **blueprint**: hairline rules, ticks at the ends of a column (dimension-line look) and drawn-once lines; the progress bar's end ticks resemble a dimension line.
- **brutalism / grotesk**: the dossier stamp (rotated, bordered) exists in brutalism and blueprint registers too, so a rotated bordered stamp alone is not distinctive.
- **pastel / retro**: "stamp" and "sticker" language (pressed sticker, calling-card stamp) in other themes' chart/meter picks.
