## 1. The world

A spectral instrument. Near-black ground with a faint ruled grid, near-white ink, and ONE mechanism: an
anodised oxide film. Anodising adds no pigment; film thickness picks which wavelength survives, so colour
shifts with viewing angle. The film is `--kp-iris`, a conic gradient through cyan, violet, magenta, lime whose
start angle turns with the pointer (`--kp-pointer: track`, `--kp-px`/`--kp-py`, default 0.5; js/effects.js writes them).
It runs along every edge that matters: panel border, button bottom edge, field underline, heading rule,
divider centre line, progress fill, spinner ring. Everything else is quiet so the film can be seen. Parts are
machined: corners cut at 45 degrees, never rounded; brackets close on labels. Motion is "notation, not
colour"; nothing loops by itself in the old register (the 2026-10-03 signature added spinner/skeleton loops).
Hard rule (anatomy): nothing moves that the reader did not move; no pure black/white; colour is only the film
and meaning.

- Fonts: display and body `'Archivo'` (700 headings, 600 buttons); mono `'KP Ticker Mono'` (brackets,
  readouts, stamp, microlabels, side note, timestamps, kbd).
- Radius `0rem`. Chamfer `--fx-notch: 0.55rem` (controls), `--kp-chamfer-panel: 1rem` (panels), clip-path polygons.
- Colours: ground `hsl(220,16%,5%)` ~#0B0D10; ink `hsl(205,20%,94%)` ~#EAF0F4; primary near-white
  `hsl(200,22%,93%)` ~#E6ECEF (an action is a light plate, not a hue); card `hsl(220,15%,8%)`; popover
  `hsl(220,16%,10%)`; footer/sidebar `hsl(220,16%,7%)`; border `hsl(214,12%,17%)`; border-strong/input
  `hsl(214,12%,30%)`; ring/selected/fx-signal `hsl(190,90%,66%)`.
  Film stops: chart-1 cyan `hsl(188,96%,62%)` ~#3DEAFB, chart-2 violet `hsl(268,90%,70%)` ~#A66CF8,
  chart-3 magenta `hsl(318,92%,66%)` ~#F560C5, chart-4 lime `hsl(78,88%,60%)` ~#BFF03B. Danger `hsl(8,92%,66%)`
  coral red. Status plates are deep tints under luminous text.
- Texture: none as a layer (starfield removed). The page `body` carries a fixed instrument grid (hairlines of ink at
  4.5 %, every `--kp-grid` 5.5rem, both axes) plus a 46x34rem pool of chart-1 light (13 %) centred on the
  pointer (`--kp-px`,`--kp-py`). No other texture, no scanlines.
- Timing tokens: `--fx-duration: 220ms`, `--fx-ease: cubic-bezier(0.16, 0.84, 0.28, 1)`,
  `--kp-settle: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` ("Dead North's shout-in ease"), `--kp-word-stagger: 28ms`,
  `--kp-redact-stagger: 90ms`. `--fx-lift: 0px`, `--fx-shadow-offset: 0px`, chart patterns none.
- Reveals declared: `--kp-reveal-headline: resolve`, `--kp-reveal-emphasis: ignite`, `--kp-reveal-rule: sweep`.
  No arrival (intro): "the page is simply there". No `--kp-update` is declared. `--kp-open: reverse-close`.

## 4. Recurring motifs

1. **The oxide film** (`--kp-iris`, cyan-violet-magenta-lime, pointer-turned): border, edge, rule, underline, fill, ring.
2. **The 45-degree chamfer on two opposite corners** (clip-path): buttons, fields, badges, panels, tooltip, skeleton, track, wizard numbers, switch thumb, meter channel.
3. **Corner/side brackets**: `[` `]` on buttons, 4-corner brackets (switch, wizard current, empty state), `]` head of the progress bar, `]` legend, `[ ]` tooltip slit opening, blue-white closing bracket mark on the spectrometer.
4. **Misregistration / two-wavelength split**: kp-resolve text-shadow halves, skeleton ghosts (chart-1 left, chart-3 right), checkbox and radio ghost marks converging.
5. **Darkroom / develop**: dialog and toast film flash fading out (develop), arriving content from pale print to contrast, leave to brightness(0).
6. **Ruled instrument grid + ruler ticks**: body grid, divider ticks, progress track ticks, empty-state graph paper, spectrometer rulers.
7. **Machined metal vocabulary** in picks: milling pass, die press, pocket, lit lip, engraved cut, keycap.
8. **Lit status hardware** in picks: lamp, tube switch-on, scope trace, ticker tape, alarm lamp, hard steps.
9. **The pool of light and the turning angle**: the only autonomous-looking motion, driven by the pointer.
10. **Panels lit by the film's four wavelengths** (halo) instead of a grey shadow.

## 5. Anchor candidates already present

Strong (recognisable, specific, ownable):

- **The oxide film that turns with the pointer** (`--kp-iris` + `--kp-px`): the most distinctive thing; every
  edge shares it. But it is a colour device, not an element with a shape or a motion of its own.
- **The bracketed button** (`[` `]` closing on the label + film edge drawn from the left + readout in film-coloured
  letters): the most complete signature element today; fully described, hover/focus/press all designed.
- **kp-resolve headline** (words converge from two wavelengths, blur clearing): unique to dark, but it is a
  once-only, text-level arrival.
- **The progress bar** (chamfered track, film fill, `]` head, hatched busy): a good object, but busy is a static hatch
  and the share arrives only by a 320ms clip-path.
- **The halo** (four coloured shadows): distinctive static atmosphere, not an element.
- **Skeleton ghosts** (misregistration drifting +/-8px) and **dialog 2.4s develop**: characterful, hardly visible in daily use.

Weak or generic:

- Leave (`brightness(0)`, scale 0.98, 460ms ease-in), the nav dropdown (plain opacity fade), menu items (accent ground
  swap), toast (6px rise), generic dialog scale 1.02, meter tone swell (identical for all three tones),
  tooltip slit-open (nice but 260ms), most pointer-less states (static wizard, empty, divider).
- The switch/checkbox/radio are quiet; the check has a ghost, nothing else.
- No intro, no update-in-place motif except the 480ms darkroom develop.
- No element has a sound-of-the-theme loop: spinner is a plain linear ring in film colours.

The gap a new anchor could fill: the film is present as colour everywhere, but there is no single object whose
BEHAVIOUR is the film (angle-of-view shifting, thickness growing, interference). The decided picks add lit-lamp /
tube / ticker behaviour, not film behaviour.

## 6. Overlaps (what an outsider may confuse with another theme)

- **titanium**: the closest twin. Same `--kp-iris` name and `--kp-px` mechanism (titanium's is a linear
  gradient whose angle moves with the pointer), same chamfer on two corners, same "anodised / oxide / machined"
  vocabulary, same "mill pass / cutter" chart loading, engraved/pocket language, press as paint not box move.
  Dark = near-black + cyan/violet/magenta/lime film; titanium = brushed grain metal + heat-tint.
- **cyberpunk**: cut corners (clip-path chamfer), neon-adjacent cyan and magenta, glow, and the kp-resolve cyan/magenta
  split text-shadow reads as an RGB split like cyberpunk's glitch (Kenny rejected cyberpunk's live update for being too
  near that effect). Differences: dark's cuts are two opposite corners, cyberpunk's bevels vary; no scanline/glitch here.
- **synthwave**: the same violet-magenta-cyan family of hues (dark's film uses the same three plus lime).
- **terminal / nostromo**: the un-ported picks (ticker mono, status lamp, scope trace, tube switch-on, hard steps,
  green oscilloscope trace, "alarm lamp") belong to a CRT-ish dialect that terminal and nostromo own.
- **blueprint**: the measurement frame / corner brackets (scope-18) were moved from blueprint to dark; brackets and
  graph-paper grid (body grid, empty state) can read as blueprint's drafting language.
- **high-contrast / formal**: near-white action plates on a dark ground can look like high-contrast; the grid and
  ruler ticks resemble formal's ledger rules only superficially.
