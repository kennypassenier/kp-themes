# Brief: deco (Art Deco), as it stands 2026-10-08

Sources read: css/deco-register.css (all 2305 lines), themes/deco/tokens.json, themes/deco/anatomy.md, research/THEME_PROFILES.md (## deco), css/themes.css + css/_rules.css (deco blocks), research/families/VERDICTS.md, every research/character-*/decided.json (deco entry) resolved through each demo.js IDEAS table.

## 1. The world

Art Deco, the dark reading: Paris 1925 and the skyscraper lobby ("The Gilded Ascent", approved 2026-09-08, the reference for the register). Gold on blue-black, jewel tones as accents, geometry "drawn with a ruler and a compass": chevrons, double rules, a display face built from circles and straight lines. Recurring props in the picks: lift/elevator, marquee bulbs, sunburst/fan, ziggurat/skyscraper setbacks, curtain, lacquer, gilt. Rule from anatomy.md: "Gold as a flat colour reads as Deco; gold as a gradient reads as a casino." No metallic gradient on gold. Radius is 0, corners are cut (chevron clip-path), never rounded. Nothing floats (no shadows; panels get one gold hairline).

- Fonts: display = Poiret One (hairline, h1-h3, card/table/alert headings only, uppercase, tracking 0.04-0.28em, weight 400); body = Josefin Sans (alias 'KP Deco Sans'); mono = ui-monospace (microlabels, stamps, timestamps, side note).
- Radius: 0rem. color-scheme: dark.
- Colours: background hsl(200,25%,8%) blue-black; card hsl(200,22%,11%); popover hsl(200,20%,14%); sidebar/footer/nav hsl(200,28%,6%); foreground ivory hsl(42,60%,88%); muted-foreground hsl(42,30%,72%); primary GOLD hsl(43,65%,52%) (= ring, sidebar-primary, fx-overprint, fx-scanline; 8.16:1 on ground); selected hsl(43,65%,48%); border-strong/input quieter gold hsl(43,35%,50%); border hsl(200,18%,20%); accent EMERALD hsl(165,60%,32%) (ink hsl(165,60%,6%)); destructive RUBY hsl(355,80%,58%) (= fx-signal); info SAPPHIRE hsl(210,55%,30%); success hsl(150,45%,28%); warning hsl(38,70%,30%). Jewels are plates/accents, never text.
- Texture: ONE page texture, a fine gold chevron/diagonal lattice (css/themes.css + css/_rules.css): two repeating-linear-gradients at 135deg and 45deg, 1px gold line every 12px, tile 24px, --fx-texture-opacity 0.05 (nearly invisible).
- Motion tokens: --fx-duration 160ms, --fx-ease cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) (the standard Material curve), --fx-notch 0, --fx-lift 0, --fx-shadow-offset 0. No --kp-update declared (the default applies; deco has no update-in-place idea of its own in the register). No intro/boot overlay ("There is no arrival": the only entry is the headline's cartouche). The register's only loops are the signature ones (spinner, skeleton, indeterminate bar, meter loading).

## 4. Recurring motifs

1. Double gold rule (`border-style: double` 3-4px; inset-shadow double frames): progress track, dialog, toast, tooltip, empty state, divider, side nav, to-top, media, switch, checkbox, alarm, most picks' "gilt frame".
2. Sunburst / fan of rays (`repeating-conic-gradient` from a lower corner or centre-foot): button hover fan, dialog crest, empty crest, spinner, KPI/menu/trend loading, calendar today, drawer open, graph, chart "fan".
3. Chevron / pennant point and chevron bite: cartouche corner cut, progress-fill tip, busy-bar chevrons (`kp-progressbar-deco-ascent`), toast glyph, columns tone option.
4. Lozenge / diamond: progress head jewel (emerald), divider centre, switch thumb, radio dot, wizard steps, skeleton hexagonal ends, `◆`, `◇`.
5. Stepped ziggurat / skyscraper setbacks: tooltip notch, chart/trend/menu/tile skyscraper, graph ziggurat steps, busy "steps climb".
6. Marquee bulbs / lamp rows: meter lamps, calendar/graph/tiles/kpi/state/busy marquee picks ("bulbs chase", hard steps).
7. Glint along the gilt (a bright sweep): loading for calendar, columns, menu, kpi, "Gilded" live updates.
8. Curtain rising / opening from the centre line (`kp-sig-deco-deco-centre`, `-unfold`, `kp-sig-deco-leave` scaleX(0)): toast, tooltip, checkbox, leave, curtain-rises arrivals.
9. Lift / elevator (floor indicator, ascent): meter lamps, "lift ascends", columns dial, busy chevrons ascending.
10. Flat gold + emerald jewel + lacquer: no gradient; emerald plates as accent (redactions, progress head, alt divider), ruby stamp.

## 5. Anchor candidates already present

Strongest (most recognisable, already in the signature layer):

- The SUNBURST FAN: `repeating-conic-gradient` rays from a point. Already the button hover (gap-4, "motif of this style, the Chrysler Building"), the dialog crest + entrance ("the sun rises", `kp-sig-deco-deco-sunrise`/`-fan`), the empty-state crest, the spinner, and the backbone of many picks (chart, KPI, menu, trend loading, calendar today, drawer). It is the one idea that appears in both the register and the picks. Caveat: it is so pervasive it risks being wallpaper, and conic rays are also a common "retro/synthwave/solstice" device.
- The PROGRESS BAR pennant (fluted gold fill ending in a chevron point, emerald lozenge head, double-rule track, ascending chevrons when busy): the most deco-specific single object and the one Kenny already approved over three rounds; it carries chevron + lozenge + double rule in one thing.
- The CARTOUCHE (triple inset frame with one chevron-bitten corner): the page's only entrance; very distinctive but scoped to the hero h1.

Medium: the double-rule + lozenge divider; the lobby-floor lamp meter (lamps + arrow); the dialog's stepped crest.

Weak / generic (fades and standard easings):

- `kp-sig-deco-leave` is just scaleX(0) + fade (a squeeze, not Deco-specific); everything opening via `--kp-open: reverse-close` inherits that.
- All hover/focus colour transitions (160ms, the Material curve), the rule draw (`kp-rule-in`, a stock left-to-right wipe), `kp-redaction-clear` (2px slide + fade), the knock (translate -3px/+1px, identical for all three tones), `kp-sig-deco-meter-pos` (a slab sliding), skeleton (`kp-sig-deco-deco-open`, a scaleX open then fade), toast/tooltip centre clip, tooltip/toast `ease` = the default curve.
- Every `--fx-ease` use is the generic cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1); nothing in the register has a signature easing. Only the progress bar, meter sweep (0.6,0,0.2,1), and "hard steps" in picks (steps(10) for the lift) have character.
- "Glint runs" and "bulbs chase" are shared with other themes' vocabularies (see 6).

## 6. Overlaps

- sepia: it also has a double GOLD rule as its section divider and rule (`border-block-start: 3px double var(--primary)`, sepia-register.css ~1289; "Public Domain Review's double gold rule"). The double-rule alone does not distinguish deco from sepia; sepia is warm brown/parchment (primary hsl(24,55%,32%)) and light, deco is blue-black.
- formal: double rules too (`3px double var(--border-strong)`, a "double rule 2px inside" the button, double text-decoration) and navy-on-gold ledger/certificate vocabulary, uppercase, radius ~0. formal's gold + engraved plate is the nearest register-level cousin of deco's gilt frame; formal ground is light/navy-ink (primary hsl(218,45%,24%)).
- retro / synthwave: "marquee" appears in retro's meter loading ("The marquee") and synthwave's calendar shape ("The arcade marquee") in THEME_PROFILES. Deco's "marquee bulbs chase" pick is the same device; make sure any anchor does not rely on bulbs alone.
- solstice: warm orange/amber primary with conic/stepped geometry (conic-gradient triangles at solstice-register.css 158/165) and a "sun" language (low sun, embers, sun flares); deco's sunburst fan reads close to a "sun" theme. Gold hsl(43,65%,52%) vs solstice orange hsl(28,85%,58%).
- retro / blueprint / synthwave: heavy use of conic/repeating-gradients (retro dithers, blueprint 13 conics, synthwave grid-sun). A bare conic sunburst is not unique to deco; synthwave's retro-sun is the usual confusion.
- cyberpunk / terminal: glint / scanline sweeps (`fx-scanline` token is set to gold in deco) look like the loading sweep used there.
- titanium/dark: titanium also uses `kp-sig-*-leave` with fades; deco's loading "glint runs across" is a generic highlight sweep shared with dark/titanium-style "milling pass" lines.
- Taxonomy note: deco is the only theme with gold-as-structure on blue-black; nostromo (amber on dark) and phantom (black/red) are the nearest dark neighbours but differ in hue and in being radius 0/angular rather than ornamental.
