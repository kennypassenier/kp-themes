# Brief: theme `light` (as of 2026-10-08, main)

Sources: css/light-register.css (1817 lines; layers kp.register + kp.signature), themes/light/tokens.json, themes/light/anatomy.md, research/THEME_PROFILES.md, research/families/VERDICTS.md, css/themes.css `[data-theme='light']` (l.173), research/character-_/decided.json resolved through each demo.js.

Every other character pick (chart, calendar, graph, trend, columns, menu, kpi, tiles, state, busy, drawer, header) exists as a decided demo in research/character-_ but is NOT yet in css/light-register.css (the "port" is a later session).

## 1. The world

- Theme "Plain Sight" (approved 2026-09-08), lifted in 5.0.0. The neutral one: pure white, near-black ink, one confident indigo. "Restraint is a decision, not an absence of one." The control against which the other themes are read; laurel line: "white isn't neutral, it's a decision".
- Anatomy rules: no mood, no display face, no ornament, no texture, no background image or gradient, nothing loops (in the register as lifted; the later signature added the spinner orbit and the meter loading band, which do loop).
- The characters Kenny picked later lean on a daylight / sun / paper vocabulary (daylight band, sunrise, gnomon shadow, soft shadow, folded paper corner) - that is a layer on top of the anatomy, not in it.
- Fonts: display and body both Instrument Sans (`'Instrument Sans', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`); mono `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas`, used for ids, urls, numerics, timestamps, kp-side-note, kp-platforms, kp-spec dt, log level, wizard steps. Headings: weight 600, letter-spacing -0.01em; brand 700/-0.02em.
- Radius: two vocabularies. Surfaces (card, input, dialog, menu) `--radius: 0.5rem`; buttons, icon buttons, chips, tooltip, switch, skeleton lines, health dot, pagination current, datepicker selected day, timeline marker = full pill (`--kp-radius-pill`, 999px). Checkbox 0.3rem, swatch 0.4rem.
- Colours: background/card/popover `hsl(0 0% 100%)` #ffffff; foreground `hsl(224 25% 12%)` ~#171b26; primary indigo `hsl(243 60% 45%)` ~#3d2e8f; ring `hsl(243 60% 55%)`; accent pale cyan `hsl(190 80% 92%)` ~#d1f4fa (accent-foreground `hsl(200 70% 18%)`); `--fx-signal` saturated cyan `hsl(190 80% 40%)` ~#14a3b8 (decorative fill only, never text: passes 3:1 not 4.5:1); muted `hsl(220 15% 95%)`; muted-foreground `hsl(224 12% 38%)`; border `hsl(220 13% 88%)`; border-strong/input `hsl(220 10% 57%)`; success/warning/info are pale tints (88% L) with dark inks; destructive `hsl(0 72% 42%)`. Charts: indigo, teal-cyan, green, orange, magenta.
- Texture: none. `--fx-notch: 0px`, `--fx-shadow-offset: 0px`, `--chart-pattern-1..5: none`, `--fx-overprint`/`--fx-scanline` are just the accent cyan. Elevation comes only from three shadow steps (relative colours of --foreground): `--kp-shadow-sm 0 1px 2px /.06`, `-md 0 8px 24px /.12`, `-lg 0 20px 48px /.22`.
- Timing tokens (css/themes.css): `--fx-duration: 150ms`, `--fx-ease: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, `--fx-lift: 2px`. Register's own: `--kp-ease-arrive: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (notion.so settle), `--kp-ease-hover: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (loom.com).

## 4. Recurring motifs

1. The seam and its circle: a 1px hairline with a small circle on it - divider, empty state, the (not picked) seam meter/chart/calendar shapes, calendar "today". The theme's only drawn ornament.
2. Indigo + cyan pair: indigo (`--primary`) is the action and the line; cyan (`--fx-signal`/`--accent`) is the light - bead heads, spinner's second bead, empty-state dot, glints, highlighter marks, laurel rule. Signal cyan only as fill, never text.
3. Daylight band: a soft cyan/primary band crossing a surface slowly (meter, columns, kpi, calendar, menu, busy, chart rise). The dominant loading idea of the picks.
4. Sunrise / rising into light: arrivals that rise and slow as they land (trend, tiles, busy, columns, chart "grows into the light"); the register's leave is the inverse (rises and whites out).
5. Soft shadow as the only elevation: three white-on-white shadow steps; "the soft card" is picked for shape in 7 of 12 components; "lifted pill" / "shadow lifts" on hover. (The button is the one exception: it settles DOWN on hover.)
6. Glare: brightness + blur (leave: blur 5px brightness 1.6; arrival: blur 4px brightness 1.4; resize: brightness 1.04 + indigo glow). Light literally overexposes instead of fading.
7. Pill vs small radius: pills for everything you act on, 0.5rem for surfaces.
8. Gnomon / sundial shadow: meter mark with a short cast shadow, "the shadow swings" leaning mark, the soft-swell family (dot / number / line / card swells once and settles).
9. Clip-path windows and wipes: headline slit-open, dialog/toast slit-open, skeleton and checkmark wipes, fill clipped to value, rule scaleX. Shapes open by a rounded clip, not by fade.
10. Restrained text hooks: highlighter sweep on marks, ink redaction bars that clear to cyan.

## 5. Anchor candidates already present

Strongest (recognisable, only this theme):

- The progress bar: hairline track + thin indigo line + white-ringed cyan bead as the head (and the three-bead waiting state). It is the most distinctive element and already carries the seam-and-circle idea. Its busy state is static, so motion is the open slot.
- The spinner: indigo and cyan beads orbiting a hairline ring on an overshoot-then-settle ease; distinctive, small.
- The seam divider (hairline + open circle, alt = cyan-filled): present on every page, instantly "light", and echoed in empty state, calendar today, wizard step joins.
- The leave/arrival glare (brightness+blur, rise 8px): unusual and cross-component, but it is a filter effect, hard to recognise in a still.
- Tooltip: ink pill with rotated-square nib.

Medium: dialog/toast slit-open window (the 2240ms keyframe is overridden by the reverse-close mechanism in practice, so what is seen is the glare leave reversed); meter "daylight" with gnomon and glint (only ported pick); button hover that settles down; headline clip window.

Weak / generic: switch, checkbox, radio (indigo + standard tick), skeleton (clip-open pills, otherwise generic grey lines), nav, link hover (cyan highlight), all fades/swells ("a soft swell", "soft pop"), `--fx-ease` 0.2,0,0,1 and the `ease-in`/`ease-in-out` on leave and meter loading (standard easings), the soft card shape (white card + shadow = every UI kit), "dashed baseline" loading.

Gaps a designer could fill: no update-in-place gesture declared (`--kp-update` absent), no press (`:active`) state, no menu/popover/drawer/chart/calendar/tile gestures in the register yet (picks wait for the port), busy progress bar does not move.

## 6. Overlaps

- pastel: the nearest neighbour - same Instrument Sans, soft pale ground family, rounded pill buttons, soft shadows, a pearls divider (circles on a line) and a mint/violet highlight mark. Light's seam+circle divider and highlighter marks read like pastel's pearls divider and mark fill; differentiate by cyan/indigo and by no halftone/overprint.
- formal: also a white-ish, restrained, Instrument Sans body theme with sweep/draw rule hooks (`kp-rule-in` is shared with formal, blueprint and deco). Formal is warm with serif display (Fraunces) and navy `hsl(218 45% 24%)`; light is cooler, sans, indigo.
- shade-light (dropped by Kenny 2026-10-06, but its picks still sit in the research demos): same "window light / paper sheet" vocabulary; the register's own comment says the button gesture is deliberately the reverse of shade-light.
- sepia / solstice: also the "paper sheet", "sunrise", "daylight" character vocabulary in their demos ("The paper sheet", folded corner is an option for light too, not picked). Daylight bands and gnomon/sundial language overlap with solstice ("sundial tray", "the sun-ring halo", "it rises like the sun"). A designer should avoid sun/sundial imagery that solstice owns; light's picks (gnomon shadow, daylight band) are already close.
- high-contrast: shares white ground and a blue-ish primary (`hsl(220 100% 30%)`) and Atkinson/Instrument Sans fallback; a plain-looking page could be confused.
- dark / titanium / grotesk: share the tooltip-as-ink-pill and sans type but not colour; low risk.
