Week agenda

Seven day columns with the items of the week placed at their time of day: a nightly backup at 02:00, a host round at 03:30. `attachWeeks(root)` in js/week.js fills `section.kp-week[data-kp-week]` with the head of each day, an hour axis and one pill per item; the page gives the items (`setWeekEvents`) and the state of the whole (`setWeekState`). `data-kp-week-start` is the first day (`YYYY-MM-DD`, else today), `data-kp-week-days` how many columns, `data-kp-week-nav` adds the week buttons. A pill has a series colour (1 to 5) and a state: `next` is ringed, `off` and `skipped` muted and struck through, `reference` dashed and not a button. A column holds as many pills as fit; the rest fold into a `+N` menu button (`data-kp-overflow` marks the folded ones). Below 40rem the columns become rows and every item is shown. A line across today’s column is now; items already past carry `data-kp-past`.

When to use it

When a reader asks what runs when over the next days: schedules, rounds, bookings. Not for a month (use the calendar), not for what already ran (use the run timeline) and not for one list in time order (use the timeline).

Accessibility

  • Built in — one tab stop: arrow up and down move within a day, left and right to the neighbouring day (the item nearest in time), Home and End to the day’s first and last, Enter and Space open the item.
  • Built in — every pill is a button named by its time and label; the now-line has an accessible name with the time; the `+N` button opens a menu of the folded items.
  • Yours — give the agenda a name (`aria-label`), keep the label of every item short and meaningful without its colour, and give the new items when the range changes.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-weekMarks the agenda; `attachWeeks` fills it.
data-kp-week-startThe first day, `YYYY-MM-DD`; without it, today in the time zone.
data-kp-week-daysHow many day columns the range has (7).
data-kp-week-navAdds the row of week buttons: previous, today and next (`data-kp-week-prev`, `data-kp-week-today`, `data-kp-week-next` mark them); a press sends the new range to the page.
data-kp-week-modeThe layout in force: `grid` (columns) or `list` (rows below 40rem); read from the stylesheet, not written by the page.
data-kp-stateOn a pill: `next`, `off`, `skipped` or `reference`. On the agenda: `loading`, `empty` or `error`.
data-kp-seriesOn a pill: 1 to 5, the colour from `--chart-1` to `--chart-5`; the label carries the meaning.
data-kp-pastOn a pill whose time has passed; it is muted.
data-kp-overflowOn a pill folded into the `+N` menu of a full column.
setWeekEvents / setWeekState / setWeekStart / setWeekLegendThe page’s four calls: the items (repainted in place by id, so a pill keeps its focus), the state of the whole (`loading` draws the data table’s busy card over the empty days; `empty`, `error` with its words), a new first day, and the legend under the agenda.
data-kp-week-upcomingOpt-in: below 40rem the agenda lists the next items (the number given) instead of its day rows, one row per item.
data-kp-week-upcoming-label / -emptyThe list’s name for a screen reader, and its sentence when nothing is still to come (else the dictionary’s `weekEmpty`).
.kp-week__upcoming / __nextThe list and one row of it. A row carries `data-kp-day-start` when it opens its day and `data-kp-today` for today; `.kp-week__next--empty` and `.kp-week__none` are the sentence of an empty list.

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
