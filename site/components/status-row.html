Status rows

One row per item that is read or checked one by one: a mark, the item’s name, a short detail and, when it failed, a Retry that tries that one item again. `data-state` is `ok`, `loading`, `failed` or `empty`; each is a shape and a word as well as a colour. js/statusrow.js (`buildStatusList`, `setStatus`, `attachStatusLists`) keeps the mark, the spoken state, `aria-busy`, the Retry and the announcement in step with it, and fires `kp-status-retry` when a Retry is pressed and `kp-status-change` when a row changes.

When to use it

For a page that reads many things separately (a backup per stack, a check per host) and must say which one is slow, which failed and why. For the steps of one run use the step list.

Accessibility

  • Built in — each row reads “name, state”, a loading row has `aria-busy`, and a change is announced once through a hidden status region; more than three changes in one tick become one summary.
  • Built in — the Retry is named after its row, and when the focused Retry goes away the focus moves to the row’s name.
  • Yours — name the list, and write a reason that says what to do.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-state`ok` (a disc with a tick), `loading` (the theme’s spinner), `failed` (a disc with a cross, a red edge and a Retry), `empty` (a dashed ring and a dashed edge).
data-state="warn"A pass with a reservation: the warning pair on the mark with a “!” as its shape, never colour alone. Like `failed` it may carry a fix.
.kp-statusrow__fixInside the row’s body: the fix written out, in the row’s own words. Shown on a `failed` or `warn` row only; `fix` of `buildStatusList` and `setStatus` fills it, and an empty `fix` clears it.
countStatus(list)How many rows are in each state (and the total) as they stand now, for a summary line such as “3 of 4 checks pass”; a calm list counts its own states.
data-kp-status-idNames a row for `setStatus(list, id, { state, label, detail })`.
data-kp-status-retry="false"On the list: no Retry button on failed rows.
.kp-statuslist--rowsOne column instead of a grid of columns of at least 16rem.
buildStatusList / setStatus / attachStatusListsBuild the list from rows `{ id, label, state, detail }`, change one row, and attach the spoken parts and the Retry. `kp-status-retry` is `{ item, id }`, `kp-status-change` is `{ item, id, state, previous }`.
statusOk / statusLoading / statusFailed / statusEmpty / statusRetryThe state words and the Retry label, in the dictionary; `statusRetryLabel` names a Retry after its row, and `statusAnnounce` and `statusAnnounceMany` are the announcements (more than three changes in one tick become one summary).
data-kp-status-vocab`calm` (open, done, paused, unknown; `standard` is the default). A row asked for `failed`, `warn` or anything else is drawn as unknown and the console says so.
data-kp-sinceOn a paused calm row: `YYYY-MM-DD`; the row says “Paused since 03/10/2026”.
data-kp-today-since`both` (the default: “Paused since 03/10/2026 (2 weeks)”), `date` or `relative` (“Paused for 2 weeks”).
data-kp-today-resume`off` leaves out Resume (present by default); `data-kp-resume-reason` on a row disables it and says why.
data-kp-today-pausePresent shows Pause on open rows.
data-kp-today-order`open-first` orders the rows once, at attach (`given` is the default); nothing moves afterwards.
data-kp-today-paused and data-kp-today-fold-after`fold` gathers rows paused longer than this many days (30) into one line that opens them; `show` is the default.
data-kp-today-mark`none` takes the glyphs away (`glyph` is the default).
data-kp-today-nowAn ISO moment that “today” is for, for a page with its own clock.
data-kp-undo-msHow long Undo is offered after Resume or Pause (6000, 0 for none).
kp-status-resume, kp-status-pause, kp-status-undoResume and Pause are cancelable and carry `{ item, id }`; Undo puts the row back as it was and carries `{ item, id, action }`. `settleToday(list)` orders and folds again; `setTodayState(card, state)` is the card’s loading, empty and failed.
.kp-statusrow--fold and .kp-statusrow__foldThe one line that gathers the long-paused rows, “3 paused for more than 30 days”, with the exact number, as a button that opens them (`aria-expanded`). It is the module’s, put last in the list, not written in markup.
CALM_STATUS_STATESThe states a calm row may be in: `open`, `done`, `paused`, `unknown`, and `loading`. Anything else is drawn as `unknown`.
marks the module writes`data-kp-detail-auto` (the since line is the module’s), `data-kp-folded` (a row gathered behind the fold line).

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
