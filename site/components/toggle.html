Toggle button

A button that stays pressed until it is pressed again: “Only errors”, “Pin”, “Follow”. It keeps its words, shows the state by shape and weight as well as colour, can carry an exact count and a short hint, and a group of them can behave as filters, as a one-of switch or as an “All” chip.

When to use it

A filter, a pin, a follow or a show/hide that is on or off. For a setting that is saved with a form, use the switch; for one choice from a few that are always shown together, use the segmented control.

Accessibility

  • Built in — `aria-pressed` is the one source of the state, `mixed` is announced as partly pressed, a group says one polite line per change (“Only errors on, 12 shown”), the hit area is 44 px on a phone, the label never changes with the state.
  • Yours — name the group (`aria-label` on the container), and answer `kp-toggle` with a promise when the change takes time; the toggle is its own undo, so no undo is offered.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
the script`attachToggles(root, options)` wires every `[data-kp-toggle]` and returns a detach; `toggle(el)` answers a button’s handle, `toggleGroup(button)` its group, `togglePressedKeys(anyMember)` the keys of the pressed members and `toggleKey(el)` the key of one (its `data-kp-toggle-value`, else its id, else its label). `TOGGLE` is the selector, `TOGGLE_LOOKS` and `TOGGLE_MODES` the allowed values.
kp-toggle / kp-toggle-changed`kp-toggle` fires on the button before anything changes, bubbling and cancelable, with `{ button, pressed, previous, group, pressedKeys, wait }`; `kp-toggle-changed` fires after, once per change (`TOGGLE_EVENT`, `TOGGLE_CHANGED_EVENT`). Space and Enter press; in a `single` group the arrow keys walk the buttons.
.kp-toggle__label / __count / __mark / __hintThe parts the script writes: the label (it never changes with the state), the exact count, the tick of `data-kp-pressed-mark="check"` and the hint line under the label (`data-kp-pressed-hint`), whose room is always there so nothing moves.
.kp-toggle__refused / __bone / __liveThe refusal word on a rolled-back button (`TOGGLE_REFUSED_MS`, 3000), the skeleton of a count that is still loading, and the polite line a screen reader hears per change (`data-kp-announce`: `state`, `all` or `off`).
.kp-toggle__clearThe “Clear filters (N)” button of a group with `data-kp-toggle-clear`: in place whether or not anything is pressed.
data-kp-toggle-value / data-kp-rememberThe member’s key in `pressedKeys`, and a name that keeps a group’s state per device (off by default: most filters start clean). A toggle that cannot be pressed says why with `data-kp-disabled-reason`.
data-kp-look`button` (default), `chip` (a pill) or `icon` (a square).
aria-pressed`false` (default), `true` or `mixed` (a dashed edge on a half wash).
data-kp-toggle-modeOn the group: `multi` (default), `single` (one is always on, arrow keys walk) or `single-or-none`.
data-kp-countAn exact number beside the label, or `loading` for a skeleton; the room for the next digit is held.
data-kp-pressed-mark`check` draws a tick while it is on, its room kept while it is off.
data-kp-toggle-asyncThe app answers `kp-toggle` with `detail.wait(promise)`: busy, rolled back and the word “Refused” if it fails.
data-kp-toggle-keyA key that presses it, listed in the shortcut sheet; never a bare digit.
