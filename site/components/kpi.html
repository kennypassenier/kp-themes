Key figures

A strip of key figures: each `.kp-kpi` tile a label, the number (with its unit and a note such as “avg 15 min” beside it), a trend line, and at the bottom a 24-hour sparkline or a meter. Every tile is as tall as the tallest on every row the strip wraps to, so labels, numbers and trend lines line up across it. js/kpi.js draws the sparklines and turns a tile into a filter toggle.

When to use it

At the top of a dashboard, for the handful of numbers a reader checks first. Not for a table of figures, and not for more than a row or two of tiles: a key figure that is one of thirty is not key.

Accessibility

  • Built in — a filter tile is a real button with `aria-pressed`, so a screen reader says whether the filter is on.
  • Built in — the sparkline is hidden from a screen reader unless it has a role of its own; the number and the trend line carry the meaning.
  • Built in — a delta’s direction is a ▲ or ▼ as well as a colour.
  • Yours — give the meter its `aria-label` and values, and say in the trend line what a sparkline would otherwise only show.
  • Yours — keep the hint `aria-hidden`; the pressed state already says what it says.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-kpisThe strip: as many tiles a row as fit, every tile one height on every row.
.kp-kpiOne tile: a `div`, an `a` (its border answers hover), or a `button.kp-kpi--toggle`.
.kp-kpi__label, __value, __note, __trendThe label (small capitals, one line, wrapping on a narrow tile), the number with a `<small>` unit, a note beside it, and the trend line.
.kp-kpi__deltaA change in the trend line: `data-kp-direction="up|down"` puts ▲ or ▼ before it, `data-kp-tone="good|bad"` colours it.
.kp-kpi__sparkAn inline SVG with `data-kp-spark="12 14 13 …"`: the script draws a line over a soft area from the numbers, and again when they change.
.kp-kpi__meterThe meter at the bottom of a tile: the same rules as `.kp-meter` (its own page), with a mark, past the end and loading; the tile’s tone colours its fill.
data-kp-toneOn the tile, `warning` or `destructive`: a coloured edge and a coloured number (and the meter’s fill).
.kp-kpi--toggleA filter tile: a click flips `aria-pressed` and fires `kp-kpi-toggle` with `detail.pressed`; the page filters. Pressed, a primary border over a faint primary background.
.kp-kpi__hintWhat a click does, beside the label, in the page’s words: one child `data-kp-when="off"`, one `data-kp-when="on"`.
data-kp-kpi-ownedLeaves a toggle to the page, for a framework that keeps the pressed state itself.
