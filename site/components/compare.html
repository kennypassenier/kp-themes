Compare slider

Two versions stacked with a draggable divider: the before pane shows on the start side, the after pane on the end side. `attachCompares(root)` in js/compare.js adds the divider (a slider: arrow keys by 1, with Shift by 10, Page Up and Down, Home and End), a label on each side that steps aside while the divider covers it, and the waiting state. On images a press anywhere moves the divider and a drag follows; on live HTML panes only the handle drags, so both panes stay usable. Give images their width and height, or the box `data-kp-compare-ratio`, so nothing moves while they load; the theme’s skeleton covers the box meanwhile, and a failed image says so in its pane.

When to use it

To show what a change did: a redesign before and after, two renders of a chart, a photo before and after an edit. For two things that differ in more than looks, put them side by side instead.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the divider is a slider with a name, its value and a value text (“40% Before, 60% After”); one tab stop; the arrow keys, Page Up and Down, Home and End move it.
  • Built in — a finger moving up and down scrolls the page; sideways on the handle it moves the divider. The labels are drawn, the slider says the sides.
  • Yours — give each image an `alt` that says what it shows, and the slider a name (`aria-label`).
