One step at a time

Cook mode: one step of a recipe large on the screen, with what that step needs, a timer button and a note, the count and the theme’s progress bar on top, and two big buttons at the foot. `data-kp-focus-steps` on the root; js/focus-steps.js (`setFocusSteps`, `goToStep`, `getStepIndex`, `setFocusStepsState`, `attachFocusSteps`) keeps the screen on with the Wake Lock API while it is attached, turns the step with the arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Space and a swipe, reads it aloud on request and fires `kp-focus-steps-change` so the page can remember where a person stopped.

When to use it

For instructions followed with the hands busy: a recipe, a repair, an exercise. For a form filled in parts use the wizard; for the steps of a running process use the step list.

Accessibility

  • Built in — every change says “Step 3 of 8” and the step; the buttons are real buttons, a step boundary is `aria-disabled` and keeps the focus.
  • Built in — keys and a swipe do what the buttons do; Read aloud uses the browser’s own voice.
  • Yours — give the view a name and a height, and remember the index the event gives.

data-kp-skip`off` leaves out Not today (present by default; a skip is an answer, `source: skip`).
data-kp-pinned-firstJSON `[{ "value": "none", "label": "Nothing to log" }]`: choices that stand first in every question.
data-kp-back`off` hides Back (present by default; going back keeps every answer).
data-kp-choice-layout and data-kp-choice-height`auto`, `stack` or `row` (a row for up to three short labels, never on a phone); `lg` (56 px, the default) or `md`.
data-kp-progress`count` (“Question 3 of 7” and the bar, the default) or `off`; never a timer, never a streak.
data-kp-resume and data-kp-resume-until`auto`, `ask` or `off` with `data-kp-remember`; `midnight` (the default), `hours:N` or `never`.
data-kp-summary`on` adds a summary screen after the last question (off by default).
data-kp-wake, data-kp-speak and data-kp-text-optionalThe screen is not kept on and Read aloud is off in answer mode (`true` turns them on); a written answer can always be passed with Not today.
kp-focus-steps-answer and related events`kp-focus-steps-before-answer` (cancelable), `kp-focus-steps-answer` `{ index, id, value, source, same?, previous }`, `kp-focus-steps-resume`, `kp-focus-steps-retry`, `kp-focus-steps-save-retry`; Done carries `{ total, answers, startedAt, finishedAt }`. `setFocusStepsState(el, "save-error")` keeps the answers.
marks the module writes`data-kp-value` and `data-kp-chosen` on an answer, `data-kp-pinned`, `data-kp-layout` on the answers, `data-kp-done` and `data-kp-current` on a dot.
