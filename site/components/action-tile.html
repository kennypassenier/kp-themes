Action tile

A button or a link drawn as a tile: a title and the one sentence that says what it does, in a grid whose tiles on one row are all as tall as the tallest. A button tile does something, a link tile goes somewhere (its mark is a chevron). The tile has a mark column that is always there, so a spinner arriving moves nothing. js/action-tiles.js (`attachActionTiles`, `setActionBusy`) stops a press on a busy or disabled tile and puts the theme’s own spinner in the mark while `aria-busy="true"`.

When to use it

For a short list of things to do or places to go that each need a sentence: a landing page, a drawer, a settings overview. For one action use a button; for a list of records use a table.

Accessibility

  • Built in — a tile is a real button or link: Tab reaches it, Enter or Space presses a button, Enter follows a link, and the focus ring is whole in every theme.
  • Built in — the chevron and the spinner are pictures (`aria-hidden`); the busy word is read after the title.
  • Yours — give every tile a sentence, and name the group.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-action--destructiveA start bar, a red-tinted edge and title. The edge goes fully red when pointed at.
disabled / aria-disabled="true"A quieter plate, no hover, no press; `aria-disabled` also stops a link or a button that must keep its focus.
aria-busy="true"The spinner appears in the mark, a hidden “Working…” follows the title, and a press does nothing until it is cleared. `setActionBusy(tile, true)` sets it and fires `kp-action-busy`.
.kp-action__metaAn optional small line under the sentence, for a live figure.
--kp-actions-minThe least width of a column (15rem); the grid fills the width with as many as fit.
