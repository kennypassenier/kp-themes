Activity feed

Who did what, and when, day by day. Each row has its outcome (a mark with a shape and a word), what happened, a muted detail line, the chip of whoever started it and the time, which is a ticking “12 min ago” for the first 24 hours and dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm after that. Rows sit under a heading per day (“Today”, “Yesterday”, then the weekday and the date) with that day’s exact count and “N failed”, and “Load earlier” stands at the foot.

When to use it

A history of operations, an audit trail, a stack’s recent runs: anything where the person asks what happened and who did it. For a list of checks that are each ok or failed use status rows; for a fixed set of releases use the timeline; for a conversation use the thread.

Accessibility

  • Built in — a row’s state is a shape and a word as well as a colour (the word is read after the title), the actor is read as “by Claude”, the time keeps its full moment in `title` and `aria-description`, the day headings are headings, and new rows are announced politely at most every ten seconds.
  • Yours — write the title in the user’s words and give the feed a name (`aria-label`); give every row a stable `key` so a refresh keeps the focus and the rows that are open.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
the script`attachFeeds(root, { now, timeZone })` wires every `[data-kp-feed]` and returns a detach; `feedOf(el)` answers the handle (`set`, `setOpen`, `openKeys`, `render`); `setFeed(el, { state, rows, total, earlier, updatedAt, stale, error, emptyText })` hands over the data. `FEED` is the selector.
events`kp-feed-retry` (Retry on the failed plate and Read it again on a stale feed), `kp-feed-earlier` (Load earlier and its Try again) and `kp-feed-open` `{ key, open }`, bubbling from the host. The page decides what a retry or an earlier page is and answers with `setFeed`.
data-kp-feedMarks the host. The script draws the head (the exact count and the freshness line), the list, the state plate and the foot inside it.
data-kp-feed-group`day` (default): a heading per day with that day’s count; `none`: one list.
data-kp-feed-summary`on` (default) or `off`: the count and “N failed” (or “all ok”) on each day heading.
data-kp-feed-unit`operation,operations`: the app’s word for a row, singular and plural; “event,events” by default.
data-kp-feed-time`auto` (default: “12 min ago” for 24 hours, then dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm), `relative` (always the ago line), `clock` (the ago line, then HH:mm under its day heading) or `moment` (always dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm). Pointing at a time gives the full moment.
data-kp-feed-actor`chip` (default: the mark and the name), `mark` (the mark alone; the name is its title and its screen-reader text) or `off`.
data-kp-feed-expand`many` (default), `one` (opening a row closes the others) or `off` (rows with lines are not openable).
data-kp-feed-rail`on` (default): the timeline’s rule joins the marks of a day; `off`.
data-kp-feed-head`on` (default) or `off`: the count and the freshness line above the list.
data-kp-feed-maxA CSS length, `26rem`: the list scrolls inside that height and the day headings stay at its top.
data-kp-feed-levelThe heading level of a day heading; 3.
states`ready`, `loading` (rows in skeletons under the busy card), `empty` (“Nothing happened yet” and `emptyText`), `error` (the failed plate with Retry) and, on a ready feed, `stale` (the rows kept, “Not refreshed: …. Rows from 14:02.” and Read it again). The foot is `earlier`: `more`, `loading`, `error` or `end`.
written by the script`data-kp-state` on the host (read by js/footprint.js, which holds the loaded height), `data-kp-feed-actor-mode`, `data-kp-feed-scroll` and `data-kp-feed-no-rail` (what the stylesheet reads) and the buttons’ markers `data-kp-feed-retry` and `data-kp-feed-earlier`.
data-kp-earlierWritten on the foot: `more`, `loading`, `error`, `end` or `none`. The foot keeps its room in the first four, so nothing moves when it changes.
.kp-feed__head / __count / __fresh / __staleThe head: the exact count (“18 of 30 operations”), the ticking freshness line, and the stale notice with its button.
.kp-feed__list / __group / __day / __rows / __rowThe one grid of every day; a group holds its heading (`__day`, with `__day-word`, `__day-date`, `__day-rule` and `__day-summary`) and its rows. A row has `data-state` and `data-kp-key`.
.kp-feed__mark / __title / __detail / __toggle / __link / __moreA row’s parts: the state mark, the title (a button that opens its lines, or a link), the muted detail and the lines that open.
.kp-feed__actor / __when / __foot / __earlierThe chip column, the time column and the foot with Load earlier.
