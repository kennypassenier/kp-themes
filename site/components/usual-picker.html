Picker with the usual on top

The combobox with the choices a person uses most standing first in a band, the rest alphabetical beneath, and a guard against a near-duplicate when a new name is typed. The band is given by the application and changes slowly; it is never re-sorted per tap. Every behaviour is an option with a default and can be switched at run time: the usual choices lead while typing (or step aside), a near name asks (or selects the closest, or is off), the full list sits behind “Show all N” (or is fully shown). “Show all 38” keeps a phone screen short with the exact number on the control; typing a name identical to an existing one selects it, a near one offers “Did you mean Hoofdpijn?” and always “Add ‘hoofdpjn’ as new”, with Undo. js/usual-picker.js (`attachUsualPickers`, `usualPicker`, `setUsual`, `setUsualState`, `usualNotToday`, `nearNames`, `similarity`) stands on the combobox, which it attaches itself.

When to use it

A choice from a long list where the same few are picked again and again (symptoms, meals, strategies). For a short fixed list use radio buttons or the segmented control; for a list with no habit use the plain combobox.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the combobox’s listbox with virtual focus; the status line says “5 usual, 33 more” and, after an add, “Hoofdpijn added”; options are 48 px and their words wrap.
  • Built in — Alt U jumps to the first usual choice while the field is empty (a bare `u` would take the first letter of a name); it is on the `?` sheet. No digit key is bound.
  • Yours — keep the vocabulary: answer `kp-usual-add` (cancel it to refuse) and merge duplicates in your own data.

NameWhat it is
data-kp-usual and data-kp-usual-sourceThe band: a comma list, a JSON array, or `{ value, uses }` with the source `counts` (ranked by uses, ties alphabetical; `given` is the default).
data-kp-usual-max, data-kp-usual-label and data-kp-usual-while-typingThe band holds 1 to 7 (5); its heading (“Usual”); `first` (matching usual items still lead while typing, the default) or `hide`.
data-kp-order`alphabetical` (the default) or `given` for the list under the band.
data-kp-pinnedJSON `[{ "value", "label" }]`: “Nothing to log” and “Not sure” stand first and are never usual.
data-kp-all`open` or `fold` (the default for more than 12 items): the band and “Show all 38”.
data-kp-multiple and data-kp-preselectTag mode; `usual` pre-ticks the band (`none` is the default: a stock picker never answers for a person).
data-kp-not-today and data-kp-not-today-labelThe usual choices’ own check under the field: `rows` (the default; a bare attribute is rows: one line per usual choice with the segmented pair “Had it” / “Not today”), `chips` (the three-way chips) or `off`. The tags carry no Not today button; the check is where it is answered, distinct from unset (`usualNotToday(el)`). The label words the check’s heading (“Your usual today”).
data-kp-usual-undo`field` (the default: “Added ‘hoofdpjn’ as new” with Undo under the field) or `toast` (the Undo toast).
data-kp-add, data-kp-near, data-kp-near-threshold and data-kp-near-max`inline` adds at the point of use (`off`); `ask`, `select` or `off` for the near-match guard; similarity from 0.5 to 1 (0.8); up to 5 names (3).
data-kp-reasonOn an option: it cannot be picked, and the sentence is written under its label (and is its description for a screen reader).
data-kp-hint, data-kp-remember and data-kp-undo-msThe app’s line under the input once something is chosen; the folded list is remembered per picker; the Undo window after an add (6000).
data-kp-state`loading` (the busy card in the place of the list), `empty` (a plate, with Add where adding is on) or `error` (a plate with Retry, `kp-usual-retry`).
kp-usual-pick, kp-usual-add and kp-usual-not-today`{ value, from: usual | all | pinned | typed }`; cancelable `{ label, nearTo }` (the app keeps the vocabulary and may refuse); `{ value, on }`.
.kp-usual__check and its partsThe check: `__check-title` and `__check-what` (the heading and the one line that says what Not today means), `__check-rows` with a `__check-name` and a `__check-pair` (the segmented pair) per usual choice, or `__check-chips` for the three-way look (`.kp-usual__check--rows` or `--chips` on the check). Made by the module.
.kp-usual__note, __note-text and __undoThe line under the field after an add (“Added ‘hoofdpjn’ as new”) and the Undo in it; Undo takes the name out again and says so.
.kp-usual__already and .kp-tag[data-kp-usual-flash]The row of the list that says a name is already picked, and the tag it lights for about a second so nothing typed is answered by silence.
segmented wordsThe three-way chips read `data-kp-segmented-words` on their group (`{ include, exclude, any }`), so the check’s own words, “had it”, “not today”, “not answered”, are spoken by the chips without a second dictionary.
usualAlreadyPicked and the other `usual…` stringsEvery word the picker adds is in the dictionary: `usualAlreadyPicked`, `usualPicked`, `usualTookClosest`, `usualAsk`, `usualAddedNote`, `usualUndo`, `usualUndoName`, `usualAddUndone`, `usualCheckTitle`, `usualCheckWhat`, `usualCheckWhatChips`, `usualHadIt`, `usualHadItWord`, `usualUnanswered` and `usualUnansweredWord`.
normaliseName, similarity and nearNamesThe guard on its own: `normaliseName(text)` ignores case, accents and spacing, `similarity(a, b)` is 0 to 1, and `nearNames(typed, names, threshold, max)` lists the close ones, also by the start of a name (“Fatigu”).
combobox handle extend(hook)How this layer stands on the combobox: `combobox(el).extend(hook)` lets a layer decide which options show, what the status line says and what a choice does. One layer per combobox; `null` takes it off.
