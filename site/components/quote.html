Quote card

A card that shows your own earlier words, word for word, with who wrote them and when (“Wandelen na het eten hielp”, you, Tuesday 14/10/2026). A figure with a blockquote and a caption whose time element carries the exact moment; a long entry folds behind Show all with the whole text still in the page; Hide is one press and can be undone. It shows; choosing which words to show, and never a negative one on a rough day, is the application’s job. js/quote.js (`attachQuotes`, `setQuote`, `setQuoteState`, `quote`, `quoteWhen`) draws it; the states are the card’s (js/card-state.js).

When to use it

To quote a person’s own notes back to them (a weekly summary, an appointment preparation). For a message from someone else in a conversation use the thread; for a plain note use the card.

Accessibility

  • Built in — `figure`, `blockquote` (with `lang` and `cite`) and `figcaption` with a `time`; the key `h` hides the quote that has the focus and Mod C copies it when copy is on, both on the `?` sheet.
  • Built in — a hide is said politely and offers Undo; the failed and empty states are the card’s, with a normal-size Retry.
  • Yours — write `data-kp-quote-lang` whenever the words are in another language than the page, and answer `kp-quote-hide` by storing the hide.
