Pipeline

Where each item stands, a lane per stage: a read-only board, not a drag-and-drop one. `attachPipelines(root)` in js/pipeline.js fills `section.kp-pipeline[data-kp-pipeline]`; the page gives the lanes and the cards with `setPipeline(el, { lanes, cards })` and the state of the whole with `setPipelineState`. Each lane has a plate with its name and exact count in the stage’s colour (`tone` 1 to 5), its share of all, and its cards (a link with `href`, else a button; `mark`, `sub` and `meta` under the title). A lane marked `closed` stands under the board across its width. `data-kp-pipeline-limit="4"` keeps four cards per lane behind “+N more”; `data-kp-pipeline-share="false"` leaves the share out. Under 40rem of its own width the lanes turn to rows.

When to use it

When a reader asks how many items stand at each stage of one process and which: applications from draft to offer, deployments from queued to live. Not for moving items between stages (that is a form), and not for counts alone (use key figures).

Accessibility

  • Built in — the board is a list of lanes named by their plates, each a list of cards; a count is the exact number.
  • Built in — one tab stop: left and right to the neighbouring lane, up and down along a lane, Home and End to its first and last; Enter follows a link or presses a button.
  • Yours — name the board (`aria-label`), and give a card a title that says what it is without its colour.
