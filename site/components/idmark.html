Identity mark

A small plate made from a name, so a company or a stack is found in a list before its name is read: two letters on a wash of one of the theme’s five chart colours, or (`--pattern`) a mirrored 5 by 5 pattern. The same name gives the same mark on every page. js/idmark.js (`idmarkFor`, `idmarkColours`, `setIdmark`, `attachIdmarks`) fills every `[data-kp-idmark]`.

When to use it

Beside the name of a company, a stack or a person in a list, a header or a cell. It is decoration: the name always stands beside it.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the mark is hidden from a screen reader.
  • Yours — always put the name beside it, and name a group in words.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-idmark--xs / --sm / --lg / --xlSizes 20, 24, 44 and 56 px around the 32 px default; extra small shows one letter.
.kp-idmark--pattern / --round / --glyphThe pattern instead of letters; a round plate; a small corner shape picked by the name.
data-kp-idmark-hue="free"A hue of its own from the name, on the lightness and strength of the first chart colour.
aria-current / data-kp-idmark-selectedThe current one in a list: a ring kept off its neighbours by a halo.
.kp-idmark.kp-skeleton.kp-skeleton--blockLoading: the plate’s own shape, drawn as the theme draws a waiting block.
data-kp-idmark-src and kp-idmark-photoThe photo; the letters stay under it until it has loaded and after it has failed. The event carries `{ state, src }`; `data-kp-photo` (`loading`, `loaded`, `failed`) is written on the mark.
data-kp-idmark-loading`lazy` (the default) or `eager`, as the image’s own `loading`.
data-kp-idmark-alt`decorative` (the default: hidden from a screen reader, the name stands beside it) or `name` (the mark is an image called by the name).
data-kp-idmark-status`online`, `away` or `busy` (`none` is the default): a dot at the foot corner, full, ring or barred, whose word is added to the label (“Ada Lovelace, online”). A status makes the mark an image.
data-kp-idmark-frameThe photo’s frame: `edge` (the default: the plate’s own edge in the name’s colour), `ring` (a ring held off the photo) or `inset` (the photo set into the plate like a picture in a mount).
setIdmarkGroup and IDMARK_STATUSES`setIdmarkGroup(el, people, { max, size, round, list })` with people `{ name, src, status }`; `.kp-idmark-group__more` is the “+N” (a button when `list` is on) and `.kp-idmark-group__list` the panel of the names left out, closed by Escape or a press elsewhere; `IDMARK_STATUSES` is `online`, `away`, `busy`.
.kp-idmark-group--sm and .kp-idmark-name--wrapThe small group’s “+N” kept in step with its marks, and a name that wraps (for a heading or a list, not a table cell) instead of being cut.
idmarkLetters and idmarkUnknown`idmarkLetters(name, count)` is the initials of the first two words, else the first two letters, upper case. A mark with an empty name is a dashed plate with a “?”, and `idmarkUnknown` is the word a screen reader gets for it.
idmarkOnline … idmarkMoreTitleThe words of the avatar are in the dictionary: `idmarkOnline`, `idmarkAway`, `idmarkBusy`, `idmarkLabel` (“Ada, online”), `idmarkGroup` (“Acme, Contoso and 7 more”), `idmarkMore` and `idmarkMoreTitle`.
