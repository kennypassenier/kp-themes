Checklist

A list a person works down, such as a shopping list by shop: every row is a checkbox’s label, a ticked row is struck through and sinks to the end of its group, gliding in the theme’s own time. `data-kp-checklist` on the root; js/checklist.js (`setChecklistItems`, `getChecklistItems`, `setChecklistChecked`, `setChecklistState`, `attachChecklists`) draws the groups, the counts and the bar with Check all, Clear checked and its Undo, and fires `kp-checklist-change`, `kp-checklist-clear` and `kp-checklist-undo` with the whole list, so the page stores it.

When to use it

For items a person ticks off and no longer needs to see on top. For a filter use the facet list; for the steps of a running process use the step list.

Events

EventWhat it means

kp-checklist-renderFired on the list (not bubbling) after every draw, so a part that adds to the rows can draw its cells [kpadopt6].

Accessibility

  • Built in — real checkboxes in labels, named by the item and described by its amount; group headings with their counts.
  • Built in — ticked with the keyboard, the focus goes to the item that took its place; a status region says what moved.
  • Yours — give the list a name and store what the events say.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
kp-checklist-renderFired on the list after every draw (not bubbling), so a part that adds a cell to the rows can draw it; the claim part does.
a detailA row’s detail (“3 pieces”) wraps inside its row and never widens the list. A checklist with no items writes `data-kp-state="empty"` and shows the empty plate; a failed one shows the failed plate alone; both, like a loading one, hold the height the list had.
