Share bar

One bar split into the parts of a whole, in the chart colours, the free rest as the track, with a legend that gives every part its exact value. js/stackbar.js (`setStackbar`, `stackbarParts`, `attachStackbars`) draws it from a list; every part has a hover card, the arrow keys walk them, and `kp-stackbar-pick` names a pressed part.

When to use it

For what fills a disk, a budget or a quota. For one share of a whole use the meter; for shares over time use the chart.

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

These are the framework-free channel's props. A selector is how a module finds an element, a read is a value you set on it, and a written one is state the module puts back for the stylesheet and for you to read.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the bar is a group of parts, each named with its value and share; one tab stop, arrow keys within.
  • Built in — the legend holds every exact value.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-stackbar--sm / --lgAn 8 px or a 20 px bar around the 12 px default.
other, marksParts under a share merged into “Other”; ticks across the bar at a share.
over the wholeParts adding up past the total: the bar is their sum, a ▸ at its end, “Over by …” in the legend.
toneA part with `tone: 'warning'` or `'destructive'` takes that status colour in place of a chart colour.
the sixth partFrom the sixth part on the chart colours come round again, hatched, so two parts never share one look.
state: loadingThe bar is the meter’s loading picture, the theme’s own way a bar waits; the legend keeps its rows as waiting lines and the root is `aria-busy`.
state: empty / errorOne calm note, `.kp-stackbar__note` with `.kp-stackbar__note-title`, over the whole footprint the bar and legend had; an error also has `.kp-stackbar__retry`, a normal-size button. A bar without a legend keeps its track and says the words in its name and hover card.
retry: falseA failed bar for a read the page cannot repeat: the words and no Retry.
