Card

A raised surface with its own ground and ink: a title, a body, and optional slots for a header row, its actions and a footer. A raised surface is never darker than the one below it.

When to use it

To group what belongs together and to separate one record from the next in a grid or a list. Not as a box around everything — a page of cards inside cards has no hierarchy left — and not as a button: a card that is entirely clickable hides where the target is.

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

These are the framework-free channel's props. A selector is how a module finds an element, a read is a value you set on it, and a written one is state the module puts back for the stylesheet and for you to read.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the card ground and its ink are a measured pair, so text on a card clears the contrast floor in every theme.
  • Yours — use a real heading for the title if the card is a section of the page rather than one of many equal records.
  • Yours — put the link or the button inside the card rather than making the whole card clickable.
  • Yours — keep the reading order the source order; a card that reads bottom to top on a screen reader is a card laid out with the visual only in mind.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-state`loading`, `empty`, `error` or `ready` on a `.kp-card`, over a `.kp-card__stage` that holds the data (`.kp-card__data`) and, in the same cell, the state’s note (`.kp-card__state`), so a state never moves the header, the footer or the card beside it. Empty and failed keep the data’s room.
.kp-card__failureA failed card’s note is a state plate: centred, at the heading size (`.kp-card__failure` holds the destructive alert, `.kp-card__retry` its Try again at the control size), filling the stage; the card grows while it is failed. The note’s other parts are `.kp-card__state-title`, `.kp-card__state-text` and `.kp-card__state-actions`.
setCardState / attachCardStates`setCardState(card, state, options)` (js/card-state.js) writes the attribute and draws the note, keeping the options for later changes written by a framework; `attachCardStates(root)` syncs every card with a `data-kp-state` and turns a press on Try again into `kp-card-retry`. `CARD_STATES` lists the four states; `kp-card-state` fires on every change.
.kp-cardThe surface: the card ground, a boundary, a rounded corner, and in one theme a hard shadow the knob paints and every other theme leaves at nothing.
.kp-card__titleThe heading, at body size — a card is not a page and its title is not a display line.
.kp-card__bodyThe text, in quiet ink.
.kp-card__headerA row that lays the title and its actions on one baseline and pushes them apart.
.kp-card__actionsThe controls in that row, spaced by the scale.
.kp-card__footerA closing row, separated by a rule and the scale’s own distance.
pastel: the thumbtackIn pastel every card is fixed to the page with a thumbtack at its top centre. A card that leaves loses its tack first, then lifts off as its landing backwards, and its space folds after the tack is out; one put back arrives as that backwards, the tack pushed in last.
data-kp-state`loading`, `empty`, `error` or `ready` on a card with a stage: the card keeps its header, footer and size, and only the stage changes. In `empty` and `error` the data keeps its room, hidden rather than removed, so a failed refresh of a full card does not collapse it.
.kp-card__stage, __data, __stateThe stage holds the data and, in the same grid cell over it, the note of the state; `.kp-card__state` is made by the module (`data-kp-card-built`) unless the page draws its own inside it.
.kp-card__empty, __failure, __state-title, __state-text, __state-actionsThe empty state, the destructive alert, and the note’s first line, sentence and action.
.kp-card__retryTry again, in an error note; a press fires `kp-card-retry` on the card. `retry: false` leaves it out of a read the page cannot repeat.
.kp-card__busyThe data table’s loading card laid over the stage while loading; the first loading card is there from the first frame and does not arrive.
[aria-busy="true"]A card waiting for its content. In pastel it becomes caterpillar: a head on its top-end corner and legs along its foot that step in turn (js/loading-pieces.js). Other themes draw nothing extra.
setCardState(card, "empty" | "error", { title, text, art })`art` is a node (an inline SVG) or a glyph. The empty note is the state plate with its title at the heading size and Try again is a normal-size button.

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
