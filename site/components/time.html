Time field

A field for a time of day in 24 hours: typed (0230, 2:30, 2h30), stepped with the arrow keys or picked from the package’s own list of times, with presets and a mark on times near a busy moment. It posts HH:MM and never shows AM or PM.

When to use it

A schedule, a reminder or a log entry that needs a time of day. For a date use the date picker; for a duration use the countdown’s field.

Accessibility

  • Built in — a combobox with a listbox, the active entry named by `aria-activedescendant`; an invalid text is `aria-invalid` with an alert line; every change and a 12 hour conversion are announced politely.
  • Yours — name the field (`data-kp-label` or a label for its id), and word the near-a-busy-moment sentence with the problem alert.
