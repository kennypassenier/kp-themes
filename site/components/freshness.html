Freshness line

A ticking “updated 42 s ago” (js/freshness.js): one timer per page, a width held at the widest text of its unit so nothing beside it moves, a stale plate once the moment is older than `data-kp-stale-after`, and the moment itself as dd/mm/yyyy HH:mm in Europe/Brussels in the title. It stays out of live regions: a line that ticks must not be read aloud every second.

When to use it

Where a page shows data that goes stale: a dashboard’s last reading, a list’s last refresh. Not for a fixed date: write that out.

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

These are the framework-free channel's props. A selector is how a module finds an element, a read is a value you set on it, and a written one is state the module puts back for the stylesheet and for you to read.

Accessibility

  • Built in — `aria-live="off"`, `aria-description` with the moment written out, a console warning when the line sits inside a live region.
  • Yours — keep the line out of `role="status"`; announce staleness with `data-kp-ago-announce="state"` if it matters.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-agoThe line itself; the moment is the `datetime`, or this attribute’s value (ISO or ms).
data-kp-ago-verbThe word before the age (“updated”, “read”); the dictionary’s `agoVerb` when absent.
data-kp-stale-afterSeconds after which the line is stale and carries `data-kp-stale`; the stale plate’s three knobs are listed below.
data-kp-staleWritten by the module while the moment is older than `data-kp-stale-after`. Read it, do not set it.
data-kp-ago-widthWritten by the module: the held width, measured once per unit and cached by font.
data-kp-ago-announce`state` announces only the change to stale and back, through a polite region of its own; the ticking itself is never announced.
data-kp-ago-voiceWritten by the module: the live region it announces through when `data-kp-ago-announce` is set.
