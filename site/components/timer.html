Kitchen timers

Named countdowns that keep running side by side, each a card with its name, the time left large, the theme’s progress bar, Pause or Resume, +1 min and Remove. `data-kp-timers` on the list; js/timer.js (`startTimer`, `pauseTimer`, `resumeTimer`, `addTimerTime`, `stopTimer`, `getTimers`, `restoreTimers`, `askTimerNotifications`, `attachTimers`) counts from each end time, so a page in the background or a phone that locked shows the right time again, and ends a timer with the done plate, a soft chime, a buzz and a system notification when allowed. A button with `data-kp-timer-start="10m"` anywhere in the page starts one.

When to use it

For clocks a person starts and keeps an eye on (cooking, a break, a session). For a notice that closes by itself use the alarm in its timed mode.

Accessibility

  • Built in — each card is named by its timer; the time is a `timer` region that is read on demand, never every second.
  • Built in — a start and the last minute are said politely, the end at once.
  • Yours — ask for notifications from a press, and keep what getTimers() gives across a reload.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-timers-sound`chime` (default, the soft two-note chime), `bell` (a wind-up kitchen timer’s hammer on two bells), `buzzer` (a harsher electronic beep) or `off`. All are made with Web Audio, no file; `playTimerSound()` plays one for a picker’s preview, from a press.
data-kp-timers-speak`on` says the timer’s message aloud with speech synthesis once the sound has played, again at every ring. The message is `data-kp-timer-say` on a starter, else “<name> is done”. Nothing is said, and nothing fails, where speech synthesis is missing.
data-kp-timer-startOn any button: the duration it starts (`10m`, `1h30m`, `90s`, `10:00` or seconds), with `-name`, `-for` (the list’s id; else the page’s first) and `-id`. `data-kp-timer-running` is written on it while its timer runs.
parseDurationRe-exported from js/countdown.js; the clock is the countdown’s `formatCountdown`.
data-kp-timers-ringHow long an ended timer chimes, in seconds (60; 0 is once).
data-kp-timers-sound="off"No chime.
data-kp-timers-notify="false"No system notification even with the permission.
data-kp-timers-emptyThe sentence of an empty list.
