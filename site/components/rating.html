Star rating

A row of stars to give something a score, and the same row read only to show one. The input is a radio group (one tab stop, the arrows move and choose, Home and End, Backspace clears); the display is one image with a full text name (“Rated 4 of 5”). Whole stars, five of them, by default; the lit stars are the theme’s accent, never red or green. js/rating.js (`attachRatings`, `rating`, `setRating`, `setRatingState`, `ratingCell`, `snapRating`) draws the stars and fires `kp-rating-change`, which the page can refuse or answer with a save.

When to use it

For a score a person gives (a recipe after cooking) or one that is shown (a list sorted by it). For a yes or no use a switch; for a choice between words use the segmented control.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the input is a radio group named “Rating for Lasagne”; each star is “4 of 5” (with a word, “4 of 5, Good”); the display is one image, “Rated 4 of 5”, or “Not rated yet”.
  • Built in — a change is said politely (“Lasagne: 4 of 5”) and offers Undo; a refused save puts the previous value back and says so under the row.
  • Yours — name the item with `data-kp-rating-for`, and answer `kp-rating-change` (call `event.detail.wait(promise)` for a save).
