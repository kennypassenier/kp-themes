Side navigation

A navigation that stands beside the content instead of above it. Three modes — beside the page, over it, or pushing it aside — a slim rail that keeps the icons and drops the words, categories that fold, and either edge.

When to use it

For an application with more places than a bar can hold, or a hierarchy two levels deep. Not for the five links every page can reach — that is the bar at the top. Not as a drawer for content: a panel that slides in carrying a form is a dialog, and it wants a dialog’s focus handling and a dialog’s dismissal.

Accessibility

  • Built in — over mode traps the focus while it covers the page, moves the focus in on open, and gives it back to the toggler on close.
  • Built in — the focus goes back to the opener when the panel has closed, not at the start of the close.
  • Built in — a shut over or push panel is inert, out of the tab order and the accessibility tree; a shortcut puts aria-keyshortcuts on the togglers, and an empty launcher toggler gets an accessible name.
  • Built in — the current page carries aria-current, so it is announced and not only drawn.
  • Built in — the toggler says whether it is expanded and which panel it controls.
  • Built in — a collapsed rail keeps every label in the accessibility tree, and the slim toggle keeps the focus while the rail changes.
  • Yours — give the nav element a name; a page with a bar and a side navigation has two of them.
  • Yours — put a label in every link. A rail of bare icons reads as a column of nothing.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-sidenav-modeside (the default, in the flow), over (above the content, with a backdrop) or push (fixed, and the content named by data-kp-sidenav-content moves over).
data-kp-sidenav-contentOn a push or over panel: a selector for the page element the panel offsets. A push moves it by the panel’s width (its start margin, or its end margin for a panel on the end edge); the module writes data-kp-sidenav-shift on it.
data-kp-sidenav-over-belowA length, or empty for 40rem: in a box narrower than that a push panel becomes an over panel, and the reserve gives way. A launcher is never hidden by it; any other toggler (an edge tab included) is shown only while the panel is narrow, so a panel with over-below takes a launcher, not a tab. data-kp-sidenav-narrow is written while it has.
data-kp-sidenav-reserveOn a push or over panel with data-kp-sidenav-content: the content keeps the panel’s width whether the panel is open or not, a gutter the panel opens into, so it covers nothing. Off by default. Not while data-kp-sidenav-over-below has made the panel an over panel on a narrow screen.
.kp-sidenav__launcherA box fixed at the window’s start corner holding the panel’s toggler (a .kp-icon-button with data-kp-sidenav-toggle and aria-controls): the same place at every width and in every state, above the panel. An empty toggler gets the package’s three bars (.kp-sidenav__bars) and its accessible name from the strings. The module marks the panel data-kp-sidenav-launched, which starts its header beside the launcher (or its first row below it), and marks the box data-kp-sidenav-lit from the opening’s first frame to the closing’s last, the accent plate.
.kp-sidenav__barsThe three bars drawn in an empty toggler inside the launcher; a toggler with words or an icon of its own gets none.
data-kp-sidenav-launchedWritten on the panel while a launcher drives it: the header starts beside the launcher. Read it, do not set it.
data-kp-sidenav-litWritten on the launcher box from the opening’s first frame to the closing’s last: the accent plate. Read it, do not set it.
data-kp-sidenav-launcher-roomOn a page element in the launcher’s corner (the page header): keeps the launcher’s width free at its start, except in a reserved gutter, where the launcher stands in the gutter.
data-kp-sidenav-openerOn an over or push panel, which opener the page shows: launcher (the fixed .kp-sidenav__launcher), tab (the .kp-sidenav__tab on the panel’s edge) or both. Absent or any other value is tab, the default; a page with no tab for the panel keeps its launcher, so a panel is never left without an opener. The box the choice turns off is not drawn, is out of the tab order and the accessibility tree and is absent from the first frame, and the header’s room for the launcher is given back. A side panel and the collapsed rail ignore it. React: opener.
data-kp-sidenav-opener-off / -onWritten by the module on an opener box (launcher or tab) the choice turns off, or keeps. Read them, do not set them.
.kp-sidenav__tabThe edge tab: a box holding a toggler (a .kp-icon-button with data-kp-sidenav-toggle and aria-controls) that rides on the far edge of a panel that slides (over, push), halfway down, and sits at the window’s edge while the panel is away; it moves on the panel’s own timing and works beside the launcher. An empty toggler gets a grip (.kp-sidenav__grip), which has no state to change.
data-kp-sidenav-inertA closed panel that slides away is inert, out of the tab order and the accessibility tree, from the closing’s last frame to the opening’s first. On by default; false leaves a closed panel reachable.
data-kp-sidenav-rail-tipsIn the collapsed rail each item shows its label as the package’s tooltip on hover and on focus, beside the rail and inside the window; it leaves as it came. On by default; false turns it off.
data-kp-sidenav-slim-belowOn a rail (data-kp-sidenav-slim): the width of the panel’s box at and below which the rail collapses to its icons, a length, or 40rem when empty. Wider again it is what the reader left it; the step’s collapse is never remembered as the reader’s. data-kp-sidenav-over-below, which takes a length too, is its sibling for the variant.
data-kp-sidenav-flyoutIn the collapsed rail a group opens beside the rail as a floating panel, in the top layer, with the theme’s popover opening and its close as that opening backwards: after the pointer rests 150 ms, on a press, or from the keyboard (Enter or the arrow away from the edge goes into it; Escape or the arrow back folds it and returns to its heading when the close is over). Placed beside the rail and turned to stay in the window. On by default; false keeps groups folding in place. data-kp-sidenav-flown is written on the group while it floats.
data-kp-sidenav-flownWritten on a .kp-sidenav__category while its group floats beside the collapsed rail. Read it, do not set it.
data-kp-sidenav-shortcutA key chord that opens and closes the panel from anywhere but a text field, such as Control+b. Control (or Mod) is the platform’s command key: Meta (⌘) on a Mac. The togglers get aria-keyshortcuts. Off by default.
data-kp-sidenav-initial-focusA selector, inside the panel, for where the focus lands when the panel opens. Without it an over panel focuses its first stop and the others leave the focus where it is; named, it is used in every mode, and the focus goes back to the opener when the panel has closed.
data-kp-sidenav-hover-delayOn a rail with data-kp-sidenav-expand-on-hover: how long the pointer rests, in milliseconds, before the rail widens (default 150). The rail also widens while the focus is inside it. The module writes data-kp-sidenav-peek (open, then leaving while it narrows); a page without the module widens on plain hover.
data-kp-sidenav-peekWritten on a rail with data-kp-sidenav-expand-on-hover: open while the rail is wide for a resting pointer or the focus, then leaving while it narrows with its words still showing. Read it, do not set it.
data-kp-sidenav-shiftWritten by the module on the content a panel offsets: open, closed, or reserved for a gutter. A push moves on the panel’s own slide timing, which the module copies onto the content for each change, so the content travels with the theme’s slide and comes back on its reverse; a register that draws the move itself wins.
data-kp-sidenav-positionfixed or absolute. Absolute puts the panel inside a positioned box rather than against the window, which is what a page with two of them needs.
data-kp-sidenav-sideend puts the panel on the other edge, logically: in a right-to-left page that is the left, and it still slides out of the side it came from.
data-kp-sidenav-slimAllows the rail. With data-kp-sidenav-slim-collapsed it starts collapsed; data-kp-sidenav-expand-on-hover gives the words back while the pointer is over it.
data-kp-sidenav-slim-toggleOn a button, collapses the rail to its icons and expands it again. aria-controls names the panel; without it the button drives every rail on the page, the way data-kp-sidenav-toggle does. The module keeps aria-expanded on it, and names a button that has no words of its own.
data-kp-sidenav-slim-hideOn an element inside, hides it in the rail; data-kp-sidenav-slim-show is its other half, so a wordmark can become a monogram instead of only disappearing.
data-kp-sidenav-accordionOne category open at a time. Without it they are independent.
data-kp-sidenav-backdropfalse takes the backdrop away in over mode; data-kp-sidenav-backdrop-class puts your own class on it.
data-kp-sidenav-close-on-escfalse keeps Escape from closing it. data-kp-sidenav-focus-trap="false" lets the focus leave; data-kp-sidenav-lock-scroll holds the page still while it is open.
data-kp-rememberA name. Name the panel and its folded groups, its open state and its rail survive a reload; name a .kp-sidenav__category as well and that group keeps its state under its own name. The same attribute is what the accordion, the tree, the split pane and the data table remember by — one mechanism, js/remember.js, and the key it composes is kp-remember:<component>:<name>:<slot>. Leave it off and this package writes nothing into your storage.
data-kp-sidenav-rememberThe older spelling of data-kp-remember, still read. New markup uses data-kp-remember.
kp-remember-clashOn the element refused a memory because another element of the same component already answers to that name: `{ name, component, other }`. It keeps its markup default and writes nothing; the first one to attach owns the key.
--kp-sidenav-inset-blockWhere a covering panel starts and ends, one value or two. A page that keeps its own bar says `3rem 0` and the panel begins under it instead of sliding beneath it.
never sidewaysThe panel clips its own horizontal overflow and a long label ends in an ellipsis, so a count or a badge at the end of a row is never pushed out of reach. A navigation you have to scroll sideways is one you cannot read.
.kp-sidenav__scrollThe part that scrolls. The header and the footer do not, so a long navigation in a short window keeps its title and its account row in view.
.kp-sidenav__categoryA heading that folds, with .kp-sidenav__category-toggle and .kp-sidenav__submenu inside it. The open height is a grid track, so it animates without anything measuring it.
--kp-sidenav-width-narrowThe panel’s width while data-kp-sidenav-narrow is on it (below the data-kp-sidenav-over-below step, when it lies over the page); unset it keeps the panel’s ordinary width. Off by default.
data-kp-sidenav-variant="floating"The panel detached from the edge as a card of its own: a gap all round, the theme’s radius, edge and shadow, each its own token below. In push, over and a reserved gutter; shut it leaves nothing on the screen. React: variant="floating".
data-kp-sidenav-variant="inset"The panel stays flush with the window and the content becomes a rounded card beside it (the gap, radius and edge are tokens below); the module marks the content data-kp-sidenav-inset. Nothing dims. React: variant="inset".
--kp-sidenav-peekA length, off by default: a shut push or over panel keeps a thin .kp-sidenav__peek strip of that width at its edge, in the muted surface (its own token). A push page keeps that strip’s room; an over panel lies over the page by that width only. The strip is not a control.
data-kp-sidenav-densitycompact or comfortable (unset is the middle): sets only the panel’s own link padding, link size, icon size and padding tokens, rows of 29, 37 and 45 px. React: density="compact|default|comfortable".
.kp-sidenav__row / .kp-sidenav__actionThe list item that holds a link and a .kp-icon-button.kp-sidenav__action as siblings: the button is reached by Tab after its link, has its own focus ring, does not open the link, follows the row’s height and hides in the collapsed rail. Its size and its gap to the link are tokens below. Give it an aria-label that names its row.
data-kp-sidenav-cancelablekp-sidenav-toggle and kp-sidenav-slim fire before the change, cancelable, with detail.reason (toggler, escape, backdrop, shortcut, api); preventDefault() leaves the state and kp-sidenav-prevented fires (kind, the state asked for, reason, source: listener). A layout’s own breakpoint, mode and init changes are told, never asked. React: cancelable with onSidenavToggle, onSidenavSlim and onSidenavPrevented.
data-kp-sidenav-resizableA .kp-sidenav__resizer (role separator, aria-valuemin, -max, -now) inside the panel on the edge that faces the content, moved by pointer or arrow keys (16 px a press, Shift five times that), written to the panel’s width token and kept with data-kp-remember; a double press clears it. data-kp-sidenav-min-width (default 12rem) and -max-width (default 32rem) bound it; not in the collapsed rail. React: resizable, minWidth, maxWidth.
data-kp-sidenav-backdrop="static"The backdrop shows and a click on it does not close: the panel is pulled back and let go (data-kp-sidenav-refused meanwhile; the travel, time and curve are tokens below, which a register may set), and kp-sidenav-prevented fires with source "static". React: backdrop="static".
data-kp-sidenav-close-on-backdropfalse makes a click on the backdrop do nothing, silently; the sibling of data-kp-sidenav-close-on-esc. Only where a backdrop exists. React: closeOnBackdrop={false}.
pastelIn pastel an over or push panel draws its own plate and pass and lands on it; a panel on the end edge swings from its top-end corner. Widths and groups move on the landing held at its value, so a size never passes it, and the panel closes as it opened, backwards. The launcher and the rows are pastel’s buttons.
