Ways to sign in

The list of ways to sign in: passkeys, magic links, passwords and tokens. One row each with its icon, its name, when it was added and, in words, when it was last used; a “This device” badge on the one in use; Rename and Remove in two fixed columns. The only way in is protected from removal.

When to use it

An account page. For a list of API keys use the secret and copyable parts; for a plain table of records, the data table.

Accessibility

  • Built in — a refusal is said beside the button, never only by the button being dead; the exact moment of last use is in the tooltip, in Europe/Brussels; the list is sorted with the one in use on top, then by last use.
  • Yours — pass `remove` and `rename` that call the server and reject with a sentence, or handle the cancelable `kp-passkeys-remove` and `kp-passkeys-rename` yourself.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-protect-last`refuse` (default: Remove stays held off with the reason), `confirm` (through behind a typed name and a warning) or `off`.
data-kp-confirmHow a removal is confirmed: `dialog` (default) or `type-name`. The one in use always needs the exact name typed.
data-kp-refuse-lookHow the refusal reads: `button` (default: Remove stays on the row, held off, reason under the name) or `note` (the reason takes its place).
data-kp-mark-current`off` leaves the “This device” badge out.
data-kp-never-used-afterDays (90): a way not used for that long is flagged as a candidate to remove; 0 never flags.
sortPasskeys, unusedDays and passkeyOptions`sortPasskeys(rows)` is the list’s order (the one in use first, then the most recently used, a way never used last); `unusedDays(row, now, limit)` is the whole days a way has gone unused, or `null` when it was used within the limit or the flag is off; `passkeyOptions(el, spec)` is the options as drawn: the spec first, then the attribute, then the default.
kp-passkeys-register, -retry, -remove and -renameRegister and Retry are the buttons of the empty and failed lists. Remove (`{ key, row }`) and Rename (`{ key, row, label }`) are cancelable and fire only when the page gave no `remove` or `rename`: `preventDefault` means the page does it and calls `setPasskeys` again.
PASSKEY_KINDS and the `passkeys…` stringsThe four kinds, each with one glyph from one set; and every word of the list (the badge, the last-used sentence, the refusals, the dialogs and the states) is in the dictionary under `passkeys…`.
states`loading` keeps the rows’ geometry as waiting rows; `empty` (“No passkey registered yet” and Register) and `error` (Retry) fill the list’s whole footprint; `insecure` puts a warning above the list, because passkeys need https.
