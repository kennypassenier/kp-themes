Bar in a table cell

A value with a proportional bar inside a table cell: the meter and its figure, the figure in a column of its own on the end edge, so a column of them lines up its bars and its numbers. `data-kp-bar="0.62"` on the span is enough (`data-kp-bar-text`, `-tone`, `-mark`, `-label`, `-loading`, `-warn` and `-bad` refine it); `setBarCell(span, { value, text })` in js/barcell.js does the same from script. From 0.75 the fill is the warning colour, from 0.9 the destructive one. A data table sorts a cell by its `data-kp-sort-value` when it has one, and `setBarCell` writes it.

When to use it

In a table where the share of something is the point of a column: capacity per stack, how full a disk is. Not alone on a page (use the meter).

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

These are the framework-free channel's props. A selector is how a module finds an element, a read is a value you set on it, and a written one is state the module puts back for the stylesheet and for you to read.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the bar is the package’s meter: `role="meter"` with a value text that names the real share, past 100 % too.
  • Yours — keep the figure beside the bar: colour and length are not the only carrier.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-bar-cell--startThe figure before the bar.
data-kp-barThe share, 0 up (1 is full; above 1 the bar is full and says so); empty is not measured.
data-kp-bar-textThe words beside the bar; without them, the rounded percentage.
data-kp-bar-tone`warning`, `destructive` or `none`; without it the share decides.
data-kp-bar-warnThe share from which the fill is the warning colour (0.75).
data-kp-bar-badThe share from which the fill is the destructive colour (0.9).
data-kp-bar-markA tick on the bar: a limit, a target.
data-kp-bar-labelWhat the share is, for a screen reader.
data-kp-bar-loadingThe bar is the busy track at its own size and the figure is empty.
