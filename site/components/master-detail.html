Master and detail panes

A list and its detail side by side, folding to one pane on a phone. It extends the split with fixed columns: the list, the detail and an optional aside. The detail has four states, and an empty or failed area is drawn with the shared state plate: centred, at the heading size, filling the whole pane.

When to use it

A page with a list of things and the thing itself: secrets, dossiers, messages. For a draggable divider alone use the split; for a table use the data table.

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the list is a listbox: arrows move the selection without the focus leaving, Home and End jump, Enter and Space open the detail when folded. Focus moves to the detail’s heading on open and back to the row on Back; Esc in the detail returns.
  • Yours — load the detail on `kp-pane-open`, write the router’s item into the address, and name every pane.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
the script`attachMasterDetails(root, { history })` wires every `[data-kp-master-detail]` and returns a detach; `masterDetail(el)` answers the handle (`key()`, `select(key)`, `open(key)`, `close()`, `narrow()`, `setState()`); `selectPane`, `openPane` and `closePane` are the same moves by element. `MASTER_DETAIL` is the selector; `PANE_WIDTHS`, `PANE_ASIDE_WIDTH`, `PANE_FOLD_AT` and `PANE_ASIDE_FOLD_AT` are the defaults behind the attributes.
events`kp-pane-select` when the selection moves (arrow keys, a click), `kp-pane-open` when the detail is opened (the page loads it and its router writes the address), `kp-pane-close` when the detail or the aside goes away, `kp-pane-retry` for Retry in a failed detail; the names are `PANE_SELECT_EVENT`, `PANE_OPEN_EVENT`, `PANE_CLOSE_EVENT` and `PANE_RETRY_EVENT`.
.kp-pane__list / __head / __back / __body / __state / __detail / __busyThe parts: the listbox, the head of a pane, the Back button of the folded layout, the detail’s body, the plate that fills it in a state, the detail itself and the busy card laid over it. The panes are marked by `data-kp-pane="list|detail|aside"`, not by a class.
statePlate(doc, options)The shared plate (js/state-plate.js) for any empty or failed area; `actionAttribute` writes a data attribute on the action so a container can listen for it by delegation. The action sits at the control size, not the plate’s heading size.
data-kp-pane-widthsThe columns: `17rem 1fr`, and a third such as `24rem` when there is an aside.
data-kp-fold-atThe layout width below which the panes fold; 48rem.
data-kp-aside-fold-atThe width below which the aside moves under the other panes; 90rem.
data-kp-narrowWhat a phone shows: `one-pane` (default, with a Back button), `sheet` or `stack`.
data-kp-resizablePresent: a separator between the list and the detail drags like the split’s; add `data-kp-remember` to keep the width.
data-kp-pane-history`off` leaves the browser’s history alone.
data-kp-aside-closableEsc in the aside closes it.
states of the detail`empty` (“Pick an item on the left”), `loading` (the busy card over the detail only; the list stays usable), `error` (the destructive plate with Retry, over the whole master-detail, unless a component in the detail already shows its own failed plate: the innermost plate wins, the root then reads `data-kp-error-own` and nothing is drawn over it) and `ready`.
.kp-stateplateThe plate an empty or failed area is drawn with: the title at the heading size and a normal-size action, centred in the whole box it is given.
