Matrix

Source by destination: a cell per pair that says its state in a plate, a word or a glyph (`data-kp-glyph`), with the pair with itself dashed and inert (`self`, `data-kp-inert`). A red ring (`data-kp-ringed`) means the traffic stopped on the way out of the source; a dashed outline (`data-kp-mark`) marks the cells a rule being read touches. The headers are buttons that light their row and their column (`data-kp-lit` on the matrix, `data-kp-hot` on the cells they touch) and stay in place while the grid scrolls inside its own box. One cell can be pinned (`aria-pressed`); `data-kp-matrix-select="follow"` pins the one the arrow keys land on, and `data-kp-matrix-owned` leaves the state to the page. `setMatrix(el, spec)` in js/heat.js writes it.

When to use it

For a table of pairs where the state of each pair matters more than a number: which service may reach which, who shares with whom, which version fits which platform.

Accessibility

  • Built in — a `grid` with row and column headers and one tab stop; arrows move between the cells that can be pressed and wrap round; Escape turns the lit names off (`kp-matrix-light`); a pinned cell sends `kp-matrix-pick`.
  • Built in — a state is a plate, a word or a glyph, and the cell’s name says the pair and its state.
  • Yours — give every cell a `label` that says its state in words, and say what is pinned in a live region.
