Menu button

A button that opens every other action of a page or a thing, grouped under small muted capitals, each entry its label over a one-line hint. It is the package’s `.kp-menu` with `.kp-menu__item` entries, made rich (`.kp-menu--rich`) and wired by js/menu-button.js: the keys of a menu, a fill that waits while the menu is open, and an action that cannot be used now that stays in the list and says why.

When to use it

For the rare actions on a page or a record, behind a “More ▾” at the end of its header, when each needs a word of explanation. Not for the one or two actions used every day, which are buttons; not for navigation, which reads better as links; and not for a plain list of short commands, which the dropdown menu does with less.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the APG menu button: `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the button; ↓ or ↑ opens it on the first or last entry; ↓ ↑ wrap, Home and End, and a letter jumps to the next entry that starts with it.
  • Built in — Esc closes it and puts the focus back on the button, and the page’s own Escape listeners hear nothing; Tab closes it and moves on; a click outside closes it.
  • Built in — an entry that cannot be used stays focusable with `aria-disabled`, and its hint and reason are its description, so a keyboard reader learns why.
  • Built in — every word the menu says is in the dictionary (`menuLoading`, `menuEmpty`); `attachMenuButtons(root, { strings })` overrides them.
  • Yours — name the menu (`aria-label`) and say in the button that it opens one (“More ▾”).
  • Yours — put a destructive entry behind the same confirmation you would give a destructive button.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-menu-buttonThe wrapper, `[data-kp-menu-button]`: a button, then its `[role="menu"]`. The menu hangs under the button at its end edge.
.kp-menu--richThe menu itself, a `.kp-menu`: wide enough for a label over a hint, and past its maximum height it scrolls inside itself.
.kp-menu__group, __headingA group of entries and its heading in small muted capitals, which names the `role="group"`. A menu of one entry, or a nameless group, has no heading.
.kp-menu__label, __hintAn entry’s label over its one-line hint; every label and every hint start at one edge.
.kp-menu__reasonUnder the hint of an entry that cannot be used now (`aria-disabled`), in italics: why. The entry keeps its focus and does nothing.
.kp-menu__item--destructiveA destructive entry: its label in the destructive colour.
loading`setMenu(wrapper, "loading")`: one grey entry at the height of a real one, the menu `aria-busy`.
emptyNo entries: the button stays, grey, and its title says why; `data-kp-menu-empty="hide"` takes it away instead.
in a page headerIn a `.kp-page-header` 40rem wide or narrower, the open menu spans the header’s buttons, inside the screen.
a refill while openWaits until the menu closes, so a live update never moves an entry under the pointer or the focus; a fill that shows the same is skipped.
