Attention band

One alert per problem, worst first, each with the action that fixes it. Each is a soft tint of its severity with a coloured edge, the page’s own text colour and a solid round icon: calm, still unmistakable. With nothing wrong the band takes no room at all. `attachAttention()` in js/attention.js keeps the order in the page itself, so a screen reader meets the problems in the order the eye does.

When to use it

At the top of a dashboard, for what needs a person now. Not for a message about the action just taken, which is a toast, and never for an “all is well” line: an empty band is the all-clear.

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the DOM order is the severity order, so reading order and visual order agree.
  • Built in — the severity is a word for a screen reader (the hidden prefix) and an icon, not only a colour.
  • Built in — the folded problems stay in the band and in its order; the toggle says how many it holds back and whether they are shown, and keeps the focus when pressed.
  • Yours — `role="alert"` for a critical problem that arrives while the reader is on the page, `role="status"` for the rest; and the hidden prefix in your words.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-attentionThe band: a region of items one under the other; with no item it is not drawn.
.kp-attention__itemOne problem, on a `.kp-alert` of its tone: a soft tint of the severity, a thick edge of it, the page’s own text colour.
data-kp-severity`critical`, `warning` or `info`: the order and the tint.
data-kp-attention-maxA number of problems: the worst that many show, the rest fold behind one toggle. Read again when it changes; none or 0 folds nothing.
.kp-attention__moreThe toggle the cap writes, last on the band: “Show 4 more” with the exact number, then “Show fewer”; `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` the band. Hidden while nothing is over the cap.
data-kp-folded / data-kp-attention-expandedWritten by the script, never by you: on a problem folded away, and on the band while the folded problems are shown.
kp-attention-toggleFired on the band at each press of the toggle: `{ expanded, shown, total }`.
.kp-attention__icon, __text, __actionsA solid round icon, the problem in a strong line and a sentence, and the action that fixes it. In a band under 34rem the actions go under the words.

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
