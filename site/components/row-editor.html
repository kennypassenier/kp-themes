Row editor

Rows of controls that a person adds and removes: the times a medicine is taken, each with its days and its dose. `data-kp-rows` on a `.kp-rows`; js/row-editor.js (`attachRowEditors`, and `js/auto.js` attaches it on its own) copies a `<template data-kp-rows-template>` for every new row and attaches the package’s controls inside it. The labels stand once, in a head row over the columns; every row is a subgrid of the same tracks, so the times, the days and the doses of all rows line up, and the remove button is the last cell. Under 40rem of its own width the head goes and each cell carries its own label: every row is a card.

When to use it

For a small, ordered set of similar entries that belong to one thing and are edited together, on its form: dose slots, reminder times, phone numbers. For a list of independent things that are saved one by one, use list rows or a data table.

Accessibility

  • Built in — every remove button is named after its unit and place (“Remove time 2”), and a hidden status line says “Added time 3. 3 in all.” or “Removed time 2. 1 left.” as it happens.
  • Built in — a new row takes the focus on its first control; a removed row hands it to the row now in its place, else to the add button, so the focus never falls to the page.
  • Built in — a button at a bound is `aria-disabled` with the reason in the line under the rows, so it can still be reached and its reason read.
  • Yours — name the editor (`aria-label` or a heading it is labelled by), and give each control in the template a label of its own: the head row is hidden from screen readers.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-rowsOn the host: makes it a row editor. Options, each with a default, are switchable at runtime: `data-kp-rows-min` (the fewest rows, 0), `data-kp-rows-max` (the most, none) and `data-kp-rows-unit` (the unit’s two words, “time,times”; the dictionary’s “row,rows” otherwise).
data-kp-rows-attach="none"The page attaches the controls of a new row itself; by default the module attaches every package control in it, as the page’s boot did for the first rows.
data-kp-rows-list / -templateThe `ol.kp-rows__list` that holds the rows (written when left out) and the `template` a new row is copied from; `{n}` in it is replaced by a number that is never reused.
data-kp-rows-add / -removeThe add button and a row’s remove button. Leave them out and the module writes them; a button of your own keeps its words (one left empty gets the dictionary’s, marked `data-kp-rows-written`). At a bound the button is `aria-disabled` and stays in the tab order.
data-kp-rows-emptyWritten on the host while it holds no row: the head goes and the empty plate (`.kp-rows__empty`, the package’s state plate) holds the room of three rows.
.kp-rows__head / __labelThe labels once over the columns, written from the first row’s (or the template’s) `data-label`s; hidden from a screen reader, which hears each control’s own name.
.kp-rows__row / __cellOne row, a subgrid of the editor’s tracks, and one cell; `data-label` on a cell is its label in the head and, as a card, above the cell.
.kp-rows__removeThe remove button, last in the row: a square “×” named after its unit and place, “Remove time 2”.
.kp-rows__statusThe line under the rows: why adding or removing is off at a bound, and empty otherwise.
kp-rows-add / kp-rows-remove / kp-rows-changeEvents on the host, bubbling: the first two with `{ row, index, count }`, then `kp-rows-change` with `{ count, rows }` after either.
addEditorRow / removeEditorRow / editorRowsOfThe calls behind the buttons: add a row (the new row, or null at the most), remove a row (nothing at the fewest), and the rows in order.
as cardsUnder 40rem of the editor’s own width: no head row, each row a bordered card, each cell under its own label, the remove button at the end of the card.

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
