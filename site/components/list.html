List rows

A list of things that each carry a few controls: a name with a line under it, a status that can hold several badges, a stepper and a switch. It is not a table: `.kp-list` is one grid of four named columns (identity, status, stepper, switch) and every `.kp-listrow` is a subgrid of it, so the four parts of every row stand on the same four edges whatever each holds. A row without a status leaves its column empty. Under 40rem of the list’s own width each row is a card: the identity and the status on full lines, the stepper filling the line beside the switch. It is pure CSS; the controls inside are the package’s own.

When to use it

For a short list of things a person manages in place: the medicines in a cabinet with their stock, the rooms of a house with a switch each. When the rows are data to sort, filter or compare, use the data table; when a row only opens a page, a plain link list is enough.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the reading order is the row order: name, status, stepper, switch, in the markup as on the screen, in rows and in cards.
  • Built in — the muted look dims only the words; the switch, not the opacity, says that the row is off.
  • Yours — `role="list"` on the `ul` (a styled list loses its list role in some browsers) and an `aria-label` that names it.
  • Yours — name every control after its row: “Stock of Ibuprofen 400 mg”, “Active: Ibuprofen 400 mg”, so a screen reader that jumps between controls knows which row it is in.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-listThe list: a `ul` of four named column tracks on the card ground, with a boundary and the theme’s corner. It measures its own width, so a list in a side panel is cards while one beside it is rows.
.kp-listrowOne row, a subgrid of the list; rows are divided by a hairline.
.kp-listrow__identity, __name, __metaThe identity column: the name in a strong line and the quiet line under it (form, what it is for). Long words wrap rather than push the columns.
.kp-listrow__statusThe status column; it holds a badge or a `.kp-badges` run. Left empty, the column stays and the card leaves out its line.
.kp-listrow__stepperThe stepper column, for a `.kp-quantity` with `data-kp-quantity-fill`, which then fills it so every row’s minus and plus line up.
.kp-listrow__switchThe switch at the end edge; in a card it shares the stepper’s line and wraps under it when the line is too short for both.
.kp-listrow--mutedA row that is off (an inactive medicine): the name and the status are quieted, the controls keep their look so they still read as usable.
as cardsUnder 40rem of the list’s own width: the identity and the status each on a full line, the stepper and the switch on the line under them.
loading, empty, failedThe card’s own states (`.kp-card[data-kp-state]`, js/card-state.js) over the list in its data, holding the list’s footprint.
