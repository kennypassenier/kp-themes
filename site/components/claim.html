Who brings it

A claim on every row of a checklist: who brings the item (their mark and name, several people allowed) or, when nobody does, a loud gap, with one button that takes the item or lets go.

When to use it

A packing list, a chore list or a potluck where each item needs a person. For a list that is only ticked off use the plain checklist.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the gap is a glyph and words, never colour alone; the people are a named group; the button is pressed when you bring it; a change and a refusal are announced politely.
  • Yours — answer `kp-claim-change` with `detail.settle(promise)`, and say who is looking with `setClaimViewer`.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-claim-filter`all` (default), `gaps` or `mine`: the chip that is on; `false` hides the chips. `claimFilter` and `setClaimFilter` read and set it; `kp-claim-filter` reports a change.
data-kp-claim-undo-msHow long Undo is offered after letting go (6000; 0 for none).
data-kp-claim-self-labelThe words of the take face of the button, in place of the dictionary’s.
data-kp-claim-keys`false` turns the keys off (`c` on a focused row takes or lets go, Shift C goes to the gaps).
data-kp-claim-copyPresent: “Copy the open list” in the bar.
data-kp-claim-disabledPresent: nobody can press; `data-kp-claim-disabled-reason` says why under the button.
data-kp-claim-viewerWho is looking, when the page writes it in the markup (`data-kp-claim-viewer-name` is the name); else `setClaimViewer`.
setClaim / setClaimPeople`setClaim(list, id, claim)` is a push update, another person took it while the page was open; `setClaimPeople(list, people)` gives the menu of assign mode its people.
data-kp-claim-mode`claim` (default, a button on each row) or `assign` (a person menu).
data-kp-claim-gap`loud` (default) or `quiet`.
data-kp-claim-gap-sort`keep` (default) or `top`.
data-kp-claim-person`both` (default), `name` or `idmark`.
data-kp-claim-maxHow many people may bring one item (0, no cap).
data-kp-claim-count`both` (default), `bar`, `group` or `none`.
data-kp-claim-take-allAdds Take every open item, behind Undo.
data-kp-claim-faceWhich face the button shows: `take`, `held` or `busy` (set by the module).

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
