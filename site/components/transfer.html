Transfer progress

A progress bar with a file name, “1.2 GB of 4.1 GB”, “12 MB/s” and “about 3 min left”. It extends the progress bar and the progress group. The app reports raw numbers; the component computes the speed and the time left, and aligns the columns of several transfers.

When to use it

An upload, a download or a copy of known size. For work with no size use the spinner; for steps use the step list.

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the bar carries the progress ARIA; a screen reader hears 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent, never every second; the figures are tabular so they do not jitter.
  • Yours — report real byte counts, answer `kp-transfer-retry` with a new try and pass the app’s own sentence in `failed`.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
the script`attachTransfers(root)` wires every `[data-kp-transfer]` and returns a detach; `setTransfer(row, { done, total, at, name, failed }, { now })` reports progress (the first report starts the transfer, a report that reaches `total` ends it); `resetTransfer(row)` clears a row for a new try; `transferRate(samples, windowS)` and `transferEta(seconds)` are the two calculations, exported for a page that draws its own reading. `TRANSFER` is the selector.
events`kp-transfer-done` `{ total, ms }`, `kp-transfer-retry` and `kp-transfer-leave` (`TRANSFER_DONE_EVENT`, `TRANSFER_RETRY_EVENT`, `TRANSFER_LEAVE_EVENT`), bubbling from the row.
.kp-progress__rate / __etaThe two parts the transfer adds to the progress bar’s label, track and value: “12 MB/s” and “about 3 min left”, in tabular figures and in columns shared by every row of the group.
.kp-transfer__failure / __retry / __voiceThe failed note with the app’s reason, its Retry, and the line only a screen reader hears (25, 50, 75 and 100 percent).
data-kp-transfer-nameThe file’s name, written in the label.
data-kp-rate-windowSeconds (5): the moving average; a single sample jumps.
data-kp-eta-afterSeconds (3): no time left until that much has been measured (“estimating…”).
data-kp-stall-afterSeconds (10): no new byte for this long is “stalled”.
data-kp-bytes-base`1000` (default) or `1024`.
data-kp-showThe parts of the reading: `done,total,rate,eta,percent`, all by default.
data-kp-lingerSeconds a finished transfer stays; 30, 0 keeps it.
data-kp-transfer-unknownWritten on the row while the total is unknown: the bar is one full band that breathes in place instead of a busy sweep that restarts.
data-kp-transfer-settleWritten for a moment when the size turns out: the bar settles from where the breathing was into the finished bar.
data-kp-announce`state` (default) says 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent; `off`.
data-kp-fold-atOn the group: the width (46rem) below which the name stands on its own line and the numbers under the bar.
statesRunning, estimating, stalled (warning plate), done (“4.1 GB in 3 min 12 s”), failed (the app’s reason and Retry) and total unknown (an indeterminate bar).
