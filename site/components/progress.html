Progress

A bar for work whose end is known, drawn by each theme in its own hand — a track, a fill clipped to the value and a head on its leading edge — and one line high whatever the theme draws inside it.

When to use it

When you can say how far along something is: an upload, an import, a step count. Not for waiting on a server that will answer when it answers — that is the spinner, or the busy bar when the wait belongs to a task with a place on the page. Not as a rating or a gauge: a progress bar means work in flight.

These are the framework-free channel's props. A selector is how a module finds an element, a read is a value you set on it, and a written one is state the module puts back for the stylesheet and for you to read.

Accessibility

  • Built in — `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow` against `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`; js/progressbar.js keeps the paint in step with them, so the number read and the bar drawn cannot drift apart.
  • Built in — the busy bar carries no `aria-valuenow`, which is how a screen reader learns the amount is unknown.
  • Built in — with reduced motion asked for nothing inside the bar moves, in any theme, and the busy bar still reads as busy.
  • Yours — give it a name. A bar with no label is progress on something unspecified.
  • Yours — add `aria-valuetext` where a percentage is not the useful number (“3 of 12 files”).
  • Yours — say when it is finished; a full bar is silent.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-progressbarThe bar: one line high, as wide as its place up to a maximum, painting the share done from 0 to 1.
.kp-progressbar__trackThe ground the value is measured against; the theme draws it.
.kp-progressbar__fillThe share done, clipped to the value, so a pattern in it never stretches and only the clip moves.
.kp-progressbar__headThe mark on the fill’s leading edge; a theme gives it a shape or leaves it empty.
.kp-progressbar--mdThe medium size: one and a half times the small bar, the theme’s drawing grown with it, and the label and number beside it at the body size.
.kp-progressbar--lgThe large size: twice the small bar, for the one task a page waits on; the label and number one step above the body size.
[data-kp-indeterminate]Busy without an amount: a moving band, and diagonal stripes standing still under reduced motion.
.kp-progress__wrapThe bar with something beside it, on one centre line.
.kp-progress__valueThe number, quiet and small, after the bar.
.kp-progress-groupA set of bars in three columns — label, track, reading — so every track begins and ends in the same place [fix-64].
.kp-progress__labelThe word in front of a bar inside a group.
