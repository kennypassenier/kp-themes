Form grid

The one layout for forms, settings, key-value panels and button groups: four tracks, `label | control | value | actions`, shared by every row so labels, controls, the words about a value and its buttons each stand on one edge. Rows are `.kp-formgrid__row`; `.kp-formrows`, `.kp-kv`, `fieldset` and `form` placed inside it become subgrids of the same tracks. Under 36rem of the grid’s own width every row reads top to bottom.

When to use it

Any page that is a list of labelled things: a settings form, the facts of a job, a group of buttons under what they act on. Reach for form rows when the page is a single narrow column of independent values and needs no value or action track.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the grid is layout only: labels stay real labels, order in the markup is reading order, and a folded row keeps it.
  • Yours — name the grid (`aria-label`) when it is a landmark of the page, and tie hints and errors to the control with aria-describedby.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-formgrid__row / __row--buttonsOne row of the four tracks; the buttons row holds a group of buttons under what they act on, on the control edge.
.kp-formgrid__label / __control / __value / __actionsThe four cells: the label (a real `label` or a `span` named by the control), the control, the words about its value, and its buttons. A short control takes the short width; a field fills its track.
.kp-formgrid__buttons / __group / __heading / __noteA group of buttons, a labelled section of rows, its heading and a quiet note. `__fill` and `__short` set a control that fills its track or takes the short width.
subgrids`.kp-formrows`, `.kp-kv`, `fieldset` and `form` inside the grid become subgrids of its tracks; a `form.kp-form` placed directly in the grid takes the grid’s gap in every theme. A `data-kp-subgrid` list (attention, problems) shares the tracks too.
rows with a JSON fieldIn a row holding a `.kp-jsonfield` the actions cell is one tool row high (the button is centred on the tools) and the value text is centred on it too.
data-kp-formgrid-label`fit` (default: the label track is as wide as the longest label) or `wide`.
data-kp-formgrid-align`baseline` (default) or `center`: where the cells of a row stand.
data-kp-formgrid-stack`auto` (fold under 36rem of the grid), `always` or `never`.
data-kp-formgrid-lines`none` (default) or `lines`: a hairline between rows.
data-kp-formgrid-spanOn a cell: `control-value`, `wide` or `all`, the tracks it covers.
other components in a rowThe refresh bar inherits the grid’s column gap and its label takes the label track; the combobox’s status line moves into the value track; an anchor mark sits at the end of the label cell; the theme picker takes its baseline from its name.

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
