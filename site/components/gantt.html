Run timeline

One bar per run on a time axis, a lane per job, stack or host, and a line for now. `attachGantts(root)` in js/gantt.js fills `section.kp-gantt[data-kp-gantt]` with range buttons (`data-kp-gantt-ranges`, the one in force in `data-kp-gantt-range`), a Live button and the selected range; the page gives the lanes and the runs (`setGanttRuns`) and the state of the whole (`setGanttState`). A run is `queued` (dashed and hollow, from queueing to now), `running` (striped and growing to now), `ok` (solid) or `failed` (solid, taller, with a cap and a cross): the tone is never the only carrier. A running bar is placed by one custom property that moves every second, so the page repaints nothing. While the window is Live its right edge follows now (`data-kp-gantt-live`); dragging across the plot selects a range, shown in a chip with Zoom and Clear (`data-kp-gantt-zoom`, `data-kp-gantt-clear`). `data-kp-gantt-ghosts="2 3 2 1"` sets the rows of the loading lanes so loading and loaded are as tall.

When to use it

When a reader asks what ran when, how long it took and what failed: operations of the last hours or days, per job. Not for what will run (use the week agenda) and not for a series of values (use the chart).

Accessibility

  • Built in — one tab stop: left and right move along a lane, up and down to the nearest bar of the neighbouring lane, Home and End to the lane’s first and last, Enter and Space open the run; range, Live, Zoom and Clear are buttons.
  • Built in — every bar has an accessible name with its label, state and times; a run that changes state is announced in a polite status region.
  • Yours — give the timeline a name (`aria-label`), name every run so it reads without its colour, and give the new runs when the window moves.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-ganttMarks the timeline; `attachGantts` fills it.
data-kp-gantt-rangesThe range buttons, space separated: `1h 6h 24h 7d`.
data-kp-gantt-rangeThe range in force; it decides how much time the window shows.
data-kp-gantt-liveOn the Live button, pressed while the window follows now.
data-kp-gantt-zoomOn the Zoom button of the selection chip: the window becomes the selected range and stops following now.
data-kp-gantt-clearOn the Clear button of the selection chip: the selection is lifted.
data-kp-gantt-ghostsThe rows of each loading lane, space separated, so loading and loaded are as tall.
data-kp-gantt-idOn a bar: the id the page gave the run; reported when it is opened.
data-kp-stateOn a bar: `queued`, `running`, `ok` or `failed`. On the timeline: `loading`, `empty` or `error`.
data-kp-activeOn the selection chip while a range is selected.
data-kp-outOn the now-line when now lies outside the window; it is hidden.
data-kp-gantt-edgeOn an axis label too close to the end edge for its name: an hour label stands before its rule and ends at it in a register that flips it; a day button stands against the end, whole.
data-kp-gantt-daysOpt-in: each day named on the axis is a button (one tab stop, arrows, Home and End between them) that selects that whole day, 00:00 to 00:00 in the zone, as a brush would. It is `aria-pressed` while it is the selection and lifts it when pressed again; a window too short to hold a midnight names no day. `data-kp-gantt-crowded` marks an axis whose day names would touch, so they thin out.
seriesOn a run (`GanttRun.series`, 1 to 5): a finished run is inked in `--chart-1` to `--chart-5` instead of the success colour. Failed, running and queued keep their own look. `setGanttLegend(el, [{ series, label }])` names the series after the states; none takes them away.
setGanttRuns / setGanttState / setGanttWindow / setGanttSelectionThe page’s calls: the lanes and runs (repainted in place by id), the state of the whole, the window (`{ span, live, to }`, no event) and a selected range (`{ from, to }` or null).
the busy cardWhile loading, the data table’s loading card (`js/busy-card.js`) stands centred over the lanes, flat over a short area, and the runs are drawn once it has left.
a register’s repairsA register may hang the end-edge axis label inside the plot (the flip knob) and give an empty or failed timeline’s sentence a plate of its own (the state-ground knob); both are in the knobs below, and formal leaves them as they were approved.
setGanttPoints / setGanttKinds / setGanttZonesMoments instead of runs [kpadopt4]: a point (`id`, `lane`, `at`, `kind`, `label`, `detail`, `planned`) is a `.kp-gantt__point` mark in its lane, in the colour and shape of its kind (`data-kp-shape` dot, diamond, square, triangle or ring; `data-kp-series` or `data-kp-tone`), hollow when `data-kp-planned`. A kind is named in the legend (`.kp-gantt__mark`); the run states leave the legend when there are no runs. A zone is a thin band in its lane (`data-kp-lane-zone`) or a band behind every lane, named in the legend (`.kp-gantt__band`); `data-kp-zone` carries its id.
data-kp-cluster / data-kp-clusteredMarks closer than the eye can tell apart: the first carries the number it stands for and a count plate (`.kp-gantt__count`, “+2”), its name and card name them all; the others are `data-kp-clustered` and step aside. Opening it reports every point in `points`.
data-kp-name / data-kp-cardWritten on a mark: its own accessible name and card words, which a mark with a count joins.
data-kp-gantt-pointsOn the timeline: its hint speaks of marks, not bars.
allA range word: the window fits everything on the timeline and now, and stays fitted while the data changes.
href / subA lane with `href` is a link (`.kp-gantt__lane-link`); `sub` is a second line under its name (`.kp-gantt__sub`), and the lanes grow to hold it. `data-kp-lane-key` on the name is what it was drawn from.
.kp-gantt--lineOne lane, no toolbar, no lane names, no hint and no brush, the axis under the line, fitted to its data: the dated line under a journey stepper.
