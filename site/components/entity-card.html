Entity card

One card per thing (a stack, a host): its identity mark, name, state in words, a few exact figures, flags, two meters and its actions. Every card is the same five rows, kept whether a row holds anything or not, so every card in every state is exactly as high as the next.

When to use it

An overview of a handful to a few dozen things that each have a state and a few actions. For many rows to sort and compare, use the data table.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the name is the card’s one link and stretches over the card (one tab stop); every figure is an exact number; the “+2” and “More” are the package’s menu button with its keys; a ticked card is a checked box with its own name.
  • Yours — keep the key of a card stable, so a refill changes it in place.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
the script`attachEntityCards(root)` wires every grid and returns a detach; `setEntityGrid(grid, list)` fills it (a refill with the same key changes the words in place); `setEntity(card, data)` draws one card; `setEntityGridState(grid, state, { count, message, title, actionLabel })` puts the grid in a state. `ENTITY_GRID` and `ENTITY_CARD` are the selectors. The card’s data is `{ key, name, vmid, state, figures, flags, meters, actions, status, error }`, and an action may be `danger` or carry a `disabled` reason.
events`kp-entity-action` `{ key, id }`, `kp-entity-flag` `{ key, flag }`, `kp-entity-tick` `{ key, ticked }`, `kp-entity-retry` `{ key }` from a card, and `kp-entity-new` and `kp-entity-grid-retry` from the grid (the `ENTITY_…_EVENT` constants).
data-kp-entity-key / -link / -mark / -min / -narrowThe card’s stable key; the template of the name’s link (`{key}`, the whole card is its click target); the mark (`idmark` default, `icon` or `none`); on the grid, the least width of a card (22rem); `data-kp-entity-narrow`, which the script writes on the grid while it is narrower than 30rem: every action then goes into “More”.
data-kp-entity-actions / data-kp-entity-fitWritten by the script: `none` on a card with no action (it has no actions row), and on the grid the count of action buttons that fit now. A button that does not fit the card’s width goes into “More” for every card of the grid, in one place, and comes back as the grid widens.
.is-ticked / .is-cursorA ticked card and the card the keys j, k and x stand on (a ring); both are rows of a selection.
.kp-card--entityThe card: the same five rows in every state, so every card is as high as the next. `.kp-card--ghost` is the placeholder card of a loading grid.
.kp-entity__id / __figures / __flags / __meters / __actionsThe five rows. The identity row holds `__tick`, `__name` (the link), `__vmid` and `__state`; the figures row `__figure` (`__value`, `__none`); the flags row `__flag` and `__more-flags`, the menu button whose `__plus` is the exact “+2”; the meters row `__meter` (`__meter-label`, `__meter-value`); the actions row `__action` and `__more`, the “More” menu button. `__bone` is a loading row, `__retry` the Retry of a failed card and `__busy` the card laid over a loading grid.
data-kp-entity-metersHow many meter rows a card holds, 0 to 3; two by default.
data-kp-entity-flags-maxHow many flags show; the rest are an exact “+2” that opens the package’s own menu. Three by default.
data-kp-entity-inlineThe most actions that stand as buttons; the rest go in “More”. The buttons come only from the data: a card with no action has no row for them, and every card has as many columns as the grid’s busiest card. Under 30rem every action folds into “More”.
data-kp-selectableA tick at the start of the card, a row of a selection with the keys j, k and x.
data-kp-stateOn a card: `loading` (the same five rows as bones), `empty` (“not measured”) or `error` (a destructive state and Retry). On the grid: `loading` (skeleton cards under the busy card), `empty` or `error` (the package’s plates).
