Drawer

A panel at the end edge of the window, full height: a head with the title, the close button and one sentence, a body that scrolls, and a foot that stays. `.kp-drawer` lays out any element; a `dialog.kp-drawer` (with `.kp-dialog`) is placed at the edge and slides in.

When to use it

For Help, settings or the detail of one thing, kept beside the page rather than over its middle. Not for a decision that must be made first, which is a dialog, and not for navigation that is always there, which is a side navigation.

Accessibility

  • Built in — as a modal `dialog` it traps the focus, closes on Escape and returns the focus to its button: the browser’s, as every dialog’s.
  • Yours — name the drawer by its title (`aria-labelledby`) and give it a close button with a name.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
.kp-drawerThree rows: the head, the body (which scrolls) and the foot (which stays). On any element.
dialog.kp-drawerAt the end edge at the full height of the window, square, sliding in from the edge unless the reader asked for less motion.
.kp-drawer__head, __descThe title (a `.kp-dialog__title`, kept clear of the close button) and one sentence under it.
.kp-drawer__bodyWhat the drawer holds, in a column with room between; it scrolls inside itself.
.kp-drawer__footThe drawer’s action, always in view at the bottom.
