Notification centre

A counter with the exact number, a panel with the newest notices, a switch per source and “Quiet until 09:00” in plain words, plus the same list as a full Inbox page. A notice says what happened, since when, what it affects, what to do, and has a Fix button.

When to use it

An application that raises notices people must act on. For one passing message use the toast; for something wrong right here use the alert.

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

These are the framework-free channel's props. A selector is how a module finds an element, a read is a value you set on it, and a written one is state the module puts back for the stylesheet and for you to read.

Accessibility

  • Built in — new notices are announced politely and critical ones assertively, once; the counter has a label that says what it counts; on a phone the panel is a full-height sheet.
  • Yours — say in each notice what it affects and what to do, and answer `kp-notice-fix` with the fix.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
the script`attachNotices(root, options)` wires every `[data-kp-notices]` and returns a detach; `noticesOf(el)` answers the handle; `setNotices(el, { state, items, sources, quietUntil, error })` hands over the data. `NOTICES` is the selector and `NOTICE_SEVERITIES` the three levels (`critical`, `warning`, `info`).
events`kp-notice-fix` `{ key }`, `kp-notice-read` `{ key, read }`, `kp-notices-read-all`, `kp-notices-source` `{ source, muted }`, `kp-notices-retry`, `kp-notices-end-quiet` and `kp-notices-open-inbox`, bubbling from the root.
.kp-notices__trigger / __label / __count / __moonThe counter that opens the panel: its label, the exact number (a `.kp-badge`) and the moon of a quiet period.
.kp-notices__panel / __page / __head / __heading / __tools / __bodyThe panel (a `.kp-popover`, a full-height sheet on a phone) or, in the page look, the page; its head with the heading and the tools, and the body that holds the list or a state.
.kp-notices__quiet / __end-quiet / __read-all / __inbox“Quiet until 09:00” with End quiet, Mark all read and the link “All 37 in the Inbox”.
.kp-notices__list / __itemOne notice: an item with `data-kp-severity` and `data-kp-unread`, holding `__mark` (the severity as a mark and a word), `__title`, `__since`, `__consequence`, `__remedy` and `__actions` (`__fix`, `__mark-read`).
.kp-notices__sources / __state / __footThe switch per source, the plate of a loading, empty or failed list, and the foot of the panel.
data-kp-notices-look`both` (default: counter, panel and a link to the Inbox), `panel` (no link) or `page` (the list stands in the page, with the per-source switches).
data-kp-countThe number the counter shows when it is not the number of unread notices; exact, never “9+”.
data-kp-count-in-title`off` leaves the tab title alone; by default it gets “(5) ” in front.
data-kp-order`severity` (default: worst first, then newest) or `time`.
data-kp-read`explicit` (default: Mark read per row and Mark all read) or `on-open` (opening the panel reads all).
data-kp-panel-maxThe newest notices per severity in the panel; 10, then “All 37 in the Inbox”.
data-kp-toast-new`none`, `severe` (default: a new critical notice also toasts) or `all`.
data-kp-inbox-hrefWhere the Inbox link goes; it also fires `kp-notices-open-inbox`.
data-kp-keys`off` leaves the keys alone: g then i opens the Inbox, m marks the focused notice read.
states`ready`, `loading` (the counter holds its place), `empty` (“Nothing waiting”), `error` (Retry; the counter says “?”) and `quiet` (a moon, “Quiet until 09:00”, End quiet).
