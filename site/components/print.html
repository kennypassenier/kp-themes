Printing a list

Ctrl+P on a page with a checklist gives a clean black-and-white sheet: drawn square boxes a pen can tick, no buttons, menus, colour or decoration, groups that stay with their first items, and the date of printing at the foot. Every theme prints the same: paper has no theme.

When to use it

A packing list, a shopping list, a checklist that someone will carry about on paper.

Live example

Framework-free markup

Server-rendered markup with the behaviour attached by js/auto.js, the one module that does anything on import. The snippets above are that markup, printed from the same string the example renders.

These are the framework-free channel's props. A selector is how a module finds an element, a read is a value you set on it, and a written one is state the module puts back for the stylesheet and for you to read.

Props

No props: this unit is CSS and markup only.

Events

EventWhat it means
kp-print-prepareFired on the document just before the sheet is built: `{ sheets }`.

Knobs

This unit reads no knobs of its own.

Accessibility

  • Built in — a list that is still loading or failed prints its sentence, never a blank sheet; folded groups open for the paper and close again afterwards.
  • Yours — give the list a name and, where the page has no heading, a title.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-print`include` or `exclude`: a part the sheet keeps or drops. Navigation, menus, buttons and toasts are dropped by default.
data-kp-print-done`show` (default: a filled box with a tick, the text kept), `hide` or `strike`.
data-kp-print-box`square` (default) or `round`.
data-kp-print-groups`keep` (default) or `columns`: two columns for a long list.
data-kp-print-titleA title, or `auto`: the page's first heading.
data-kp-print-date`on` (default) or `off`: "Printed dd/mm/yyyy HH:MM" at the foot.
data-kp-print-notes`show` (default) or `hide`: the detail line of an item.
data-kp-print-notes-linesPresent: a dotted line under each item for handwritten notes.
data-kp-print-links`none` (default inside a checklist) or `url`: the address after a link.
data-kp-print-actionOn a button: pressing it prints the page.
data-kp-print-sheetMarks a list that is not a checklist but should get the sheet's title, state sentence and date.
kp-print-prepareFired on the document just before the sheet is built: `{ sheets }`.
--kp-print-boxThe size of the drawn box on paper: 1.1em, never under 4 mm, so a pen can tick it.
