Month heatmap

A month as a grid of days, each a plate in the colour of its state, with a short count under the number: a month of nightly backups, of shifts covered, of readings received. js/calendar.js builds it into a `section.kp-calendar[data-kp-calendar]`: the month’s title between ‹ Prev and Next ›, Today, a line for the state, a six-week `table[role=grid]` with one tab stop and the keys of a date grid, and a legend. The page hands it every day’s state by date; today is the day in Europe/Brussels, whatever the reader’s zone.

When to use it

Where a reader looks for the bad days in a month of one daily outcome and picks one to see why. Not for picking a date in a form, which is the date picker, and not for a value over time, which is a time chart.

Accessibility

  • Built in — one `table[role=grid]` named by the month’s title, seven column heads with the full weekday as their `abbr`, and one tab stop: arrows, Home/End, Page Up/Down (Shift: a year), Enter or Space to pick.
  • Built in — every day is named by its date as dd/mm/yyyy and the page’s words for it, so the colour is never the only carrier.
  • Built in — a live update repaints in place: the focus and the pick stay on the same element.
  • Yours — give each day words that say what its colour says (`label`), and name the calendar (`aria-label`) when the month’s title alone does not say what it counts.
  • Yours — put the detail of a picked day in a polite live region, or move the focus there, so a screen reader hears it.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
[data-kp-calendar]The calendar; `data-kp-calendar-month="YYYY-MM"` is the month it shows first (today’s by default) and `data-kp-calendar-selected="YYYY-MM-DD"` the day picked at first.
.kp-calendar-layoutThe calendar beside the page’s own detail of the picked day; one column under 45rem of its own width.
data-kp-tone="ok|warn|bad"A day on its status plate, the text in the plate’s own ink: all done, partly done, nothing done.
data-kp-tone="muted|none"Nothing to do that day (a muted plate), or nothing known about it (an outline).
data-kp-tone="future|before"A day still to come, or from before anything was kept: a dashed outline, no count.
data-kp-tone="loading"Every day while `setCalendarState(el, "loading", words)`: a muted plate that pulses, at rest under reduced motion.
data-kp-todayWritten on today’s day: an inner ring in the text colour.
td[aria-selected="true"]The picked day: an outer ring in the primary colour; the focus ring sits further out.
.kp-calendar__padThe neighbouring months’ days in the first and last weeks: their numbers, quiet and inert, so every week reads full.
.kp-calendar__stateThe line under the month: the words of `setCalendarState()`; an error’s after a dot in the destructive colour.
.kp-calendar__legendA swatch and its words per tone, from `setCalendarLegend(el, [{ tone, label }])`; hidden until given.
.kp-calendar--busy-whole / --busy-daysHow a loading month shows: one card with the theme’s spinner and the state’s words over the whole grid, or the theme’s spinner in every loading day. Without either, the loading days pulse.
pastel: the caterpillarIn pastel a loading day gets a pom-pom rim and two legs and rises in turn, with a head beside the last loading day of its week; the card over the whole month is a plate with its own pass, a head on its corner and stepping legs, stuck on as the landing and taken off as that backwards.
--kp-calendar-busy-wordsIn a register: `hold` keeps the card’s words on the card while it leaves, so the card that goes is the one that came (pastel); js/calendar.js writes `data-kp-busy-words-held` on the card until it has gone. Unset, the words clear at once.
data-kp-tones`calm` on a calendar, heat grid or uptime strip (`standard` is the default): `logged`, `quiet` and `paused` instead of ok, warn and bad.
data-kp-quiet-words`none` leaves the words of a quiet day out of its name (`label`, “nothing logged”, is the default); a quiet cell never prints a count.
data-kp-logged-fill`success` asks for the success plate instead of the theme’s accent.
data-kp-calm-map`none` passes ok, warn and bad through; by default ok is logged and the rest quiet, with one console warning per foreign tone.
data-kp-paused-range`2026-10-03/2026-10-14` (an end is optional): quiet days inside it are drawn as paused.
data-kp-legend`hide` leaves out the calm legend (Logged, Nothing logged, Paused).
kp-calendar-retry and setCalendarCaptionA calm month with nothing logged keeps its six quiet rows under a centred plate; a month that could not be read is dimmed under one with Retry, which fires `kp-calendar-retry`. `setCalendarCaption(el, text)` writes the page’s own sentence under the grid.
.kp-calendar__plate and .kp-calendar__captionThe plate that stands over a calm month with nothing logged or one that could not be read (its sentence and, when failed, a normal-size Retry), and the page’s own sentence under the grid. Both are made by the module.
calmTone, calmWord, inPausedRange and CALM_TONESjs/calm-tones.js on its own, for a page that draws its own cells: `calmTone(tone, el)` maps a tone to the calm vocabulary (ok to logged, the rest to quiet, one console warning per foreign tone), `calmWord(tone)` is “logged”, “nothing logged” or “paused” and never “missed”, `inPausedRange(range, iso)` says whether a `YYYY-MM-DD` lies in a `from/to` pause, and `CALM_TONES` lists the six tones: logged, quiet, paused, future, before, loading.
marks the module writes`data-kp-plate` (which plate stands over the grid) and `data-kp-dim` (a failed month’s grid).
