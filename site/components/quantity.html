Quantity

A number between a minus and a plus button with its unit after it, for servings and amounts. `data-kp-quantity` on the root with `-min`, `-max`, `-step`, `-unit`, `-unit-one`, `-label` and `-locale`; js/quantity.js (`attachQuantities`, `setQuantity`, `getQuantity`) writes the buttons, the field box and the unit, and fires `kp-quantity-change` with `{ value, previous, source }`. The number is a spin button that can be typed in the page’s language, with a comma or a point.

When to use it

For a small whole or stepped amount a person nudges up and down: portions, pieces, grams in steps. For a free number use a field; for a share of a range use a slider.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the number is a spin button with its value and unit in words; ArrowUp, ArrowDown, PageUp, PageDown, Home and End move it; the buttons are named after the label.
  • Built in — a button at a bound is marked `aria-disabled` and keeps the focus.
  • Yours — give it a label (`data-kp-quantity-label` or a `<label>` for the input).

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-quantity-stepThe step a press moves by; its decimals are the number’s decimals (0.5 shows one).
data-kp-quantity-disabledThe whole control dimmed and out of reach.
.kp-quantity--lgLarger buttons and number, for a screen at arm’s length.

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
