Visual timer

The time left as a shrinking disc, the Time Timer’s idea: no figures tick, a coloured disc grows smaller as the time goes, so the time left is seen at a glance. `attachTimerDiscs(root)` in js/timer-disc.js draws the face with twelve marks on its rim, the disc (drawn by one number, written only when its edge has moved a tenth of a degree), the name and length under it, one line for Paused or Time is up, and Start and Reset as the package’s buttons. Reset fills the disc in the theme’s own time and curve; at the end the face breathes three times. Under reduced motion the disc steps once a minute and nothing glides or breathes.

When to use it

For a span of time a person should feel rather than read: focus, a break, homework, a child’s turn. When the exact seconds matter, use the countdown in digits.

Accessibility

  • Built in — the face is an image named by the time left in whole minutes, so a screen reader is not told every second; a status region says when it starts, pauses and ends.
  • Built in — Start keeps one width for Start, Pause and Resume; Reset is off while there is nothing to reset; the end is a word and a ring as well as a breath, and nothing moves under reduced motion.
  • Yours — name the timer, and say elsewhere what happens at the end if anything does.

Variants and states

NameWhat it is
data-kp-timer-discThe length: `25m`, `1h30m`, `45s`, `90` (seconds) or `25:00`.
data-kp-timer-disc-nameThe timer’s name, under the face with its length.
data-kp-timer-disc-scaleMinutes for a whole turn of the face (`60`: the classic dial); without it the whole disc is the set length.
data-kp-timer-disc-disabledEvery button is off.
data-kp-timer-disc-stateSet by the script: `idle`, `running`, `paused` or `ended`.

Options written as attributes are read again when they change. A component of your own that reads options from attributes does the same with watchOptions(element, attributes, onChange) from js/live-options.js.
