sepia

FILE 09 · STATUS: reference demo · ROUND SIX

Cyberpunk shouts in signal yellow on a void ; sepia sits still and reads . The palette holds to one warm family, twenty-four to forty-five degrees , and nothing here is trying to be seen from across the room.

Open the file to clear the redactions. Each bar lifts left to right, the way ink lifts off a page under a blade — not the way a curtain opens.
