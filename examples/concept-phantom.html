Phantom

FILE 15 · STATUS: PREVIEW · CLEARANCE: KENNY

A phantom theme for kp-themes. Cyberpunk emits light; this one is print, not screen . The red is a plate, never a word , the headings are condensed italic capitals, and every gesture is a transform or a clip-path , which is why the flash threshold never enters.

Open the file to lift the censor plates. They shear off one after another, right edge first, the way a page is torn rather than faded.
