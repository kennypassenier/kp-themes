theme 26 · titanium · anodised

The colour is not paint. It is thickness.

Anodising adds no pigment. It grows an oxide film, and the thickness of that film decides which wavelength survives — so the blue-violet on every edge here really is interference, and it really does shift with the angle you look from. Underneath it, carbon twill : two diagonals crossing, the weave of the cloth the part was laid up on.
