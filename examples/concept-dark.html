Dark

FILE 15 · STATUS: PREVIEW · READ AT: NIGHT

The theme that replaced the old dark outright. The face sits at 5% lightness with a blue cast, never true black . The signal is a pale near-white, so the interface itself carries no hue and the one coloured thing on the page is the film . It runs along a control under the pointer, closes two brackets on a label, and turns as your hand does — because that is what interference does .

Open the log to clear the redactions. They lift right to left, one after another, the way a line settles once you have read enough of it to trust it.
