FILE 15 · STATUS: PREVIEW · READ AT: NIGHT

The ordinary dark theme, done properly. The ground sits at 8% lightness with a blue cast, never true black . The one ornament is a starfield of 112 irregular points, ten with a JWST-style shimmer, seen, softly . Everything else is quiet on purpose, because the whole job of this theme is two hours of reading .

Open the log to clear the redactions. They lift right to left, one after another, the way a line settles once you have read enough of it to trust it.
