Low Sun — Round Six

FILE 18 · STATUS: LIFT · REGISTER: SOLSTICE

Solstice keeps its palette close: primary and accent are the only pair in the set that share a hue family , the radius is the softest of the twenty-five , and reduced motion still holds — 240ms is the slowest resting duration of all eleven lifted themes .

Open the file to clear the marks. They lift right to left, one after another, the way a page catches the last of the light.
