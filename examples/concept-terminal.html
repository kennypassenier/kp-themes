dossier.terminal-06

The theme, on record

terminal

FILE 06 · STATUS: PREVIEW · CLEARANCE: KENNY

A phosphor CRT theme. Cyberpunk is loud and diagonal ; this one is quiet and gridded : eighty columns, twenty-four rows, and one hue at 120° . It does not merely look like a terminal — it accepts a terminal's constraints.

Open the file to clear the redactions. They repaint left to right, one cell at a time, the way a screen draws a line — not the way a curtain opens.
