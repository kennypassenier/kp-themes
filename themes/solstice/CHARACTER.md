# Solstice — character

The reference for how solstice looks and moves. Read the grammar before
adding or changing any solstice component; the inventory, families and
outliers below it record where the decided picks (2026-09-08 … 2026-10-07)
stand against that grammar, and the numbered proposals (proposal-1 …) are the
plan to bring them in line. Nothing here is applied yet: Kenny fixes the
grammar first, question by question, in `research/solstice-character` (the
titanium way, Kenny 2026-10-07 02:54: "waar jij eerst uitzoekt wat bij mekaar
past, wat niet past en dan zo voorstellen doet"; solstice fifth in the series,
Kenny's order of 04:23: cyberpunk, synthwave, solstice, brutalism, grotesk,
blueprint).

Sources measured (2026-10-07): every `research/character-*/decided.json`
pick for solstice resolved to its CSS (shape, loading, arrival, tone,
interaction, live, phone …) with a rule extractor over each demo's
stylesheets (`busy-c.css`, `round2-b.css`, `calendars.css`, `round2.css`,

`kpi-c.css`, `menu-c.css`, `meters.css`, `state-c.css`, `tiles-c.css`,

## 1. The solstice grammar (proposed)

The theme is **the low sun**: the last hour before dusk, or the first after
dawn, on warm charcoal. The light is low, so it comes **from below**: it
catches the foot of every plate and rises up it, warm. Everything that
happens is something the low sun does: what opens **catches the first light**
(kindled, from the foot up, overbright and cooling), what is new **rises**
like the sun clearing the horizon, what waits **watches the sun cross**, what
leaves is **eclipsed** and what arrives **comes out of the eclipse**, and a
warning **reddens the sky**. Nothing is neon, nothing glitters, nothing
hurries: solstice is the slowest theme in the set.

The parts have a fixed division of labour: **the sun is the clock, the
light at the foot is the state, the serif is the voice**. The sun (a small
amber disc) is the only body that travels: it crosses while the system
waits, it arcs as the spinner, it stands as the meter's mark and the current
step. The light at a plate's foot says how the part is: faint at rest, risen
when pointed at, kindled when it opens, red in a warning. The words are set
in Instrument Serif in sentence case, unhurried.

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| G1  | **Motion follows the sun's sine.** The height of the sun through a day is a sine, so is the light at dawn and dusk. What comes (opens, arrives, kindles, rises) moves on the sine's last quarter, `cubic-bezier(0.61, 1, 0.88, 1)` (ease-out-sine): it sets off at an even pace, with no ramp up and no burst (a starting slope of 1.6, against titanium's 4 and the standard curve's 0), and settles softly. What goes (closes, leaves, cools) moves on its first quarter, `cubic-bezier(0.12, 0, 0.39, 0)`, the same curve played back. The sun's own travel (a loop) is linear in its angle. Never an overshoot, never a spring, never a step. Not the register's standard curve `(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`: that is sepia's and deco's too (§7).                          |
| G2  | **Light comes up from the foot; the sun travels start → end.** What opens, arrives or is new comes up from its own foot; the sun and what crosses with it (the moon of the eclipse) travel inline-start → end, the way the sun crosses a page read from the start: in logical terms, mirrored by `dir`. Nothing falls from above, nothing slides in from a side edge, nothing jumps, nothing runs diagonal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| G3  | **What opens is kindled.** A menu, the header's menu, a popover, the dialog, the drawer, a toast and a tooltip open as the first light of the sun spreads up a wall: a dome of light grows from the middle of the panel's foot (`clip-path: ellipse(0 0 at 50% 100%)` → `ellipse(150% 150% at 50% 100%)`), and what it reaches is overbright and warm for a moment (`brightness(1.35) saturate(1.3)` → 1) and cools to its colour. 2 units (480 ms) on G1's curve. Closing is the dome sinking back into the foot. The panel never moves, never scales.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| G4  | **Durations count the register's 240 ms.** Contact 1 unit (240 ms, the register's `--fx-duration`, the slowest in the set). Opening and closing a panel 2 units (480 ms). A live change, arriving and leaving 3 units (720 ms). A loop 10 units (2400 ms, the spinner's and the busy bar's own period). Every duration a whole number of units; the band's 44 s pass is scenery and stays.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| G5  | **Amber acts, rust glows, cream reads, red fails.** `--primary` (amber, 6.4:1 on the card) is what a person does: an action, a pick, the lit foot under the pointer, focus, the sun. `--accent` (rust, 3.8:1 on the card) is the heat of the low light: the wash at a plate's foot, the embers, a chart's horizon, a link's resting underline; never an ink for words under 18 px. `--foreground` (cream) reads. `--destructive` only fails. A warning's light is its ink `--warning-foreground` (13.9:1), never the `--warning` plate (2.1:1 on the card) as a line, a glow or a sun. Every hue between 0° and 45° (the anatomy), statuses and chart series excepted.                                                                                                |
| G6  | **One soft radius.** Every plate and every control (card, tile, key figure, panel, menu, dialog, drawer, toast, button, field, tag, chip, tooltip, a day) takes the register's `--radius` (0.625 rem); a part nested inside another takes the outer radius less its inset, so the curves run concentric. Round only for the sun, the moon, an ember, a dot and the radio. No square corner, no pill, no notch, no chamfer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| G7  | **A plate is lit from below.** The charcoal plate (`--card`) is warmed at its foot: a rust wash rising from the foot edge to 40 % of its height (`--accent` at 22 % → transparent), and the foot edge itself catching the light (1 px of `--primary` at 55 %). Words stand above the wash, never in it. No texture, no grid, no bevel, no scan line, no glowing rim. A panel that opens (menu, popover, dialog, drawer) has the same foot light, fainter (12 %). The light never comes from above.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| G8  | **Pointing raises the sun under it.** The part pointed at catches more of the low light: its foot edge lights in full amber (2 px) and the wash climbs to 70 % of its height and warms to amber, in 1 unit. Nothing lifts or moves; the siblings are untouched. A link's underline turns from rust to amber and thickens to 2 px.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| G9  | **A new value rises.** When a value changes in place, it rises 0.6 em into its line from below its own baseline, clipped there, the sun clearing the horizon: 3 units (720 ms) on G1's curve, once; it is fully in place and readable after a fifth of the time. On a carrier without words (a state light, a trend line, a tile) the carrier rises the same way (the light, the line's last point). Declared as `--kp-update: risen` (a new idea for `js/update.js`). No glow, no flare, no swell, no jump.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| G10 | **Loading is the sun crossing.** On every waiting surface a small amber sun (0.55 rem, a warm glow round it) crosses along the surface's foot from the start to the end, rising out of the foot line as it leaves the start and setting into it at the end, a shallow arc: 10 units (2400 ms), linear in its travel. The content stands faint above it. Nothing breathes, pulses, sweeps or goes back and forth (§6).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| G11 | **The spinner is the sun's arc** (the signature): the sun rises over a short horizon, travels its arc and sets, one day in 10 units (2400 ms), linear in its angle. The busy progress bar keeps its sweep (the sun's head crossing the bar), at the same period.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| G12 | **Leaving is an eclipse, arriving the light returning.** What leaves is crossed start → end by the moon, a disc in the ground's own charcoal with an amber corona at its rim; as it covers the part the part dims to the ground and is gone: 3 units (720 ms) on G1's going curve. What arrives plays that backwards (reverse-close): the moon moves off toward the end and the part comes back into the light, overbright at the last edge (the diamond ring) and cooling, which is G3's first light. Not the morning mist (light's leave nearly exactly, §7).                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| G13 | **A warning reddens the sky.** A warning or failed part's foot light turns to its tone: the wash rising from its foot and the foot edge in the tone's light (a warning: its hue lit to 50 %, `hsl(from var(--warning) h s 50%)`, because the `--warning` plate itself is a dark ochre at 26 %; a failure: `--destructive`), the title or figure in the tone's ink (`--warning-foreground`, 13.9:1 on the card; for a failure the red lifted to 68 % lightness, `hsl(from var(--destructive) h s 68%)`, 5.4:1, where `--destructive` itself reads 4.1:1, under AA for words of a title's size; the alarm lifts it the same way). The change sits on a chip with the sun (☀) for up and the moon (☾) for down. It never flashes, jolts, bumps or frames the whole part. |
| G14 | **A press glows inside, like an ember blown on.** The pressed part's ground takes the `-active` token and an inset amber glow (0.5 rem, 40 %) for as long as it is held; the part does not move (titanium's press drops 1 px, §7). Focus is DI2's two-channel ring, with the sun risen under the part as under the pointer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| G15 | **The serif speaks, in sentence case.** Titles, figures, labels, legend words and ticks: Instrument Serif, sentence case, the figures at 400; nothing in capitals, nothing tracked wider than 0.02 em. Prose and buttons: Instrument Sans. The mono (`--theme-font-mono`) only for what a machine wrote: identifiers, timestamps, log lines. Not mono capitals for labels: that is every other dark theme's label (§7).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| G16 | **Every motif means one thing.** The sun: time passing (loading, the spinner, the busy bar, the current step, the meter's mark, the empty state). The light at the foot: a part's state (rest, pointed, open, warning). The sun and the moon as signs: up and down. The halo (a ring round a day): today and the pick. Embers: heat in a chart only (an area's speckle). The eclipse: leaving. The horizon: the divider and a chart's floor. The calibration wipe and the dossier stay where the concept demo put them (the headline, the dossier card).                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| G17 | **An element inside a composite is the theme's own element.** A button in a header, menu, drawer, tile or alert hovers, focuses and presses exactly like solstice's `.kp-button` (the sun risen under it, the two-channel ring, the ember press, the soft radius); a link like its link; a menu entry like `.kp-menu__item`; a tag like the badge. A composite may add its own accent around them, never replace their hover, focus ring, press, corner or font (§4b).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| G18 | **Every animation sits under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`;** the reduced pose is the finished picture (the sun standing at its noon over the foot, the panel lit, the value in place, the part gone), never a missing one. **The network graph is never a target** (Kenny, 2026-10-07 02:54): its shape (the low sun), loading (embers catching), arrival (kindled), focus (in the sun) and live update (a flare) stay as decided, in solstice and every theme.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## 6. The loading picture: the sun crosses

Not decided by a family pick (solstice has none); proposed from the key
figure's _The sun crosses_ (loading 3, research/character-kpi), the tiles'
of the same name, the meter's _The shadow lengthens_, the spinner's arc and
the busy bar's sweep, which already make the sun the one thing that travels
while solstice waits. What it draws, and how each part of it carries to
every other loading element:

1. **The sun.** A disc of 0.55 rem in `--primary` with a soft warm glow
   (`0 0 0.45rem` amber 55 %), as the key figure's pick draws it.
2. **The foot line.** The waiting surface's own foot edge is its horizon: a
   1 px line in `--border-strong`, the rust wash faint above it.
3. **The crossing.** The sun enters at the inline start just below the line,
   rises out of it, travels along the foot at a shallow height (0.35 rem
   above the line at the middle) and sets into the line at the end, then the
   next day starts: one crossing in 10 units (2400 ms), linear in its
   travel. Logical: in a right-to-left page it crosses from the right.
4. **A body, slowly.** The sun is the only part that moves; nothing
   breathes, pulses or glows in and out; it never comes back the way it
   went.
5. **On every waiting surface** (a tile, a key figure, the busy panel, a
   table, a menu entry, the chart's plot, the meter while it measures): the
   sun crosses along the surface's own foot; the content above it stays as
   it is, its placeholders faint.
6. **Skeleton lines**: the block's lines stand still in `--muted` with their
   rust cap; one sun crosses along the block's foot.
7. **Where there is no room for a crossing** (a day in the month): each day
   carries a small sun at its foot that rises and sets in place, the days of
   a week a tenth of the period apart, so the light travels across the week.
8. **The spinner** is the sun's whole arc (G11); the busy bar its sweep at
   the same period.
9. **Standing still** (reduced motion): the sun stands at its noon, in the
   middle of the foot.
10. **Exceptions.** The network graph keeps its embers catching (G18).
    Nothing else.
