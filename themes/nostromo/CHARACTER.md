# Nostromo — character

The reference for how nostromo looks and moves. Read the grammar before
adding or changing any nostromo component; the inventory, families and
outliers below it record where the decided picks (2026-10-05 … 2026-10-06)
stand against that grammar, and the numbered proposals (proposal-1 …) are the
plan to bring them in line. Nothing here is applied yet: Kenny fixes the
grammar first, question by question, in `research/nostromo-character` (the
titanium way, Kenny 2026-10-07 02:54: "waar jij eerst uitzoekt wat bij mekaar
past, wat niet past en dan zo voorstellen doet"; nostromo next, 03:50).

Sources measured (2026-10-07): every `research/character-*/decided.json`
pick for nostromo resolved to its CSS (shape, loading, arrival, tone,
interaction, live, phone …) with a rule extractor over each demo's
stylesheets, and to the option's own words in the demo's `demo.js` and round
files, `css/nostromo-register.css` (2318 lines, layers `kp.register` and
`kp.signature`), `css/themes.css` (nostromo tokens, the vent texture, every
theme's `--fx-ease`), `js/motion.js` (`themeMotion()`, reverse-close
opening), `themes/nostromo/anatomy.md`, `research/THEME_PROFILES.md`,

Every motion claim quotes its selector or keyframe and its values.

## 1. The nostromo grammar (proposed)

The theme is **the ship's console, 1979**: a moulded beige case with vent
slots, label tape, screws and an indicator lamp on every switch, and amber
CRT screens set into it where the ship reports. Everything that happens on
screen is something a console does: it is **switched on, struck on the tube,
printed, lit, computed, switched off**. Nothing grows, glides, floats,
bounces or is cut; nothing eases smoothly, because a 1979 display redraws in
whole frames.

The two materials have a fixed division of labour: **the case is what you
touch** (plates, keys, controls, tags: moulded plastic, lamps, label tape,
vents), **the screen is what the ship reports** (a chart's plot, the month,
the busy panel, the trend's plot, the graph: dark glass, amber phosphor,
scanlines). This is the reading of Kenny's picks taken together: the meter's
backlit vents and the tiles' indicator panel (the case), the amber CRT, the
CRT duty roster and MU/TH/UR's screen (the screen), the klaxon five times
(tone), the warm-up five times (arrival), and scope-12's lamp on every
control.

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| G1  | **Motion runs at the ship's frame clock.** Every one-shot motion follows the register's decelerating curve `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` but is shown in whole frames of 80 ms (12.5 a second, the ship's computer redrawing its screen; half a 1979 raster's picture rate, slow enough to see): the token `--kp-sig-no-frames` is a `linear()` with held values, one step per frame, so a move lands in a few visible frames and stops hard. A change of state (a lamp, an inversion, a tone) is one switch, `steps(1)`. A loop advances in whole frames. Never a spring, never an overshoot, never a smooth glide. Not the smooth curve alone: that is formal's, light's, brutalism's and grotesk's too (§7). |
| G2  | **The raster's order.** What is printed or drawn goes the way a screen is written: rows from the top, each row start → end, in logical terms (`inset-inline-start`, `clip-path` mirrored by `dir`). What is switched on strikes from its centre line (G3). Nothing slides in from a side, rises, falls or walks back.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| G3  | **What opens strikes on like a tube.** A menu, the header's menu, the dialog, the drawer and a toast open as a CRT warms: a bright line across the panel's middle opens to its full height (`clip-path: inset(50% 0 50% 0)` → `inset(0)`), overbright on the first frames (`brightness(1.7)` → 1), 4 frames (320 ms). Closing is the tube switched off (G12). By `clip-path`: the words are never squashed by `scale`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| G4  | **Durations count frames.** Contact 2 frames (160 ms, the register's `--fx-duration`). A strike (open, close, arrive) 4 frames (320 ms). Printing and a live change 8 frames (640 ms). Leaving 6 frames (480 ms, the register's leave). One loop 20 frames (1600 ms, `--kp-sig-no-loop`), the reel one turn in it. Every duration a whole number of 80 ms frames.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| G5  | **Ink acts, the lamp indicates, amber is the screen's.** `--primary` (the ink) is the only colour of what a person does: an action, a pick, focus, a pressed key. The LED orange (`--sidebar-primary`, drawn as the lens `--kp-sig-no-lens`) is light only: a lit lamp, a lit vent, a lit LED; never text on the case, never a frame, never an underline. The deeper orange (`--selected`, `--accent`) is the signal on the case (the meter's share, a link's underline). On a screen the phosphor is amber and cream (`--sidebar-primary`, `--sidebar-foreground`) on the dark glass. Green is a state (`--success`) only, never phosphor: green phosphor is terminal's.                                      |
| G6  | **Moulded plates are rounded, keys less, tape is cut, glass is a tube.** Plates (card, panel, menu, dialog, tile, key figure) `--radius` 0.75rem; keys and controls (button, field, chip, switch, the progress bezel, a legend key, a day) 0.3rem; label tape (tooltip, tag, badge, a change, the meter's mark, the empty state's title) 2 px; a screen's glass 0.875rem inside its bezel. Round only for lamps, reels and the tracker's rings. The focus ring never changes a corner.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| G7  | **The case and the screen.** The case: moulded beige, a raised plate with a lit top edge and a shadow under (`--kp-sig-no-raised`), a pressed key a well (`--kp-sig-no-well`), the page's vent ribs (`--fx-texture`, 1 px every 6 px); no scanlines and no glow on plastic. The screen: dark glass (`--sidebar-background`) in a thick beige bezel, an inner vignette, scanlines 1 px every 3 px, amber phosphor with its glow. A part is one or the other.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| G8  | **Every switch has its lamp** (scope-12). Every control (button, menu entry, link tile, key figure as a link or toggle, legend key, a day you can pick) carries a lamp at its start: unlit at rest (ink, 25 %), lit under the pointer and on focus (70 %), full when pressed. The control's lamp is lit in ink: it is the person's hand (G5).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| G9  | **A change lights the reading's lamp.** When a value changes in place, the lamp beside its label strikes on in the LED orange in one frame, holds, and goes out: 8 frames (640 ms), once. The figure, the word and the line never move, glow or invert, so the new value reads at once. The package declares it as nostromo's `--kp-update`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| G10 | **Loading is the lamp bank computing.** On every waiting surface the signature progress bar's own LED window (§6) runs along its foot, and its LEDs switch in a fixed, unordered pattern on the frame clock (a new pattern every 4 frames, five patterns, one period of 1600 ms): MU/TH/UR thinking. No direction, no sweep. The busy progress bar computes the same way. The spinner is the tape reel (G11); the network graph keeps its blips.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| G11 | **The spinner is the tape reel** (the register's): a dark tape pack, a three-spoke hub turning, the LED lens at its centre; one turn per loop (1600 ms), in frames. The one loading part that turns.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| G12 | **Leaving is the tube switched off, arriving is it struck on.** What leaves collapses to a bright line through its middle (3 frames), the line shrinks to a dot (2), the dot goes out (1): 480 ms. What arrives plays that backwards (reverse-close), which is G3's strike, so opening, arriving and leaving are one picture. No other theme leaves into a line and a dot (§7).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| G13 | **A warning sounds the klaxon** (Kenny's tone pick five times). The change sits on label tape (mono capitals, square, ‹▲ 6 %›); a warning or failed carrier is framed all round, 3 px, in its tone's ink (`--warning`, `--destructive`), as the bridge alarm frames the screen. A tone never jolts, flashes or moves the carrier.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| G14 | **A press pushes the key in.** A pressed part's moulding turns from raised to a well (`--kp-sig-no-well`: the shadow inside its top edge, the light under it, its ground one step into shadow) and its lamp is full; the part itself does not move (titanium's press drops 1 px, §7). Hover and press never translate, rotate or scale a part. Focus is DI2's two-channel ring with the lamp lit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| G15 | **Label tape names, Michroma counts, the screen reads out.** Labels, buttons, tags and the change are label tape (the system mono, capitals, tracked 0.12 em). Titles and the figures on the case are Michroma, the case's printed type. Everything on a screen is the mono in phosphor. Prose is Titillium Web. No other face.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| G16 | **Motifs only where the ship has them.** A lamp on every switch and reading; label tape on labels; vents on the case (the texture, the meter, the divider); screws only on what is screwed shut (the dialog, the drawer); scanlines, the block cursor and the tracker's ring only on a screen; hazard chevrons nowhere (never picked).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| G17 | **An element inside a composite is the theme's own element.** A button in a header, menu, drawer, tile or alert hovers, focuses and presses exactly like nostromo's `.kp-button`, lamp included; a link like its link; a menu entry like `.kp-menu__item` with its lamp; a tag like the label tape. A composite may add its own accent around them, never replace their hover, focus ring, press, corner or font (§4b).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| G18 | **Every animation sits under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`;** the reduced pose is the finished picture (the lamp bank standing in one pattern, the struck panel, the lit share), never a missing one. **The network graph is never a target** (Kenny, 2026-10-07 02:54): its shape (the CRT radar), loading (blips surface round the scope), arrival (switched on), focus (the warning lamp) and live update (the lamp blinks) stay as decided, in nostromo and every theme.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## 2. Inventory of the decided picks

As built on 2026-10-07. Legend for easing: **R** the register's curve
`--fx-ease` = `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` (also `--kp-sig-no-ease`), **Q**
the quick curve `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` (titanium's), **IO**
`ease-in-out` or a symmetric bezier, **O** `ease-out`, **I** `ease-in`, **L**
linear, **St** steps, **Sp** a spring or a keyframe past the rest pose.
Direction: S→E inline-start to end, ↓ top to bottom, ↕ from the centre line,
↑ up, ⟳ rotation, ∅ in place. Verdict: **fits** the grammar, or the outlier
it is (§4).

## 6. The loading picture (proposed): the lamp bank

Proposed, not decided: Kenny's family picks for nostromo are still open
(research/families/VERDICTS.md). The bank is built from the theme's own
signature progress bar (`css/nostromo-register.css`, `kp.signature`), the LED
bargraph that already draws a share; how each part of it carries to every
other loading element:

1. **The window.** The bar's dark LED window (`--sidebar-background`) in its
   moulded bezel; the LEDs are rounded-off lenses with 2 px of window between
   them and above and below (`--kp-np-led`). An unlit LED is
   `--sidebar-accent`; a lit one the LED orange with its hot top
   (`--sidebar-primary`, 18 lightness points brighter in its top 30 %) and a
   3 px glow.
2. **Whole LEDs only.** A lit LED is lit whole; nothing travels between
   them, nothing is half lit (the bar's share keeps its partly lit head; the
   bank does not).
3. **Computing.** Five fixed patterns of lit LEDs, five lit in every
   sixteen, each shown for 4 frames (320 ms): one period of 1600 ms
   (`--kp-sig-no-loop`). The demo builds them as one sixteen-LED tile
   shifted by 0, 6, 3, 13 and 9 LEDs, an order that never steps the same
   way twice, so nothing reads as travelling. No direction: MU/TH/UR
   thinking, not a light chasing. Each LED changes at most once per 320 ms,
   so it flashes at most 1.6 times a second (DI5).
4. **On every waiting surface** (a tile, a panel, a table row, a menu entry,
   a busy button or card, a chart's plot): the bank along the surface's foot
   at 0.6rem, the content above it
   standing faint. Skeleton lines are three such grooves, 4 frames apart.
   While the meter measures, its slots compute the same patterns.
5. **The busy progress bar** computes instead of standing still with every
   other LED lit.
6. **Where there is no room for a row** (a day in the month): the day's own
   lamp, in its corner, joins the bank: a week's lamps compute together.
7. **Standing still** (reduced motion): the bank shows its first pattern, lit,
   so it never reads as empty or as a share.
8. **Exceptions.** The spinner is the reel (G11). The network graph keeps its
   blips (G18). Nothing else.
